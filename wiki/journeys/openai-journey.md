# OpenAI: the accidental consumer company that chose work

**Thesis:** OpenAI won the biggest consumer audience in AI almost by accident, then in 2026 bet that the money is in doing people's work, not chatting with them.

## The whole arc

ChatGPT's launch in November 2022 was the first big turning point for AI chatbots ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]). More than 1 billion people now use it each month, and most do not pay ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT|ChatGPT Ad Limits]]). In 2026 OpenAI turned its desktop app into a work tool built on Codex, its coding agent ([[2026-07-14-The-OpenAI-Super-App-ChatGPT-Codex-Whither-Chat|The OpenAI Super App]]). It also loosened its tie to Microsoft and spread out across many clouds and chips ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents|Altman and Garman]]). Because of the Hugging Face attack, OpenAI moved about 10 percent of all its computing power, hundreds of megawatts of data centers, to safety work ([[2026-10-13-OpenAI-Scrambles-to-Fix-Safety-Issues-Before-Shipping-Its-Next-Flagship-Model|OpenAI Safety Scramble]]).

## The lesson

Thompson argues Anthropic chose right by focusing on businesses, because companies pay for productivity and most consumers do not ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]). The idea behind it is [[Verification-Advantage]]: work tasks can be checked, chats mostly cannot ([[2026-07-09-Muse-Image-Grok-4-5-Alex-Karp-on-CNBC|Muse, Grok, Karp]]).
