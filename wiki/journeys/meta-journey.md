# Meta: No Phone, No Cloud, and That Might Be the Point

**Thesis:** Meta owns no phone and rents out no cloud, yet those gaps push it to spend everything on free AI for ordinary people, paid for and trained by its ad machine.

## The whole arc

Thompson argued in 2015 that Facebook is an aggregator whose content comes free from its own users ([[2015-07-21-Aggregation-Theory|Aggregation Theory]]). That attention feeds one of the world's two biggest ad businesses ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher Ch 8]]). In 2026 Meta poured that money into AI, expected to top $130 billion this year ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent|Meta Launches Personal Agent]]). Its latest attempt to earn that money back is Muse, a mostly free personal agent ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent|Meta Launches Personal Agent]]).

## The eras

1. [[journeys/meta-journey-2013-2026|Meta, 2013 to July 2026: the ad machine turns to AI]]. WhatsApp, Orion, the Neural Band, the AI spending bet. Rival: Google.
2. [[journeys/meta-journey-late-2026|Meta, August to September 2026: the agent arrives]]. The teen settlement, Hatch, Muse, camera-free glasses. Rival: Apple.
3. [[journeys/meta-journey-oct-2026|Meta, October 2026 onward: the frontier and the bill]]. Cutting its own Claude bill, the Muse Wonder frontier models, banks locking out agents. Holds "Moving forward."

## The lesson

Meta must spend billions on AI because it is purely digital, but with no cloud to fall back on it will focus on consumers like no one else ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|MoffettNathanson interview]]). The idea behind it is [[Opportunity-Cost-of-Compute]]: with no cloud to rent out, Meta gives up nothing by spending chips on free users.
