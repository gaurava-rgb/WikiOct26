# Apple: The Best Body, a Rented Brain

**Thesis:** The company whose creed was "own the core technology" now rents its AI brain from Google, and bets that the best device, not the best model, wins.

## The whole arc

Tim Cook turned Steve Jobs' iPhone into a money machine: $436 billion in revenue and $118 billion in profit in 2025 ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]). Apple made its hardware chief, John Ternus, CEO, and Thompson says it has chosen not to build frontier AI models ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|MoffettNathanson interview]]). Its new Siri runs on Google's Gemini ([[2026-07-13-Apple-Sues-OpenAI|Apple Sues OpenAI]]). Now AI demand is squeezing the supply chain Cook built, and AI firms want to reach users without the iPhone ([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|Apple and Intel]]; [[2026-07-13-Apple-Sues-OpenAI|Apple Sues OpenAI]]).

## The eras

1. [[journeys/apple-journey-1998-2024|Apple, 1998 to 2024: owning the core]]. Cook's supply chain, the iPhone, the Cook Doctrine, the 30 percent cut, Apple's chips, the Siri stumble.
2. [[journeys/apple-journey-2025-2026|Apple, 2025 to 2026: the rented brain]]. Gemini in Siri, Ternus as CEO, the chip squeeze, the foldable, a server return. Rivals: OpenAI and Meta. Holds "Moving forward."

## The lesson

Thompson says Apple is betting the best hardware wins and AI becomes a commodity ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor|Ternus and Apple's Hardware]]). The idea behind it is [[Commodity-Cost-Advantage]].
