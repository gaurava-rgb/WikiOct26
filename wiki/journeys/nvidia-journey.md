# Nvidia: the chip seller that became the AI industry's banker

**Thesis:** Nvidia sells far more than chips. It sells whole systems, and in 2026 it began lending money and buying companies so that many buyers, not a few giants, keep needing it.

## The whole arc

Demand for scarce, costly AI chips helped make Nvidia a company worth over a trillion dollars ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher ch.8]], p.298). Since then its sales are capped by how many chips its suppliers can make, not by how many buyers want them ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia Earnings, Aug]]). Its biggest buyers, the cloud giants, also build their own chips to need it less ([[2026-09-13-Nvidias-Growing-Dependence-On-a-Few-Big-Customers|Nvidia's Few Big Customers]]). So Nvidia now spends its cash to grow a wider circle of buyers ([[2026-10-07-Where-Nvidias-100-Billion-Dealmaking-Juggernaut-Will-Go-Next|Nvidia's Dealmaking]]).

## The eras

1. [[journeys/nvidia-journey-2023-2026|Nvidia, 2023 to 2026: selling every chip it can make]]. The shortage, power as the yardstick, the OpenAI deal, record quarters, Anthropic won back. Rival: the customers' own chips.
2. [[journeys/nvidia-journey-late-2026|Nvidia, late 2026: the seller becomes the banker]]. Lending to buyers, buying Hugging Face, pulling chip rivals inside, venture firms stockpiling GPUs. Holds "Moving forward."

## The lesson

Huang called not investing early in Anthropic a mistake ([[2026-05-05-Amazons-Durability|Amazon's Durability]]). Nvidia's 2026 deal spree, $30 billion in OpenAI alone, is that lesson applied ([[2026-10-07-Where-Nvidias-100-Billion-Dealmaking-Juggernaut-Will-Go-Next|Nvidia's Dealmaking]]). The idea behind it is [[Commoditize-Your-Complements]]: Thompson argues buying Hugging Face keeps open models thriving, which protects Nvidia's margins ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia Earnings, Aug]]).
