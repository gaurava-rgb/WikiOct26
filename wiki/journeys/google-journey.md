# Google: the search cash machine that is learning to be a landlord

**Thesis:** Google owns every layer of AI, from chips to models to apps, yet its clearest AI payoff so far comes from renting its chips and cloud to a rival lab, Anthropic.

## The whole arc

Google got rich on search ads, a business Thompson calls the most beautiful model of all time ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company|The Google Capital Company]]). Its cloud arm lost money for years and only turned a profit in 2023 ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings|Google and Meta Earnings]]). In 2026 that cloud arm is growing fast, partly by serving Anthropic ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings|Google and Meta Earnings]]). Meanwhile Google's own Gemini models have slipped behind Anthropic and OpenAI ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model|Gemini 4 Nears]]).

## The eras

1. [[journeys/google-journey-2002-2026|Google, 2002 to June 2026: building the landlord]]. The Safari default deal, a late cloud, TPUs, the Anthropic stake, the Q1 2026 payoff, the $80 billion share sale.
2. [[journeys/google-journey-late-2026|Google, September to October 2026: Gemini catches up on paper]]. Paying publishers, Gemini wins users back, Gemini 4 Argon held in testing. Rival: OpenAI. Holds "Moving forward."

## The lesson

Thompson's comparison: search is the cash machine that funds the cloud, the way candy profits once funded Buffett's railroad ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company|The Google Capital Company]]). The idea behind it is [[Opportunity-Cost-of-Compute]]: TPUs made for Anthropic come out of Google's own supply, so Google chooses to earn by selling instead of using them ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|Thompson at MoffettNathanson]]).
