# Apple, 1998 to 2024: owning the core

**Thesis:** The company whose creed was "own the core technology" now rents its AI brain from Google, and bets that the best device, not the best model, wins. This era builds the creed and the machine. Part of the [[journeys/apple-journey|Apple journey]]; next era: [[journeys/apple-journey-2025-2026|Apple, 2025 to 2026]].

## The story so far

Tim Cook turned Steve Jobs' iPhone into a money machine: $436 billion in revenue and $118 billion in profit in 2025 ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]).

## The journey

**1998: Cook sells the factories.** After joining Apple in 1998, Cook shut its own factories and warehouses and moved manufacturing to suppliers in China, on a just-in-time chain ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]). (Just-in-time means parts arrive right when needed, so little cash sits in stock.) Because that chain ran so well, Thompson says Apple had no major product problem or recall under Cook ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]).

**2007: The iPhone.** Jobs sold it as three products in one: iPod, phone and internet device ([[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future|Gen AI Bridge]]). Thompson argues that because iOS was built on the Mac's system, it reached the real internet far better than BlackBerry or Palm ([[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future|Gen AI Bridge]]). Google's deal to be the default search engine, begun on the Mac in 2002, moved to the iPhone that year ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]).

**2009: The Cook Doctrine.** While Jobs was on leave, Cook said Apple must "own and control the primary technologies" behind its products and say no to thousands of projects ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]). Thompson argues Cook later drifted from it, picking the best money move over long-term strength on the App Store, China and possibly AI ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]).

**2011: Keep the 30 percent.** In July, Phil Schiller asked whether Apple should lower its 30 percent App Store cut; it did not ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]). Cook became CEO on August 24, and Jobs died on October 5 ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]). Thompson argues that because Jobs left only a few years after the iPhone, Cook got to grow Apple's biggest invention ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]).

**2020 to 2023: Apple wins on chips.** As apps reached rough parity with Android, Thompson argued Apple would win on its own chips, since buyers must buy the whole device to get them ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor|Ternus and Apple's Hardware]]). To get those chips, Apple paid to be TSMC's first customer on each new process, even eating early defects, as with the A17 Pro and M3 ([[2026-04-20-TSMC-Earnings-New-N3-Fabs-The-Nvidia-Ramp|TSMC Earnings]]). (TSMC is the Taiwanese firm that makes most advanced chips.)

**2024: Vision Pro and a stumble on Siri.** Because the Vision Pro was stuck in the old paradigm, even running iPad apps, Thompson likened it to Windows Mobile ([[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future|Gen AI Bridge]]). Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports it flopped, and that Ternus had been more cautious about it than Cook ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor|Ternus and Apple's Hardware]]). The same year brought an embarrassing new Siri launch ([[2026-07-13-Apple-Sues-OpenAI|Apple Sues OpenAI]]).

## The foils: BlackBerry, Palm and Android

Thompson argues that because iOS was built on the Mac's system, it reached the real internet far better than BlackBerry or Palm ([[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future|Gen AI Bridge]]). Later, as apps reached rough parity with Android, Thompson argued Apple would win on its own chips, since buyers must buy the whole device to get them ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor|Ternus and Apple's Hardware]]).

## The idea behind it

- [[Vertical-Integration]] and [[Custom-Silicon]]: Apple sells chip, software and device as one package, so its best chips come only inside its own products ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor|Ternus and Apple's Hardware]]).
- [[Contract-Manufacturing]]: Cook's China supply chain made scale possible, and left Apple exposed to U.S.-China tension ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]).

## The loop

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[First in line at TSMC] --> B[Best chips]
  B --> C[Best devices, premium prices]
  C --> D[Huge iPhone base]
  D --> E[Services: App Store 30% cut]
  E --> A
```

## Misstep box: announcing Siri too early

> Apple had Siri three years before Amazon's Alexa, yet Amazon won the smart speaker and Apple's HomePod lagged ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher Ch 8]]). In 2024, Apple announced a new Siri, and the launch proved embarrassing ([[2026-07-13-Apple-Sues-OpenAI|Apple Sues OpenAI]]). By April 2026 it still had not shipped ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook's Timing]]). Thompson says the real problem was announcing far too early, out of fear of looking behind in AI ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence|iPhone Duo]]).
>
> **Lesson:** Being late is survivable; promising early is not. Thompson expects the now-working Siri to get heavy use ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence|iPhone Duo]]).
