# Anthropic, 2023 to 2025: the safety lab finds its agent

**Thesis:** Anthropic was founded to build safer AI, but it won by selling coding agents to businesses, and that success made it the hungriest, highest-paying buyer of computing power. This era covers the founding, the cloud money and the first usable agents. Part of the [[journeys/anthropic-journey|Anthropic journey]]; next era: [[journeys/anthropic-journey-2026|Anthropic, 2026]].

## The story so far

Anthropic was started by people who left OpenAI, largely to build safer AI ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher ch.8]], p.297). It bet almost entirely on businesses, which pay for productivity, while most consumers will not pay for AI ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]). Its Opus 4.5 model and Claude Code tool brought the first usable agents in late 2025 ([[2026-05-11-The-Inference-Shift|The Inference Shift]]).

## The journey

**Up to 2023: a safety lab with little revenue.** Siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, both OpenAI vice presidents during GPT-3, founded Anthropic as a public benefit corporation ([[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching|Six OpenAI Rivals]]). (That is a company legally allowed to weigh a public mission next to profit.) By January 2023 it had raised $704 million, was valued at $4 billion, and had 80 staff and almost no revenue ([[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching|Six OpenAI Rivals]]).

**September 2023: cloud money, cloud chips.** Amazon put in $1.25 billion, with the option to go up to $4 billion; Google was also an investor ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher ch.8]], p.297). Because Google and AWS invested so it would use their computers, Anthropic ran on Google's TPUs and Amazon's Trainium, not Nvidia chips ([[2026-05-26-Nvidia-Earnings-The-AI-Stack-Nvidias-New-Reporting|Nvidia Earnings, May]]). (Both are custom AI chips the clouds design themselves.)

**November 2025: the agent unlock.** Anthropic released Opus 4.5 on November 24, 2025, and by December Claude Code could do things never possible before ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]). Thompson says agents are so hungry for tokens that AI building is not a bubble ([[2026-09-21-Frontier-Overhangs|Frontier Overhangs]]). (A token is a small chunk of text; AI use is billed in tokens.) So Anthropic launched Claude Cowork, an agent for office tasks, in January 2026 ([[2026-07-14-The-OpenAI-Super-App-ChatGPT-Codex-Whither-Chat|The OpenAI Super App]]). What that demand did to Anthropic's growth and its hunt for compute is the [[journeys/anthropic-journey-2026|next era]].

## Two "why?" questions

<details><summary>Why did Anthropic pass OpenAI in revenue?</summary>
It focused almost entirely on businesses, which pay for productivity ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]). It was long the only frontier model on every major cloud ([[2026-05-05-Amazons-Durability|Amazon's Durability]]).
</details>

<details><summary>Why did Nvidia barely sell to the fastest-growing lab?</summary>
Anthropic took Google and Amazon money and ran on their chips ([[2026-05-26-Nvidia-Earnings-The-AI-Stack-Nvidias-New-Reporting|Nvidia Earnings, May]]). Even its Mythos model appears to have been trained on Google TPUs ([[2026-04-20-TSMC-Earnings-New-N3-Fabs-The-Nvidia-Ramp|TSMC Earnings]]).
</details>
