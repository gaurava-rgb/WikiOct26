# Amazon

US e-commerce, logistics, and cloud company (AWS). Known for long-term, capital-heavy bets in the physical world.

- Shein is raiding Amazon's supplier base, recruiting sellers with at least $2 million in Amazon sales with zero commissions for three months and free advertising. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-95ad4f346e1288335dab|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Shein faces a steep learning curve managing new merchants, geographies, and product categories, and that turning off Amazon's best suppliers would set back its expansion. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-03bd3cef8b13fd1ec918|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues the Forever 21 deal has unclear benefits, since Shein's model does not gain from static store inventory and Amazon has backpedaled on its own retail store experiments. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-dfcd941a7e010e31f6df|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues that with its marketplace strategy Shein has 'poked the bear', Amazon, a brutal competitor unlikely to cede customers or suppliers, with Shopify another strong rival. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-b3def27ca2ada82e8c4b|cite]])
- Gallaugher questions whether Shein has a sustainable competitive advantage: Temu shows the model is replicable, supplier lock-up is up to the courts, and Amazon and Alibaba have deep data and AI too. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-4aa68e5261633eca4d4a|cite]])
