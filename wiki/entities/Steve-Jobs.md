# Steve Jobs

Apple co-founder and CEO who launched the Mac, iPod, iPhone, and iPad; died October 5, 2011.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-21 [[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook’s Impeccable Timing]]

- Steve Jobs died of cancer on October 5, 2011, six weeks after Tim Cook became Apple CEO; Cook had already served as interim CEO while Jobs was treated in 2009. ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing#^clm-4e348b3d2c38daba7791|cite]])
- Thompson argues the timing of Jobs' death made Tim Cook arguably the greatest non-founder CEO ever: founders usually stay past their peak, but Jobs left Apple only a few years after the iPhone, so Cook was the one who grew Apple's most revolutionary device. ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing#^clm-aef418d49a754f515fee|cite]])
- Thompson calls the Cook Doctrine brilliant but argues it is ultimately about maintaining, nurturing, and growing what Steve Jobs built, not about creating new things. ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing#^clm-70b4c9046017294e6a6a|cite]])
