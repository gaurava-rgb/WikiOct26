# John Ternus

Apple's longtime hardware chief, named in April 2026 to succeed Tim Cook as CEO.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-21 [[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook’s Impeccable Timing]]

- Apple announced in April 2026 that Tim Cook will step up to Executive Chairman on September 1, 2026, with John Ternus becoming Apple's CEO. ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing#^clm-a816c64354ace11a9d83|cite]])

### 2026-04-22 [[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor|John Ternus and Apple’s Hardware-Defined Future, SpaceXAI and Cursor]]

- Ternus, 50, joined Apple in 2001, four years after earning a mechanical engineering degree from the University of Pennsylvania. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-163cee5e275da4869b0c|cite]])
- Bloomberg's Mark Gurman reports that Ternus was more cautious than Tim Cook on the Vision Pro, which flopped, and on the roughly $10 billion car project Apple scrapped, opposing both to varying degrees. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-65abac4d891e9c4d2c3d|cite]])
- Gurman reports that Ternus championed the MacBook Neo, a product that broke with Apple's usual premium-first approach. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-e5ff38ba881e652257ff|cite]])
- Thompson argues Ternus was the obvious internal pick because Apple's hardware, from the iPhone to the Mac and the Apple Silicon inside both, is in excellent shape. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-4057e2ecceef0b6660d4|cite]])
- Thompson says the MacBook Neo is the peak of his 2020 hardware-differentiation thesis, and that naming Ternus CEO is its ultimate affirmation. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-81cc14fe5e40f2f97543|cite]])
- Thompson argues the decision to base Apple's AI on Google's Gemini and the decision to make Ternus CEO are in many respects the same decision: compete in AI through devices and edge compute. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-c557ba611f0b2d617480|cite]])
