# Biohub

Primary philanthropic science effort of Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan, focused on AI-powered biology.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-10 [[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment|Zuckerberg’s Big Biotech Moment]]

- Biohub is partnering with the NIH and the Department of Energy in a $1.8 billion investment to build technologies and scientific infrastructure for AI-powered biology, announced at an October 2026 reception. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-d48d60dac553d9dfa7ac|cite]])
- Meta, Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs agreed to put $300 million into Biohub's AI biology initiative. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-84850df6105e1cabf1d3|cite]])
- Biohub launched its Virtual Biology Initiative in April 2026 with a $500 million commitment. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-080d3f9234dac1b1a0dd|cite]])
- Mark Zuckerberg said he and Priscilla Chan started the first version of Biohub almost 10 years earlier aiming to cure and prevent all diseases by 2100, and that thanks to AI this no longer seems so crazy. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-144e9a93dde9c0ff85ea|cite]])
- Lori Goler, president of the Chan Zuckerberg Initiative, said Biohub was inspired by the Human Genome Project and hopes its AI biology initiative has the same level of impact. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-e3c2c8f423afcbd00775|cite]])
- Biohub's NIH and DOE partnership grew out of a Biohub team's visit to the first Genesis Mission Summit in Washington in July 2026. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-a79b780dd979cc8cee5b|cite]])
- Lori Goler said Biohub plans to host an annual AI and bio event, calling the moment a cultural one. ([[2026-10-10-Zuckerbergs-Big-Biotech-Moment#^clm-ac6bee234c01588f9a20|cite]])
