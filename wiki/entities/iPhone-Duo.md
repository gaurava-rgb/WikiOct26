# iPhone Duo

Apple's first foldable iPhone, unveiled September 2026.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-10 [[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence|The iPhone Duo, The Intelligent Personal Hub, Apple Watch Audio Intelligence]]

- Apple unveiled its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, at its September 9, 2026 event, and new CEO John Ternus mocked rival foldables as two phones awkwardly stuck together. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-a264f00d3a9712b4d3ae|cite]])
- The iPhone Duo unfolds to a 7.6-inch screen, the largest display ever on an iPhone, has an outer 5.4-inch screen for use when folded, and will work with the Apple Pencil later in 2026. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-e7040cd0c5276fb15b10|cite]])
- With the iPhone Duo, Apple aims to refine the foldable phone category pioneered by Samsung, with a sturdier feel and less of a crease in the middle of the screen. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-902813e4f6dcb40f32db|cite]])
- Thompson calls the iPhone Duo Apple at its best, and says the most noteworthy part is not the hardware but how Apple redesigned iOS to fit the device, which is why software chief Craig Federighi appeared in the launch video. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-54df0895c3380e838d3a|cite]])
- Thompson notes the irony that Apple's iPhone Duo video praised combining two products into one, when Tim Cook once mocked Microsoft's Surface for doing the same thing. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-916c8a4f4e1c17da9207|cite]])
- Craig Federighi said Apple made it easy for developers to adapt their apps for the iPhone Duo with new and updated APIs, showing Zoom, Slack, and Netflix apps built for the larger screen. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-5d0e159f9ee86f5d4954|cite]])
- Thompson complains that the iPhone Duo puts its dock on the right so it stays the same open or closed, which makes it a right-handed device for anyone using one hand. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-c21f5298831ec0c54629|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson predicts most developers will do the work to support the iPhone Duo, even though apps that skip it will look terrible, because the iPhone is too important to ignore. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-092ca97a31fa9d7e92d7|cite]])
- Thompson notes that Apple calling it easy for developers to adopt new Siri capabilities means developers still have to do the work, on top of updating their apps for the iPhone Duo. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-4cdabbee02ad383f600d|cite]])

### 2026-09-20 [[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones|Why Apple Is Betting on Foldable iPhones]]

- New Apple CEO John Ternus showed the first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, at an event in September 2026. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-a2274ab79590210da364|cite]])
- Apple is betting that the demand which drove double-digit foldable growth outside North America for Huawei, Samsung and others will lift its flagship iPhone sales. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-841e0c34a8d5ec7469e6|cite]])
- The iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 with 256 gigabytes of storage, far pricier than any earlier iPhone. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-2973b6a7f3fd099f3797|cite]])
- Prediction: IDC expects 10 million iPhone Duo units sold in the first 12 months after launch, bringing in $27 billion, about 6% of Apple's expected fiscal 2026 revenue. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-299a27a4fb5f593b8559|cite]])
- Prediction: Linda Sui of Smart Analytics Global projects 6 million iPhone Duo units sold in 2026 alone. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-355c12ad86567ac7b85f|cite]])
- IDC's Francisco Jeronimo argues the foldable brings novelty, can attract high-end buyers, and could get people upgrading more often again. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-797e3cc0bfb7d58818ea|cite]])
- Trade-ins of some recent iPhones could cut almost $900 off the iPhone Duo's sticker price, and installment plans could help buyers justify the price. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-0f110e7d9e5d579deaf6|cite]])
- Linda Sui argues Apple will educate the market and drive up foldable adoption in the US, including for cheaper rivals. ([[2026-09-20-Why-Apple-Is-Betting-on-Foldable-iPhones#^clm-83276c425ce34ebd09ae|cite]])
