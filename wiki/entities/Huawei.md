### 2026-09-21 [[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls|DeepSeek Bets Big on Huawei Chips to Bypass U.S. Export Controls]]

- DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors that training models on more Chinese-made chips is a major priority and expects Huawei to start delivering training chips as early as Q4 2026, per two people with direct knowledge. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-803076f04e6580530f19|cite]])
- Liang called shifting training to Huawei chips one of DeepSeek's biggest bets and said it has to work. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-d7e460d3abe8a0ac91fd|cite]])
- Prediction: Liang thinks Huawei chips will match Nvidia's within a few years, so DeepSeek should adapt first. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-91aa14d90e01b14cfbc1|cite]])
- In May 2026 Liang estimated that a model on par with OpenAI's largest would need about 50,000 Nvidia GB300 chips or 200,000 Huawei Ascend 950 chips, and said Huawei could not supply enough. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-1248b8a1fe3452c17966|cite]])
- Huawei is held back by limited access to advanced memory and other component shortages, per two people. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-e0d7f230d21aaa1fca42|cite]])
- Prediction: Huawei's roadmap puts the Ascend 950DT, for training and part of inference, in Q4 2026 and the stronger 960DT in Q1 2027. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-f1d437361fc654f49e11|cite]])
- Huawei said in April 2026 its chips handled part of training DeepSeek's smaller V4-Flash model, and the two adapted the V4 family to Huawei's Ascend systems. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-33437af44ab2563368e0|cite]])
