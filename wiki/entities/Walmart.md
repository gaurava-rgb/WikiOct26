# Walmart+

Walmart's paid membership, its answer to Amazon Prime.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Walmart+, Walmart's copy of an Amazon-style subscription, costs about $100 a year and includes free delivery from stores on orders over $35, free shipping, fuel discounts, and Paramount+. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-207c09d3bee3b4dd3719|cite]])
- Walmart+ mobile scan-and-go still requires checking at a self-checkout register, unlike Amazon's just walk out technology. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-543d952f2f151d2ff387|cite]])
