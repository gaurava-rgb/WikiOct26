### 2026-10-15 [[2026-10-15-Cognitions-Plan-to-Limit-Tokenmaxxing-to-the-Most-Efficient-Engineers|Cognition’s Plan to Limit Tokenmaxxing to the Most Efficient Engineers]]

- Cognition introduced dynamic token allocations for Devin that adjust employee usage caps based on calculated productivity and compute cost efficiency. ([[2026-10-15-Cognitions-Plan-to-Limit-Tokenmaxxing-to-the-Most-Efficient-Engineers#^clm-1bc9292e11db366f6e16|cite]])

- Cognition introduced Devin Coach to identify common prompting mistakes, such as underspecified requests or selecting unnecessarily expensive models. ([[2026-10-15-Cognitions-Plan-to-Limit-Tokenmaxxing-to-the-Most-Efficient-Engineers#^clm-738cf05937abdb9191d2|cite]])
- Devin Coach instructs developers to break multi-part requests into concurrent parallel tasks to reduce processing time. ([[2026-10-15-Cognitions-Plan-to-Limit-Tokenmaxxing-to-the-Most-Efficient-Engineers#^clm-0e434a947e9435b04e90|cite]])
