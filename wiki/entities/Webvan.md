# Webvan

Dot-com era online grocer whose billion-dollar failure became a cautionary tale.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch04 FreshDirect - A Tech-Heavy Online Grocer]]

- Webvan's billion-dollar failure was one of the biggest crash-and-burns of the dot-com era, part of a long run of failed online grocers. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-b4a4b399f96414c7d21f|cite]])
- Besides Webvan, earlier failed online grocery efforts included Streamline, Home Runs, and Shoplink. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-c03113de9a4300a31aa9|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues early online grocers like Webvan failed from a lack of product-market fit: suburban customers liked driving to the store on their own schedule and would not trust someone else to pick their produce. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-b3c25cf23a62059002cd|cite]])
