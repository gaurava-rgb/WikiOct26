# TikTok

Short-video social app whose recommendation algorithm is controlled by the Chinese company ByteDance.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia]]

- The Shein app emphasizes discovery over search, unlike Amazon and Google; Bessemer's Sisi Song says it presents products the way TikTok shows endless video recommendations. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-73d3a77ab279686bd477|cite]])
- Shein built its own proprietary deep-learning AI that browses TikTok and other social media and uses neural-network image recognition to spot popular clothing trends in images and video. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-5cc8153fdeb0a2d93335|cite]])
- Shein is followed by over three times as many TikTok users as Zara, with ASOS and H&M further behind. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-eb47f303a808597ee3c0|cite]])
- TikTok parent ByteDance launched an e-commerce effort similar to Shein's marketplace, with buying inside the app, which could use its data as first-party data if privacy laws limit third-party sharing. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-2e12288b8b5b209cfc2f|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Cory Doctorow calls the pattern of maturing tech firms such as Google, Meta, and TikTok degrading service to boost profits 'ensh*ttification', and Gallaugher asks whether Amazon is following it. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-67eef5a10ce037e6b719|cite]])
- During the pandemic, Shopify-powered social media commerce boomed as people at home found products on AliExpress, had Chinese suppliers drop-ship them, and ran ads on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-d1d3ac7b308f7e30f51b|cite]])
- Shopify faces competition from Shein and Temu, which recruit its customers with similar tools, and from TikTok, a Shopify partner that launched its own e-commerce tools for vendors. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-10079b201b837a7357fb|cite]])

### 2026-05-18 [[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent|Data Center Discontent, Understanding the Opposition, Fixing the Problem]]

- Thompson notes TikTok is a major vector for data center opposition, and its algorithm is still controlled by a Chinese company, while China has an interest in the US not building more AI. ([[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent#^clm-43df69af8d2bd17403cd|cite]])

### 2026-08-31 [[2026-08-31-Meta-Settles-A-Framework-For-Regulating-Content-The-Rest-of-Big-Tech|Meta Settles, A Framework For Regulating Content, The Rest of Big Tech]]

- A $5.3 billion part of Meta's payout over the next decade depends on rivals committing to similar platform changes and comparable payments; TikTok, YouTube, and Snap have stayed publicly silent. ([[2026-08-31-Meta-Settles-A-Framework-For-Regulating-Content-The-Rest-of-Big-Tech#^clm-10d462be62aaf27550a3|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson predicts Meta will use the settlement to make TikTok look bad in particular and to downplay YouTube's educational benefits, and would count it a win if TikTok looks like the worst actor. ([[2026-08-31-Meta-Settles-A-Framework-For-Regulating-Content-The-Rest-of-Big-Tech#^clm-90f0280818eebc3c8089|cite]])
