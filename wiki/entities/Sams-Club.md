# Sam's Club

Walmart's warehouse club chain; ran the cashierless Sam's Club Now test store in Dallas.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Walmart answered Amazon Go with Sam's Club Now, a Dallas store with no cash registers or checkout lines, about a quarter the size of a normal Sam's Club with about a quarter of the staff. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-1ce8dfae5c898ad928e9|cite]])
- It took Walmart only five months to develop Sam's Club Now and get it ready to open. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-04f1b56b5b5ff6c3a29a|cite]])
- The CEO of Sam's Club said most of the technology in Sam's Club Now was built in house, which lets Walmart iterate and move quickly. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-06cd0067e32ccba3c3a9|cite]])
