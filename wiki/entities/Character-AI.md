# Character.AI

Palo Alto startup founded in 2021 by former Google LaMDA builders where users chat with role-playing bots.

## Facts by source

### 2023-01-09 [[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching|Six OpenAI Rivals Google and Microsoft Are Watching]]

- Character.AI founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas built Google's LaMDA and left Google over red tape; the startup had 18 staff and no revenue. ([[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching#^clm-63727b31835d4f0a1f09|cite]])

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference|Neocloud Prime Intellect Acquires Startup To Speed Up Inference]]

- Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and agreed a deal in which a Walt Disney affiliate pays $20 million for a non-exclusive license to some of its IP. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-ab6ce1cc0e0ec15c0d38|cite]])

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit|OpenAI’s Bug and Bumble’s AI-Powered Exit]]

- Early AI startup Character.AI filed for bankruptcy on October 12, 2026, likely one of the final steps in its long decline. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-42aedb604891c026f41b|cite]])
- In 2024 Google paid $2.7 billion to license Character.AI's models, letting investors cash out at a profit, and co-founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas rejoined Google; Shazeer later left for OpenAI. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-4d2ace177c08be2be859|cite]])
- In September 2026 Disney hired Character.AI CEO Karandeep Anand, formerly of Meta, as chief technology officer along with other Character.AI technical staff, and it has bid $20 million to license Character.AI's IP in the bankruptcy. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-4b1baec843a188664fc1|cite]])
