# Prime Intellect

San Francisco neocloud and inference provider that runs its own Nvidia hardware; bought Moondream in 2026.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference|Neocloud Prime Intellect Acquires Startup To Speed Up Inference]]

- Prime Intellect, a San Francisco neocloud valued at $1 billion in a July 2026 equity financing, is acquiring Moondream to make open models run faster and cheaper on its Nvidia servers. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-ee769de8b8b34804e2e8|cite]])
- Moondream has fewer than 10 employees and has raised $4.5 million; Prime Intellect CEO Vincent Weisser would not share the deal terms. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-4564540eec41a2da21c8|cite]])
- Vincent Weisser says Prime Intellect is buying Moondream mainly for its staff's skill at writing software that makes models run efficiently, not for its vision-language model. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-0a31646aa8012f883338|cite]])
- Moondream's megakernel compiler and Photon, its proprietary tech for running models fast, will be folded into Prime Intellect's technology. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-f7fb558fcbf14107aefe|cite]])
- Prediction: Prime Intellect hopes Moondream's rare experience with image models will help it win robotics and computer-using AI agent customers that other neoclouds serve poorly. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-e1b306f16a3105b5ec93|cite]])
- Prime Intellect said in July 2026 it had passed $100 million in annualized revenue, a little over $8 million a month. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-827469bb63bec0d0e1f9|cite]])
- As of October 2026 Fal generated more than $800 million in annualized revenue and Together AI over $1 billion, far more than Prime Intellect. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-2eed0379742ce314e251|cite]])
- Many inference providers pay a large share of revenue to cloud firms that own the servers, but Prime Intellect operates its own hardware. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-42cd18abc581ff14835c|cite]])
- Besides compute, Prime Intellect sells post-training (reinforcement learning) software and has trained its own models and agents for coding and AI research. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-a4c63bcae09af5652902|cite]])
