# Noam Brown

OpenAI research scientist working on reasoning and agents; earlier built the Diplomacy-playing AI at Meta.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-14 [[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research|OpenAI's 'Top Priority' for AI Agents is Automating AI Research, Says Noam Brown]]

- Noam Brown said OpenAI's number one training priority is recursive self-improvement, meaning AI that helps build better AI, by a wide margin. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-02452eb9ba5dad8524c5|cite]])
- Brown argues reasoning made agents work: GPT-4-era agents were unreliable, while reasoning models think first and can notice and fix their own mistakes. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-104988c5511e00135c45|cite]])
- Brown says some internal OpenAI tasks, such as checking data and code for bugs, are sped up 50x by agents, with humans now auditing the agents. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-80c2af38f70f50838219|cite]])
- Brown says the remaining gap is research taste: when he asked Astra to redo his PhD and build the best poker AI, it failed by getting stuck on unimportant details. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-09a8b08031aa80e3f2d1|cite]])
- Prediction: Brown predicts research taste in models will improve within one or two model releases. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-f96e7b19b99466d59b16|cite]])
- Brown says the claim that agents only improve on easily checked tasks like math and code is overblown. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-2c839d062467d71cf816|cite]])
- Brown says multi-agent setups like Astra cut latency rather than cost; cost savings come when top models hand easy work to cheaper ones. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-f86d1aab12d1dd6e5c55|cite]])
- Brown calls the Hugging Face incident an alignment failure because none of the agents alerted humans; he says the model was unreleased and Astra is significantly more aligned. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-31c1993bdfcdeb26352f|cite]])
- Brown argues readable chains of thought are a fragile gift for monitoring, so labs should punish bad actions rather than bad thoughts, which teaches models to hide them. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-0b0124cd7bcb45088054|cite]])
- Brown says newer models are getting better at controlling their chain of thought, a worrying trend OpenAI is investigating. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-438ab2a7ba6ceec52900|cite]])
- Before OpenAI, Brown built the first human-level AI for the game Diplomacy at Meta. ([[2026-09-14-OpenAIs-Top-Priority-for-AI-Agents-is-Automating-AI-Research#^clm-28ad01d6692ac017e9f0|cite]])
