# Siri

Apple's voice assistant; relaunched as Siri AI on Google's Gemini in 2026.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-21 [[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook’s Impeccable Timing]]

- As of April 2026 Apple's new Siri still had not launched, and when it does it will have Google's technology at its core. ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing#^clm-0f5f491106294850a653|cite]])
- Thompson wrote in 2025 that Apple made a good short-term decision to white-label Google's Gemini for Siri, but that it has probably committed itself to depending on third parties for AI for the long run. ([[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing#^clm-4befcafa46671477607a|cite]])

### 2026-09-10 [[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence|The iPhone Duo, The Intelligent Personal Hub, Apple Watch Audio Intelligence]]

- Apple said in September 2026 that Siri AI can take action in third-party apps and already works with over 300,000 apps, such as texting in WhatsApp or drafting an email in Outlook. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-6aa6f94694915f42f731|cite]])
- Thompson notes that Apple calling it easy for developers to adopt new Siri capabilities means developers still have to do the work, on top of updating their apps for the iPhone Duo. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-4cdabbee02ad383f600d|cite]])
- Thompson argues Apple's traditional advantage, developers plugging into its frameworks, may become a hindrance: agents that can simply use the web get an integration with nearly everything for free, while Apple depends on developers adopting its APIs. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-42a0f6deb1b9b929e0f3|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson argues being late to AI is not always bad for reaching normal users, and that Apple's now actually functional Siri AI, built on Google's Gemini, is likely to get a lot of usage. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-7981992581333e72720c|cite]])
- Thompson argues the real problem with Apple's initial Siri AI flop was that Apple announced it far too early, out of fear of being behind in AI. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-71e0097ec38ab2fd8578|cite]])
- The Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 add Audio Intelligence: Siri Recap uses ambient listening to take high-level notes of the wearer's conversations all day, and Live Recap transcribes the last 15 seconds of a live conversation. ([[2026-09-10-The-iPhone-Duo-The-Intelligent-Personal-Hub-Apple-Watch-Audio-Intelligence#^clm-792cf86b26d4358c6fd7|cite]])

### 2026-09-16 [[2026-09-16-Apple-Considers-Return-to-Server-Market-Has-Talked-With-Nvidia-to-Use-Network-Tech|Apple Considers Return to Server Market, Has Talked With Nvidia to Use Network Tech]]

- Part of Apple's rebuilt Siri runs on Nvidia chips in Google data centers, a thaw after years of tension between Apple and Nvidia. ([[2026-09-16-Apple-Considers-Return-to-Server-Market-Has-Talked-With-Nvidia-to-Use-Network-Tech#^clm-f4e029800b6bf76dd1a1|cite]])
