# Xiaomi

Chinese smartphone and electric car maker that builds its own MiMo AI models.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-09 [[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded|China’s AI Model Race Is Just Getting More Crowded]]

- Three big Chinese tech firms from outside AI, game maker miHoYo, phone and car maker Xiaomi, and delivery app Meituan, are now building their own large language models from scratch. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-5b7dc6af8c5e7d825a9c|cite]])
- Xiaomi builds the AI for its devices in-house, with CEO Lei Jun planning to put its MiMo models into gadgets and cars, unlike Apple, which partnered with Google for the models behind Apple Intelligence. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-78a4856a0ace5daf7a53|cite]])
- Xiaomi made a splash in 2025 by poaching a star researcher from DeepSeek. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-4bc3dd716383e4e545f3|cite]])
- Xiaomi's flagship MiMo-V2.6-Pro, released in September 2026, ranks ninth on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, on par with xAI's Grok 4.7 and ahead of the flagships Alibaba and Z.ai launched weeks earlier. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-3ab0c87a5cd99e1c7bc0|cite]])
- Bernstein analyst Robin Zhu says Chinese founders worry that not owning a model could leave them vulnerable if they lose access to outside platforms. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-a3de32689e7be77e8971|cite]])
