# Eugene Wei

Former Amazon employee and writer on Amazon's low-margin strategy.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Former Amazon employee Eugene Wei said Amazon's sales scale lets it run thin margins and low prices to 'thin the oxygen' of competitors. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-0fc7dafbbe2a37a13819|cite]])
