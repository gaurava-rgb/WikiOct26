# Fire Phone

Amazon's 2014 smartphone, canceled about a year after launch.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Gallaugher rates Amazon's device results as mixed: category-monopolizing (Kindle), dominant but not money-making (Echo and Alexa), and an utter flop (Fire Phone). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ee3c25532de5371c3f0b|cite]])
- The Fire Phone launched at $199 with contract, in line with Apple and Samsung rather than Amazon's value pricing, and came with a year of Prime worth $100. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-cbe37b09910030bc62ae|cite]])
- Amazon cut the Fire Phone's price to $99 in less than a year, canceled it a little over a year after launch, and took a $170 million write-off. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-db445fc62f7860041c35|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues the Fire Phone failed because it ran an Android fork without most Android apps, so it fought switching costs with a malnourished network effect; its lesson for late movers is that high price, entrenched rivals, switching costs, and missing apps sink a device. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-f3820d68aae2a553bf8b|cite]])
