# Rum Group

Formerly Rumble; runs the Rumble video platform, hosts Truth Social, and became a neocloud after buying Northern Data in 2026.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-13 [[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group|Anthropic Strikes $13.7 Billion Compute Deal With Trump-Linked Rum Group]]

- Anthropic signed a computing deal worth $13.7 billion over six years with Rum Group, per a person familiar. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-1ac7cf2b351d3743e981|cite]])
- Rum Group has roots in social media and long-standing ties to the Trump administration. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-486372ef07838f7807c9|cite]])
- Rum disclosed a $13.7 billion contract in an August 2026 securities filing without naming the customer. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-3b5698f8500e9c2388b4|cite]])
- Rum Group, formerly Rumble, runs the Rumble video platform popular with conservatives and hosts Donald Trump's Truth Social. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-abb8dac547de8d138c24|cite]])
- In May 2021 Rumble raised $25 million from investors including Peter Thiel and J.D. Vance at a $500 million valuation. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-f09ef209f59b98a1117d|cite]])
- In June 2026 Rumble bought German neocloud Northern Data in an all-stock deal valued at about $767 million, then renamed itself Rum Group. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-eec292292016af37b560|cite]])
- Northern Data brought 22,000 Nvidia Hopper GPUs and a data center site under development in Georgia. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-1ce032c855f98b763e45|cite]])
- Anthropic would lease compute at Rum's unfinished Maysville, Georgia data center, whose key asset is 120 megawatts of scarce grid access, with potential to reach 180 megawatts. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-a79059ce449b7977485e|cite]])
- The Georgia site could cost more than $10 billion to build and fill, based on prior reporting that one gigawatt of AI data center capacity costs about $60 billion. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-cb1fe9b26c82c330a3aa|cite]])
- Rum's August filing said it did not yet have financing for the building, GPUs or other equipment and expects to fund much of it with debt or equity. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-f0a6ef46bbfa7eed2ae5|cite]])
- As of June 2026 Rum wanted to buy $270 million of Nvidia Blackwell 300 chips for a separate contract with Together AI. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-cc44ebe2facc79a125da|cite]])
- Anthropic gets an option to buy up to 51 million Rum shares for one cent each, per the person. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-929ea0afdc795ccdb196|cite]])
- Rum stock closed at $7.17 on September 11, 2026, giving it a market value of about $2.8 billion on 400 million shares. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-7f1654918f5153430ab5|cite]])
- Half of Anthropic's Rum options are tied to the Maysville site, and the other half unlock if Anthropic buys about 400 to 500 megawatts more at another Rum site. ([[2026-09-13-Anthropic-Strikes-13-7-Billion-Compute-Deal-With-Trump-Linked-Rum-Group#^clm-d43d070160d64138e3c4|cite]])
