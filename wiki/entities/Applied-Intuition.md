# Applied Intuition

Sunnyvale maker of software to build, test and run self-driving and software-defined vehicles.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation|Applied Intuition Fields Offers at $30 Billion Valuation]]

- Applied Intuition has received investment offers valuing it at $30 billion or more, per people who spoke to its leaders, and will likely raise at least $1 billion in the next few months. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-90da410e945d4a028ab5|cite]])
- A $30 billion valuation would double Applied Intuition's June 2026 valuation. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-f394d9237a72d548e47c|cite]])
- After three years in which AI labs like OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI took most investor attention, more AI app and agent startups are reaching valuations of $20 billion or higher, including Cognition and Perplexity. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-62b6c599f760e2a33c09|cite]])
- Applied Intuition focuses on the software side of self-driving instead of also building and running the cars, and the new valuation would back that choice. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-09e3d948ea6187b9d43d|cite]])
- Applied Intuition had $400 million in annual recurring revenue at the end of 2024 and has doubled revenue every year in recent years, Andreessen Horowitz said in July 2026. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-9f5f611191b0c6946270|cite]])
- Applied Intuition was founded by Qasar Younis, a former Y Combinator COO and its CEO, and Peter Ludwig, a former Google product manager. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-52bc4efc41bb45d94b5b|cite]])
- Applied Intuition sells software to develop, test and validate driver-assistance and automated driving systems, plus vehicle operating systems; customers include Isuzu, Volkswagen and Saudi Arabia's Humain. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-e2a7788aac80dee86111|cite]])
- Applied Intuition is expanding into autonomous military vehicle tech, setting it up to compete with Anduril and Saronic. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-9ff681f23ec53e6249e6|cite]])
- In summer 2026 Applied Intuition launched Dana, an AI agent tool that automates building systems for physical AI and software-controlled vehicles. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-778c0519f2c3d966b141|cite]])
- In early October 2026 Applied Intuition announced deals to supply vehicle operating systems and other tech to Honda and Nissan. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-f79c395335bb2f065a10|cite]])
- Waymo's expansion of commercial service across the U.S. is encouraging investors to back self-driving startups. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-7fecd9b7813f8c02807d|cite]])
- Applied Intuition has raised $1.2 billion from investors including Andreessen Horowitz, General Catalyst, Lux Capital and Kleiner Perkins, and had not used most of it as of July 2026. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-Intuition-Fields-Offers-at-30-Billion-Valuation#^clm-bb0f7b48d20574bcafee|cite]])
