# Moonshot AI

Chinese AI startup that makes the Kimi open weight models, including Kimi K3.

## Facts by source

### 2026-07-20 [[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models|Who’s Afraid of Chinese Models?]]

- Moonshot AI's Kimi K3, an open weight Chinese model, approached state-of-the-art capabilities in July 2026 and set off heated arguments on X. ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models#^clm-26e1bd25561936d85b82|cite]])
- As of July 2026, Kimi K3 cost $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, against $5 and $30 for OpenAI's Sol. ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models#^clm-5020c0e3651316b0b49c|cite]])
- Kimi reportedly uses significantly more reasoning tokens than OpenAI's Sol to reach an answer, which Thompson says makes its lower price per token moot. ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models#^clm-c7f92d677b7a8d1470a3|cite]])
- Thompson argues the reaction to Kimi and Chinese models is overblown economically: they only seem cheaper because a compute shortage creates a price umbrella under which Anthropic and OpenAI charge far above what full supply would allow. ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models#^clm-c3725d194154fd003aa4|cite]])
- Per Bloomberg, Alibaba shares rose as much as 5.4% on July 20, 2026 after it released a preview of Qwen3.8 Max, a 2.4 trillion parameter model it called second only to Anthropic's Fable 5; Kimi K3 has 2.8 trillion parameters. ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models#^clm-bd514ba916acea8b6e28|cite]])
