# Terence Tao

Famed mathematician who warned that AI solution tools flatten math's difficulty landscape and threaten open science.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-09 [[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent|OpenAI Does Math, Reward-Hacking, Meta Launches Personal Agent]]

- Terence Tao argues AI tools have flattened the difficulty landscape in many areas of math, and there is no clear boundary between AI-feasible and AI-hard problems, partly because AI companies refuse to disclose negative results. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent#^clm-9437e977127c9aa59274|cite]])
- Tao argues that identifying a promising problem is now the scarce resource, and that incentives may push mathematicians to stop sharing research directions, reversing centuries of open science. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent#^clm-c13ab8a08787f4237726|cite]])
