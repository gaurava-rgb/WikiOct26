# Fair Factories Clearinghouse

Nonprofit where apparel firms share audit data on contract factories.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems]]

- Reebok built an in-house system to track audits of its hundreds of suppliers on labor, safety and environmental practices, then donated it to help create the nonprofit Fair Factories Clearinghouse. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-d1ec99f940bdfd5b958e|cite]])
- The U.S. Department of Justice had to grant the Fair Factories Clearinghouse a special exemption, after being convinced buyers would not use it to collude; the system holds no pricing data. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-edc128355e73a225903b|cite]])
- The Fair Factories Clearinghouse database grows more valuable as more firms use it, and Nike, long dogged by sweatshop allegations, has since joined. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-e1de67afd7b987c9b1be|cite]])
