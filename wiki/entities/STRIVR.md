# STRIVR

VR training startup that supplies Walmart's employee training headsets.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Walmart put VR headsets in the back of its 4,600 US stores, and over a million workers have trained on them for tasks like stocking shelves and serving customers; Walmart also uses VR to help pick middle managers. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-56fe86ea9ae0fe887b95|cite]])
- Walmart's VR training system comes from the startup STRIVR, which also sells it to Chipotle, JetBlue, and Fidelity Investments. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-a39b23b5f2c92765129b|cite]])
