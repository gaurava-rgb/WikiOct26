# Inflection

Palo Alto AI startup founded in 2022 by Mustafa Suleyman, Karén Simonyan and Reid Hoffman.

- Inflection, led by DeepMind co-founder Mustafa Suleyman with co-founder Reid Hoffman, had raised $225 million, was valued at $1.23 billion in April 2022, and had 20 staff and no product. ([[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching#^clm-06d1a2e9bb441c6a074f|cite]])
