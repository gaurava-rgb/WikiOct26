# MCX

Failed retailer group mobile payments standard backed by Walmart, Target, Best Buy and others.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Walmart joined over a dozen retailers, including Best Buy, Target, Sears, and 7-Eleven, to create MCX, a pay-by-phone standard with coupons and loyalty programs. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-8f6f3483f001ddf2f63f|cite]])
- MCX failed despite big-name retail backing, and its technology was eventually bought by JP Morgan Chase. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-21f611fc950b95bcb922|cite]])
