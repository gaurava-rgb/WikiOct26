# H&M

Swedish fashion retailer; world's number two clothing retailer and Zara's nearest rival.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia]]

- Shein posted $23 billion in sales in 2022, making it the world's number two fashion firm, just ahead of H&M ($22.25 billion) and behind only Inditex ($35.2 billion). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-eb1020c431c142eb4992|cite]])
- At one point in 2022, Shein raised money at a valuation higher than the market capitalizations of Inditex and H&M combined. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-57c865be736b87f6480b|cite]])
- Shein is followed by over three times as many TikTok users as Zara, with ASOS and H&M further behind. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-eb47f303a808597ee3c0|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems]]

- Zara is the crown jewel of Inditex (Industrias de Diseno Textil), a group that has far outperformed its nearest global rival H&M. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-fd4203a8ddd74d7309de|cite]])
- While Neiman Marcus, H&M, Gap and Macy's shrink or close stores, Zara mostly closes stores only to open bigger ones to meet demand (cited to a 2015 article). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-c96ed6f7110e633f43ec|cite]])
- Gap was not implicated in Rana Plaza but became a protest target in 2013 as one of the largest importers of Bangladeshi clothing and for refusing to sign a safety accord backed by H&M and Zara. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-071644700e9ed80efadd|cite]])
- H&M, the world's number two clothing retailer, reported $4.3 billion in unsold inventory after six straight quarters of declining results, and one analyst called it a slow-motion train wreck. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-1b39146acb2d2c7376ec|cite]])
- About 300 Zara designers at The Cube produce 30,000 items a year (2014), versus 2,000 to 4,000 items at big chains like H&M and Gap. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-ced18144998f9d1659a4|cite]])
- H&M takes three to five months from creation to delivery and is considered one of the best; other retailers average six months to design and three to manufacture, VF Corporation can take nine months to design jeans, and J.Jill a year. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-022a8d74808be35be7f0|cite]])
- H&M has 900 suppliers and no factories, while nearly 60 percent of Zara's merchandise is produced in-house. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-62bd5106b32cfa302d08|cite]])
- About half of Zara's cloth arrives undyed so it can react to midseason taste shifts; when cotton hit record prices, Zara moved away from costly fabrics and kept margins, while H&M's profits fell 10 percent (2011). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-0c9be467da0a6b539d42|cite]])
- Fashion retailers like H&M spend as much as 4 percent of revenue on promotion, while Inditex ad spending ranges from 0.3 to 0.05 percent (2015). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-55f7aa98812656a551a0|cite]])
- H&M's pandemic loss was estimated at four times Inditex's, and Primark, with no online sales, said COVID store closures cost it $1.4 billion (2021). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-d3ebc22d3ac3045214dc|cite]])
- Zara has experimented with hybrid mirrors that read a garment's RFID tag to suggest outfits (2018), and H&M, Macy's and Sears have also tried smart mirrors. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-5b109ed4cb89a4fecb98|cite]])
- Inditex committed to buying 30 percent of Infinited Fiber Company's output, invested in Spanish recycler Moda Re, and partnered with H&M and Mango on a nonprofit to manage clothing waste (2023). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-b9cc56650b11dad3bab5|cite]])
- H&M has increased how often new items reach stores and is eyeing moving some contract manufacturing from Asia closer to Europe (2017). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-54a218ddfbd99b591c32|cite]])
- Shein's U.S. sales exceeded Zara's and H&M's combined, and in 2022 investors valued the private Shein above the combined market cap of Inditex and H&M. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-f9e6623b8fe1cc39f607|cite]])
