# Waymo

Alphabet's self-driving car and robotaxi company.

## Facts by source

### 2026-05-07 [[2026-05-07-Interview-Joanna-Stern-Living-With-AI|An Interview with Joanna Stern About Living With AI]]

- Stern says the book was meant from the start to cover AI beyond LLMs (large language models, the tech behind chatbots), such as Waymo robotaxis and AI radiology. ([[2026-05-07-Interview-Joanna-Stern-Living-With-AI#^clm-04841967262a81dfd712|cite]])
- Prediction: Stern predicts self-driving cars will take over roads in the next 10 years. ([[2026-05-07-Interview-Joanna-Stern-Living-With-AI#^clm-419c6941155f557dec2d|cite]])

### 2026-06-02 [[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company|The Google Capital Company]]

- Thompson notes Waymo has rejected an asset-light model of licensing its software to carmakers and instead owns and operates its own cars; he says that choice always felt at odds with Google Services but may fit Google Cloud and a capital-heavy Google. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-94e351877a3a2ffa9906|cite]])

### 2026-06-18 [[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI|An Interview with Michael Morton About E-Commerce in the Age of AI]]

- Prediction: Morton predicts Waymo will dominate robotaxis for the next 18 to 24 months, while over 3 to 5 years multiple operators will need demand aggregation such as Uber. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-d5f79dd2da119129213f|cite]])
- Morton says the Uber and Waymo partnership appears to have broken down, and he admits he got that call wrong. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-25a02db36cd314e1ebfe|cite]])
- Thompson argues Google's asset-heavy robotaxi push looks strategically dumb right up until it works, like Berkshire moving cash into railroads, and could leave an impregnable moat. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-8e760ada9fa277f9eb8f|cite]])
- Morton's model says robotaxis turn EBIT-per-mile positive at low-to-mid 30s trips per car per day, and Waymo's California cars are near high-20s. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-6793558427714fa8a345|cite]])
- Using bill-of-lading shipping records, Morton found Waymo importing about 300 Zeekr-built Ojai vans a month despite 100% tariffs, putting it on pace to double its fleet in 12 months. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-62a076d03ae13db28c23|cite]])
