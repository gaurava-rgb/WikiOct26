# Federal Trade Commission

U.S. consumer protection and antitrust agency; sued Amazon in September 2023.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- According to the FTC, advertised products on Amazon are forty-six times more likely to get clicks, so many suppliers feel compelled to pay for placement. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-c89f14bdff5da2e41cd8|cite]])
- In September 2023 the Federal Trade Commission and seventeen states sued Amazon on some twenty charges, accusing it of inflating prices and locking sellers into its platform and into buying its extra services. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-132849342d8d16290686|cite]])
- The 2023 FTC case against Amazon was led by Biden-era FTC Chair Lina Khan. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-4c34a2e2df20acd23c39|cite]])
- The FTC says Amazon punishes sellers who offer lower prices elsewhere by burying them in search results. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-acdfb80b520ce7e68a17|cite]])
- Regulators cite Amazon's pay-to-play model: sellers pay for listings, advertising, warehousing, and fulfillment to qualify for Prime, and shoppers often filter searches to Prime-eligible goods. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7a9e7bca9fc819c9904d|cite]])
- With few strong rivals, governments worry Amazon can raise prices and worsen service without losing customers or sellers; they also question how Amazon picks the featured seller when several offer the same item at the same price. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-8ba3910b05c5a3453b22|cite]])
- Amazon says the FTC is wrong: it will not promote products that are not competitively priced, its tools help small businesses reach larger markets, and its screening protects shoppers from fraud and counterfeits. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-a55b4c1ce4c9690ca271|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues the FTC case is not a slam dunk, noting Lina Khan had failed in earlier cases against Meta and Microsoft. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-b60899dee8b0e33f7585|cite]])
