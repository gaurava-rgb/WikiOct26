# Michael Dalton

OpenAI security researcher who presented the Hugging Face incident at Black Hat USA.

## Facts by source

### 2026-08-24 [[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation|Autonomy and Innovation]]

- OpenAI's Eric Wallace and Michael Dalton presented the Hugging Face incident at the Black Hat USA conference. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation#^clm-1ddc81de62da77435729|cite]])
- Dalton said fully automated offense is now shown to be possible, but there is no existence proof yet for fully automating core defensive loops. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation#^clm-97351691f1d15b96ce99|cite]])
- Dalton warned that automating vulnerability finding without automating patching would just move the bottleneck and drown human engineers in fixes. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation#^clm-d030ba31f42539105390|cite]])
- Dalton argued the full defensive loop must be automated: an agent finds the bug, proposes a patch, and infrastructure rolls it out and rolls it back on an outage. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation#^clm-f3a2ea9f44a773546e84|cite]])
