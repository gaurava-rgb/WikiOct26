# Parallel

Startup founded in 2023 by Parag Agarwal that sells web search and deep research to AI agents through a pay-per-use API, and pays content owners via Index by Parallel.

## Facts by source

### 2026-05-21 [[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel|An Interview with Parallel Founder Parag Agarwal About Valuing Content on the Agentic Web]]

- Parallel, founded by Parag Agarwal in 2023, is a search engine for AI agents that mixes compute, models, and search, and delivers results based on how much the customer is willing to spend. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-c173324ef5a87661668d|cite]])
- In the week of May 21, 2026, Parallel launched Index by Parallel, which shows what the content on any given site is worth to agents and pays content creators based on that value. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-a44560fb776c5d3413a4|cite]])
- Prediction: Agarwal says that three years ago he became convinced agents will use the web 1000x more than humans ever have. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-3ba8477483de4dcadd8f|cite]])
- Agarwal says exact Shapley values cost more to compute than the payment they decide, so Parallel spent a year to a year and a half building a model that estimates them, trained on costly simulations. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-6f864a396a60e9a69ff2|cite]])
- Index by Parallel gives each site four scores: Impressions, Citations, Value, and Uniqueness; Uniqueness is where the Shapley math rewards content that others do not also supply. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-9226c02d87ef22445018|cite]])
- Agarwal says Parallel's early data shows unique facts win, with local news and PR Newswire getting outsized value, and plans to publish findings over time. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-ac2fb07c088e957ec64f|cite]])
- Thompson reports that Stratechery scored highest on Value in the Index but dropped sharply on Uniqueness, while nyc.gov shot up on Uniqueness. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-9733f7eddc34009b3404|cite]])
- Agarwal says content that gives agents a recipe, a plan for how to tackle a kind of problem, is also cited and rewarded, not just raw facts. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-c5f66ebcb55b87d91ec4|cite]])
- Agarwal says Parallel's product has been in market for about nine months, selling a search index plus search agents on top, used by customers like Notion and Harvey. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-1aa8af7805969c39e2de|cite]])
- All of Parallel's APIs are pay per use; a customer can set a budget like $0.10 for a query and pay more to continue, and the API flags low confidence so an agent can decide to spend more. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-95fe5f70ee9cac3ee600|cite]])
- Agarwal argues the open web will not stay open if its business model fails, and that paying content owners is a business argument, not a moral one. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-0c38de769e038d04b50a|cite]])
- Parallel's first paid content partners include PR Newswire, PitchBook, Tracxn, Fortune, and The Atlantic, plus independent writers like Azeem Azhar and Alex Heath. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-bcb3ff738b03e5a8d745|cite]])
- Agarwal says payouts come from a shared pool, like Spotify, that grows with how much agents use the web, and split by Shapley value. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-e60abd820e9bf9f172d0|cite]])
- Agarwal says paywalled or login content can only be reached through an Index content partnership, and open content is crawled only if the owner allows it. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-2b5508db4182092724d3|cite]])
- Agarwal says Parallel complements model makers like OpenAI and Anthropic rather than competing with them, and is usually bought alongside their models. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-e33601b53b917c709c93|cite]])
- Agarwal says Cloudflare's Matthew Prince has the same diagnosis that the open web could die, and Cloudflare can create scarcity while Parallel knows what content is worth from the demand side. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-03b15684007ed44c0db4|cite]])
- Agarwal says the 1000x agent usage forecast from two and a half years ago now looks too low, and demand is growing very fast. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-d53aa5589f963ee3a8a3|cite]])
- Agarwal says Parallel's marketplace covers only data agents use at inference time (when answering), not training data, because no one yet knows how to attribute training data. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-5618f36d93f27c14caef|cite]])
- Prediction: Agarwal predicts that if demand keeps growing as forecast, within two years people will change behavior and create content aimed at gaps agents need filled. ([[2026-05-21-Interview-Parag-Agarwal-Parallel#^clm-171d726d35fdf0ed75f7|cite]])
