# Whole Foods

Grocery chain Amazon bought in 2017.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch04 FreshDirect - A Tech-Heavy Online Grocer]]

- Amazon has pushed into the $700 billion grocery retail market, buying Whole Foods and a large northern New Jersey distribution center formerly owned by PathMark. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-8a14a944ee5dea6fe736|cite]])
- Instacart uses a network of Uber-style contract delivery workers to pick groceries from retailers such as Whole Foods and Costco. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-11c9ae0be37e73cb36e7|cite]])
- By 2023 Amazon had begun closing some Amazon Fresh and Amazon Go stores, including two Go stores in New York City, though it launched Just Walk Out tech in a Washington DC Whole Foods. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-c39495f767c64f659757|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues FreshDirect grew when New York's grocery market was fragmented among local players, but stronger chains like Trader Joe's and Whole Foods have since arrived. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-c402cd492d5ff8d1e97a|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Amazon owns MGM Studios and, through its Whole Foods purchase, is one of the leading US grocers, where it is rolling out pay-with-your-palm and just-walk-out cashierless checkout. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6d09e19e2e1aec47e433|cite]])
- Amazon runs Whole Foods as a separate business with its own CEO. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-469861236cf27566abc0|cite]])
- Amazon bought Whole Foods and its then 450 stores for $13.7 billion, its largest grocery move, and has since opened about 100 more stores. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7df1f80333900a6f2e58|cite]])
- Before Amazon bought it, Whole Foods was struggling and looking for a buyer, and about 70 percent of the $13.7 billion price was booked as goodwill (intangible value such as brand and customer base). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-9aa8cde0d02874003055|cite]])
- Amazon has woven itself into Whole Foods: Amazon lockers, box-free Amazon returns, Amazon One palm payment, Just Walk Out tech in some stores, Alexa end-caps, and deep discounts for Prime members. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-f7978f44801a302c8dcc|cite]])
- Amazon One palm payment went nationwide across many Whole Foods stores and was licensed to venues such as Coors Field, where it can also verify age for alcohol. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-30eac68264a04758f5d6|cite]])
- Amazon combined its website, Fresh, and Whole Foods orders into one delivery cart and opened a delivery-only dark store Whole Foods in Brooklyn. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-85ffc2263c7356074e51|cite]])
- Only two Republican-led states joined the FTC suit, but Gallaugher notes Amazon has few friends on left or right; Senator Elizabeth Warren has urged breaking up Amazon and splitting off AWS and Whole Foods. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ef4d767e649394610f59|cite]])
