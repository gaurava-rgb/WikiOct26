# Flipkart

India's largest e-commerce firm, about 80 percent owned by Walmart.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Walmart has invested a total of $19.5 billion over several years for an 80 percent share of Flipkart, India's largest e-commerce firm; its first stake was the largest foreign direct investment ever made in an Indian company. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-740fd13c85838e38c8c0|cite]])
- At the time of Walmart's latest investment, Flipkart had 350 million customers, over 10,000 employees, and sold over 150 million products in 80 categories. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-9c97ca17b8e7fd242d75|cite]])
- In India almost all of Amazon's investment has gone to AWS, while Walmart leads in dollars invested and in partner share of Indian e-commerce. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-7e9ba063e6541f2d139c|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues that Walmart sees India as a market where it can beat Amazon, and that with its US market near saturation it is looking abroad, especially to India, for growth. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-c020418041b29e940dea|cite]])
