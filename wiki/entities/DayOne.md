# DayOne

Singapore-based data center operator spun off from GDS Holdings, preparing a New York IPO.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-16 [[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok|Singapore’s DayOne Pushes Ahead With Data Center IPO Tied to TikTok]]

- Singapore-based data center operator DayOne is preparing an initial public offering in New York to raise as much as $5 billion. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-d34a23c88ac7ff7562ef|cite]])
- ByteDance accounts for nearly 70% of DayOne revenue, representing 69.2% of sales in the first half of 2026. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-be1aaf55d4dbfc08c71e|cite]])
- DayOne revenue from ByteDance supports conventional workloads like video streaming and e-commerce rather than AI training or inference. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-25453dd7d765f0b0e5be|cite]])
- GDS International spun off DayOne in late 2024 after raising $1.87 billion, leaving Coatue, Hillhouse, and GDS each with about 19% stakes. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-9e88019c90b86ed1d5b3|cite]])
- As of September 2026, ByteDance committed to 838 megawatts of data center capacity across DayOne sites in Malaysia, Thailand, and Finland. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-f5d3fba059a1765df445|cite]])
- TikTok is investing 2 billion euros ($2.2 billion) in two Finnish data centers in Kouvola and Lahti under Project Clover to store European user data locally. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-a0efc43f207f5fe7ca9f|cite]])
- Oracle is DayOne second-largest customer, generating 15% of DayOne revenue in the first half of 2026. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-9011ab03030f1f0c50a4|cite]])
- Prediction: For contracts starting in 2027, ByteDance represents less than 50% of DayOne total commitments as non-Chinese customers expand. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-668ce00240fc4baaebd5|cite]])
- In June 2026, Firmus agreed to partner with DayOne on a data center in Batam, Indonesia, with at least 170,000 advanced Nvidia chips, with Meta leasing capacity. ([[2026-10-16-Singapores-DayOne-Pushes-Ahead-With-Data-Center-IPO-Tied-to-TikTok#^clm-bce3b565b118481ac857|cite]])

### 2026-10-20 [[2026-10-20-Nvidia-Moves-to-Back-Firmus-After-Pulled-IPO|Nvidia Moves to Back GPU Cloud Provider Firmus After Investors Balk at Its IPO]]

- Nvidia agreed in June 2026 to guarantee Firmus' $15 billion purchase of server racks housing 170,000 Rubin and Blackwell GPUs for DayOne's facility in Batam, Indonesia. ([[2026-10-20-Nvidia-Moves-to-Back-Firmus-After-Pulled-IPO#^clm-0c6d2f7ba953dec5f4ac|cite]])
