# Gallup

US polling company that surveys public opinion.

## Facts by source

### 2026-05-18 [[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent|Data Center Discontent, Understanding the Opposition, Fixing the Problem]]

- A Gallup poll found 71% of Americans oppose building AI data centers in their area, 48% strongly, while only 7% strongly favor them. ([[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent#^clm-9067680c25cd99433361|cite]])
- In the same Gallup survey, 53% oppose a local nuclear plant, and nuclear opposition has never been above 63% since Gallup began asking in 2001. ([[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent#^clm-34aa796c9d5daec53f88|cite]])
- Majorities of every party oppose a local data center, but Democrats are more likely to be strongly opposed than Republicans (56% vs 39%), with independents at 48%. ([[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent#^clm-cbf1b169c46aeac38884|cite]])
