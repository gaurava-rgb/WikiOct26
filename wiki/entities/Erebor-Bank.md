# Erebor Bank

Venture-backed de novo bank founded by Palmer Luckey serving defense and crypto firms.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-18 [[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money|Why Upstart Banks May Have to Keep Raising Money]]

- De novo banks face 10% to 12% minimum tier 1 leverage ratios for their first three years, compared to 5% to 6% for established banks. ([[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money#^clm-817213633458a5c3bad7|cite]])
- Unprofitable startup banks must repeatedly raise equity from investors to fund regulatory capital cushions as deposits and assets expand. ([[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money#^clm-ea292a7e2d7bbdb51008|cite]])
- Palmer Luckey's Erebor Bank is set to raise $1.5 billion at an $8 billion valuation after launching with $625 million in February 2026. ([[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money#^clm-34fd969fd391db053710|cite]])
- Erebor Bank deposits surged from $4 billion in June 2026 to $7 billion at the end of September 2026. ([[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money#^clm-7b0fc706687207f31ef5|cite]])
- The FDIC imposed a capital-call condition on Erebor Bank requiring investors to contribute additional equity if regulatory capital drops below minimums. ([[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money#^clm-d57fcb71f0dc4659e6aa|cite]])
- Erebor Bank focuses on customers traditional banks avoid, including defense contractors, crypto firms, and businesses operating in Venezuela and Syria. ([[2026-10-18-Why-Upstart-Banks-May-Have-to-Keep-Raising-Money#^clm-de1442880067a14941ec|cite]])
