# Sparc Group

Retail brand operator that owns Forever 21; partly owned by Simon Property Group.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia]]

- Forever 21 filed for bankruptcy in 2020 and was taken over by Sparc Group, housed with brands like Aeropostale and Brooks Brothers; Sparc is partly owned by Simon Property Group, the largest U.S. mall owner. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-78336758f3b9b804b74f|cite]])
- In a 2023 deal, Shein took a one-third stake in Sparc while Sparc became a minority shareholder in Shein; Shein goods will be sold and returned in Forever 21's 540 stores. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-d8dd5d37a676520ee287|cite]])
