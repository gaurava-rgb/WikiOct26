# Annapurna Labs

Israeli chip designer Amazon bought (2015; the Gallaugher book says 2016); designs AWS's Nitro, Graviton, Trainium, and Inferentia chips.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- On p.291 the book says Amazon acquired chip designer Annapurna in 2016, and many AWS servers use chipsets Annapurna designed. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-c0f5c066b7a3d31a8a26|cite]])
