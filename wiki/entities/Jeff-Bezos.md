# Jeff Bezos

Founder of Amazon and its CEO until 2021.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Amazon began as an online bookstore, then expanded to other media such as CDs and DVDs, then to nearly every category, and then opened its store to third-party sellers against pundits' advice. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-20089db239e50f2d5c43|cite]])
- Cloud computing was so profitable that Jeff Bezos picked Andy Jassy, the former head of AWS, to succeed him as Amazon CEO. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-1264697facd951fcc097|cite]])
- At his last annual results presentation as CEO, Jeff Bezos said: 'When you look at our financial results, what you're actually seeing are the long-run cumulative results of invention.' ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-bbe8c0d36365c97b31c6|cite]])
- Amazon's first shareholder letter, in 1997, was titled 'It's all about the long term', and long-term thinking has remained a core tenet of its strategy. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-218195a1b14cb2747fcb|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos said that on a three-year horizon you compete against many firms, but on a seven-year horizon you compete against only a fraction of them, because very few companies are willing to wait that long. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-01f2b9346843e0ba3418|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos said Amazon likes things to work in five to seven years and is 'stubborn on vision and flexible on details'. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-29189d0de64da315ab4d|cite]])
- Through Bezos Expeditions, Jeff Bezos owns Blue Origin, a rocket firm that flew William Shatner to space and holds NASA contracts including the next lunar lander. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-3c33e310a0fc28750f64|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos personally invested in Twitter and Uber, and personally (not Amazon) bought The Washington Post. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6fc3150e39a87495077a|cite]])
- Amazon started by selling only books, from an early Seattle office with a 400-square-foot basement warehouse in a low-rent area next to a needle exchange, a pawn shop, and 'Wigland'. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6e98e08eb9dd2325273b|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos's napkin sketch, framed in Amazon's headquarters lobby as the 'Wheel of Growth', rests on three pillars: large selection, customer experience (convenience), and lower prices. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ff596a2216cf7b6cbee5|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos said the more important question is what will NOT change in ten years, because you can build a business on stable things like low prices, faster delivery, and vast selection. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-fb6863b63e2576acc9e4|cite]])
- To stay nimble as it grew, Jeff Bezos mandated 'two-pizza teams', meaning any project team must be small enough to be fed by two pizzas, which helps ideas flourish, discourages groupthink, and lets several efforts compete. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-b97df474e468df57f5f1|cite]])
- Amazon built the Kindle because media made up well over 20 percent of its revenue, and Jeff Bezos saw that media was going digital and that whoever controlled the platform would dominate, since readers want one library on one device. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ce7ea73c8412103d3f7d|cite]])
- Under wholesale pricing Amazon paid publishers for eBooks and set its own prices; Jeff Bezos said trade eBooks should not cost more than ten dollars, and Amazon sold many near or below cost to build the Kindle standard. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-32a441324f5780f3bd9c|cite]])
- Amazon picked AWS chief Andy Jassy, rather than someone from its retail or device businesses, to replace Jeff Bezos as Amazon CEO, a sign of how important the cloud is to the company. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ea55c163fffb4d641e07|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos has described AWS as having a seven-year head start before facing serious, like-minded competition. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-344effddeb146ff7f8b3|cite]])
- Jeff Bezos has joked that Barnes & Noble took only a year to launch a Kindle rival, while big tech rivals took years to build anything close to AWS. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-2d2e2a66840d07cebc30|cite]])
- Warren Buffett, praising Jeff Bezos for building two major industries at once, said: "You do not want to give Jeff Bezos a 7-year head start." ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-644bdcebe50f8b37b198|cite]])
