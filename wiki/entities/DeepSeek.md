# DeepSeek

Chinese AI lab based in Hangzhou that makes cheap, efficient open models such as V4 and R1.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-21 [[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls|DeepSeek Bets Big on Huawei Chips to Bypass U.S. Export Controls]]

- DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors that training models on more Chinese-made chips is a major priority and expects Huawei to start delivering training chips as early as Q4 2026, per two people with direct knowledge. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-803076f04e6580530f19|cite]])
- Liang called shifting training to Huawei chips one of DeepSeek's biggest bets and said it has to work. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-d7e460d3abe8a0ac91fd|cite]])
- DeepSeek is finalizing its second funding round of 50 billion yuan ($7.5 billion) at a 500 billion yuan valuation. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-b4275ae679e4fd7e0542|cite]])
- DeepSeek held its investor meeting under strict anti-leak rules after the transcript of a May investor call leaked to Chinese media, which led it to pause the round for a week. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-ecfb3398e95b75a94615|cite]])
- DeepSeek still trains new models on Nvidia chips, likely obtained from the black market. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-a1d88914764431ddaf83|cite]])
- Liang said DeepSeek is investing about 30 billion yuan to expand computing capacity, after raising 50 billion yuan in June. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-544e842dc8e43a64635c|cite]])
- In May 2026 Liang estimated that a model on par with OpenAI's largest would need about 50,000 Nvidia GB300 chips or 200,000 Huawei Ascend 950 chips, and said Huawei could not supply enough. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-1248b8a1fe3452c17966|cite]])
- DeepSeek is training a 2-trillion-parameter model, bigger than its 1.4-trillion-parameter V4 flagship, and plans an 8-trillion-parameter model. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-d51b4c3bf4f2c6d92959|cite]])
- Huawei said in April 2026 its chips handled part of training DeepSeek's smaller V4-Flash model, and the two adapted the V4 family to Huawei's Ascend systems. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls#^clm-33437af44ab2563368e0|cite]])

### 2026-09-23 [[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising|DeepSeek's Annualized Revenue Hits $1 Billion as Startup Finalizes $7.5 Billion Fundraising]]

- DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate hit $1 billion, more than double a few months earlier, per two people with direct knowledge. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-e97a19a2235d9d92959c|cite]])
- DeepSeek aims to close its second round of 50 billion yuan ($7.5 billion) at a valuation of about $75 billion by the end of October, and is preparing to go public on the Shanghai Stock Exchange. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-c04a9c3d73b67dde8cb5|cite]])
- A DeepSeek price hike in August 2026 raised model costs for customers by 2.3 to 4.5 times, yet DeepSeek remains among the cheapest major models. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-91644e44a312028a3c0c|cite]])
- Liang Wenfeng said the price hike did not shrink DeepSeek's customer base because demand stays strong. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-9873e342e66383a28033|cite]])
- DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash and V4-Flash perform like much larger models, and customers worldwide use them to power autonomous agents cheaply. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-6415ff92c8b0ac8c6683|cite]])
- In July 2026 The Information reported a DeepSeek run rate of $400 million to $500 million and an 82.9% gross margin on API sales in the first seven months of 2026, beating Anthropic and OpenAI over similar periods. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-eadc2bbdf7ce9ac01ca1|cite]])
- DeepSeek's healthy margins come from low running costs, by making infrastructure efficient so tasks need fewer chips. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-6cf7a36b6f401073b2ba|cite]])
- Liang told investors that making more revenue is not a priority; revenue comes almost entirely from API access, and the chatbot app is free with no ads. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-6423ff61b89095af7fd3|cite]])
- DeepSeek devotes more than 70% of its computing capacity to training new models and under 30% to inference. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-b427f9060caa81e7e184|cite]])
- Internal tests show DeepSeek's smaller models run well on gaming graphics chips and can handle most everyday user tasks, which could ease the chip shortage. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-aacebebfdbcbe84b987c|cite]])
- After DeepSeek's R1 release in 2025, black-market demand and prices for Nvidia gaming chips in China surged. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-c973270001afa227336a|cite]])
- Prediction: DeepSeek's biggest challenge is getting enough compute to train new models under US export limits on Nvidia chips; Liang expects Huawei training chips as early as Q4 2026. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-8aaa070d95ad852f5bc4|cite]])
- China's internet regulator is investigating DeepSeek and Moonshot AI after Anthropic alleged both routed sensitive user data to Claude models. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising#^clm-05a800c8d1b6cf5b9b84|cite]])
