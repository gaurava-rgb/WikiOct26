# miHoYo

Shanghai videogame maker behind Genshin Impact, now building its own large language models.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-09 [[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded|China’s AI Model Race Is Just Getting More Crowded]]

- Three big Chinese tech firms from outside AI, game maker miHoYo, phone and car maker Xiaomi, and delivery app Meituan, are now building their own large language models from scratch. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-5b7dc6af8c5e7d825a9c|cite]])
- miHoYo, the Shanghai maker of Genshin Impact, plans to invest up to 100 billion yuan ($14.9 billion) in AI development over the next three years and aims to be a top-tier Chinese lab within a couple of years. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-e4dd0d4edd45e68625f4|cite]])
- miHoYo is trying to get access to Nvidia's Blackwell chips to train more competitive models, like leading Chinese AI firms do, per a person with direct knowledge. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-ddae05e3b2dd3e68e31e|cite]])
