# USPS

The U.S. Postal Service, which handles much of Amazon's rural delivery.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- As late as 2015, FedEx, UPS, and the U.S. Postal Service delivered more than 97 percent of Amazon's U.S. packages. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-2143b1d5ef230745091f|cite]])
- Amazon's software picks the cheapest and fastest carrier for each package, weighing cost, fuel prices, routing, delivery promise, weather, traffic, and fleet availability before dispatching to UPS, the USPS, or itself. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6491414765f23f2e1f60|cite]])
- Amazon cut its reliance on the U.S. Postal Service in half in just two years, per a 2019 report. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-e650d02246771c511055|cite]])
