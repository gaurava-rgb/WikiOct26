# Peapod

Ahold Delhaize's long-running online grocery delivery service.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch04 FreshDirect - A Tech-Heavy Online Grocer]]

- Ahold Delhaize, the Netherlands-based parent of Stop & Shop, completed its acquisition of a majority stake in FreshDirect in January 2021, even though it had tried for years to grow its own delivery service, Peapod. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-bd841440ff0234dfb444|cite]])
- In February 2020 Ahold Delhaize shut down Peapod's grocery delivery in the Midwest for poor performance, cutting 500 jobs, in the same year it agreed to buy FreshDirect. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-382718831f4b53b373d8|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Ahold's purchase of FreshDirect shows how hard it is to adapt traditional grocery models, since Peapod struggled to grow despite years of experimentation. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-4115a3607e313019eafa|cite]])
