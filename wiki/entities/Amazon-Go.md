# Amazon Go

Amazon's cashierless 'just walk out' convenience stores.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Amazon closed nine Amazon Go stores in 2023 but still ran 23 in the U.S. and 20 in the UK by fall 2023. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-56b802c185cec345b0a7|cite]])
- Amazon Go stores have no cashiers: shoppers scan an app on entry, and image recognition, motion and weight sensors, and AI charge them for what they take when they leave. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-50f6b5977ea30cb1e4a7|cite]])
- Rivals followed Amazon Go with cashierless stores: Walmart's Sam's Club Now in Dallas, and Tesco and Carrefour in the UK and France. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-3379453b5d59a71eb8eb|cite]])
- Amazon began licensing its grab-and-go technology to other retailers, with Hudson News airport stores as the first licensee. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-4ee6187d20284a3b4f3c|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues that because the product-recognition models learn from data, more cashierless stores mean more data and more accurate AI. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-a0b9c9b3b9e6d03906ca|cite]])
