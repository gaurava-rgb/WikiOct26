# Shein

China-founded fast-fashion e-commerce company and marketplace.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia]]

- Chris Xu is the CEO and founder of Shein, a direct-to-consumer fashion site started in Nanjing, China; the name is a shortened form of an earlier name, SheInside. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-dc8cd663471173482849|cite]])
- Shein is now headquartered in Singapore, with significant operations in the southern China manufacturing hub of Guangzhou. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-5b098c93bb931e05865e|cite]])
- Shein posted $23 billion in sales in 2022, making it the world's number two fashion firm, just ahead of H&M ($22.25 billion) and behind only Inditex ($35.2 billion). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-eb1020c431c142eb4992|cite]])
- Shein claims to be solidly profitable, with sales spanning 220 countries; the United States, Europe, and Brazil make up most of its revenue. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-c1cb718cd44ccdecffdc|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Shein has out-Zara'ed Inditex, using AI and analytics to automate design and information systems to unbundle much of Zara's vertically integrated supply chain in ways that skirt import restrictions and lower costs. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-1a51b9431972fd508f84|cite]])
- A TikTok with over 10 million views compared a $79 Zara dress with an indistinguishable one bought from Shein for $15, part of a wave of #zaravshein posts. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-06969642fed200857873|cite]])
- Prediction: Shein founder Chris Xu claimed the company would reach $58.5 billion in sales by 2025, signaling Amazon-sized goals beyond apparel. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-081ceca022710028c48c|cite]])
- By 2021, Shein's iPhone app had overtaken Amazon's to become the leading shopping app in over fifty countries, including the United States. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-fdc6c2c3394b1edf8b3c|cite]])
- At one point in 2022, Shein raised money at a valuation higher than the market capitalizations of Inditex and H&M combined. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-57c865be736b87f6480b|cite]])
- In 2023 a down-round cut Shein's valuation by one-third, but the privately held company still raised $2 billion more and remained the world's third most valuable startup, behind ByteDance and SpaceX. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-56b47500f11898c09693|cite]])
- Temu, an upstart launched in China, mimics large portions of the Shein model; it began with a wide variety of goods rather than clothing but has started a money-losing assault on fast fashion. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-074680dc6a55869cba08|cite]])
- Temu's shopping app passed Shein's to become the most popular shopping app in the United States. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-507599e1c6b4d2db66c7|cite]])
- Prediction: Gallaugher argues it is hard to imagine the U.S. legislature, with its bipartisan critique of China, allowing the de minimis loophole that speeds Shein's direct-to-consumer shipments to continue. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-10e09c8989fc1907bcb3|cite]])
- Shein can go from spotting a trend to shipping a product in as little as three days, faster than Zara. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-78bd346ab58cdf05b517|cite]])
- Shein introduces some 10,000 new styles every single day, an outrageous figure even for fast fashion, and may have as many as 600,000 items on its site at a time. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-d0558a1c1ca88da1cc96|cite]])
- A Key Takeaway in the chapter says Shein's data and AI operation lets it create thousands of new products a year, many more than even Zara. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-490e4fdd8601e105b4dd|cite]])
- Shein uses AI and analytics to mine social media, ad response, and app usage for trends, then pushes automated, predictive product specs to thousands of small contract manufacturers who use Shein-provided automation and cheap labor. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-5d13109351090fb1f883|cite]])
- Shein's produced lots are tiny, sometimes fewer than 100 goods, and with no stores it holds little warehouse stock and very little unsold inventory, in an industry whose saying is 'inventory = death'. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-30ed95db5c207a7fb133|cite]])
- Shein's fast product-to-manufacture cycle has been called 'real-time retail', and others compare it to a ghost kitchen that goes from order to delivery without a physical restaurant. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-8f5fe2a4163673253229|cite]])
- Shein is often described as secretive; several publications said it started by selling wedding dresses, but the company told The Guardian this was false, and a spat between founders is part of its early history. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-15e71ab692b9207a3b23|cite]])
- Shein is the largest fashion retailer, online or off, in the United States and is growing rapidly in Brazil and Europe. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-edafccdc0d30360b8fcb|cite]])
- The Shein app emphasizes discovery over search, unlike Amazon and Google; Bessemer's Sisi Song says it presents products the way TikTok shows endless video recommendations. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-73d3a77ab279686bd477|cite]])
- Shein app use is about twice Amazon's even though Amazon sells a far greater range of products, and every engagement becomes a data point for design. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-2b9a251fb57062a9e1dd|cite]])
- Shein built its own proprietary deep-learning AI that browses TikTok and other social media and uses neural-network image recognition to spot popular clothing trends in images and video. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-5cc8153fdeb0a2d93335|cite]])
- Shein combines its proprietary data with third-party data and tools such as Google Trend Finder to map customer behavior to purchasing decisions for design and production levels. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-5ed9c57fb42fb1ee18e1|cite]])
- Shein is followed by over three times as many TikTok users as Zara, with ASOS and H&M further behind. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-eb47f303a808597ee3c0|cite]])
- UBS reports the average Shein customer spends about $100 a month on clothing, 60 percent more than the average U.S. woman. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-8f33ff14d664ce27bf48|cite]])
- Unlike Zara, which gathers in-store demand and mimics catwalk fashions, Shein ignores the runway and responds to consumer trends mined from social media, ads, and app use. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-178256e79d817026ce26|cite]])
- Andreessen Horowitz partner Connie Chan sees Shein as a new phase of fast fashion where consumers care less if Vogue thinks a piece is cool. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-e42acf6be463fc28732d|cite]])
- Shein's 800-plus person product team works as prototype-designer hybrids, assembling products from machine-generated suggestions; Gallaugher calls them the next generation of AI worker. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-dae4d86c7e442b595727|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Shein's reduced human touch explains its huge design output but has led to unauthorized use of others' work and racially or culturally insensitive products. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-e8f1a4a645ea5fbbfe80|cite]])
- Shein picks thousands of small to mid-sized contract manufacturers able to make small runs quickly, and has persuaded them to drop their own software for a custom Shein system that coordinates the whole supply chain. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-336a489abba86d2790a8|cite]])
- One analysis compares Shein's contract manufacturing model to Uber, but for contract manufacturers instead of drivers: anyone meeting Shein's standards can play. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-09eb17255789330efaa7|cite]])
- Shein's first order for a new product is usually a few dozen units; the item is surfaced in ads and the app as an A/B test, and hot items get larger runs, sometimes across several suppliers. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-2cb87b170237858d2342|cite]])
- Shein calls its model of real-time data, trial test, then more units if warranted the large-scale automated test and reorder (LATR) model. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-9bf55c96b797b393b8ff|cite]])
- Because Shein's first runs are so small and fast, losses from misfires are a low single-digit percent, compared with the 30 percent write-off levels of conventional retailers. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-9ef8409ab9717f47636d|cite]])
- Nearly all products in the Shein app have been live three months or less, while at Zara about 53 percent of products are less than three months old. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-54dfb87288c91cd5fb19|cite]])
- Suppliers that adopt Shein's systems get virtually guaranteed, constant business; the system sends exact specs and unit counts, reports which products fail or sell, coordinates shipping, and tracks delivery speed and perceived quality. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-1937659342af6349fa29|cite]])
- Shein cuts suppliers that fail its standards, and tight integration is thought to convince factories to behave well rather than break contracts or leak partner information, as Chinese firms are notorious for doing. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-bcd1fc1a173d9235aeba|cite]])
- Shein has a reputation for paying suppliers on time and faster than competitors, a big incentive in China where suppliers often cite late payments. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-b0010473b05f311353a7|cite]])
- Shein's manufacturing base is focused on China's Guangzhou region, but it is recruiting suppliers worldwide to diversify beyond single-country sourcing. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-add545ce8d2620b2e741|cite]])
- Under the U.S. de minimis provision, imports are subject to duty only when a shipment is worth over $800, so a Shein order under $800 arrives with no import duty assessed. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-50acc65ac8608b62ac57|cite]])
- China has not taxed exports by Chinese direct-to-consumer companies since 2018. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-b71228bd395d3606e189|cite]])
- An industry pundit says Zara cannot get around U.S. import duties because it sells to stores and imports in bulk rather than shipping to individuals. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-2a29e459196ef4e1ed18|cite]])
- Under the U.S. Tariff Act of 1930, orders of $800 or less also skip customs checks, so Shein goods almost always arrive in under two weeks and many in four days. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-1c2410be385b2d234ba5|cite]])
- Lawmakers in the U.S. and other nations are exploring legislation to close the factory-to-consumer de minimis provision, and the lighter inspections raise concern about banned Xinjiang cotton. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-75e5184bf2e51398aad1|cite]])

- Amazon's shifting standards and pressure on Chinese sellers to warehouse goods in its fulfillment centers for extra fees angered them, giving Shein an opening to recruit them; many now sell on Amazon under Shein's name. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-4ba4a82527aba04038a9|cite]])
