# CAISSI

Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence, the small Commerce Department agency that tests frontier AI models; formerly the AI Safety Institute.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-12 [[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House|Trump Administration Plans to Move AI Testing Agency Officials to White House]]

- In September 2026 the Trump administration began restructuring CAISSI so that three of its senior officials report directly to the White House, per a White House official. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-13ecdcfeabef20299907|cite]])
- After months of weighing a move of all of CAISSI into the White House, Chief of Staff Susie Wiles and other officials chose the smaller move to better fit CAISSI into White House AI decisions. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-be4fdc7fae67b48011db|cite]])
- The three CAISSI officials will report to OSTP director Michael Kratsios and National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross; Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick had invited both to co-manage CAISSI about six months earlier. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-17dbef951a7583fe44aa|cite]])
- The White House and the Commerce Department have been confused about the CAISSI move; a White House official said Commerce's poor coordination shows why CAISSI leadership must move. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-9b8de668807a39c6b1c8|cite]])
- CAISSI recently renamed itself to match Trump's preferred term Super Intelligence, its third name in less than two years. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-a7ad7028433604ba31d6|cite]])
- Some officials want CAISSI moved out of Commerce for better resources; Michael Kratsios has argued for moving it to the Department of Energy to use the national laboratories. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-389fca409f8221e93d34|cite]])
- As of October 2026 CAISSI has only about 40 staff and about $15 million in annual funding, yet plays a key role in testing new AI models for capabilities and safety. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-27549ac2f39a859244ea|cite]])
- Under a White House framework finalized in August 2026, CAISSI works with the NSA to test frontier models such as OpenAI's Astra and Anthropic's Mythos before broader release, per four people familiar. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-00d387e98c2774218f9b|cite]])
- Google said in a September 30, 2026 blog post that it had submitted Gemini 4 Argon to the White House for voluntary testing; as of October 12 it still had not widely released the model. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-0dfb9e274d88634f411b|cite]])
- The White House still has not published its pre-deployment testing framework, and the split of duties between CAISSI and the NSA remains unclear, which worries the AI industry. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-d54a21decbef6670f57b|cite]])
- Google, OpenAI and Anthropic explored forming their own industry group to supplement CAISSI's model testing, partly because they worry the agency lacks resources. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-8089045f8e9efad823b8|cite]])
- In early October 2026 Trump named a new super intelligence task force led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton, with nobody from Commerce and no mention of CAISSI. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-9c39e9a431008b2ef9ba|cite]])
- The Biden administration created the agency as the AI Safety Institute under a 2023 executive order; under AI czar David Sacks the Trump White House renamed it the Center for AI Standards and Innovation. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-59b79c19b74add32bc0c|cite]])
- The Trump administration hired AI testing expert Collin Burns to lead the agency but fired him within days because he had worked at Anthropic, The Washington Post reported. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-8ec869eb3465cbd84763|cite]])
- The Trump administration imposed export controls on Anthropic in June 2026 over security concerns about Mythos and Fable, after which the White House leaned more on CAISSI's expertise. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-d1cf9ab13f48fa77239f|cite]])
- CAISSI director Chris Fall resigned unexpectedly in July 2026 after three months, and NIST director Arvind Raman is overseeing the agency while it seeks a new head. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-be28767e965c1be199dc|cite]])
- CAISSI cyber lead Andrew Fasano left for Anthropic in June 2026, and adviser Paul Christiano joined OpenAI's nonprofit board in September 2026. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House#^clm-908ae93b1c6956e20ce1|cite]])
