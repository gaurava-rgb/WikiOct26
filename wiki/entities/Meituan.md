# Meituan

China's biggest food delivery and local services app; builds its own LongCat AI models.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-09 [[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded|China’s AI Model Race Is Just Getting More Crowded]]

- Three big Chinese tech firms from outside AI, game maker miHoYo, phone and car maker Xiaomi, and delivery app Meituan, are now building their own large language models from scratch. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-5b7dc6af8c5e7d825a9c|cite]])
- The consolidation many predicted during China's 2023 'war of 100 models' has not happened, because Chinese tech firms deeply distrust each other and see building their own AI as cheaper than buying rivals. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-ac0678eb7f78e646a12c|cite]])
- Meituan CEO Wang Xing said on a 2025 earnings call that the only sensible AI strategy is to play offense by investing in Meituan's own models rather than defending its existing business. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-d85f580272abc0e8f2d8|cite]])
- Meituan says its LongCat 2.0 model, released in June 2026, gives it a structural advantage in long-term cost control, though the model still trails many Chinese models. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-b6bd5048a531949a34b6|cite]])
- Bernstein analyst Robin Zhu says Chinese founders worry that not owning a model could leave them vulnerable if they lose access to outside platforms. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded#^clm-a3de32689e7be77e8971|cite]])
