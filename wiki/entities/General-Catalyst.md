# General Catalyst

Venture capital firm building a data center to supply compute to its startups.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-12 [[2026-10-12-Andreessen-Horowitz-General-Catalyst-Race-to-Secure-the-Chips-Their-Startups-Need|Andreessen Horowitz, General Catalyst Race to Secure the Chips Their Startups Need]]

- General Catalyst is working to secure a data center to supply compute to portfolio companies from early stage to growth stage, per people with knowledge of the project. ([[2026-10-12-Andreessen-Horowitz-General-Catalyst-Race-to-Secure-the-Chips-Their-Startups-Need#^clm-2442c986fd3ebbf2218d|cite]])
- Shadeform COO Jeff Jewett said compute access will be a requirement for VC firms to attract the hottest startups. ([[2026-10-12-Andreessen-Horowitz-General-Catalyst-Race-to-Secure-the-Chips-Their-Startups-Need#^clm-d7ee5816b1952eb1e3b3|cite]])
- In May 2026 General Catalyst hired Alexis Black Björlin, who formerly helped run Nvidia's cloud computing division, as chief strategy officer focused on infrastructure. ([[2026-10-12-Andreessen-Horowitz-General-Catalyst-Race-to-Secure-the-Chips-Their-Startups-Need#^clm-5f89af033eb4a84d5057|cite]])
- Earlier in 2026 General Catalyst CEO Hemant Taneja surveyed founders on their compute access, and the firm weighed a shared capacity pool or negotiating on startups' behalf.
