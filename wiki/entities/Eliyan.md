# Eliyan

Chip interconnect startup founded in 2021 whose optical-electronic links let chiplets and AI racks share data faster.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-08 [[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm|Networking Startup Eliyan Draws Takeover Interest, Held Early Talks With Arm]]

- Eliyan told some investors in early October 2026 that it had received a takeover bid, per people familiar with the matter. ([[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm#^clm-313ab88b78ed3f688fa8|cite]])
- Eliyan was valued at $1 billion in July 2026 and will likely aim for a $3 billion valuation in a sale, per some people familiar. ([[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm#^clm-de54df45a1b2d2c6b9ed|cite]])
- Arm, owned by SoftBank and an existing Eliyan investor, held early talks about buying Eliyan but did not bid and would be unlikely to at that valuation. ([[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm#^clm-9e6e06af7fb7da6a8b2e|cite]])
- Founded in 2021, Eliyan has raised more than $250 million from backers including AMD, Cisco, Meta, Seligman Ventures and Tiger Global Management. ([[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm#^clm-a46892086a06cd20ff8e|cite]])
- Copper links that chiplets use to reach each other and high-bandwidth memory can be slow, leaving AI chips idle; Eliyan says its light-plus-electronics design fixes some of this. ([[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm#^clm-4d3aa5be6b370a5c288e|cite]])
- Eliyan CEO Ramin Farjadrad said its optical solution matches copper on reliability, power and cost but with much longer reach to connect AI racks. ([[2026-10-08-Networking-Startup-Eliyan-Draws-Takeover-Interest-Held-Early-Talks-With-Arm#^clm-fcc85dd36eb335c0b21d|cite]])
