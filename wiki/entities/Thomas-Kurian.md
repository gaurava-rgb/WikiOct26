# Thomas Kurian

CEO of Google Cloud.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-23 [[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|An Interview with Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian About the Agentic Moment]]

- Kurian says the framing for Google Cloud Next 2026 is that customers are moving from using AI to answer questions like a chatbot to having it automate tasks and whole process flows. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-e2d19bdafd9089e7231f|cite]])
- Kurian says agents got much better in the past year because models reason better, keep long-running memory, and use tools through standard abstractions like skills and MCP. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-9ca6435e3992477dc7d7|cite]])
- Kurian argues that business processes with many constraints need smarter models, not simpler ones, because there are too many odd cases to program ahead of time, so the model must write code to handle them. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-c75ef9f9a2138e452240|cite]])
- Kurian argues Google is unique in AI because it owns the whole stack, and says that stack improves the models 'literally every hour of every day'. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-e395ebd23b95d977ed81|cite]])
- Kurian says most of the large AI labs, including Anthropic, use Google's stack, and Google makes money from whichever layer they buy, such as TPUs, Gemini, or security, so it does not see this as zero sum. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-ce3b89243008384860dd|cite]])
- Kurian argues selling TPUs to outsiders fills idle chips at night when chat demand drops, and the higher volume gets better supplier terms, which lowers cost of goods sold (the direct cost of what Google sells). ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-8dd38ead112a81582a5c|cite]])
- Kurian says compute is always short, even though Google could not grow as fast as it does without enough of it. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-a7eb6e8057f126a37430|cite]])
- Kurian says Google Cloud splits its business into Gemini as a product, Gemini embedded in other products, and selling compute, and argues placing hardware in customer sites changes cash flow compared with spending its own capex (money on data centers). ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-7d8d877aa2ff3015b6cd|cite]])
- Kurian's team builds all the infrastructure for Alphabet and also runs the cloud business, and he calls the idea that giving compute to one side takes from another overly simplistic. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-22f9a23809101cc510de|cite]])
- Kurian says Google bought Wiz because AI will find software holes much faster, so customers must fix them much faster, and cloud is the most valuable place to protect. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-e32ac02bb9849f3bacb6|cite]])
- Kurian says Knowledge Catalog uses Gemini, not human consultants, to build a map of what each company's data means, and says Google is not competing with Palantir's ontology. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-0bc4d31bd9e07aa97814|cite]])
- Kurian rejects Thompson's idea that the model will eat all software, saying many SaaS vendors are embedding Gemini as an agent platform and Google will invest to grow its partner ecosystem. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-dfa422f63505c65bb16c|cite]])
- Kurian claims Google is the only company with chips, models, data context, cyber tools, and a world-class agent platform. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-b8e3d3fa3dca974631bb|cite]])

### 2026-05-13 [[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|The Deployment Company, Back to the 70s, Apple and Intel]]

([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel#^clm-6ae12ffb15d6ad19fb42|cite]])

### 2026-05-14 [[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|An Interview with Ben Thompson at the MoffettNathanson Media, Internet & Communications Conference]]

- Thompson says TPU (Google's custom AI chip) supply made for Anthropic comes out of Google's own allotment at TSMC, so Google is choosing to earn more by selling to Anthropic than by keeping the compute. ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson#^clm-8572a3ad48de70e32b7b|cite]])
