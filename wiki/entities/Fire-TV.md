# Fire TV

Amazon's streaming TV devices, running the Android-fork Fire OS.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Fire OS is a modified Android fork, and at launch Fire TV had only 180 apps while the older Roku offered over 1,200. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-89f1d9346ccea98db580|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues owning the TV matters because it gathers viewing data for profiling and targeted ads, and for Amazon the goal may be less winning TV than making Prime Video available everywhere. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-00a687716692029bebd4|cite]])
