# Amancio Ortega

Spanish founder of Zara and Inditex.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems]]

- Amancio Ortega first wanted to name his clothing shop Zorba, after the film Zorba the Greek, but a bar in town already had that name, and since the sign letter molds were already cast, his team rearranged them into Zara. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-deabeaae742c54320079|cite]])
- Zara's success made founder Amancio Ortega the world's second wealthiest person, and at one point he briefly held the top spot (endnote dated 2017). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-3684016189dfce998cb5|cite]])
- Zara's founder has called advertising a pointless distraction, and during Gap's collapse Gap raised ad spending while sales dropped. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-b804462d5118d64e1b45|cite]])
- Many of Zara's tech initiatives were led by Jose Maria Castellano, a technophile business professor who became Amancio Ortega's right-hand man in the 1980s. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-98e33754446992619816|cite]])
