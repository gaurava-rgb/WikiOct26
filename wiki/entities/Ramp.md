# Ramp

US corporate card and spend company that publishes an AI Index of business spending on AI models.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-02 [[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards|Fable 5.1, Enterprise Frontier Safeguards]]

- Ramp data showed that one month after launch, Fable 5 made up only 6% of tokens businesses bought from Anthropic and 11.4% of dollars spent on Anthropic models. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards#^clm-ecfa3c4cb141a7e291bb|cite]])
- Per Ramp, OpenAI's flagship GPT-5.6 Sol made up 25% of OpenAI tokens and 23% of OpenAI spend, and in July Fable 5 generated about 75% as much model-attributed spend as GPT-5.6 Sol. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards#^clm-428370793e928b3a6013|cite]])
- Ramp put Fable 5's price at roughly $10 per million tokens, twice the price of GPT-5.6 Sol, and argued this marks an upper bound on what businesses will pay for AI performance. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards#^clm-1fb817e7e354d9326d7c|cite]])
- Prediction: Ramp argued labs must beat even Fable 5 on performance while keeping rivals from getting close, which looks harder as open source models close to only a few months behind. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards#^clm-797b8db92c0d9aebaa47|cite]])
