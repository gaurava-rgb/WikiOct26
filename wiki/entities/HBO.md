# HBO

Premium pay-TV network known for prestige original series; long the must-have subscription for top TV talent.

## Facts by source

### 2015-07-08 [[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and the Conservation of Attractive Profits]]

- In 2013 Thompson called Netflix just another network, HBO with a unique delivery system, and in 2015 he says he got this wrong in a big way. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-60441fe4122f7f4b59b4|cite]])
- Netflix faces strong competition for attention from traditional pay-TV, Amazon Prime, a revitalized Hulu, and especially HBO. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-3c80aed15bd8cc78adcf|cite]])
- A 2015 Hollywood Reporter profile found HBO remained the most desirable place for top producers because of its audience, budgets, promotion and experience. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-dced7e5b0780473a54aa|cite]])
- HBO had more than 100 projects in development, and Netflix and Amazon lured talent frustrated by the queue; that speed reportedly won Netflix Bloodline and The OA. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-e15d8956e4052126ba48|cite]])
- Thompson argues a new entrant like Netflix is free to integrate around the customer while staying modular in content creation, and the most promising companies make the biggest breaks with the past. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-81ae40c007f25ea51707|cite]])
