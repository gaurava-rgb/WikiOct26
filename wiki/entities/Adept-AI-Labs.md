# Adept AI Labs

San Francisco AI startup founded in 2021 that built a browser agent for office tasks.

## Facts by source

### 2023-01-09 [[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching|Six OpenAI Rivals Google and Microsoft Are Watching]]

- Adept AI Labs, led by former OpenAI and Google engineer David Luan, built a browser extension that does tasks from plain-language instructions; it had raised $66 million, had 20 staff and nominal revenue. ([[2023-01-09-Six-OpenAI-Rivals-Google-and-Microsoft-Are-Watching#^clm-e376cefcc89594b403c6|cite]])

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit|OpenAI’s Bug and Bumble’s AI-Powered Exit]]
