# d-Matrix

AI inference chip startup focused on speculative decoding; Nvidia plans to invest in it.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-08 [[2026-10-08-Nvidia-to-Invest-in-Chip-Rival-d-Matrix-as-Its-Challengers-Choose-Partnership|Nvidia to Invest in Chip Rival d-Matrix as Its Challengers Choose Partnership]]

- Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, a seven-year-old AI server chip maker that aims to compete with Nvidia, per three people with knowledge of the deal. ([[2026-10-08-Nvidia-to-Invest-in-Chip-Rival-d-Matrix-as-Its-Challengers-Choose-Partnership#^clm-5d6003d8bb5de6f9aec5|cite]])
- It is Nvidia's first deal with an AI inference chip startup since its $20 billion licensing agreement with Groq in December 2025. ([[2026-10-08-Nvidia-to-Invest-in-Chip-Rival-d-Matrix-as-Its-Challengers-Choose-Partnership#^clm-66a5bc8a67abd17ebfd7|cite]])
- In the planned system, d-Matrix chips run a small model that guesses a bigger model's answer, which an Nvidia GPU checks, a technique called speculative decoding. ([[2026-10-08-Nvidia-to-Invest-in-Chip-Rival-d-Matrix-as-Its-Challengers-Choose-Partnership#^clm-9c536b1053160ec307fe|cite]])
- d-Matrix last raised $275 million at a $2 billion valuation in November 2025 and was reported to be seeking funding at a $5 billion valuation. ([[2026-10-08-Nvidia-to-Invest-in-Chip-Rival-d-Matrix-as-Its-Challengers-Choose-Partnership#^clm-150921d439804859a1fb|cite]])
