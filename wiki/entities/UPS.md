# UPS

US package delivery company, long a key Amazon carrier.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Scale let Amazon vertically integrate into its own logistics fleet of trucks, planes, and ocean freight, so it now ships most of what it sells and is larger than FedEx or UPS, firms it once relied on. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-5755b5179df9cca7b8f8|cite]])
- As late as 2015, FedEx, UPS, and the U.S. Postal Service delivered more than 97 percent of Amazon's U.S. packages. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-2143b1d5ef230745091f|cite]])
- By late 2023 Amazon delivered the vast majority of its own U.S. packages and was a larger shipper than FedEx and UPS. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-90934c886b39ad4016d2|cite]])
- Amazon's interest in controlling logistics grew after December 2013, when UPS could not handle Amazon's holiday volume, costing Amazon millions in refunds and hurting its reputation for reliable delivery. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-b09ff17ea5e21b34e5cd|cite]])
- In the 2020 holiday season, FedEx and UPS failed to deliver a million packages by Christmas, while Amazon, now running most of its own shipping, still offered overnight delivery with Christmas Day guarantees. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-d67e5b8cb5cc20549416|cite]])
- Amazon provides warehousing and inventory services to its own sellers through Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) and began offering them to third-party clients in the United Kingdom, a threat to the logistics businesses of FedEx and UPS. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-435e2ec243175d6cdef9|cite]])
- Amazon's software picks the cheapest and fastest carrier for each package, weighing cost, fuel prices, routing, delivery promise, weather, traffic, and fleet availability before dispatching to UPS, the USPS, or itself. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6491414765f23f2e1f60|cite]])
- Amazon won FAA approval in 2020 to fly delivery drones beyond the operator's line of sight, approval UPS, Alphabet's Wing, and Walmart partner Zipline also hold. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-a309958e9b7e644052e4|cite]])

### 2026-05-05 [[2026-05-05-Amazons-Durability|Amazon’s Durability]]

- Amazon Supply Chain Services packages air and ocean freight, trucking, and last-mile delivery for other companies, and sent FedEx and UPS shares lower. ([[2026-05-05-Amazons-Durability#^clm-9af9229107d6d938e48d|cite]])
