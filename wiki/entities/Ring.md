# Ring

Smart doorbell and home security maker Amazon bought for $1 billion.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Amazon Key lets couriers drop packages inside the home, a service boosted by Amazon's $1 billion purchase of Ring, a smart home security maker once rejected on Shark Tank. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-8838a73409901bb17d24|cite]])
- Amazon's device line grew from the Kindle to Fire tablets and TV devices, Echo speakers with Alexa, Ring doorbells, and eero home networking, and as of the book's writing Amazon was seeking regulatory approval to buy Roomba maker iRobot. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-936a11796bd60fbf4425|cite]])
- Amazon's connected devices have faced privacy and security problems, from customers unaware that humans may transcribe some Alexa interactions to security issues with Ring. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-84dbf0f9f9d67e10beb6|cite]])
