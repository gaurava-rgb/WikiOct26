# DeForest

Village of about 11,500 people near Madison, Wisconsin.

## Facts by source

### 2026-05-18 [[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent|Data Center Discontent, Understanding the Opposition, Fixing the Problem]]

- The planned DeForest, Wisconsin data center was expected to be 1.6 GW and could bring in about $3 billion a year in operator revenue; the village has about 11,500 people. ([[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent#^clm-4080c2c93e2d388e3eb8|cite]])
- Thompson calculates that paying every DeForest resident $10,000 a year would cost 3.8% of the data center's annual revenue, and says operators could pass that cost on to users. ([[2026-05-18-Data-Center-Discontent#^clm-61e929d1ee7ff87830a6|cite]])
