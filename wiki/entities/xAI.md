- All 11 original xAI co-founders had left by late March 2026, an exodus that sped up after SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026. ([[2026-04-22-John-Ternus-and-Apples-Hardware-Defined-Future-SpaceXAI-and-Cursor#^clm-bb815ef416fb29aa9da8|cite]])

- Thompson argues the SpaceXAI and Cursor tie-up makes a lot of sense: one side has compute, the other a product, proprietary data, and distribution in coding, the most important AI use case. ### 2026-05-12 [[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future|SpaceX and Anthropic, xAI’s Two Companies, Elon Musk and SpaceXAI’s Future]]

- Anthropic signed a deal with SpaceX in May 2026 to access more than 300 megawatts of computing capacity at Colossus 1, SpaceX's large data center in Memphis; terms were not disclosed. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-d34e81a0d1085267078f|cite]])
- Per Bloomberg, data center developers have recently locked in tenants at $1.5 million to $2 million per megawatt per year, so the Anthropic deal would be worth hundreds of millions of dollars a year to xAI. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-21b5326bc78f25df083d|cite]])
- Thompson says OpenAI is almost certainly paying less per equivalent unit of compute than Anthropic, and that Anthropic getting more compute means someone else, here xAI, gets less. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-59f9878c8a4a74ccc7e2|cite]])
- Thompson argues xAI has always been two companies in one, a model maker and an infrastructure company, with conflicting goals: software wants to run everywhere to spread R&D costs, while hardware wants maximum utilization at the highest price. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-72441aecfb34c3a05a0d|cite]])
- Thompson argues that because AI hardware is in short supply, the best strategy for a hardware owner is to sell to the widest set of customers to find the highest willingness to pay, rather than differentiate it with its own software. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-ab4eb408d417e5a38ef6|cite]])
- xAI's Colossus 1 has been reported to run at only 11% utilization, and Thompson argues that even full use by xAI's own models would earn less than opening the hardware to the market. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-f20ddfeabdcb72b6c76d|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson argues Musk should also open Colossus 2 to outside customers, as he did with Colossus 1. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-3df9daf4769eedf6e9da|cite]])
- Thompson argues the biggest driver of the deal was that Anthropic offered a price too good to turn down as SpaceX prepares for an IPO, turning Colossus 1 from an underused, depreciating asset into a revenue generator. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-9d852b1e1f7395864940|cite]])
- Thompson revives his 2022 view that Tesla and SpaceX thrive because physics constrains hardware companies, while software like Twitter has no such limits, and says it fits xAI: praise goes to how fast Musk built Colossus 1 and 2, while the software has struggled. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-1953ab48ed02fc0cae50|cite]])
- Thompson calls Grok being used to undress women on Twitter the low point of xAI's software story, driven by a desperate push to raise usage and put idle GPUs to work. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-c113ce30e773a1788691|cite]])
- Thompson now argues the model fight does not suit xAI temperamentally or economically, that Musk should stick to the physical world, and that Cursor makes more sense for Microsoft or Amazon, though the Cursor deal is not dead since Colossus 2 can still train Cursor's models. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future#^clm-eddb00044f85599ce5d6|cite]])

- Prediction: Thompson predicts Anthropic will probably make 3x SpaceX's revenue on the Colossus 1 capacity, and that xAI could earn more per rack if it gets back to the state of the art. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-b71de43675140daf12e5|cite]])
