# Stripe

Online payments company; co-developed OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-12 [[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond|How Agentic Commerce Is Transforming Retail and Beyond]]

- As of April 2026 the post says consumers are already buying directly inside ChatGPT, made real by OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), launched with Stripe and commercetools. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-fed6735dd4e64d956f58|cite]])

### 2026-09-04 [[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe|Anthropic's In-House Payments Tech Push Could Chip Away at Stripe]]

- Job postings show Anthropic wants to build more billing, fraud detection and other financial systems itself and is weighing which payment services to build instead of buy. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-a5f390b555d22f888c49|cite]])
- Anthropic uses many Stripe products beyond core processing, including Link checkout, Radar fraud scoring, invoicing and usage-based billing. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-289ceaa02081fae01e34|cite]])
- Stripe earns most revenue by taking 2.9% of payment volume plus a fixed per-transaction fee, with big customers often paying less. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-672e6dd6983f0a006749|cite]])
- The job posts do not say Anthropic plans to drop outside payment processors, and fully replacing Stripe would take a long time. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-8d5e1c03949aef2d8296|cite]])
- In August 2026 OpenAI, a longtime Stripe customer, added Adyen as an extra payment provider. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-922ce5e11037d3ab830c|cite]])
- Earlier in 2026 OpenAI moved storage of customer card data from Stripe's vault to an intermediary, making it easier to use several processors. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-97c39fb5cdaf259d7616|cite]])
- Stripe is buying OpenRouter, which sits between developers and AI model providers, for more than $7 billion. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-c56f64a418c2a8186851|cite]])
- Stripe's revenue growth sped up to about 33% in 2025, its fastest since the 2021 e-commerce boom. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-d77665422c8a7128a624|cite]])
- In January 2026 Stripe bought usage-based billing startup Metronome for a reported $1 billion to meter and charge for AI. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-c53ac1ed447f77eff7d0|cite]])
- Shopify added PayPal for some US transactions in 2024 and is getting state money transmitter licenses, which could cut its need for Stripe. ([[2026-09-04-Anthropics-In-House-Payments-Tech-Push-Could-Chip-Away-at-Stripe#^clm-a1c9d86aefe4beeaf8d4|cite]])
