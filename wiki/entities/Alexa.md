# Alexa

Amazon's voice assistant, used in Echo smart speakers.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Gallaugher rates Amazon's device results as mixed: category-monopolizing (Kindle), dominant but not money-making (Echo and Alexa), and an utter flop (Fire Phone). ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ee3c25532de5371c3f0b|cite]])
- Amazon's connected devices have faced privacy and security problems, from customers unaware that humans may transcribe some Alexa interactions to security issues with Ring. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-84dbf0f9f9d67e10beb6|cite]])
- Apple had Siri three years before Alexa and Google had a two-year voice assistant lead, but Amazon's master stroke was putting its voice AI in a simple smart speaker; Google took two years to catch up and Apple lagged with the HomePod. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7e994379b2921bb27ef2|cite]])
- Alexa held 70 percent of the smart speaker market. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7ca7e94acbc1ff6f284d|cite]])
- Alexa became a platform: over 100,000 devices from over 9,600 brands offered Alexa connectivity, so smart home makers must support it or miss the biggest share of the market. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-0f8729a655702604aa2c|cite]])
- Amazon said roughly 1 billion Alexa-powered devices had been sold by Amazon and partners. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-87868cf342d9c46707ad|cite]])
- The original Echo had seven far-field microphones tied to Amazon's cloud, and because Alexa's brains sit in the cloud and it gets a steady flow of voice data, it keeps getting smarter without device updates. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6a2e6411af3680fed91c|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues each Alexa device is a touchpoint gathering behavior data for better AI, but warns that low likelihood of use and added device cost, as with a talking microwave, may thwart network-effect strategies. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-30c2d827f3896b6308ce|cite]])
- Amazon rents the whole Alexa technology stack through AWS so firms can build white-label voice assistants with their own wake words; Fiat Chrysler was the first announced licensee. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-4e6c3423ff8a54d754ed|cite]])
- Amazon's electronics initiatives lose about $10 billion a year, mostly from Alexa, few customers actually shop by voice, and there is no real evidence Alexa boosts sales. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-c4fee442f170fc86ecce|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Alexa skills have been a dud because a voice interface is poor for discovering new services. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-dab7e3b18689c0584202|cite]])
- Amazon uses natural language processing to understand and craft Alexa interactions, and AI lets Alexa understand commands in a growing number of languages, from Arabic to Hindi. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-2342c366cd63f9a7ace0|cite]])

### 2026-02-27 [[2026-02-27-What-Amazon-and-Microsoft-Will-Win-or-Lose-From-OpenAIs-New-Deals|What Amazon and Microsoft Will Win or Lose From OpenAI's New Deals]]
