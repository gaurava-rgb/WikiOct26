# AliExpress

Alibaba's international cross-border shopping site.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-30 [[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon|Alibaba's AliExpress Tests Membership Program in Challenge to Amazon]]

- AliExpress is testing an invitation-only VIP membership called Elite for customers who spend more than $800 a year, in a challenge to Amazon. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-ef1ce3a3b6270f31ccf1|cite]])
- Elite is piloting in Australia, the U.K., Germany and Spain, with more European markets and South Korea planned next, and a U.S. launch planned at an undecided time. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-27d4b6250c5251c9ceb2|cite]])
- Prediction: AliExpress internally projects 1 million Elite members by the end of 2026. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-fb05abfe163c6ac8e0f5|cite]])
- Unlike Amazon Prime's paid subscription, Elite charges no fee and offers an extra 5% off many products, round-the-clock support and hassle-free returns. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-97aadc825f86d4bbd933|cite]])
- In November 2026 the EU adds a 2 euro handling fee per parcel from outside the region, on top of a 3 euro levy per parcel worth up to 150 euros. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-8617add02f05de4ab3f8|cite]])
- Analyst Juozas Kaziukenas says Europe is now the least favorable market for direct-from-China apps because fees forced price rises and marketing cuts that effectively killed new customer acquisition. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-a6cdcfddad449b260c28|cite]])
- AliExpress is testing next-day delivery in European cities such as Madrid, Paris and Warsaw, starting with items from local warehouses. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-a8db76e82f83be1241f4|cite]])
- As of June 2026, AliExpress had 157.6 million monthly active users in Europe versus Amazon's 193.9 million. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-56d4e914938889314d59|cite]])
- AliExpress's ultra-low-price Choice program lifted revenue but drove major losses; Alibaba's international e-commerce arm lost $566 million before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization in the quarter through March 2024. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-c0916c859f9700f9b398|cite]])
- Branded goods are now about 50% of AliExpress sales in Spain, Germany and Poland, per a person familiar. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-ae716ba4a78ea7806bfa|cite]])
- AliExpress posted an operating profit in the quarter through June 2026, while Alibaba's international e-commerce revenue fell 1% to $4.09 billion. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-fad26e33dc340dcbc4db|cite]])
