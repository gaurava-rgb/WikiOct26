# IBM

Old-line computing company; sells z-series mainframes, the software that runs on them, and consulting.

## Facts by source

### 2024-12-02 [[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future|The Gen AI Bridge to the Future]]

- IBM's first System/360, the Model 30, ran batch jobs; two years later IBM's Model 67 with the TSS/360 operating system let users interact with a program through a terminal, which Thompson calls a new Applications paradigm. ([[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future#^clm-f8954ce3c253b42fd38d|cite]])

### 2026-07-15 [[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems|IBM Misses, IBM’s Mainframe Moat, IBM’s Many AI Problems]]

- IBM's preliminary second-quarter 2026 revenue was $17.2 billion, below analyst estimates of $17.9 billion. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-ff131f24f6fd9290c0ff|cite]])
- Revenue in IBM's infrastructure division, which includes mainframes, fell 7% in the second quarter of 2026. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-8cd0647fd19cc1608a75|cite]])
- IBM shares fell 25% to $217.07 on July 14, 2026, the biggest one-day loss since at least January 1968. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-8ae1c910913cc3feb36b|cite]])
- IBM's miss briefly pulled down other software stocks such as ServiceNow and Workday, which recovered part of the loss later that day. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-56a36ae0d02e14cf5a5c|cite]])
- Krishna says clients shifted their quarterly capex to servers, storage, and memory in late June to lock in scarce supply ahead of expected price rises, cutting mainframe purchases. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-d2167beceb0dd2891473|cite]])
- IBM had guided in April 2026 that infrastructure revenue would fall low single digits for the year after the z17 launch, but the result was worse, driven by Z and its Transaction Processing software. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-c0532491d28691e84b0a|cite]])
- Krishna also says clients were distracted by fast-changing, industry-wide cybersecurity concerns during the quarter. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-ca04517c51e64dc7fbbf|cite]])
- Thompson argues the selloff in SaaS stocks was not clearly deserved, because SaaS is an opex seat license while IBM's miss came from capex on mainframes. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-819e658be61cd352d828|cite]])
- Thompson explains that IBM's mainframe software is tied to the hardware and priced more like usage than per seat, so skipped upgrades also mean skipped growth in software spending. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-d11ca606e438fd544657|cite]])
- In Q3 2025, IBM said Transaction Processing revenue fell 3% because clients prioritized spending on z17 hardware. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-c94b8bfb8f5b5d162168|cite]])
- Prediction: In Q3 2025, Krishna predicted Transaction Processing software would grow low single digits into 2026 as new z17 capacity was deployed. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-619ab1dd5d76ef1afe81|cite]])
- Thompson points out IBM reversed its story: a year ago it blamed strong z17 sales for weak Transaction Processing revenue, and now blames weak z17 sales for the same thing, so he suspects this quarter is the anomaly. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-0541ef1c6289294ee1f9|cite]])
- Thompson argues IBM makes money coming and going: mainframe customers pay capex for the hardware and opex to IBM for the software that runs on it. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-e57f93597cc8f3fb2386|cite]])
- Thompson argues almost no new company picks a mainframe; IBM sells to long-time customers like banks and airlines that are trapped because their transactions are too many and too complex to move. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-c55e6e354b6010c6d783|cite]])
- Thompson argues moving off a mainframe removes IBM's combined hardware-and-software tax and lowers long-term costs, though the migration itself can be extremely expensive. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-8a86b304a86275eb65af|cite]])
- Thompson argues most companies will keep paying IBM because they lack the developers or budget to port mainframe applications to the cloud. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-a3c584dfbd236307ac73|cite]])
- Thompson calls IBM's short-term AI problem the crowd-out: mainframe upgrade money went to other hardware, most likely for AI projects. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-e6d1cd2ca2ee51dbd26a|cite]])
- Thompson argues IBM's long-term AI problem is worse: porting code is one of the tasks AI suits best, and mainframe code is the most valuable code to port. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-9c0b26065d1060aa697b|cite]])
- Thompson suspects customers did not move off mainframes this quarter, but became aware AI makes it possible, so they delay new mainframes and shift new usage elsewhere. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-9878d5ddce92ec2878d1|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson predicts IBM's high-margin mainframe business is in trouble: the longer customers wait without buying, the better AI porting will get. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-0dddcd89296ddec1650c|cite]])
- IBM's consulting business grew on demand for help adding AI, IBM framed Mythos as a boost to its cybersecurity business, and its other hardware lines gained from the capex shift. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems#^clm-ce6e0050f078f0b3eb41|cite]])
