# Matt Garman

CEO of AWS, Amazon's cloud business; joined AWS as an intern.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-28 [[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents|An Interview with OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and AWS CEO Matt Garman About Bedrock Managed Agents]]

- Garman says the vast majority of scaling startups still grow on AWS, citing scale, availability, security, reliability, and the partners and customers already there. ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents#^clm-281f51f7e1298bedbd62|cite]])
- Garman agrees that a common startup pattern is AWS for regular computing plus the OpenAI API directly for AI. ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents#^clm-1cb112ea83e55e1bd4db|cite]])
- Garman says that before this product, customers had to wire agents into their own data, tools, and systems on their own; the joint product builds in identity and keeps database access inside the customer's AWS VPC (a private network in AWS). ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents#^clm-54497147a8dd6eddd6d3|cite]])
- Garman argues most customers will reach AI chips only through abstractions like a model API, since most inference runs on a handful of models, so the chip underneath matters little to them. ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents#^clm-6fddd7e98470cfa3ff53|cite]])
- Prediction: Garman predicts a mix of models over time: smaller, cheaper, faster ones alongside very large frontier ones. ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents#^clm-174239575fb294c2d30e|cite]])
- Garman says AWS's partner-first, model-neutral stance was partly deliberate, contrasting it with rivals like Google that try to own the whole stack from chip to agent. ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents#^clm-e0e97bcf55c4530251b2|cite]])
