# AI Overviews

Google's AI-generated summaries at the top of search results.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-29 [[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews|Google Is Paying About 100 Digital Publishers for AI Overviews]]

- The pilot covers AI Overviews, AI Mode and the Gemini chatbot and started less than a year ago. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-927830ada2c8baa26383|cite]])
- Google long argued it should not pay publishers whose content answers queries because search sends them traffic, but AI Overviews sharply cut that traffic. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-5d252025fb9b98068fba|cite]])
- World History Encyclopedia says its Google traffic fell 25% to 35% in each of the past two years and is down about 50% from 2024. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-04b0939622d47a16853a|cite]])
- Penske Media sued Google in September 2025 claiming AI Overviews drained traffic and affiliate revenue and illegally tied AI Overview inclusion to search indexing. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-8b63a2211d080d8988b0|cite]])
- In August 2026 Google finished an opt-out letting publishers refuse use in AI Overviews, AI Mode or Google Discover without losing search visibility. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-68a80505faa4bf79ac5a|cite]])
