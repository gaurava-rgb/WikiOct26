# Shopify

Canadian e-commerce software company that runs online stores for independent brands.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia]]

- Gallaugher argues that with its marketplace strategy Shein has 'poked the bear', Amazon, a brutal competitor unlikely to cede customers or suppliers, with Shopify another strong rival. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-b3def27ca2ada82e8c4b|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Gallaugher argues Marketplace lets Amazon build a long tail without the risk of owning unproven inventory, and that allowing cheaper competing listings keeps shoppers from looking first to Shopify, Temu, Google Shopping, or eBay while Amazon keeps the customer data. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-abbfb178d851f70209ee|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues rising alternatives like Shein, Temu, and Shopify pressure Amazon, creating a three-way tension between customer experience, growth, and rivals as customers and squeezed suppliers may leave. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-bafe5f2a176a1c01e230|cite]])
- Amazon holds roughly 40 percent of U.S. e-commerce, far more than any rival, though it faces rising competition from Shopify, Shein, and Temu. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7998f2281e7fa80e1845|cite]])
- Shopify, based in Ottawa, Canada, is a tool and services provider rather than a reseller of goods, and unlike Amazon it is not a one-stop searchable marketplace; sellers subscribe to its cloud tools to build their own branded stores. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-e536cd1b5324986067b1|cite]])
- Most Shopify sellers use drop shipping: they take orders and have the manufacturer ship directly to the customer, so neither Shopify nor the seller holds the goods. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-199cbd4a9790ccc77714|cite]])
- During the pandemic, Shopify-powered social media commerce boomed as people at home found products on AliExpress, had Chinese suppliers drop-ship them, and ran ads on Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-d1d3ac7b308f7e30f51b|cite]])
- Shopify's president boasted that for $39 a month, you can build a multimillion-dollar or multibillion-dollar company. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-6a8b12e15c946c3fe185|cite]])
- Shopify's market value soared past $200 billion in the pandemic e-commerce boom, and at one point over 28 percent of U.S. e-commerce websites used Shopify. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-4334fdb39eb09b26c042|cite]])
- One study of over 124,000 Shopify stores found over 25,000 had engaged in some form of fraud. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-990b44ed89a2e21dd13f|cite]])
- After quarantine ended, e-commerce fell and rising interest rates, inflation, and a global shipping crisis hurt Shopify; one study found 90 percent of new drop-ship sellers quit after one month. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-a5f03fa6c55fd684452f|cite]])
- Less than a year after its all-time high, Shopify lost 80 percent of its value, with market cap falling below $35 billion. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-a7a1ef41c2ba0e48f935|cite]])
- Shopify made nearly $3 billion in profit in 2021 but lost almost $3.5 billion in 2022. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-12abf941d8bfde56e41f|cite]])
- Shopify once looked set to challenge Amazon in fulfillment, but in May 2023 it announced the sale of its Deliverr logistics operation (to Flexport) and its 6 River Systems robotics unit, and a 20 percent layoff after 10 percent cuts in 2022. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-fa06c47989c167286a2a|cite]])
- Amazon launched Buy with Prime, letting firms sell from their own storefronts and social media with Amazon's trusted brand and stored checkout details to encourage impulse buys. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-b61ac76206b63f93ab68|cite]])
- After retreating from fulfillment, Shopify struck a 2023 deal letting its merchants plug into Amazon's logistics network and use Buy with Prime. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-f925787a1a85597a55cc|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Amazon is unlikely to buy Shopify given antitrust concerns, and may even want a healthy Shopify as a real rival to point to against monopoly charges. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-3ca1169d6b9ac01d274c|cite]])
- Shopify faces competition from Shein and Temu, which recruit its customers with similar tools, and from TikTok, a Shopify partner that launched its own e-commerce tools for vendors. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-10079b201b837a7357fb|cite]])

### 2026-06-18 [[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI|An Interview with Michael Morton About E-Commerce in the Age of AI]]

- Morton's data shows that from traditional Google Search, brand.com sites (merchants' own stores, Shopify's base) grow traffic about 2x faster than the big marketplaces. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-0e077e1f66491acee4cc|cite]])
- Morton's data shows that traffic from AI platforms to brand.com sites is growing 4 to 8 times faster than traffic to legacy marketplaces like Amazon, Wayfair, Chewy, eBay, and Etsy. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-fcbe54e74115146cd4a3|cite]])
- Prediction: Morton upgraded Shopify to buy earlier in 2026, believing its GMV (total value of goods sold on the platform) will grow faster for longer than most expect. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-fef98fcb9c83c294e2f1|cite]])
- Morton argues merchants cannot vibe-code (build with AI coding tools) a cheaper copy of Shopify, because Shopify's partner deals, such as better Stripe fees and one-click Meta CAPI integration, give it bargaining power no merchant has. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-1a72d7c526e19f7171e2|cite]])
- Morton says Shopify merchants get one-click Conversions API integration with Meta because Tobi Lütke and Mark Zuckerberg know each other and worked on the deal together. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-eacce248fa4765367651|cite]])
- Shopify Catalog lets merchants plug their product feeds straight into AI platforms, and is now open even to non-Shopify merchants, because models do not like scraping raw HTML for shopping. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-9a599c6eddb948f1b935|cite]])
- Morton says Shopify built Google's Universal Commerce Protocol (UCP) together with Google, merchants can bring their own payment option, and a council now includes Amazon, Meta, Stripe, and Shopify. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-af7b0fd4cac3ffa43fa9|cite]])
- Morton reports that growth in traffic sent from AI platforms to marketplaces has slowed since November 2025, partly because Amazon blocks most AI discovery platforms and more traffic goes to brand.com. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-692c1118fa6bfadbd822|cite]])
- Morton estimates Etsy earns over 100% of its EBIT (operating profit) from on-site ads, and warns big Etsy sellers may leave its 25% take rate for Shopify stores found through AI. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-a2072cf96c976563d2b7|cite]])
- Morton says Shopify GMV and Meta revenue have a near-perfect R-squared (a statistic for how closely two numbers move together), but he views the two stocks' weakness as separate. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-b41794bedd8e2ab7d1bd|cite]])
