# Mickey Drexler

Former Gap CEO who made Gap iconic, then led its youth-market misstep.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems]]

- Gap CEO Mickey Drexler turned Gap's button-down shirts and khakis into America's business casual uniform, but later chased the youth market with miniskirts, low-rise jeans and purple leather pants that flopped. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-f383ba4ae8dfeff4ffae|cite]])
- Drexler's youth push failed: teenagers never came, Gap's core customers left for rivals that copied its classic styles, and Gap's same-store sales declined for twenty-nine straight months. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-0292d61212acce9d39fb|cite]])
