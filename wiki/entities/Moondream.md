# Moondream

Small open-source vision-language model developer with megakernel compiler tech; acquired by Prime Intellect.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference|Neocloud Prime Intellect Acquires Startup To Speed Up Inference]]

- Prime Intellect, a San Francisco neocloud valued at $1 billion in a July 2026 equity financing, is acquiring Moondream to make open models run faster and cheaper on its Nvidia servers. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-ee769de8b8b34804e2e8|cite]])
- Moondream has fewer than 10 employees and has raised $4.5 million; Prime Intellect CEO Vincent Weisser would not share the deal terms. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-4564540eec41a2da21c8|cite]])
- Vincent Weisser says Prime Intellect is buying Moondream mainly for its staff's skill at writing software that makes models run efficiently, not for its vision-language model. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-0a31646aa8012f883338|cite]])
- Megakernels speed up AI inference by fusing many separate GPU math operations into one continuous operation, cutting GPU idle time between tasks; Moondream's staff specialize in them. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-b357e540687f911d7c6f|cite]])
- Moondream's megakernel compiler and Photon, its proprietary tech for running models fast, will be folded into Prime Intellect's technology. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-f7fb558fcbf14107aefe|cite]])
- Together AI has also worked on megakernels, which engineers say have been written by hand, while Moondream says its compiler generates them largely automatically. ([[2026-10-13-Neocloud-Prime-Intellect-Acquires-Startup-To-Speed-Up-Inference#^clm-24892cc0d1a1bfa34b3b|cite]])
