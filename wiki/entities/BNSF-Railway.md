# BNSF Railway

Berkshire Hathaway-owned U.S. freight railroad; a capital-hungry business with large absolute profits.

## Facts by source

### 2026-06-02 [[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company|The Google Capital Company]]

- BNSF Railway, bought by Berkshire Hathaway partly with See's Candies profits, consumed $3.8 billion of capital in 2025 and earned net income of $5.5 billion on revenue of $23.4 billion. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-ca6f81f39e4c3511bf3e|cite]])
- Thompson estimates Berkshire Hathaway's total earnings from See's Candies are probably under $3 billion (the last disclosure, in 2019, was over $2 billion), less than capital-hungry BNSF Railway earned in one year. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-a2a62bfee43d1d85ca35|cite]])
- Thompson argues advertising is only a fraction of the economy while Google Cloud's growth is AI, which could take over the whole economy, so Google Services may prove to be the cash flow that built a lower-margin but absolutely bigger business, as See's Candies helped fund BNSF. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-2ebdf1c7006307e0ed9d|cite]])
- Thompson argues Greg Abel is replaying Buffett's strategy, with Berkshire Hathaway now playing See's Candies (the cash machine) and Google playing BNSF Railway (the capital-hungry business that earns large absolute profits). ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-e83ea5215438a04a2692|cite]])
