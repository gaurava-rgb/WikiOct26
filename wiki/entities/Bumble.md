# Bumble

Dating app founded by Whitney Wolfe Herd; agreed in October 2026 to be bought by Ithaca Holdings.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-12 [[2026-10-12-Meta-Reaches-AIs-Crowded-Frontier-Will-Users-Care|Meta Reaches AI’s Crowded Frontier—Will Users Care?]]

- Bumble is in talks to go private at a $1 billion valuation, The Wall Street Journal reported on October 12, 2026; the stock jumped 50%, or $1.26, to $3.80. ([[2026-10-12-Meta-Reaches-AIs-Crowded-Frontier-Will-Users-Care#^clm-a6fd749a2001773788c7|cite]])
- Bumble's 2021 IPO priced shares at $43 and the stock nearly doubled in its first days, giving it a $9.5 billion market value before growth slowed in a crowded dating app market. ([[2026-10-12-Meta-Reaches-AIs-Crowded-Frontier-Will-Users-Care#^clm-8d7426ac0cbf81f4f352|cite]])
- Bumble's would-be buyers are Ravi Gupta, a former Sequoia partner, and John Hegeman, former chief revenue officer at Meta, whose new company aims to make acquisitions starting with Bumble. ([[2026-10-12-Meta-Reaches-AIs-Crowded-Frontier-Will-Users-Care#^clm-e3b2d6eb6312b9c8bfcc|cite]])

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit|OpenAI’s Bug and Bumble’s AI-Powered Exit]]

- On October 13, 2026 investment firm Ithaca Holdings unveiled a deal to buy dating app Bumble at a $1.3 billion valuation. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-3dbc0fcd55ce9453fafa|cite]])
- Ithaca co-CEO Ravi Gupta, a former Sequoia partner, said Bumble stood out among hundreds of companies Ithaca reviewed for which would become more valuable as AI advances. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-aa7287d3f87b08606518|cite]])
- Peers argues acquisitions of distressed companies are being dressed up as AI-driven purchases, and that Bumble could adopt AI without being sold, for example with forward-deployed engineers from Google or Palantir; the real reason it is selling is a troubled business. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-f685252bd1afb529d325|cite]])
- Bumble's revenue fell 10% in 2025 and another 15% in the first half of 2026. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-b6dac0988975b00ae582|cite]])
- As of June 30, 2026, Bumble had $450 million of debt but only $154 million in cash. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-c24df6be9280214f3330|cite]])
- Bumble is selling at $6.05 a share, one-seventh of its $43 a share IPO price in 2021, far from the bright future founder Whitney Wolfe Herd promised in the prospectus. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAIs-Bug-and-Bumbles-AI-Powered-Exit#^clm-cf23b1f06a0f75fa4850|cite]])
