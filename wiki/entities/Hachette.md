# Hachette

Big Five book publisher that fought Amazon over eBook terms in 2014.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- In a 2014 eBook terms dispute with publisher Hachette, Amazon blocked pre-orders, delayed shipping to one to two months, and pulled some eBooks, hurting authors like J.K. Rowling and drawing mockery from Stephen Colbert. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-e88b4c2ab43ec72dd5a4|cite]])
- Gallaugher says the Hachette dispute eroded Amazon's goodwill with authors and called into question its commitment to the customer; it settled in November 2014 with Hachette setting its own eBook prices plus incentives to price lower. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-8efb142a449cfe935490|cite]])
