# Smith + Howard

Atlanta-based accounting and professional services firm backed by private equity firm TPG.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-14 [[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions|Inside a TPG-Backed Accounting Firm’s Plans for AI and More Acquisitions]]

- Smith + Howard, backed by private equity firm TPG, is preparing a new round of acquisitions, including two deals expected to close by the end of 2026. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-91f5615c9b269d58da49|cite]])
- Smith + Howard plans to use artificial intelligence to expand its business without hiring accountants at its previous pace. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-a5166901496942f8b039|cite]])
- Smith + Howard employs over 800 people across seven states and projects roughly $175 million in revenue in 2026, growing 10% to 15% annually excluding acquisitions. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-0bf9ef721ab77cccbcd1|cite]])
- Smith + Howard aims to add 20% to 25% of forward revenue through acquisitions, lifting its target purchase size to firms with $5 million to $50 million in annual revenue. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-1115d4ceab121ac722ae|cite]])
- Smith + Howard increased its acquisition target size to companies generating between $5 million and $50 million in annual revenue, up from $2 million to $20 million previously. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-6fa4557c5ab13167c9c7|cite]])
- Audit fees in the accounting industry continued climbing even after major cost-cutting moves such as offshoring reduced labor expenses. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-bde5f610b3e4556f9233|cite]])
- Private equity investors in accounting divided between those excited about AI productivity and those fearing automation would shrink billable hours and revenue. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-42a5e3e3836bb60b0fcf|cite]])
- Smith + Howard rolled out Anthropic's Claude to all employees to experiment with automating basic tasks like industry-specific audits. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-f207d00bd58d95d082c7|cite]])
- Smith + Howard engaged external AI trainers partnering with labs like Anthropic, receiving weeks of employee training in exchange for minimum spending commitments. ([[2026-10-14-Inside-a-TPG-Backed-Accounting-Firms-Plans-for-AI-and-More-Acquisitions#^clm-4930d454444507985f9e|cite]])
