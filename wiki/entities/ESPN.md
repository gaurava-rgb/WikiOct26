# ESPN

Sports cable network; the most profitable network because every cable subscriber pays for it.

## Facts by source

### 2015-07-08 [[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and the Conservation of Attractive Profits]]

- Thompson notes TV network revenue shifted over several years from advertising toward affiliate fees, which are more profitable, predictable and sustainable than chasing mass audiences. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-36939613114d69b2a547|cite]])
- Thompson argues the affiliate-fee model made must-see shows and events matter more than mass-appeal programming, since only fans basing their pay-TV decision on them count; sports fans are why ESPN is the most profitable network. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits#^clm-b695bbcffb8da942df58|cite]])
