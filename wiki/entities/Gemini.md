# Gemini

Google's family of AI models and its chatbot app.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-23 [[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model|Google Nears Release of Flagship Gemini 4 AI Model]]

- Google is close to releasing Gemini 4, its next flagship AI model, according to Google DeepMind head Koray Kavukcuoglu. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-d3e7a57b11f54551b4e6|cite]])
- Prediction: Gemini 4 is early in post-training and Kavukcuoglu hopes to ship it much earlier than the end of 2026, releasing an early version and then iterating fast. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-16501dac69d5b3b11fdc|cite]])
- Google engineers already use Gemini 4 internally to run Antigravity, Google's AI coding tool, while safety testing continues. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-78be174ab0c9d9c3b801|cite]])
- Google has fallen behind Anthropic and OpenAI in the model race; Anthropic's Mythos family and OpenAI's GPT-6 models are more capable than Google's best current models. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-db5b5dde3ae522f54eb5|cite]])
- At Google I/O in May 2026, Sundar Pichai said Gemini 3.5 Pro would come in June, but it never shipped; Google focused on cheaper Flash models instead. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-894de43f68de903889a8|cite]])
- Gemini 3 was such a jump that OpenAI declared a code red to catch up. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-ec806a6fa0cdab5e4077|cite]])
