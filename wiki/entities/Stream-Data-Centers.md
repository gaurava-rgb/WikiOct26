# Stream Data Centers

Dallas-based data center developer, majority-owned by Apollo Global Management.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-22 [[2026-09-22-Anthropic-in-Talks-for-1-Gigawatt-Data-Center-Lease-With-Apollo-Backed-Developer|Anthropic in Talks for 1-Gigawatt Data Center Lease With Apollo-Backed Developer]]

- Anthropic is in early talks to lease up to 1 gigawatt of compute capacity from Stream Data Centers, a developer majority-owned by Apollo Global Management, to rely less on cloud providers. ([[2026-09-22-Anthropic-in-Talks-for-1-Gigawatt-Data-Center-Lease-With-Apollo-Backed-Developer#^clm-e635d57fa832a7fd6ea1|cite]])
- Anthropic would be a direct tenant of the Stream sites and fill them with TPUs, AI chips designed by Google and Broadcom, per people with knowledge of the talks. ([[2026-09-22-Anthropic-in-Talks-for-1-Gigawatt-Data-Center-Lease-With-Apollo-Backed-Developer#^clm-b5d855443228a4920dd2|cite]])
- The Stream deal is months from done and could fall apart; Anthropic would need financial guarantees for the leases and financing for the chips. ([[2026-09-22-Anthropic-in-Talks-for-1-Gigawatt-Data-Center-Lease-With-Apollo-Backed-Developer#^clm-ba375f54af6fa78fa540|cite]])

### 2026-10-11 [[2026-10-11-The-Attractive-Economics-Behind-Anthropic-Data-Center-Sprint|The Attractive Economics Behind Anthropic Data Center Sprint]]

- Anthropic's data center partners matter because they hold grid-connected power rights at old aluminum smelters, failed green hydrogen sites and other places bigger cloud providers missed, and such rights are rare given long grid waits. ([[2026-10-11-The-Attractive-Economics-Behind-Anthropic-Data-Center-Sprint#^clm-de328d1cdff1e66ec842|cite]])
