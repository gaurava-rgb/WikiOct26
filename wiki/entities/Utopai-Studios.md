# Utopai Studios

Video AI film studio that produces films using customized models.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-22 [[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off|Utopai Becomes a Unicorn as Video AI Takes Off]]

- Utopai Studios secured a $60 million funding round at a $1 billion valuation, and received term sheets for a follow-on round valuing the company at $2 billion to $3 billion. ([[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off#^clm-439b857fc59c435cf651|cite]])
- Utopai Studios Chief Executive Cecilia Shen, formerly an AI researcher on Google's X moonshot team, founded the company in 2022. ([[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off#^clm-9f8b00c1b758769f2daf|cite]])
- Utopai Studios operates as an independent film studio rather than a software seller, arguing that selling software alone offers negligible profit margins in entertainment. ([[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off#^clm-5afc1c29e6d2fdbd4566|cite]])
- Utopai Studios fine-tuned the open-source MiniMax H3 model to create a video engine that ranks second on Artificial Analysis text-to-video benchmarks. ([[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off#^clm-022f146898813350082c|cite]])
- Deploying AI tools cuts scene planning and computer-generated imagery costs for films by 80% to 90%. ([[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off#^clm-691378f285ba5c442b2c|cite]])
- Utopai Studios has more than 20 projects in development, including two television series and three feature films planned for release in approximately 3,000 North American theaters in 2027. ([[2026-10-22-Utopai-Becomes-a-Unicorn-as-Video-AI-Takes-Off#^clm-f750788f6ee920c3aba2|cite]])
