# Retail Link

Walmart's proprietary system that turns each checkout scan into reorders and shares sales data with suppliers.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Retail Link, Walmart's proprietary and continually refined system, records each sale at the register and automatically triggers inventory reordering, scheduling, and delivery, which keeps shelves stocked with minimal inventory. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-57d0c29a296e89ef82da|cite]])
- Walmart shares sales data with its suppliers so they can become more efficient, which lets Walmart keep cutting prices, and so they can find patterns that help them sell more. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-d7c5a789557c57cfaa73|cite]])
- More than 17,000 Walmart suppliers can see their products' daily sales, shipments, returns, orders, invoices, claims, and forecasts, and they run about 21 million queries a year against Walmart's data warehouses. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-b043c1e17b39ba9e9472|cite]])
