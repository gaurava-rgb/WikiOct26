# Google Cloud

Google's cloud computing unit (GCP), which rents computing, AI models, and tools to businesses.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- As of 2023, AWS held about one-third of the world's cloud computing market, a share about as large as the next two cloud providers (Microsoft and Google) combined. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-67c0b6a739fa16cb30a1|cite]])
- While Microsoft and Google have grown their cloud businesses, AWS's market share stayed fairly steady with even better margins; rivals' gains came from smaller players, not from Amazon. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7ce804e21e9b6f984110|cite]])
- Google's cloud business, the number three cloud provider, lost billions for years and turned its first quarterly profit only in Q2 2023, while AWS was gushing profits. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-9d8de97fff58a658a6a3|cite]])
- Google's rival App Engine did not arrive until 2008 and Microsoft's Azure cloud platform came out in 2010, giving AWS a multi-year head start. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-bd0a33cfd7b85b11f8ff|cite]])

### 2026-04-23 [[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|An Interview with Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian About the Agentic Moment]]

- Kurian says the framing for Google Cloud Next 2026 is that customers are moving from using AI to answer questions like a chatbot to having it automate tasks and whole process flows. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-e2d19bdafd9089e7231f|cite]])
- Kurian says about 500 customers, including Citigroup, Comcast, Walmart, and Unilever, would tell their agent stories at Next. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-36811c2697191eb7e8ed|cite]])
- Kurian says customer workflows from Google Cloud are put into a harness that feeds the reinforcement (training) loop for Gemini, with Cloud staff sitting next to Demis Hassabis's DeepMind team. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-4a09a0efbb5a100e2248|cite]])
- Kurian says Gemini tokens grew 40% since the prior quarter and Gemini Enterprise, Google's core agent platform, grew 40% quarter over quarter. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-70180926a8a80d1ecc6e|cite]])
- Kurian says most of the large AI labs, including Anthropic, use Google's stack, and Google makes money from whichever layer they buy, such as TPUs, Gemini, or security, so it does not see this as zero sum. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-ce3b89243008384860dd|cite]])
- Kurian argues selling TPUs to outsiders fills idle chips at night when chat demand drops, and the higher volume gets better supplier terms, which lowers cost of goods sold (the direct cost of what Google sells). ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-8dd38ead112a81582a5c|cite]])
- Google announced TPU 8t for training, a pod of 9,600 chips with three times the performance of the current generation. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-454e87fc20e0c656f0e3|cite]])
- Google announced TPU 8i for inference (running models), with 1,152 chips, three times the SRAM (fast on-chip memory), and a new Collectives Engine. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-1c687a52b72ab9a10d1d|cite]])
- Google Cloud also announced Nvidia VR200 instances, more ARM computers for classical tasks, a 10 terabit-per-second Lustre storage product, and a low-latency Rapid Storage product. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-4a4af3dee2be5b50cbf9|cite]])
- Kurian says new TPU buyers include capital markets firms like Citadel Securities that want to replace algorithmic trading on x86 chips, and high performance computing users like the Department of Energy's Genesis mission. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-0899fd794565259f747c|cite]])
- Google will put TPUs in other companies' own sites and sell them as a system, not only as cloud rental, for customers like trading firms and national labs. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-ee542fdfc04b5ff8358a|cite]])
- Kurian says compute is always short, even though Google could not grow as fast as it does without enough of it. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-a7eb6e8057f126a37430|cite]])
- Kurian says Google Cloud splits its business into Gemini as a product, Gemini embedded in other products, and selling compute, and argues placing hardware in customer sites changes cash flow compared with spending its own capex (money on data centers). ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-7d8d877aa2ff3015b6cd|cite]])
- Kurian's team builds all the infrastructure for Alphabet and also runs the cloud business, and he calls the idea that giving compute to one side takes from another overly simplistic. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-22f9a23809101cc510de|cite]])
- Kurian says Gemini in Google's threat intelligence tools is 98% accurate at ranking threats and processed 3.9 million threats in the last year. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-aadd804e10695c22bcb1|cite]])
- Kurian says Google bought Wiz because AI will find software holes much faster, so customers must fix them much faster, and cloud is the most valuable place to protect. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-e32ac02bb9849f3bacb6|cite]])
- Google and Wiz launched three Gemini agents: Red Agent tests attacks, Blue Agent finds what to fix, and Green Agent fixes it. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-69f4c9640e73e136870e|cite]])
- Google's cross-cloud lakehouse lets customers query data left in AWS, Azure, and SaaS apps, avoiding the egress fees (charges to move data out) other clouds impose, and Kurian claims 2x better price performance than the market leader. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-5f0a092fcf9b6b2c54aa|cite]])
- Kurian says Knowledge Catalog uses Gemini, not human consultants, to build a map of what each company's data means, and says Google is not competing with Palantir's ontology. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-0bc4d31bd9e07aa97814|cite]])
- Kurian says Google would love to offer OpenAI models on Vertex, and is adding third-party models including Anthropic and open source ones. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-37a06c8b86bca54338e9|cite]])
- Kurian rejects Thompson's idea that the model will eat all software, saying many SaaS vendors are embedding Gemini as an agent platform and Google will invest to grow its partner ecosystem. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-dfa422f63505c65bb16c|cite]])
- Sundar Pichai said at the Next keynote that half of Google's capex (spending on data centers) goes to Google Cloud, which runs the same stack as Google itself. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud#^clm-84614cc34ce7f2617249|cite]])

### 2026-05-04 [[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings|Google Earnings, Meta Earnings]]

- Google's cloud backlog grew to $460 billion in Q1 2026, up from $240 billion the prior quarter, and Google expects to record about half of it as revenue over the next two years. ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings#^clm-d8779378a425f96443b0|cite]])
- Google Cloud reported Q1 2026 revenue of $20.0 billion, up 63%, and operating profit of $6.6 billion, a 33% margin, up from 30% in Q4 2025. ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings#^clm-9b462387a7f2f6ae3c68|cite]])
- Thompson's table shows Google Cloud turned its first quarterly profit in Q1 2023, $191 million on $7.45 billion of revenue, after losing money every quarter since at least Q4 2019. ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings#^clm-bbe2a34738e1e2674044|cite]])
- In October 2025 Anthropic said it would expand its use of Google Cloud to up to one million TPUs, worth tens of billions of dollars and bringing well over a gigawatt of capacity online in 2026. ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings#^clm-f7092ff1b588384d2513|cite]])
- Sundar Pichai said Enterprise AI solutions became Google Cloud's primary growth driver for the first time, and revenue from products built on Google's gen AI models grew nearly 800% year over year in Q1. ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings#^clm-9e135056c3c8f65e8203|cite]])
- Thompson notes Enterprise AI solutions include Vertex AI, which also serves Anthropic models, so it is unclear whether the growth reflects Gemini usage or Anthropic inference running on Google Cloud. ([[2026-05-04-Google-Earnings-Meta-Earnings#^clm-9dcc121b928b4223aefc|cite]])

### 2026-06-02 [[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company|The Google Capital Company]]

- In Q4 2019, the first year Alphabet disclosed Google Cloud revenue, Google Services made $43.2 billion in revenue and $13.5 billion in operating profit, while Google Cloud made $2.6 billion in revenue and lost $1.2 billion, 6% of Services' size. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-55585662883c9610890d|cite]])
- Google Cloud made its first profit in Q1 2023: $0.2 billion on $7.5 billion of revenue, while Google Services made $21.7 billion in profit on $62.0 billion of revenue; Cloud revenue was 12% and its profit 1% of Services. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-32c681241217d6bbf32e|cite]])
- In Q1 2026 Google Services made $89.6 billion in revenue and $40.6 billion in profit, while Google Cloud made $20.0 billion in revenue and $6.6 billion in profit; Cloud was 22% of Services' revenue and 16% of its profit. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-aa4af92023e02fb444f6|cite]])
- From Q4 2019 to Q1 2026 Google Services more than doubled revenue and tripled profits, but Google Cloud grew faster, and its operating margin of 33% in Q1 2026, versus 45% for Google Services, is expanding more rapidly. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-a7e36597b8dd23ee7519|cite]])
- Thompson argues advertising is only a fraction of the economy while Google Cloud's growth is AI, which could take over the whole economy, so Google Services may prove to be the cash flow that built a lower-margin but absolutely bigger business, as See's Candies helped fund BNSF. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-2ebdf1c7006307e0ed9d|cite]])
- Thompson argues Google has optionality on AI outcomes: its Services business benefits from the investment, it competes at the model layer with Gemini, and it can sell capacity to the frontier labs. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-016366868b9b4f047ec6|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson predicts TPUs give Google a sustainable cost advantage, so if compute ever becomes a commodity Google is the hyperscaler poised to make the most profit. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company#^clm-7a4f9b7f20fba95a7f1b|cite]])
