# David McInerney

Co-founder and former CEO of FreshDirect; former Michelin-star restaurant chef.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch04 FreshDirect - A Tech-Heavy Online Grocer]]

- FreshDirect is a New York City online grocer whose co-founder David McInerney, a former chef who worked in Michelin Star restaurants, served as CEO during much of its growth. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-331b3e78a45991d2cbde|cite]])
- David McInerney's main goal in launching FreshDirect was to close the quality gap between store-bought produce and the food restaurants used. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-5d4cdfc171a146246940|cite]])
- After the Bronx failure, FreshDirect's prior CEO stepped down and co-founder David McInerney became CEO to steer the transition. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-03a46f44b9997124d30d|cite]])
- To win back trust, FreshDirect launched a "Direct to David" line to the CEO and sent drivers, sometimes a worker in an Uber, to fix a flubbed order immediately instead of giving a credit. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-e6a216ff6771e80c168e|cite]])
