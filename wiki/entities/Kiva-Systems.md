# Kiva Systems

Massachusetts warehouse-robot maker Amazon bought; now the core of Amazon Robotics.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): S17.7 Walmart - Data Asset in Action and the Rise of Walmart]]

- Amazon bought Kiva and builds its own warehouse robots, while Walmart partnered with robotics firm Symbotic and hopes to automate 65 percent of such facilities in the coming years. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart#^clm-f2450d0b76e01320c882|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Amazon's warehouse robots came from Kiva Systems, a Massachusetts firm Amazon bought for over three-quarters of a billion dollars, now the core of Amazon Robotics. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ae8105a0dd80f0aafb8d|cite]])
- In Amazon's robotic warehouses the shelves come to the workers: ottoman-sized robots haul shelving weighing up to 3,000 pounds. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-b40f04a311f08fb326c4|cite]])
- Robots let Amazon store up to 50 percent more product per warehouse and cut inbound unload time from hours to as little as thirty minutes. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-53a9ed648d10ebbbbdd3|cite]])
- Other Amazon acquisitions include robotics firms from Kiva to Roomba maker iRobot, Alexa (web analytics), Audible, Goodreads, Woot, Lovefilm ('Netflix of Europe'), Souq ('Amazon of the Middle East'), and One Medical. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-c8522d9e92c75eb2aefc|cite]])
