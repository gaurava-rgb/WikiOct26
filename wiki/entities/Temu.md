# Temu

Discount online marketplace owned by PDD Holdings.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia]]

- Temu, an upstart launched in China, mimics large portions of the Shein model; it began with a wide variety of goods rather than clothing but has started a money-losing assault on fast fashion. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-074680dc6a55869cba08|cite]])
- Temu's shopping app passed Shein's to become the most popular shopping app in the United States. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-507599e1c6b4d2db66c7|cite]])
- Prediction: Gallaugher argues it is hard to imagine the U.S. legislature, with its bipartisan critique of China, allowing the de minimis loophole that speeds Shein's direct-to-consumer shipments to continue. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-10e09c8989fc1907bcb3|cite]])
- Shein's biggest shift is a marketplace strategy that lets third-party firms list their own brands, like Amazon and Temu, trading lower revenue per item for usually far greater profits. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-2da646cff02da8ef2c51|cite]])
- Temu's parent PDD Holdings (PDD stands for Pinduoduo, its original name) was based in Shanghai until 2023, then moved headquarters to Dublin; Temu is headquartered in Boston. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-3903f832acaaa2c565aa|cite]])
- Few Americans knew Temu in 2022, but by 2023 it had the most downloaded e-commerce app in the United States and many other nations. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-758afdb1867c1a394206|cite]])
- Temu launched in the second half of 2022, and five months later spent $14 million on two Super Bowl ads promising users could 'shop like a billionaire'. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-83ad7057cae100e4de2b|cite]])
- Temu's ad spend is estimated at $1.4 billion in 2023, rising to $4.3 billion in 2024, largely on social media. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-e311a01d5591f503e805|cite]])
- Wired's analysis, confirmed by an insider, showed Temu losing about $30 on each order in 2023. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-f8efd0d1d26ad232c595|cite]])
- Temu is estimated to lose 30 to 35 percent on U.S. orders and 40 percent on orders to other nations. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-ed3b193d70901d4c2394|cite]])
- Temu offers goods from over 100,000 suppliers and is even more gamified than Shein, with spinning wheels, countdown clocks, and free items for buying more, aiming to draw Walmart and Costco shoppers. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-838c8ff367c2fa1a3892|cite]])
- Many of Temu's 100,000-plus mostly Chinese suppliers bristle at its demands: Temu sets their prices and specs and forces them to share shipping costs, a risk as supplier-friendly Shein courts the same firms. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-6a1a0b0e030dc6546317|cite]])
- Temu faces complaints of shoddy quality and knock-offs, with one site rating it 1.9 out of five stars, and has been accused of sourcing from suppliers with questionable ethics. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-c152031155ddc7927aef|cite]])
- Prediction: Temu uses the same de minimis loophole as Shein, which Gallaugher expects will close at some point, raising shipping costs and lengthening ship times. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-a43b1aee2ff96653dc53|cite]])
- In 2023 Shein sued Temu for trademark violations, alleging bogus social media accounts impersonating Shein and use of Shein's copyrighted images in listings. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-9b5ca205c00c8f9a5a01|cite]])
- Temu sued Shein under U.S. antitrust law, claiming Shein forces suppliers into loyalty oaths and that its suppliers are 70 to 80 percent of those able to serve direct-to-consumer fast fashion. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-c424c27503465e81df66|cite]])
- Only about one-third of all fast fashion sales happen online, suggesting upside for Shein, Temu, and rivals. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-6f7b5db450891b9d5b18|cite]])
- Gallaugher questions whether Shein has a sustainable competitive advantage: Temu shows the model is replicable, supplier lock-up is up to the courts, and Amazon and Alibaba have deep data and AI too. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-4aa68e5261633eca4d4a|cite]])
- Gallaugher's Law, 'Any idiot with funding can ruin a market,' comes from Pets.com selling pet supplies at a loss until bankruptcy; Gallaugher asks whether Temu fits the pattern. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch09-Shein-and-Temu#^clm-8dd7c389bd79ca3e904d|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems]]

- Gallaugher argues the biggest threat to Inditex and all fast-fashion firms is direct-to-consumer retailer Shein and similar rivals like Temu, which use technology to squeeze out costs. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-9bb2512ac9d9ad584524|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Gallaugher argues Marketplace lets Amazon build a long tail without the risk of owning unproven inventory, and that allowing cheaper competing listings keeps shoppers from looking first to Shopify, Temu, Google Shopping, or eBay while Amazon keeps the customer data. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-abbfb178d851f70209ee|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues rising alternatives like Shein, Temu, and Shopify pressure Amazon, creating a three-way tension between customer experience, growth, and rivals as customers and squeezed suppliers may leave. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-bafe5f2a176a1c01e230|cite]])
- Amazon holds roughly 40 percent of U.S. e-commerce, far more than any rival, though it faces rising competition from Shopify, Shein, and Temu. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-7998f2281e7fa80e1845|cite]])
- Shopify faces competition from Shein and Temu, which recruit its customers with similar tools, and from TikTok, a Shopify partner that launched its own e-commerce tools for vendors. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-10079b201b837a7357fb|cite]])

### 2026-09-30 [[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon|Alibaba's AliExpress Tests Membership Program in Challenge to Amazon]]

- Shein and Temu's growth slowed sharply in 2025 after the U.S. raised tariffs on e-commerce parcels, and the EU is copying that move. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-8d58d74be07849f2348f|cite]])
- In November 2026 the EU adds a 2 euro handling fee per parcel from outside the region, on top of a 3 euro levy per parcel worth up to 150 euros. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-8617add02f05de4ab3f8|cite]])
- Analyst Juozas Kaziukenas says Europe is now the least favorable market for direct-from-China apps because fees forced price rises and marketing cuts that effectively killed new customer acquisition. ([[2026-09-30-Alibabas-AliExpress-Tests-Membership-Program-in-Challenge-to-Amazon#^clm-a6cdcfddad449b260c28|cite]])
