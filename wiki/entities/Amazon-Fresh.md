# Amazon Fresh

Amazon's full-service grocery store chain, known for Dash Carts.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch04 FreshDirect - A Tech-Heavy Online Grocer]]

- By 2015 many items were cheaper from Amazon Fresh than from FreshDirect, which went from being known for low prices to being seen as a premium-price, premium-quality supplier. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-fa2849ac5d4e80a5d303|cite]])
- By 2023 Amazon had begun closing some Amazon Fresh and Amazon Go stores, including two Go stores in New York City, though it launched Just Walk Out tech in a Washington DC Whole Foods. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-c39495f767c64f659757|cite]])
- Early reviews of Amazon Fresh in New York City criticized its poor online shopping experience and uneven selection. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect#^clm-636efec312405cade3a6|cite]])

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Amazon Fresh stores offer the Dash Cart, a networked cart whose sensors, cameras, and scale track items so shoppers skip checkout, designed so customers need few new shopping habits. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-4fb5dd946e0622262c87|cite]])
- Gallaugher argues Dash Carts are clearly version 1.0, too small for a big family's haul, but likens them to the first Kindle, which was clunky and expensive until a few iterations made it a hit. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-65d3cf3f932f980da2b6|cite]])
- Amazon combined its website, Fresh, and Whole Foods orders into one delivery cart and opened a delivery-only dark store Whole Foods in Brooklyn. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-85ffc2263c7356074e51|cite]])
