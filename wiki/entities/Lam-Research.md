# Lam Research

Chipmaking equipment maker, Applied Materials' nearest rival, strongest in NAND and packaging tools.

## Facts by source

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks|Applied And Lam Are Overlooked Chip Equipment Stocks]]

- Applied Materials and Lam Research stocks tracked each other closely over the 12 months to October 2026 and both rose 125%. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-017d56fb78b03917ed12|cite]])
- As of October 2026 Applied Materials traded at a forward price-earnings multiple of 28.8 versus 33.7 for Lam Research, per S&P Global Market Intelligence. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-a5b3ee1cec10485ae782|cite]])
- On a revenue multiple, Applied Materials was at 9.1 versus 11.4 for Lam Research as of October 2026. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-32d36e31815c8efc7173|cite]])
- Prediction: Lam Research revenue grew 24% in fiscal 2025 and slightly faster in fiscal 2026, and S&P data expects 50% growth over the next 12 months. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-ba4805a296a0f9993cfb|cite]])
- In their most recent fiscal quarters, DRAM was 14.5% of Lam Research revenue and 20.1% of Applied Materials revenue; DRAM is the fastest-growing memory category. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-fbe6e067367840165c9b|cite]])
- Lam Research said its NAND revenue in the most recent quarter had doubled compared with the March 2026 quarter. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-d78901e8c3bcd96be06d|cite]])
- Prediction: HSBC analysts argued in an August 2026 report that NAND supply could reach parity with demand by the end of 2027, which could slow Lam Research's growth. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-a6705d2e4d995c862f07|cite]])
- Lam Research also grows by selling tools that package memory wafers onto chips, which makes it more exposed than Applied Materials if AI spending pulls back. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-b743789cfb7ed37eafea|cite]])
- Applied Materials and Lam Research each get around a third of their revenue from China and have been hit by trade restrictions limiting what they can sell there. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-829153b90016021f8731|cite]])
- Prediction: Jim Schneider says chip equipment is still cyclical but expects at least one more year of accelerating growth and two more years of growth. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks#^clm-c1d3f8a61e9a9e984223|cite]])
