# Greg Peters

Co-CEO of Netflix.

## Facts by source

### 2026-07-21 [[2026-07-21-Netflix-Earnings-Is-Netflix-Washed-Additional-Notes|Netflix Earnings, Is Netflix Washed?, Additional Notes]]

- Greg Peters says view hours do not map linearly to revenue: live events will be about 5% of Netflix's 2026 content budget but only about 1% of view hours, while kids and family animation is also about 5% of spend but about 8% of view hours. ([[2026-07-21-Netflix-Earnings-Is-Netflix-Washed-Additional-Notes#^clm-1d71bbe16d702073f920|cite]])
- Peters says 6 of Netflix's top 10 new member sign-up days over the past five years came from live events. ([[2026-07-21-Netflix-Earnings-Is-Netflix-Washed-Additional-Notes#^clm-875d83bdc1112ed75b4d|cite]])
- In an earlier 2026 Stratechery interview, Peters explained Netflix dropped subscriber counts because extra members, widely varying revenue per member, and advertising made the subscriber number less complete, and Netflix wants to be judged on revenue and profit. ([[2026-07-21-Netflix-Earnings-Is-Netflix-Washed-Additional-Notes#^clm-70bfa1b36de6e7596c42|cite]])
- Thompson argues Peters' own example undercuts him: advertising is very much a function of engagement, yet Netflix is reducing the only real engagement numbers it reports. ([[2026-07-21-Netflix-Earnings-Is-Netflix-Washed-Additional-Notes#^clm-22d3f4ec9b14674f33d5|cite]])

### 2026-08-25 [[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku|Netflix to Sell Streaming Services?, Streamers as Aggregators, Revisiting Roku]]

- Netflix co-CEO Greg Peters, asked on the Q2 2026 earnings call about distributing third-party streaming services, did not rule it out and said Netflix would consider more deals that serve members, partners, and Netflix. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku#^clm-89abadf6df22da654ed5|cite]])
- Netflix integrated French broadcaster TF1 into its service in France in June 2026, its first such move, and Peters called early member engagement very promising four weeks in. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku#^clm-f9bcbec715f2f7c65291|cite]])
- Thompson argues Peters is signaling a possibly massive shift toward selling other streaming services while framing it, in classic Netflix style, as something the company has always done. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku#^clm-6873f118736c02ff372e|cite]])
- Thompson argues Netflix has not saturated its market because it does not compete in free streaming, the fastest-growing area, which has synergy with pay TV as an extra window, marketing, and ad inventory. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku#^clm-d52227919611c4f05214|cite]])
