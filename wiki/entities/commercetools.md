# commercetools

Commerce software vendor selling a modular, API-first platform to retailers; partnered with OpenAI and Stripe on the Agentic Commerce Protocol.

## Facts by source

### 2026-04-12 [[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond|How Agentic Commerce Is Transforming Retail and Beyond]]

- The post describes agentic commerce as AI agents that research, pick a bundle, apply promotions, complete checkout, and trigger follow-ups on a shopper's behalf, learning from each outcome. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-e79ad2812526db2fefaa|cite]])
- The post argues retailers should use agentic AI in their own sites and apps too, quoting Forrester that guided selling with generative AI is the future of owned digital shopping. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-923fbe17f484c57a2a1a|cite]])
- The post argues agentic product discovery replaces a generic search results page with the single best product or a curated bundle, combining search, personalization, recommendations, and bundling. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-a6ee7542ec04e3d3ca86|cite]])
- The post says rich, machine-readable product data is crucial for retailers to benefit from agent-driven discovery and bundling. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-5f338d76d6f1dcc832be|cite]])
- As of April 2026 the post says consumers are already buying directly inside ChatGPT, made real by OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), launched with Stripe and commercetools. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-fed6735dd4e64d956f58|cite]])
- UK retailer Frasers Group and Mexican department store chain Liverpool use commercetools AI Hub and Agent Gateway so shoppers can find and buy their products inside ChatGPT. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-81b45b7fee8423969865|cite]])
- The post says agents can handle checkout for repeat consumables, noticing low supply and reordering by push confirmation or auto-checkout under pre-approved rules. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-0e805d61b60c6079b2f9|cite]])
- The post says agents can recommend dynamic markdowns to clear seasonal stock while keeping margin, with a human able to override for brand or legal limits. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-0e99c62bf037f12dc2b8|cite]])
- commercetools CMO Jen Jones argues emotional brand resonance will not be enough to win placement, because an agent returns a ranked list built on data clarity, not a brand story. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-4e821a050dbf8020638d|cite]])
- The post argues loyalty in an agentic world is won by structured product data, transparent pricing and stock, and algorithmic trust signals such as return policy and ratings encoded for machines. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-34c64f73e5d63df76da7|cite]])
- The post argues agentic AI needs real-time, reliable data first, which unified commerce supplies by connecting fragmented systems into one view. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-c6c8fdb54e5e7a8aaac5|cite]])
- Prediction: The post predicts agentic commerce will spread beyond retail wherever transactions are repetitive and data-rich, naming CPG, fashion, electronics, groceries, B2B, pharmacy, and travel. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-d0e9f47b121203f33f46|cite]])
- The post admits agentic commerce threatens retailers with disintermediation by AI platforms, zero-click shopping, disrupted loyalty schemes, and dependence on AI platforms for visibility and revenue. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-bf4c88bfdd83a8e3cb3f|cite]])
- The post advises retailers to make data agent-ready via partnerships with AI platforms such as ChatGPT and Perplexity, and to map ownable moments where brand-led agents add value. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-541880fcce19020e986b|cite]])
- The post advises retailers to build trust with privacy protections, transparent receipts, event logs, and one-tap reversals for every agent action, on a modular, API-first foundation. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond#^clm-46d1d049bafb2f2608ae|cite]])
