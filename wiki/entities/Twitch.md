# Twitch

Live game-streaming service Amazon bought in 2014.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- The chapter calls Twitch a billion-dollar acquisition, and the Key Takeaways say Amazon spent over $1 billion on it. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-8a61f9d59ce9af9a049f|cite]])
- Amazon agreed to buy Twitch for $970 million in August 2014, per the Wall Street Journal article the book cites. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-74e5f6dd6cc1dbe28d57|cite]])
- Twitch revenue was $2.8 billion in 2022; it averaged over 30 million daily visitors, about 2.6 million concurrent viewers, and 7.6 million monthly streamers, a long tail of content. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-1bc1463349d500827c9c|cite]])
- Google and Facebook's attempts to copy Twitch flopped, leaving Twitch an Amazon-owned near monopoly with over 90 percent market share. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-4eb424d7feb3c734f0a2|cite]])
- Amazon pays $1 billion a year in a ten-year deal for exclusive NFL Thursday Night Football rights, bundled with Prime and free on Twitch. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-772c87c0a30659ac760f|cite]])
