# Adobe

Maker of creative and document software, including Acrobat and the Firefly AI image generator.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-09 [[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT|OpenAI Restricts Adobe and Other Competitors From Advertising in ChatGPT]]

- The ad restriction had not been reported before, is not in OpenAI's published ads policy, and caught advertisers including Adobe off guard. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-8a5284fbe5eac02172c2|cite]])
- Adobe was part of ChatGPT's early 2026 ads pilot and ran ads for Acrobat Studio and its Firefly image generator. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-841c401649f44b31c968|cite]])
- Adobe's Doug Wyatt said ChatGPT is narrowing both paid and organic pathways for tools in categories where it competes, with image and audio closed and video still open. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-2381092abe0c959d3f77|cite]])
- Consultant Alan Chapell argues platforms usually do not cut off partners until they reach a certain size, and he expected this clash only a year or two later. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-6e4763de252d9e391c28|cite]])
