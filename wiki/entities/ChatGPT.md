# ChatGPT

OpenAI's consumer AI chatbot, used by more than 1 billion people a month in 2026.

## Facts by source

### 2026-09-09 [[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT|OpenAI Restricts Adobe and Other Competitors From Advertising in ChatGPT]]

- OpenAI told some business partners it will no longer take ChatGPT ads for products that generate images or audio, which compete with ChatGPT's own features. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-31c73ece6850b39caaac|cite]])
- Adobe was part of ChatGPT's early 2026 ads pilot and ran ads for Acrobat Studio and its Firefly image generator. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-841c401649f44b31c968|cite]])
- Adobe's Doug Wyatt said ChatGPT is narrowing both paid and organic pathways for tools in categories where it competes, with image and audio closed and video still open. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-2381092abe0c959d3f77|cite]])
- The restriction came as OpenAI announced ChatGPT Images 2.5 in September 2026, after unveiling ChatGPT Live voice models in July. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-6dec9984c018c2be81ee|cite]])
- More than 1 billion people use ChatGPT at least once a month, and most do not pay. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-f306b04b571634dc170c|cite]])
- ChatGPT ads started showing for some US users in February 2026, with launch advertisers such as Ford. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-5a5f5abf98264911381d|cite]])
- ChatGPT ads have spread to more than 40 countries, with more expansion planned. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Restricts-Adobe-and-Other-Competitors-From-Advertising-in-ChatGPT#^clm-7679ac403322713dd3c3|cite]])
