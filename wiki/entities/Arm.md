# Arm

British chip design firm whose instruction set powers most phones and a growing share of server CPUs such as Graviton, Axion and Nvidia's Vera.

## Facts by source

### 2015-07-08 [[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and the Conservation of Attractive Profits]]

- Thompson argues Christensen explained in 2003, four years before the iPhone, why Intel was doomed in mobile and ARM would rise: a device that is not good enough needs a modular, conformable processor.
