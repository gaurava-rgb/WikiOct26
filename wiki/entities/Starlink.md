# Starlink

SpaceX's satellite Internet service, its consumer-facing business.

## Facts by source

### 2026-05-27 [[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space|The SpaceX IPO and Data Centers in Space]]

- American Airlines announced in May 2026 it will install Starlink Wi-Fi on more than 500 narrowbody aircraft beginning in Q1 2027. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-abd52413b578bd3e816d|cite]])
- Thompson argues Starlink on planes has gone from a premium perk, as with United's 2024 deal, to table stakes, because airlines fear rivals will offer it and customers will choose flights by Internet quality. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-a396f50691ba5902909d|cite]])
- Starlink, SpaceX's consumer-facing business, generated $8.7 billion in revenue and $4.4 billion in profit in 2025, helped by access to SpaceX's launch capacity. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-45387c54ff89b732f21b|cite]])
- SpaceX's launch capacity has put over ten thousand active Starlink satellites in low Earth orbit as of May 2026. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-78786b9892260e1bc52c|cite]])
- The largest Starlink satellite, the V2 Mini Direct-to-Cell, measures about 7.4 by 2.7 by 0.3 meters and uses up to about 25kW, while an Nvidia NVL72 rack measures 2.2 by 1.1 by 0.6 meters, uses up to 135kW, and can fit a 1 trillion parameter model at FP4. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-0c8743d1a3cb4c8bfbae|cite]])
- Thompson argues data centers in space are possible if each satellite is treated as a rack rather than copying earth data centers, linked by lasers with its own solar panels and radiators, though 200+ square meters of radiators per rack will be a huge challenge. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-681e0c18b63a45e15b8f|cite]])
- Thompson argues radiation and reliability problems for rack-satellites can be eased with larger, less efficient but lower-power chips, and by making the satellites disposable like Starlink; about 100kW might match an NVL72 since earth-style cooling and power distribution are not needed. ([[2026-05-27-The-SpaceX-IPO-and-Data-Centers-in-Space#^clm-179ec5b1f6235315a20c|cite]])
