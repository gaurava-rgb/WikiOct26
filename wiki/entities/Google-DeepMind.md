### 2026-05-20 [[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti|Google I/O, World Models, I/O Spaghetti]]

- Although Sundar Pichai featured Demis Hassabis in his promotional tweet, Hassabis was barely in the keynote; he first appeared to present Gemini Omni, a world model whose first product, Omni Flash, targets video editing and generation. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-ed07533ad80f8b3ab4fb|cite]])
- Prediction: Hassabis said at I/O that artificial general intelligence is just a few years away. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-68fa5eb1e176df1e5d29|cite]])
- Hassabis called Gemini Omni a step change in simulating physics like kinetic energy and gravity, starting with video but aiming to generate any output from any input. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-975cffd5f55e12297bef|cite]])
- Hassabis argued in December 2025 that language models understand more of the world than he expected, but spatial and physical dynamics are hard to put into words, so robotics and universal assistants need world models. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-ea5dee3ab8a65678b18d|cite]])
- Hassabis argues that generating realistic worlds is the test of world understanding, which is why he sees Veo and Genie as steps toward generalized world models. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-be83e43a9a63d7a4963f|cite]])
- Thompson says DeepMind instead believes the gap is real-world data best gathered through realistic simulation, which yields multimodal models, unlike text-only Claude, but much weaker coding. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-0b7a619ed4fb895493ae|cite]])
- Thompson argues Google may trail Anthropic and OpenAI in coding, especially long-running agentic work, simply because its research team has other priorities, which is why the Antigravity team, not DeepMind, handled coding at I/O. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-f862b5b438e4a71d52c7|cite]])
- Hassabis closed the keynote saying people will look back and realize they were standing in the foothills of the singularity. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-29ec5059c5517da9c1c0|cite]])
- Prediction: Thompson suspects that if Google's AI leader does not care about prosaic matters like coding, video, and Search, Google may not go far in them, though if Hassabis reaches AGI first it may not matter. ([[2026-05-20-Google-IO-World-Models-IO-Spaghetti#^clm-7bc33c35415c71216b58|cite]])

### 2026-09-23 [[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model|Google Nears Release of Flagship Gemini 4 AI Model]]

- Google is close to releasing Gemini 4, its next flagship AI model, according to Google DeepMind head Koray Kavukcuoglu. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-d3e7a57b11f54551b4e6|cite]])
- Prediction: Gemini 4 is early in post-training and Kavukcuoglu hopes to ship it much earlier than the end of 2026, releasing an early version and then iterating fast. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-16501dac69d5b3b11fdc|cite]])
- Kavukcuoglu took over Google DeepMind in August 2026, moving from CTO to senior vice president; Demis Hassabis became chairman of the unit and chief scientist of Alphabet. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-54dc0e355695e20f7730|cite]])
- Kavukcuoglu says DeepMind's frontier model work shows Google's hardware engineers how to design the next two or three generations of TPU chips. ([[2026-09-23-Google-Nears-Release-of-Flagship-Gemini-4-AI-Model#^clm-abd8f40ca78fdd64ddca|cite]])
