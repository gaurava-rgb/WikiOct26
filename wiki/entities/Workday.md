### 2026-10-06 [[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday|Why the SaaSpocalypse Isn't Over For Workday]]

- Some Workday customers now use outside agents from Anthropic, Microsoft and others to pull data out of Workday, so nobody visits the app. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-22eeed44c734f9d00b91|cite]])
- The reporters argue the risk is that Workday becomes dumb infrastructure, a trusted database agents call, while the valuable work happens above it. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-ab7c93341ddd41acb1b5|cite]])
- At one consultancy with more than 400 Workday customers, about half signed deals with usage-based AI access but only 10% to 20% actively use Workday AI, and none use Sana Enterprise. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-1462478c222ad2b8769e|cite]])
- Workday will charge when outside AI reads or changes its data, but it waived API overage fees from May 2026 to February 2027. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-38585545f942c563a8ac|cite]])
- 5,500 of Workday's about 11,500 customers use its agents, up 35% quarter on quarter in the July 2026 quarter, per the company; it is unclear how many pay. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-cdf35f7fa78fb5c6614d|cite]])
- Workday said in August 2026 its AI agents plus Sana are on pace for $600 million a year, three times a year earlier. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-ed985aa026dc2881504a|cite]])
- Workday's revenue growth slowed to 12.8% in the July 2026 quarter, analysts expect 9.7% in the January 2027 quarter, and its market cap is about $45 billion. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-b4109106c78c5f350531|cite]])
- Prediction: Analysts expect Workday's revenue growth to slow to 9.7% in the January 2027 quarter, per Koyfin. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-a061a8a34a88cfa3c7a8|cite]])
- Reuters reported in August 2026 that Silver Lake considered taking Workday private. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-8ff55f7f56d773aa3ea3|cite]])
- Workday founders Duffield and Bhusri hold 69% of voting power; the company laid off about 400 people in February 2026 and announced another 500 in late September. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-8d063ee44a772a882c8b|cite]])
- Co-founder Aneel Bhusri has been back as Workday CEO since February 2026. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-f19fd9fc20ce8bf2cbca|cite]])
- Workday CTO Gabe Monroy says the focus is adoption, and he is leading work to make its in-house XpressO code language usable by AI coding tools. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-2b6e59c3ba999b193a53|cite]])
- Adviser Raphaelle D'Ornano summed up the situation: Workday is called, not visited, and the toll is waived. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-a190c2da765eb557fe54|cite]])
- The earlier fear that AI kills enterprise apps outright has faded and Workday shares partly recovered; the new worry is slower growth and lost pricing power. ([[2026-10-06-Why-the-SaaSpocalypse-Isnt-Over-For-Workday#^clm-5c9debb06e561fdd11da|cite]])

### 2026-10-13 [[2026-10-13-Why-Companies-Cant-Prove-Their-AI-Spending-Pays-Off|Why Companies Can't Prove Their AI Spending Pays Off]]

- On October 13, 2026 Workday unveiled tools letting customers use AI agents from Anthropic, Microsoft and OpenAI to automate tasks in Workday, similar to Salesforce's SIforce, earlier called AIforce and Headless 360. ([[2026-10-13-Why-Companies-Cant-Prove-Their-AI-Spending-Pays-Off#^clm-e173711615ae09d3702d|cite]])
- Some employees and investors fear these headless tools turn Workday and Salesforce into dumb infrastructure, basically glorified databases, while their leaders say they will still make plenty of money. ([[2026-10-13-Why-Companies-Cant-Prove-Their-AI-Spending-Pays-Off#^clm-295717aa76ad0220d408|cite]])
- Workday CEO Aneel Bhusri said Workday is giving customers choice, and that headless use through other companies' AI will be equally good for Workday, though he prefers its own interface. ([[2026-10-13-Why-Companies-Cant-Prove-Their-AI-Spending-Pays-Off#^clm-6440c2b7a83e5192c4e4|cite]])
- Starting in February 2027, after a fee-free grace period, Workday will charge customers based on how often AI agents tap connectors and take actions in Workday. ([[2026-10-13-Why-Companies-Cant-Prove-Their-AI-Spending-Pays-Off#^clm-abc24be218565eca8040|cite]])
