# Andy Jassy

CEO of Amazon.

## Facts by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Cloud computing was so profitable that Jeff Bezos picked Andy Jassy, the former head of AWS, to succeed him as Amazon CEO. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-1264697facd951fcc097|cite]])
- Amazon picked AWS chief Andy Jassy, rather than someone from its retail or device businesses, to replace Jeff Bezos as Amazon CEO, a sign of how important the cloud is to the company. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-ea55c163fffb4d641e07|cite]])

### 2026-04-30 [[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium|Amazon Earnings, Trainium and Commodity Markets, Additional Amazon Notes]]

- Amazon Trainium chips annual revenue run rate is over $20 billion, making AWS a top 3 data center chip business. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-2c624232cdd221771294|cite]])
- Jassy says Amazon's chip business grew nearly 40% quarter over quarter in Q1 2026 and has an annual revenue run rate over $20 billion, growing triple digits year over year. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-bdf83c9776a9958879c8|cite]])
- Jassy says that if Amazon's chips business sold to AWS and third parties as a standalone company, its run rate would be $50 billion. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-deb009b6bca4ec2da9be|cite]])
- Prediction: Jassy expects Trainium to save Amazon tens of billions of dollars of capex each year and give several hundred basis points of operating margin advantage versus relying on others' chips. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-87b4d9d1be3c62f5ca44|cite]])
- Jassy argues AWS will win the agentic era because of its cost structure from its own chips and because it won the commodity cloud era, so customers' data is already there. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-68900ca801eea1bc3ce3|cite]])
- Jassy said memory price and supply shortages push companies from on-premises servers into the cloud, since suppliers prioritize the largest customers. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-06d0d96824e1f55ece73|cite]])
- Jassy said Amazon wants to work with third-party horizontal shopping agents, though agentic commerce referrals are a small fraction of search-engine referrals today. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-45195098beeb7020ff14|cite]])
- Jassy argues advertising will do well in agentic commerce because multi-turn conversations create many chances to show sponsored products and sponsored prompts; Rufus is Amazon's own shopping agent. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium#^clm-06b3d9af71385ecd8d9a|cite]])
