# Chip Export Controls

**In one sentence:** US rules that limit selling advanced AI chips, like Nvidia's, and chipmaking tools to China. ([[2026-09-23-DeepSeeks-Annualized-Revenue-Hits-1-Billion-as-Startup-Finalizes-7-5-Billion-Fundraising|DeepSeek Revenue]]; [[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks|Applied and Lam]])

## Why it matters
They decide how China's labs work. DeepSeek still trains on Nvidia chips, likely from the black market. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls|DeepSeek Bets on Huawei]]) Lab staff say the limits pushed firms toward distillation, training a model on a US lab's answers, as a shortcut. ([[2026-10-09-Chinas-AI-Model-Race-Is-Just-Getting-More-Crowded|China's Crowded AI Race]]) They also cost US firms: Applied Materials and Lam Research each get about a third of revenue from China and have been hit. ([[2026-10-13-Applied-And-Lam-Are-Overlooked-Chip-Equipment-Stocks|Applied and Lam]]) The tool reaches US labs too: the Trump administration put export controls on Anthropic in June 2026. ([[2026-10-12-Trump-Administration-Plans-to-Move-AI-Testing-Agency-Officials-to-White-House|AI Testing Moves to White House]])

## Worked example: DeepSeek's Huawei bet, 2026
DeepSeek's CEO Liang Wenfeng calls moving training to Huawei chips one of its biggest bets and says it has to work. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls|DeepSeek Bets on Huawei]]) In May he estimated a top model needs about 50,000 Nvidia GB300 chips or 200,000 Huawei Ascend 950 chips, four times as many, and said Huawei could not supply enough. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls|DeepSeek Bets on Huawei]]) Huawei is held back by limited access to advanced memory. ([[2026-09-21-DeepSeek-Bets-Big-on-Huawei-Chips-to-Bypass-US-Export-Controls|DeepSeek Bets on Huawei]])
