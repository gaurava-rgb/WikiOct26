# Chip Export Controls

US rules that block or limit sales of advanced chips, such as Nvidia's top AI GPUs, and chipmaking tools to China. They aim to slow China's AI progress by starving it of compute. In response, Chinese labs like DeepSeek buy chips on the black market, squeeze more work out of fewer chips, and shift to homegrown chips such as Huawei's Ascend line.
