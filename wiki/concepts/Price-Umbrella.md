# Price Umbrella (Price Overhang)

A price umbrella is when the market leader charges more than its costs require, because demand is bigger than supply. Rivals can then price below the leader and look cheap, even if their own costs are not lower. When supply catches up, the umbrella shrinks and prices fall toward cost.
