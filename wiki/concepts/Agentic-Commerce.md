# Agentic Commerce

**In one sentence:** Agentic commerce is AI agents that research, pick products, apply deals, check out, and follow up on a shopper's behalf. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond|Agentic commerce post]])

## Why it matters
It raises the question of who owns the customer, the store or the agent that shops for them. Even a pro-agent vendor post admits the risk of disintermediation (stores getting cut out of the customer relationship) and of relying on AI platforms to be seen. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond|Agentic commerce post]]) Thompson argues physical networks change the math: Amazon's scarce warehouses and delivery let it keep value even if a chatbot takes the order. ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|MoffettNathanson interview]])

## Worked example: Amazon, April to June 2026
Andy Jassy said in April 2026 that Amazon wants to work with outside shopping agents, though agent referrals are still a small fraction of those from search engines. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium|Amazon earnings, Trainium]]) He expects ads to do well, because long back-and-forth chats create many slots for sponsored products; Rufus is Amazon's own shopping agent. ([[2026-04-30-Amazon-Earnings-Trainium|Amazon earnings, Trainium]]) Meanwhile Amazon blocks most AI discovery platforms, which helped slow the growth of AI traffic to marketplaces. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI|Michael Morton interview]]) Yet when analyst Michael Morton asked AI models which stores they prefer, they ranked Amazon first and Walmart second. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI|Michael Morton interview]])

## Where analysts disagree
- An April 2026 vendor post says shoppers already buy inside ChatGPT through OpenAI's checkout protocol. ([[2026-04-12-How-Agentic-Commerce-Is-Transforming-Retail-and-Beyond|Agentic commerce post]]) Morton says ChatGPT's in-chat checkout was shut down soon after launch, because ChatGPT needs Amazon more than Amazon needs ChatGPT. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI|Michael Morton interview]])
