# Full-Stack AI Integration

**In one sentence:** One company owns many layers of AI at once, such as chips, models, customer data, and the platform agents run on, and makes each layer improve the others. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]])

## Why it matters
Thompson argues that while chips are scarce there is money at every layer, but once supply catches up, most value goes to whoever controls the bottleneck and makes everyone else a commodity (easy to swap, so cheap). ([[2026-05-26-Nvidia-Earnings-The-AI-Stack-Nvidias-New-Reporting|Nvidia earnings, May]]) He sees the AI labs doing this by reaching up into the product people use, since Claude Code and Codex are proving sticky. ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models|Who's Afraid of Chinese Models]]) The foil is xAI: Thompson says a hardware owner in a shortage should sell to everyone rather than tie its chips to its own software. ([[2026-05-12-SpaceX-and-Anthropic-xAIs-Two-Companies-Elon-Musk-and-SpaceXAIs-Future|SpaceX and Anthropic]])

## Worked example: Google, April 2026
Google Cloud boss Thomas Kurian says Google is unique because it owns the whole stack, which improves its models every hour of every day. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]]) Every Google product runs the same Gemini version and the same agent harness (the software that steers the model) at the same time. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]]) Half of Google's capex (spending on data centers) goes to Google Cloud, which runs the same stack as Google itself. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]]) Customer workflows feed back into Gemini's training loop. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]]) Thompson says this gives Google options: its search and ads business gains, Gemini competes, and it can rent capacity to other labs. ([[2026-06-02-The-Google-Capital-Company|The Google Capital Company]])

## Where analysts disagree
- Thompson says the key joint is the model plus its harness. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) Satya Nadella says it is a harness that uses many models plus fresh company data. ([[2026-05-06-Microsoft-Earnings-Apple-Earnings|Microsoft and Apple earnings]])
