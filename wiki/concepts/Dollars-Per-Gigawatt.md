# Dollars Per Gigawatt (Nvidia Revenue per Unit of Data Center Power)

A way to measure how much of a data center's spending one supplier captures, stated as revenue per gigawatt of power the data center uses. Because power is the hard limit on how many AI data centers get built, a supplier grows by selling more of the parts inside each gigawatt (GPUs, CPUs, networking, systems). Nvidia uses it to argue its opportunity per gigawatt rose from about $18 billion with Hopper to $40 billion with Vera Rubin.
