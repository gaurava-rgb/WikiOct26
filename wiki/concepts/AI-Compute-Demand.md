# AI Compute Demand vs Supply

**In one sentence:** Demand for AI computing power keeps running ahead of the chips and data centers the world can build, and every big cloud company says buyers want more than it can supply. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]])

## Why it matters
Each new kind of AI eats more compute: chatbots were cheap, reasoning models use many more tokens (word pieces a model reads or writes) per answer, and agents call those models many times over. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) Thompson argues the big change is that the world has a fixed amount of compute, which breaks the old tech idea that serving one more user costs almost nothing. ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|MoffettNathanson interview]]) So the limit on sales is supply, not buyers. ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia earnings, September]])

## Worked example: Nvidia, August 2026
Nvidia's sales grew 100% in a year, and it forecast 70% growth next year. ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia earnings, September]]) Analysts had expected only 45%. ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia earnings, September]]) Jensen Huang said real demand is much greater than 70%, but 70% is what its supply lets it deliver. ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia earnings, September]]) Thompson compares this to the early iPhone: sales were set by how many could be made, and true demand stayed unknown until supply caught up. ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia earnings, September]])

## Where analysts disagree
- Huang says frontier labs have fantastic margins (profit on each sale) and are limited only by compute ([[2026-09-01-Nvidia-Earnings-Dollars-Per-Gigawatt-Open-and-Hugging-Face|Nvidia earnings, September]]). Thompson warns labs, clouds, and neoclouds all need AI revenue to rise sharply before outside money runs out ([[2026-09-21-Frontier-Overhangs|Frontier Overhangs]]).
- Thompson predicts that if compute stops being scarce, chipmakers' fixed costs will cause the worst down cycle ever ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|MoffettNathanson interview]]).
