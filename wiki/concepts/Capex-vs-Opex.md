# Capex vs Opex

Two budgets a company spends from. Capex (capital spending) is a big up-front payment for something used for years, like a mainframe or a server. Opex (operating spending) is an ongoing payment, like a monthly software subscription. Shifting money inside the capex budget is easier than cutting opex, so a hardware seller can lose a sale fast when buyers want other hardware.
