# Distillation (Training on Another Model's Outputs)

Training your own AI model on the answers produced by a stronger model, so the student learns to imitate the teacher without the teacher's huge training cost. Chinese labs use outputs from frontier models such as those of OpenAI and Anthropic this way, partly because U.S. chip export controls limit their compute. It is a fast shortcut, but it can leave the copy jagged and hard to tell apart from rivals that use the same teachers.
