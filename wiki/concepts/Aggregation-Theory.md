# Aggregation Theory

**In one sentence:** Aggregation Theory is Ben Thompson's idea that on the internet, value moves from firms that control supply to "aggregators" that own the user relationship (2015 source). ([[2015-07-21-Aggregation-Theory|Aggregation Theory]])

## Why it matters
The internet made shipping digital goods free and made the cost of a sale close to zero. ([[2015-07-21-Aggregation-Theory|Aggregation Theory]]) So the best aggregators win on user experience, which brings users, which brings suppliers, which makes the experience better again. ([[2015-07-21-Aggregation-Theory|Aggregation Theory]]) In 2026 Thompson argues AI agents could become the new gatekeeper, with apps as mere suppliers to the agent. ([[2026-09-28-Apps-Agents-and-Aggregation|Apps, Agents, and Aggregation]])

## Worked example: Netflix and Amazon, August 2026
Amazon has for years gotten tens of millions of people to subscribe to services like HBO Max and Apple TV inside its Prime Video app. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku|Streamers as Aggregators]]) Thompson says this works because Amazon already owned the customer relationship. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku|Streamers as Aggregators]]) Rival studios run money-losing streaming services, and their hardest problem is finding customers. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku|Streamers as Aggregators]]) So Thompson urges Netflix to copy Amazon: sell other services, keep the customer, show their shows in its app. ([[2026-08-25-Netflix-to-Sell-Streaming-Services-Streamers-as-Aggregators-Revisiting-Roku|Streamers as Aggregators]])

The limit: Thompson argues the theory does not fit physical businesses, so Amazon's scarce warehouses keep their value even if chatbots front the purchase. ([[2026-05-14-Interview-Ben-Thompson-MoffettNathanson|MoffettNathanson interview]])

## Where analysts disagree
- Thompson argues the AI model will eat software, with apps as suppliers to the agent. ([[2026-09-28-Apps-Agents-and-Aggregation|Apps, Agents, and Aggregation]]) Google Cloud's Thomas Kurian rejects this, saying many software firms are building Gemini into their products instead. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]])
