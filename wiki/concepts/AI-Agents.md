# AI Agents

**In one sentence:** An AI agent is a model steered by a harness (the software around the model: tools, memory, permissions) that does a task and checks its own work with ordinary tools. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) ([[2026-04-28-Interview-Altman-Garman-Bedrock-Managed-Agents|Altman and Garman interview]])

## Why it matters
Thompson dates the agent era to Anthropic's Opus 4.5 in late November 2025, and says agents use so many tokens (chunks of text a model reads or writes) that AI data centers are not a bubble. ([[2026-09-21-Frontier-Overhangs|Frontier Overhangs]]) He argues one person can run many agents, so demand for computing power can soar even if few people use them. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) TSMC's C.C. Wei said in April 2026 this shift drives another step up in demand for top chips. ([[2026-04-20-TSMC-Earnings-New-N3-Fabs-The-Nvidia-Ramp|TSMC earnings]])

## Worked example: Meta Muse, September 2026
Meta launched Muse on September 8, 2026, a personal agent that can shop online and answer emails. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent|OpenAI Does Math]]) Every US user gets a cloud virtual machine (a rented computer in Meta's data center) with 2 processor cores, 8GB of memory, and 8GB of storage. ([[2026-09-28-Apps-Agents-and-Aggregation|Apps, Agents, and Aggregation]]) Muse built Thompson a custom recipe app from his saved Instagram posts in 5 minutes, while he walked his dog. ([[2026-09-28-Apps-Agents-and-Aggregation|Apps, Agents, and Aggregation]]) Thompson calls it the best personal agent he has tried, but reads it as bad news for the big AI labs, because its model is not the best yet is good enough. ([[2026-09-21-Frontier-Overhangs|Frontier Overhangs]])

## Where analysts disagree
- Thompson argues the model plus its harness is where agents stand apart, so profits flow to Anthropic and OpenAI. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) Microsoft's Satya Nadella says it is a harness using many models plus fresh company data. ([[2026-05-06-Microsoft-Earnings-Apple-Earnings|Microsoft and Apple earnings]])
