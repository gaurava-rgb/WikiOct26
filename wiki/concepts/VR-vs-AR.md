# Virtual vs Augmented Reality

Thompson's split: virtual reality is an immersive experience that pulls you out of your surroundings; augmented reality adds to the world you are already in. He argues the difference is about immersion and input method, not whether your eyes are covered.

## Evidence by source

- Thompson says the best thing about Meta's Orion AR glasses is that you see the real world directly, so its 70 degree field of view felt massive even though the Vision Pro offers 100 degrees. ([[2024-12-02-The-Gen-AI-Bridge-to-the-Future#^clm-f3c0925125f7d16ba984|cite]])
