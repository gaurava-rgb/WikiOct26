# AI as Referral Channel vs Distributor

A way to judge whether AI hurts a consumer business. If AI acts as a referral channel, it sends shoppers on to the business, which keeps the customer relationship, like a better Google Search. If AI acts as a distributor, it sits between the business and the customer and reduces the business to an API call (a back-end service the AI calls). The first is mostly good for incumbents; the second is the real threat.

## Evidence by source

### 2026-06-18 [[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI|An Interview with Michael Morton About E-Commerce in the Age of AI]]

- Morton's data shows that traffic from AI platforms to brand.com sites is growing 4 to 8 times faster than traffic to legacy marketplaces like Amazon, Wayfair, Chewy, eBay, and Etsy. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-fcbe54e74115146cd4a3|cite]])
- Morton argues that attempts to make AI a distributor have fallen short, and AI now looks like a referral channel, a better Google Search that sends clearer intent, which should de-risk consumer internet stocks. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-56d0a4bee2c5b68ed6e3|cite]])
- Morton cautions the risk is not gone: hyperscalers are not spending record capex (capital spending on data centers) just to build a better Google Search, and Google and Meta are reportedly building personal agents. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-b585c4253cc9f24c65d3|cite]])
- Thompson argues Google and Meta learned that owning checkout does not work and advertising, where advertisers own the customer, is more profitable, so OpenAI's checkout failure may have helped it. ([[2026-06-18-Interview-Michael-Morton-E-Commerce-AI#^clm-5891bc9c99836ac4aa05|cite]])

### 2026-09-29 [[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews|Google Is Paying About 100 Digital Publishers for AI Overviews]]

- Google has been paying roughly 100 digital publishers based on how much their content contributes to AI answers in search and other products, per people familiar. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-b11dfd166d318690a9b8|cite]])
- Google long argued it should not pay publishers whose content answers queries because search sends them traffic, but AI Overviews sharply cut that traffic. ([[2026-09-29-Google-Is-Paying-About-100-Digital-Publishers-for-AI-Overviews#^clm-5d252025fb9b98068fba|cite]])
