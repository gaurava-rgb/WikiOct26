# Zero Data Retention

**In one sentence:** A promise from an AI provider that it does not store a customer's data at all, which Anthropic gave some business customers before June 2026. ([[2026-09-14-Anthropic-Data-Fears-Prompt-Nvidia-Palantir-and-Booz-Allen-to-Restrict-Model-Use|Anthropic Data Fears]])

## Why it matters
Thompson argues anything but zero data retention is a no-go for most large enterprises. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards|Fable 5.1]]) The fear is that labs could learn from customers' intellectual property (their trade secrets and code), which led Palantir, Nvidia and Booz Allen Hamilton to demand guarantees or cut use of top models. ([[2026-09-28-Anthropic-Moves-to-Cut-Off-Discounts-When-Customers-Hit-Their-Cap|Anthropic Discount Caps]]) Karp said clients will ask whether a lab keeps their data. ([[2026-07-09-Muse-Image-Grok-4-5-Alex-Karp-on-CNBC|Muse Image, Grok 4.5, Alex Karp on CNBC]])

## Worked example: Anthropic's Fable rule, 2026
In June 2026 Anthropic required 30-day storage of all traffic on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 to defend against attacks, while pledging not to train on it. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards|Fable 5.1]]) Palantir would not offer Fable until Anthropic gave zero data retention it cannot later revoke, and a large US utility dropped a Fable test for the same reason. ([[2026-09-14-Anthropic-Data-Fears-Prompt-Nvidia-Palantir-and-Booz-Allen-to-Restrict-Model-Use|Anthropic Data Fears]]) Meanwhile Palantir says it has zero data retention access to OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra. ([[2026-09-14-Anthropic-Data-Fears-Prompt-Nvidia-Palantir-and-Booz-Allen-to-Restrict-Model-Use|Anthropic Data Fears]]) Thompson says growth slowed and Fable usage was stagnant, so Anthropic announced safeguards that keep data in cloud systems the customer controls, with zero retention offered until then. ([[2026-09-02-Fable-5-1-Enterprise-Frontier-Safeguards|Fable 5.1]])
