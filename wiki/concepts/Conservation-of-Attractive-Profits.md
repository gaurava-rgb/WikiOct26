# Conservation of Attractive Profits

**In one sentence:** Clay Christensen's rule that when one step of a value chain (the chain of steps from making a product to selling it) turns into a cheap commodity, the profits usually move to a step next to it (2015 source). ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and Attractive Profits]])

## Why it matters
Thompson argues that breaking up an old integrated system destroys the incumbents' value, while a newcomer ties together a different part of the chain and takes the new profit. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and Attractive Profits]]) He applies it to AI in 2026: while chips are short there is money at every layer, but once supply meets demand, most value goes to whoever controls the bottleneck. ([[2026-05-26-Nvidia-Earnings-The-AI-Stack-Nvidias-New-Reporting|Nvidia earnings, May]])

## Worked example: Netflix, 2015 (2015 source)
Thompson argues streaming made time a commodity: with no prime time, Sunday at 9pm equals Tuesday at 11am. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and Attractive Profits]]) Netflix offered its whole library on demand and tied content buying to owning the customer. ([[2015-07-21-Aggregation-Theory|Aggregation Theory]]) In 2013 Thompson called Netflix just another TV network; in 2015 he said that was badly wrong. ([[2015-07-08-Netflix-and-the-Conservation-of-Attractive-Profits|Netflix and Attractive Profits]])

## Where analysts disagree
- Where do AI profits land? In March 2026 Thompson argued the model plus its harness (the software that steers the model) is the integration that matters. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) Microsoft's Satya Nadella says it is a harness that uses many models plus fresh company data. ([[2026-05-06-Microsoft-Earnings-Apple-Earnings|Microsoft and Apple earnings]]) By September 2026 Thompson had wavered, since users can now pick which model runs Copilot Cowork. ([[2026-09-21-Frontier-Overhangs|Frontier Overhangs]])
- Horace Dediu calls Apple's Gemini license brilliant because models are becoming commodities. ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]])
