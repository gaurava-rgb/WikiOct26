# Constitutional AI

Anthropic's way of training AI models to follow an explicit written set of principles, instead of relying only on people rating answers (reinforcement learning from human feedback, RLHF). Supporters say it makes models safer and more reliable.

## Evidence by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud]]

- Anthropic trains its models with constitutional AI, where a model follows an explicit set of principles, instead of relying only on human feedback (RLHF) as ChatGPT does; Anthropic claims this makes its AI safer and less likely to hallucinate. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon#^clm-1a08fa5a0bf55e60eecb|cite]])
