# AI Diffusion

**In one sentence:** AI diffusion is how fast AI actually spreads through companies and daily life, which Thompson says depends on whether costly mistakes can be avoided, not on what the AI can do. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation|Autonomy and Innovation]])

## Why it matters
Sam Altman admitted he was wrong about how fast AI would upend software businesses after GPT-4, and blamed the economy's inertia. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation|Autonomy and Innovation]]) Thompson argues fear of risk will keep big, established firms using AI only to do old work a bit better. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation|Autonomy and Innovation]]) The Wall Street Journal notes that consumers lag too: the big agent businesses are built mostly on coding and office work, which leaves an opening for Meta and Apple. ([[2026-09-09-OpenAI-Does-Math-Reward-Hacking-Meta-Launches-Personal-Agent|OpenAI Does Math]])

## Worked example: OpenAI, May 2026
On May 11, 2026, OpenAI set up the OpenAI Deployment Company with more than $4 billion to help organizations build and roll out AI. ([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|The Deployment Company]]) The next day Google said it would hire hundreds of forward deployed engineers (engineers placed inside customer offices). ([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|The Deployment Company]]) Thompson's read: OpenAI and Anthropic need the revenue, companies need the imagination, and Google needs to stay in the game. ([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|The Deployment Company]])

*Lesson (editor's summary):* even strong AI needs people to carry it into a business.

## Where analysts disagree
- In 2024 Thompson said the biggest AI prize was top-down rollouts inside big companies. ([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|The Deployment Company]]) By August 2026 he predicts AI-native startups, not incumbents, will win each category. ([[2026-08-24-Autonomy-and-Innovation|Autonomy and Innovation]])
- In April 2026 Kurian said Google uses Gemini, not human consultants, to map a company's data. ([[2026-04-23-Interview-Thomas-Kurian-Google-Cloud|Kurian interview]]) Weeks later Google began hiring hundreds of deployment engineers. ([[2026-05-13-The-Deployment-Company-Back-to-the-70s-Apple-and-Intel|The Deployment Company]])
