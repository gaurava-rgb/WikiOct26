# Scale Economies

**In one sentence:** The cost of each unit falls as a firm grows, because big fixed costs are spread over more sales; the largest cloud provider, for example, can run its machines at their best load. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Amazon]], p.284)

## Why it matters
Gallaugher calls scale Walmart's key edge; in three months it sells more than Home Depot, the number two US store chain, sells in a year. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Walmart]], p.640) Scale also wins price wars: a former Amazon employee said a rival challenged Amazon to a breath-holding contest, and Amazon had "bigger lungs." ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Amazon]], p.249) The flip side is the entry bill: FreshDirect spent over $75 million on buildings and systems before serving a single customer. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, FreshDirect]], p.126)

## Worked example: Amazon's delivery network, 2019 to 2023
Scale let Amazon build its own fleet of trucks, planes, and ocean freight, now larger than FedEx or UPS, firms it once relied on. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Amazon]], p.238) Running its own shipping saves Amazon about a third of what it would pay outside carriers. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Amazon]], p.260) By a 2021 estimate, roughly one in three US packages went through Amazon's own network. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Amazon]], p.259)

## Where analysts disagree
