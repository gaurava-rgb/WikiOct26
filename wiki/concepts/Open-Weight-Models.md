# Open-Weight Models

**In one sentence:** AI models whose trained files (weights) anyone can download and run, unlike closed models such as GPT and Claude that you reach only through the maker's service. ([[2026-10-08-Chinese-Open-Models-Win-Over-One-in-Five-of-The-Informations-Subscribers|Subscriber Survey]])

## Why it matters
They are widely used but rarely the main tool: 46% of surveyed subscribers use some open models for work and 21% use Chinese ones, yet only 20% run most of their AI on them. ([[2026-10-08-Chinese-Open-Models-Win-Over-One-in-Five-of-The-Informations-Subscribers|Subscriber Survey]]) Money is chasing them: Nvidia is in early talks to buy, or invest more in, Reflection AI, an open-weight maker valued at about $25 billion. ([[2026-10-12-OpenAIs-Plan-To-Revive-Sora|OpenAI's Sora Plan]]) Open files also let outsiders study models cheaply: Goodfire picked a small Qwen model so experiments cost about 100 times less. ([[2026-10-12-Researchers-Need-to-Learn-How-AI-Makes-Decisions-Says-Goodfires-Tom-McGrath|McGrath interview]]) The flip side: CrowdStrike says an attacker on South Korean banks used a Chinese open-source agent and Chinese models. ([[2026-10-13-OpenAI-Eyes-Acquisition-That-Could-Boost-Cybersecurity-Business|OpenAI Eyes Trail of Bits]])

## Worked example: a garage data center, October 2026
The YouTuber Digital Spaceport runs Alibaba's open model Qwen 3.8 27B on five AMD GPUs in his garage to edit and analyze his videos. ([[2026-10-10-GPUs-in-the-Wine-Cellar-Why-Techies-Are-Hoarding-AI-Compute-in-Their-Homes|GPUs in the Wine Cellar]]) No lab sits between him and the model, which is what [[Local-Inference]] means.
