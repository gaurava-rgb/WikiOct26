# Open-Weight Models

AI models whose trained settings (weights) are published so anyone can download and run them, unlike closed models such as GPT or Claude that are only reached through the maker's service. Chinese labs like DeepSeek, Moonshot and Alibaba's Qwen lead here, and their low prices pressure closed-model makers.
