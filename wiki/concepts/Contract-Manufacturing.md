# Contract Manufacturing

Paying outside factories to make your products instead of owning the plants or employing the workers. It lowers costs and lets a firm grow without building factories, but it adds long lead times and exposes the brand to the contractor's labor, safety, and environmental failures.

## Evidence by source

### 2025 [[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara|Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems]]

- Contract manufacturing in developing countries keeps goods cheap, but race-to-the-bottom bidding pushes some contractors to skimp on safety, ignore the environment and use child labor. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-ca2622db0d244e5f9cd4|cite]])
- The spring 2013 Rana Plaza collapse in Bangladesh, an illegally built eight-story building housing garment factories, killed more than 1,100 people; Walmart, The Children's Place and Benetton were said to have bought from its firms. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-b0b43142ac5e3c7657dd|cite]])
- A video caption in the chapter describes the Bangladesh garment factory collapse as killing at least 230 people. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-278fe1d89100f06e9424|cite]])
- Gap was not implicated in Rana Plaza but became a protest target in 2013 as one of the largest importers of Bangladeshi clothing and for refusing to sign a safety accord backed by H&M and Zara. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-071644700e9ed80efadd|cite]])
- Reebok built an in-house system to track audits of its hundreds of suppliers on labor, safety and environmental practices, then donated it to help create the nonprofit Fair Factories Clearinghouse. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-d1ec99f940bdfd5b958e|cite]])
- Zara's contract manufacturers for trendy clothes are nearby in Portugal, Morocco and Turkey, because for Zara speed beats manufacturing cost and drives profits. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-339c30ec5c5bc5739207|cite]])
- Zara uses distant contractors in Asia and Latin America only for long-shelf-life staples like T-shirts and jeans, about one-eighth of dollar volume, which also limits exposure to partner labor abuses. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch02-Zara#^clm-7837f90c1d13194c7b6f|cite]])

### 2026-04-21 [[2026-04-21-Tim-Cooks-Impeccable-Timing|Tim Cook’s Impeccable Timing]]
