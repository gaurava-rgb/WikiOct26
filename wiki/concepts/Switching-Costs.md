# Switching Costs

**In one sentence:** What a customer loses by moving to a rival, like the work of re-creating past grocery orders at a new site. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, FreshDirect]], p.119)

## Why it matters
Switching costs keep customers from even trying a competitor. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, FreshDirect]], p.119) Amazon's Fire Phone failed partly because it had to fight them with too few apps. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch08-Amazon|Gallaugher, Amazon]], p.275) In AI, Thompson notes people rarely try other models once used to one, and that coding agents like Claude Code and Codex are proving sticky. ([[2026-07-09-Muse-Image-Grok-4-5-Alex-Karp-on-CNBC|Muse Image, Grok 4.5]]) ([[2026-07-20-Whos-Afraid-of-Chinese-Models|Who's Afraid of Chinese Models]])

## Worked example: IBM mainframes, July 2026
Thompson argues almost no new company picks a mainframe (a big, old-style central computer). ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems|IBM Misses]]) IBM sells to long-time customers like banks and airlines, trapped because their transactions are too many and too complex to move. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems|IBM Misses]]) He argues leaving would cut IBM's combined hardware-and-software charge over time, but the move itself can be extremely expensive. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems|IBM Misses]]) Thompson suspects customers did not leave that quarter, but learned AI makes a move possible, so they delay new mainframes and put new work elsewhere. ([[2026-07-15-IBM-Misses-IBMs-Mainframe-Moat-IBMs-Many-AI-Problems|IBM Misses]]) The lesson: a switching cost is a wall, and AI can lower walls.
