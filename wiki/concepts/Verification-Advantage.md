# Verification Advantage (Verifiable Data)

Thompson's idea that AI models improve fastest on tasks where you can check the answer, like code that runs or fails. So a company that owns a clear pass or fail signal, such as whether an ad led to a sale, has an edge in training models. Markets are verification machines: with enough volume, their outcomes tell a model what worked.
