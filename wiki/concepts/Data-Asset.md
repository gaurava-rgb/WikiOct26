# Data as a Competitive Asset

**In one sentence:** Data a firm gathers from its own customers and operations over time, which rivals cannot buy even when they can buy the same software. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, FreshDirect]], p.119)

## Why it matters
Gallaugher argues the edge grows with time: more time brings more data, and more data brings a stronger advantage. ([[2025-Gallaugher-Ch04-FreshDirect|Gallaugher, FreshDirect]], p.119) In AI, Thompson argues Meta's ads, trillions of which either lead to a sale or not, give a model a signal that helps it improve sooner. ([[2026-07-09-Muse-Image-Grok-4-5-Alex-Karp-on-CNBC|Muse Image, Grok 4.5]]) But old data helps little when retail changes radically; Walmart's data could not foresee the rise of Target. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Walmart]], p.648)

## Worked example: Walmart, 2006 to 2021
Walmart gathers data on over a million sales each hour. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Walmart]], p.640) Its Retail Link system records each sale at the register and orders new stock by itself, so shelves stay full with little spare inventory. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Walmart]], p.641) More than 17,000 suppliers can see their own daily sales and run about 21 million data searches a year. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Walmart]], p.643) The data showed Pop-Tarts sales rise sevenfold before hurricanes, so Walmart trucks them to Gulf states when a big storm is forecast. ([[2025-Gallaugher-S17-7-Walmart|Gallaugher, Walmart]], p.643)

## Where analysts disagree
- Thompson argues the key AI integration is the model plus its harness (the software that steers it). ([[2026-03-16-Agents-Over-Bubbles|Agents Over Bubbles]]) Nadella argues it is a many-model harness plus constantly updated company data like e-mail and documents. ([[2026-05-06-Microsoft-Earnings-Apple-Earnings|Microsoft and Apple earnings]])
