# Commoditize Your Complements

A strategy where a firm makes the products that go with its own core business cheap or free, so that demand for its core business grows and no rival can capture the value. Thompson uses it in 2026 to explain why China pushes open weight AI models: free, widely available AI helps China's physical industries like robotics, which are its real strength.
