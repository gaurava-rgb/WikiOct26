# How Thoma Bravo Found a Discount in a Hot Cybersecurity Market

Alex Eichenstein, The Information, published 2026-10-21.

## Summary

Thoma Bravo portfolio company Proofpoint agreed to acquire data security platform Varonis Systems for $6.3 billion, or roughly six times forward ARR, capitalizing on temporary stock pressure during Varonis' SaaS transition. The transaction expands Proofpoint into securing access permissions for autonomous AI agents ahead of a planned IPO. Cybersecurity M&A reached a record 170 transactions in the third quarter, driven by AI-driven threat vectors, strategic corporate takeovers like Alphabet's Wiz deal, and private equity demand for companies generating verifiable agentic revenue.

## Key claims

- Thoma Bravo portfolio company Proofpoint agreed to acquire data security software provider Varonis Systems for $6.3 billion.
