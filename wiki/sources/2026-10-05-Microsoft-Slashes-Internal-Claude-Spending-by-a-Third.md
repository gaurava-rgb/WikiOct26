# Microsoft Slashes Internal Claude Spending by a Third

Aaron Holmes, Jyoti Mann, Kevin McLaughlin, The Information, published 2026-10-05.

## Summary

Microsoft and Meta, two of Anthropic's biggest corporate customers, are moving their own staff off Claude and onto in-house tools and models, mainly to cut costs. Microsoft cut its internal Claude spend by more than a third and made OpenAI models its internal coding default, while its payments to Anthropic for customer-facing Copilot features keep growing. Meta's Claude Code users roughly halved as it pushed its own coding tools and added token budgets. The shift adds to headwinds for Anthropic ahead of its IPO, and shows big buyers now ration AI tokens like any other cost.

## Key claims

- Microsoft and Meta, two of Anthropic's biggest corporate customers, are pushing their own staff off Claude and onto in-house tools and models, mainly because of cost. (§ The core story: “pushing their own staff off Claude and onto in-house tools and models”) ^clm-ae4642bfdcb93ec648d3
- Microsoft's internal Claude spend was projected at more than $1 billion a year as of May 2026 and has now been cut by more than a third, per people familiar. (§ Key facts and numbers: “projected at $1 billion+ a year as of May 2026, now cut by more than a third”) ^clm-f376c564f5327d0910d7
- Microsoft pays Anthropic at least $2 billion a year to power Copilot features for customers; that spend has grown with Copilot subscribers and offset the internal cut, keeping total payments roughly flat, per one person. (§ Key facts and numbers: “pays Anthropic at least $2 billion a year to power Copilot features for customers”) ^clm-0679795fd271648dc13c
- In July 2026 Microsoft began routing complex Copilot queries to OpenAI's GPT-5.6 by default, per one person. (§ Key facts and numbers: “began routing complex Copilot queries to OpenAI's GPT-5.6 by default”) ^clm-e473781981d7af6bd09d
- Microsoft's cloud and AI group, with more than 60,000 staff, cut per-employee AI budgets from $100,000 a month to about $10,000 in most cases, per one person. (§ Key facts and numbers: “cut per-employee AI budgets from $100,000 a month to about $10,000”) ^clm-891f73750c2ebb8e1618
- GitHub Copilot's auto mode sends most queries to OpenAI models, which Microsoft does not pay for under its OpenAI deal. (§ Key facts and numbers: “sends most queries to OpenAI models, which Microsoft does not pay for”) ^clm-44bd901ce4c9dedb6f7b
- Meta's Claude Code users fell from about 60,000 earlier in 2026 to about 30,000 recently, per internal data, after spring layoffs cut about 10% of its 78,000 staff. (§ Key facts and numbers: “about 60,000 earlier in 2026, about 30,000 recently”) ^clm-32b53c401a64bbc35b01
- Meta's in-house coding tool MetaCode has more than 30,000 users, and Muse Code, sold externally since August 2026, has more than 6,000 employee users. (§ Key facts and numbers: “MetaCode 30,000+ users; Muse Code (sold externally since August) 6,000+ employee users”) ^clm-8b8e77c8249fb96f8f41
- Meta spent over $105 million on Claude Code in a recent 28-day period, per internal data. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Meta spent over $105 million on Claude Code in a recent 28-day period”) ^clm-1fba178da515b4f3f1f0
- Anthropic's annualized revenue passed $65 billion in July 2026; about 6,000 customers spent $100,000 or more in the year to June, over 1,000 spent $1 million or more, and over 100 spent $10 million or more. (§ Key facts and numbers: “annualized revenue passed $65 billion in July 2026”) ^clm-1f09345bd5aec832df0e
- Two customers made up nearly a quarter of Anthropic's 2025 revenue, per Reuters citing a draft prospectus. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Two customers made nearly a quarter of 2025 revenue”) ^clm-0601af34f51a144d51aa
- OpenAI's annualized revenue jumped about 70% in recent months to nearly $70 billion. (§ Key facts and numbers: “OpenAI annualized revenue jumped about 70% in recent months to nearly $70 billion”) ^clm-6d3e2c47335a8bfa4a96
- Microsoft's Jay Parikh wrote in an August 2026 memo that shifting to OpenAI models gives greater value from the company's token investment; he and Scott Guthrie told staff to watch spend and prefer non-Anthropic models. (§ Named people and roles: “greater value from our token investment”) ^clm-5d7859dc553992f29460
- Meta added token budgets and a usage dashboard after tokenmaxxing and a Claudeonomics leaderboard drove a spring 2026 spike in internal AI spend. (§ Change versus earlier reporting: “Meta then added token budgets and a usage dashboard”) ^clm-4f1d0ba257d5d0ffe282
- Mark Zuckerberg launched Meta Enterprise Platform to sell AI to businesses. (§ Named people and roles: “launched Meta Enterprise Platform last week to sell AI to businesses”) ^clm-4156bd89a9e0ae358892
