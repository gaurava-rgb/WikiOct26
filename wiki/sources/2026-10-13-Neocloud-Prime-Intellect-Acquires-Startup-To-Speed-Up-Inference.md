# Neocloud Prime Intellect Acquires Startup To Speed Up Inference

Stephanie Palazzolo, The Information, published 2026-10-13.

## Summary

Prime Intellect, a $1 billion neocloud, is buying Moondream, a tiny open-source model maker, mainly for its engineers' skill at megakernels, a way to fuse GPU operations so models run faster and cheaper. Moondream's compiler automates what has been manual work, and its image-model experience could help Prime Intellect win robotics and computer-use agent customers. At $100 million in annualized revenue, Prime Intellect is far smaller than Fal or Together AI, but it owns its hardware. The newsletter also covers an Anthropic data center in Japan, Microsoft's Maia 300 chip, Character.ai's bankruptcy and Meta's new Muse models.

## Key claims

- Prime Intellect, a San Francisco neocloud valued at $1 billion in a July 2026 equity financing, is acquiring Moondream to make open models run faster and cheaper on its Nvidia servers. (§ The deal: “valued at $1 billion in an equity financing in July 2026”) ^clm-ee769de8b8b34804e2e8
- Moondream has fewer than 10 employees and has raised $4.5 million; Prime Intellect CEO Vincent Weisser would not share the deal terms. (§ The deal: “Moondream has raised $4.5 million in funding”) ^clm-4564540eec41a2da21c8
- Vincent Weisser says Prime Intellect is buying Moondream mainly for its staff's skill at writing software that makes models run efficiently, not for its vision-language model. (§ What Moondream brings: “mainly buying it for its staff's skill at writing software that makes models run more efficiently”) ^clm-0a31646aa8012f883338
- Megakernels speed up AI inference by fusing many separate GPU math operations into one continuous operation, cutting GPU idle time between tasks; Moondream's staff specialize in them. (§ What Moondream brings: “fusing many separate math operations on a GPU into one continuous operation”) ^clm-b357e540687f911d7c6f
- Moondream's megakernel compiler and Photon, its proprietary tech for running models fast, will be folded into Prime Intellect's technology. (§ What Moondream brings: “That compiler and Photon, Moondream's proprietary tech for running models fast”) ^clm-f7fb558fcbf14107aefe
- Together AI has also worked on megakernels, which engineers say have been written by hand, while Moondream says its compiler generates them largely automatically. (§ What Moondream brings: “Moondream says its compiler works largely automatically”) ^clm-24892cc0d1a1bfa34b3b
- Prediction: Prime Intellect hopes Moondream's rare experience with image models will help it win robotics and computer-using AI agent customers that other neoclouds serve poorly. (§ New customers beyond text: “serve customers building AI for robotics and computer-using AI agents”) ^clm-e1b306f16a3105b5ec93
- Prime Intellect said in July 2026 it had passed $100 million in annualized revenue, a little over $8 million a month. (§ How big Prime Intellect is: “it had passed $100 million in annualized revenue”) ^clm-827469bb63bec0d0e1f9
- As of October 2026 Fal generated more than $800 million in annualized revenue and Together AI over $1 billion, far more than Prime Intellect. (§ How big Prime Intellect is: “Fal, at more than $800 million in annualized revenue, or Together AI, at over $1 billion”) ^clm-2eed0379742ce314e251
- Many inference providers pay a large share of revenue to cloud firms that own the servers, but Prime Intellect operates its own hardware. (§ How big Prime Intellect is: “Prime Intellect is different: it operates its own hardware”) ^clm-42cd18abc581ff14835c
- Besides compute, Prime Intellect sells post-training (reinforcement learning) software and has trained its own models and agents for coding and AI research. (§ How big Prime Intellect is: “sells software for post-training, also called reinforcement learning”) ^clm-a4c63bcae09af5652902
- Goodfire AI chief scientist Tom McGrath says mechanistic interpretability should be the first science revolutionized by AI, because AI research agents can run its experiments at scale. (§ AI Deep Dive: Goodfire's Tom McGrath: “interpretability should be the one that gets most revolutionized”) ^clm-043e1a0cafa790d0fd18
- NTT Data is in final talks to build Anthropic's first dedicated data center in Japan, an Osaka site of up to 500 megawatts that could cost up to 1 trillion yen ($6.3 billion). (§ Deals and news roundup (selected): “build Anthropic's first dedicated data center in Japan”) ^clm-29378b0bce986420c97b
- Microsoft announced the Maia 300, its latest homegrown AI chip, and plans multi-gigawatt deployments of it starting in 2027. (§ Deals and news roundup (selected): “Microsoft announced the Maia 300, its latest homegrown AI chip”) ^clm-ac04bb04cbe7a08b9a3c
- Meta unveiled two new AI models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, on October 12, 2026. (§ Deals and news roundup (selected): “Meta unveiled two new AI models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full”) ^clm-4fbbf96b00ae90868f83
- Character.AI filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy and agreed a deal in which a Walt Disney affiliate pays $20 million for a non-exclusive license to some of its IP. (§ Deals and news roundup (selected): “Character.ai filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy”) ^clm-ab6ce1cc0e0ec15c0d38
- Tencent is preparing to raise at least $4.5 billion in US dollar and offshore yuan bonds, mainly to fund AI capital spending, chiefly chip purchases. (§ Deals and news roundup (selected): “Tencent is preparing to raise at least $4.5 billion”) ^clm-959939f0117a9a6cd1cf
- JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs, leading a $5 billion loan sale for Volta Infrastructure Holdings, underwrote a $1.3 billion term loan themselves but not a separate $3.7 billion loan for GPU purchases. (§ Deals and news roundup (selected): “underwrote a $1.3 billion term loan themselves rather than a separate $3.7 billion loan”) ^clm-a6c4b6ebdc7a9f26a927
