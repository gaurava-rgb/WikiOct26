# Networking Startup Eliyan Draws Takeover Interest, Held Early Talks With Arm

Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-10-08.

## Summary

Eliyan, a five-year-old startup whose technology speeds data between chips in AI systems, received a takeover bid and will likely seek a $3 billion valuation. Arm, an existing investor, held early talks but did not bid. The interest follows rich chip exits like Nvidia's $20 billion Groq licensing deal and Cerebras' $56 billion IPO. Buyers are snapping up chip component makers, such as Marvell's purchase of Celestial AI, because slow links between chips can leave AI chips idle.

## Key claims

- Eliyan told some investors in early October 2026 that it had received a takeover bid, per people familiar with the matter. (§ The core story: “Eliyan told some investors it had received a takeover bid”) ^clm-313ab88b78ed3f688fa8
- Eliyan was valued at $1 billion in July 2026 and will likely aim for a $3 billion valuation in a sale, per some people familiar. (§ The core story: “will likely aim for a valuation of $3 billion”) ^clm-de54df45a1b2d2c6b9ed
- Arm, owned by SoftBank and an existing Eliyan investor, held early talks about buying Eliyan but did not bid and would be unlikely to at that valuation. (§ The core story: “held early talks about buying Eliyan. Arm did not bid”) ^clm-9e6e06af7fb7da6a8b2e
- Recent rich chip exits include Nvidia's $20 billion licensing deal for Groq and Cerebras' $56 billion IPO, plus Etched's $21 billion fundraise. (§ Why founders expect rich exits: “Nvidia's $20 billion licensing deal for Groq, and Cerebras' $56 billion IPO”) ^clm-61825103bab9aa622353
- Founded in 2021, Eliyan has raised more than $250 million from backers including AMD, Cisco, Meta, Seligman Ventures and Tiger Global Management. (§ The company: “Backers include large chip and tech companies such as AMD, Cisco and Meta”) ^clm-a46892086a06cd20ff8e
- Copper links that chiplets use to reach each other and high-bandwidth memory can be slow, leaving AI chips idle; Eliyan says its light-plus-electronics design fixes some of this. (§ The problem it solves: “Slow links can leave AI chips idle no matter how fast they are”) ^clm-4d3aa5be6b370a5c288e
- Eliyan CEO Ramin Farjadrad said its optical solution matches copper on reliability, power and cost but with much longer reach to connect AI racks. (§ The problem it solves: “matches copper on reliability, power and cost but with”) ^clm-fcc85dd36eb335c0b21d
- In February 2026 Marvell bought optical interconnect developer Celestial AI for up to $5.5 billion, though Celestial had no revenue at the time. (§ Other chip-component deals: “Marvell Technologies bought Celestial AI, which develops optical interconnect technology, for up to $5.5 billion”) ^clm-f1665b7fdf9a0e98d530
- In July 2026 Analog Devices bought Empower Semiconductor, which helps AI chips manage electricity more efficiently, for $1.5 billion.
