# Nvidia Has Put More Than $170 Billion on the Line for Neoclouds

Shane Burke, Phoebe Liu, The Information, published 2026-10-20.

## Summary

Nvidia has disclosed up to $164.5 billion in data center lease guarantees and commitments as of its July 2026 filings, alongside more than $7 billion in direct AI cloud investments. Its equity portfolio reached nearly $100 billion, while non-hyperscalers drove 45% ($40 billion) of quarterly data center revenue. As equity markets soured on AI cloud firms, CoreWeave, Nebius, and IREN shares tumbled, while Firmus, Lambda, SB Energy, and Nscale delayed IPOs, prompting Nvidia talks for fresh direct funding to Firmus.

## Key claims

- Nvidia disclosed up to $164.5 billion in lease guarantees and other commitments to support new data centers as of its July 2026 quarter filings. (§ Central bank for neoclouds: “disclosed up to $164.5 billion in guarantees and other commitments to support new data centers”) ^clm-b0ecd0ceea9d6dbf44f8
- Nvidia data center commitments include agreements to pay landlords if cloud tenants default on leases and promises to purchase compute capacity. (§ Central bank for neoclouds: “pay landlords if cloud tenants default on leases, as well as promises to purchase compute capacity”) ^clm-ad3ba8711b612b1e649f
- Nvidia has invested or agreed to invest more than $7 billion directly into AI cloud providers on top of its lease commitments, putting over $170 billion on the line. (§ Central bank for neoclouds: “invested or agreed to invest more than $7 billion directly into AI cloud providers”) ^clm-396b2e1c478d32bc297b
- Nvidia's total equity portfolio was valued at nearly $100 billion as of July 2026, spanning data center builders like SB Energy and AI research labs like OpenAI and Anthropic. (§ Central bank for neoclouds: “total equity portfolio was valued at nearly $100 billion as of July 2026”) ^clm-f19d056c75bf0bb36f61
- Non-hyperscaler customers, including neoclouds and large corporations, accounted for 45% of Nvidia data center revenue, or $40 billion, in the July 2026 quarter. (§ Financial risks and revenue mix: “accounted for 45% of Nvidia data center revenue in the July 2026 quarter”) ^clm-fa4d60d5f5d87a8cce3e
- Shares in publicly traded AI cloud firms CoreWeave, Nebius, and IREN dropped sharply in mid-October 2026 as public markets soured on AI cloud expansion plans. (§ Market backlash and delayed public listings: “Shares in publicly traded AI cloud firms CoreWeave, Nebius, and IREN dropped sharply”) ^clm-fdcc1bd8c82f28ae9cdd
- Nvidia-backed cloud startups Firmus and Lambda pulled or postponed planned initial public offerings in October 2026. (§ Market backlash and delayed public listings: “startups Firmus and Lambda have pulled or postponed planned initial public offerings”) ^clm-bd6f064d8d587cf99877
- Data center developer SB Energy and GPU cloud operator Nscale published IPO registration documents in September 2026 but have not proceeded with public listings. (§ Market backlash and delayed public listings: “published IPO registration documents in September 2026 but have not proceeded with listings”) ^clm-d5608103dca204d3b681
- Nvidia entered discussions to provide a fresh direct investment of hundreds of millions of dollars to Firmus after Firmus cancelled its IPO. (§ Market backlash and delayed public listings: “discussions to provide a fresh direct investment of hundreds of millions of dollars to Firmus”) ^clm-3ffd37c995c1fd48bf4f
- Nvidia Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress stated on an August 2026 earnings call that the company's financial risk from backing AI labs is limited because it can redeploy GPUs to other buyers. (§ Financial risks and revenue mix: “financial risk from backing AI labs is limited because it can redeploy graphics processing units”) ^clm-a96910b5f037c9318632
- Investor demand grew for financial protection against default on Nvidia's own corporate debt amid rising neocloud financing risks. (§ Market backlash and delayed public listings: “demand has grown for financial protection against default on Nvidia's own corporate debt”) ^clm-f008664b4ed4e83ea338
- Google, Amazon, and Microsoft accelerated investments in their own custom AI chips to replace Nvidia GPUs. (§ Financial risks and revenue mix: “accelerating investments in their own in-house custom AI chips to replace Nvidia GPUs”) ^clm-9d13622a29ce67ffb75d
