# OpenAI Eyes Acquisition That Could Boost Cybersecurity Business

Valida Pau, Amir Efrati, The Information, published 2026-10-13.

## Summary

OpenAI is in early talks to buy Trail of Bits, a 40-plus person firm that finds and fixes open-source software vulnerabilities, as it tries to build a substantial cybersecurity business. Trail of Bits already works with OpenAI on Patch the Planet, using Codex and cyber-specialized models to fix bugs in projects like Python. OpenAI and Anthropic are racing on vulnerability-finding AI, offering their strongest models to large businesses with White House coordination while neutering public versions. OpenAI also hired former Wiz executive Dali Rajic as chief revenue officer.

## Key claims

- OpenAI is in early talks to buy Trail of Bits, a firm that finds and fixes vulnerabilities in open-source software, per people familiar with the matter; talks could still fall apart. (§ The deal talks: “OpenAI is in early talks to buy Trail of Bits”) ^clm-0f8add7f85d270a8810c
- OpenAI is trying to build a substantial cybersecurity business, and The Information calls it one of OpenAI's most promising business opportunities. (§ The deal talks: “Trail of Bits staff could help with one of OpenAI's most promising business opportunities”) ^clm-90fd4fce7f7d188318e6
- The size of a Trail of Bits deal could not be learned; OpenAI has sometimes paid in the low hundreds of millions of dollars in stock for small startups, and sometimes less. (§ The deal talks: “OpenAI has sometimes paid in the low hundreds of millions of dollars in stock”) ^clm-159f9f3026b3eb645733
- In 2026 OpenAI and Anthropic have tried to one-up each other building specialized AI that finds software vulnerabilities. (§ OpenAI vs Anthropic in security AI: “tried to one-up each other building specialized AI that finds software vulnerabilities”) ^clm-2ab529b77fc739daa016
- Working with the White House, OpenAI and Anthropic let large businesses use their most advanced models to find and patch holes in their own software, while neutering those abilities in publicly released models to deter attackers. (§ OpenAI vs Anthropic in security AI: “Both have neutered those abilities in the models released to the wider public”) ^clm-ef3db10401fbfd222141
- Security software firms such as Palo Alto Networks have used OpenAI and Anthropic models to improve the products they sell to businesses. (§ OpenAI vs Anthropic in security AI: “Security software firms such as Palo Alto Networks have used OpenAI and Anthropic models”) ^clm-6d433bea06d7a7caf21a
- OpenAI recently hired Dali Rajic, a former executive at Google-owned cloud security unit Wiz, as its chief revenue officer, another sign of its cybersecurity ambitions. (§ OpenAI vs Anthropic in security AI: “it recently hired Dali Rajic, a former executive at Wiz”) ^clm-f71335275f9968681e64
- Trail of Bits, founded in 2012, provides security research and tools, helps maintain open-source projects, and has more than 40 employees, mostly security engineers. (§ About Trail of Bits: “More than 40 employees, mostly security engineers, per its website”) ^clm-4ca54b316619c7e9102a
- Trail of Bits never took typical venture funding; it got a roughly $10,000 Department of Defense grant in 2015 and a $1 million Paycheck Protection Program loan in 2020. (§ About Trail of Bits: “It never took typical venture funding”) ^clm-74679c5fea958730bd87
- In June 2026 Trail of Bits partnered with OpenAI on Patch the Planet, a program where its staff find, review and fix bugs in open-source projects such as Python using OpenAI Codex tools and cyber-specialized models. (§ Patch the Planet: “partnered with OpenAI on a software-patching program called Patch the Planet”) ^clm-cd510971d821b80412c3
- Trail of Bits CEO Dan Guido said Codex let his team ship things in a day that would otherwise have taken weeks. (§ Patch the Planet: “Codex let his team "ship things in a day" that would otherwise have taken weeks”) ^clm-f40c6d732c5661edfaaf
- Dan Guido said Trail of Bits had earned the open-source community's trust over more than a decade of work. (§ Patch the Planet: “had earned the open-source community's trust "over more than a decade of work."”) ^clm-afefb19abcbdada14649
- Hackers have already used publicly available AI in many cyberattacks worldwide, per independent security researchers. (§ AI in real attacks: “Hackers have already used publicly available AI in many cyberattacks worldwide”) ^clm-7057fbe1ea52251d2268
- CrowdStrike said the attacker behind a recent hack of South Korean financial institutions used an open-source Chinese AI agent and Chinese large language models. (§ AI in real attacks: “used an open-source Chinese AI agent and Chinese large language models”) ^clm-e3ecc69f13637708c8fc
