# Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch08 Amazon - An Empire from Cardboard Box to Kindle to Cloud

John Gallaugher, FlatWorld, published 2025.

## Summary

Gallaugher's Amazon chapter traces Amazon from a doubted, money-losing Seattle bookstore to a firm leading in e-commerce, cloud, ads, e-readers, and delivery, framed by Jeff Bezos's long-term thinking and the napkin flywheel where selection, experience, and low prices feed scale, data, and network effects. Sections 8.1 and 8.2 explain the retail engine: Kiva robot warehouses, a negative cash conversion cycle, scale-driven pricing, A/B testing, ads, the third-party marketplace and long tail, and acquisitions such as Zappos and Quidsi. Sections 8.3 to 8.5 cover how Amazon took delivery in-house from UPS, FedEx, and USPS, its mixed physical-retail bets (Whole Foods, Go, closed bookstores), and devices: the Kindle win, Alexa's losses, and the Fire Phone flop. Sections 8.6 to 8.9 cover AWS (launched 2006, most of Amazon's profit), AI and custom chips, the 2023 Anthropic investment, the September 2023 FTC antitrust suit, and Shopify as the contrasting merchant-first model.
