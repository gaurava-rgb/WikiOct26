# Anthropic Strikes $13.7 Billion Compute Deal With Trump-Linked Rum Group

Valida Pau and Phoebe Liu, The Information, published 2026-09-13.

## Summary

Anthropic signed a $13.7 billion, six-year compute deal with Rum Group, the former Rumble, which has ties to the Trump administration and became a neocloud by buying Northern Data. Anthropic would lease capacity at an unfinished Georgia site whose main asset is 120 megawatts of scarce grid power. Rum has no financing yet and plans to borrow, while Anthropic gets penny options on up to 51 million Rum shares. A $13.7 billion contract against a $2.8 billion company shows how much risk sits with small suppliers in the compute rush.

## Key claims

- Anthropic signed a computing deal worth $13.7 billion over six years with Rum Group, per a person familiar. (§ The core story: “Anthropic signed a computing deal worth $13.7 billion over six years with Rum Group”) ^clm-1ac7cf2b351d3743e981
- Rum Group has roots in social media and long-standing ties to the Trump administration. (§ The core story: “long-standing ties to the Trump administration”) ^clm-486372ef07838f7807c9
- Rum disclosed a $13.7 billion contract in an August 2026 securities filing without naming the customer. (§ The core story: “Rum had disclosed a $13.7 billion contract in an August securities filing”) ^clm-3b5698f8500e9c2388b4
- Rum Group, formerly Rumble, runs the Rumble video platform popular with conservatives and hosts Donald Trump's Truth Social. (§ Who Rum Group is: “Runs the Rumble video platform, popular with conservatives, and hosts President Donald Trump's Truth Social”) ^clm-abb8dac547de8d138c24
- In May 2021 Rumble raised $25 million from investors including Peter Thiel and J.D. Vance at a $500 million valuation. (§ Who Rum Group is: “raised $25 million from investors including Peter Thiel and J.D. Vance”) ^clm-f09ef209f59b98a1117d
- In June 2026 Rumble bought German neocloud Northern Data in an all-stock deal valued at about $767 million, then renamed itself Rum Group. (§ Who Rum Group is: “bought German neocloud Northern Data in an all-stock deal”) ^clm-eec292292016af37b560
- Northern Data brought 22,000 Nvidia Hopper GPUs and a data center site under development in Georgia. (§ Who Rum Group is: “Northern Data brought 22,000 Nvidia Hopper-generation GPUs”) ^clm-1ce032c855f98b763e45
- Anthropic would lease compute at Rum's unfinished Maysville, Georgia data center, whose key asset is 120 megawatts of scarce grid access, with potential to reach 180 megawatts. (§ The Georgia data center: “Its key asset is scarce grid access: 120 megawatts, with potential to grow to 180 MW”) ^clm-a79059ce449b7977485e
- The Georgia site could cost more than $10 billion to build and fill, based on prior reporting that one gigawatt of AI data center capacity costs about $60 billion. (§ The Georgia data center: “one gigawatt of AI data center capacity costs about $60 billion to build and power”) ^clm-cb1fe9b26c82c330a3aa
- Rum's August filing said it did not yet have financing for the building, GPUs or other equipment and expects to fund much of it with debt or equity. (§ Paying for it: “did not yet have financing for the building, GPUs or other equipment”) ^clm-f0a6ef46bbfa7eed2ae5
- As of June 2026 Rum wanted to buy $270 million of Nvidia Blackwell 300 chips for a separate contract with Together AI. (§ Paying for it: “buy $270 million of Nvidia Blackwell 300 chips”) ^clm-cc44ebe2facc79a125da
- Anthropic gets an option to buy up to 51 million Rum shares for one cent each, per the person. (§ Anthropic's stock option: “option to buy up to 51 million Rum shares for one cent each”) ^clm-929ea0afdc795ccdb196
- Rum stock closed at $7.17 on September 11, 2026, giving it a market value of about $2.8 billion on 400 million shares. (§ Anthropic's stock option: “its market cap (total value of all shares) is about $2.8 billion”) ^clm-7f1654918f5153430ab5
- Half of Anthropic's Rum options are tied to the Maysville site, and the other half unlock if Anthropic buys about 400 to 500 megawatts more at another Rum site. (§ Anthropic's stock option: “The other half unlock if Anthropic buys about 400 to 500 MW more”) ^clm-d43d070160d64138e3c4
