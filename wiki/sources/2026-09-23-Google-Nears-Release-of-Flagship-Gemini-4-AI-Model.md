# Google Nears Release of Flagship Gemini 4 AI Model

Laura Bratton, The Information, published 2026-09-23.

## Summary

Koray Kavukcuoglu, the new head of Google DeepMind, said Google is close to releasing Gemini 4, its next flagship model, and hopes to ship it well before the end of 2026. Google has fallen behind Anthropic's Mythos and OpenAI's GPT-6 models after a promised Gemini 3.5 Pro never shipped. Kavukcuoglu took over from Demis Hassabis in August, reframes the goal from AGI to trustworthy agents, and says DeepMind's model work guides the design of Google's TPU chips, which Google began selling directly this year.

## Key claims

- Google is close to releasing Gemini 4, its next flagship AI model, according to Google DeepMind head Koray Kavukcuoglu. (§ The core story: “Google is close to releasing Gemini 4, its next flagship AI model”) ^clm-d3e7a57b11f54551b4e6
- Prediction: Gemini 4 is early in post-training and Kavukcuoglu hopes to ship it much earlier than the end of 2026, releasing an early version and then iterating fast. (§ The core story: “Gemini 4 is early in post-training”) ^clm-16501dac69d5b3b11fdc
- Google engineers already use Gemini 4 internally to run Antigravity, Google's AI coding tool, while safety testing continues. (§ The core story: “Engineers already use Gemini 4 internally to run Antigravity, Google's AI coding tool”) ^clm-78be174ab0c9d9c3b801
- Google has fallen behind Anthropic and OpenAI in the model race; Anthropic's Mythos family and OpenAI's GPT-6 models are more capable than Google's best current models. (§ The core story: “Google has fallen behind Anthropic and OpenAI in the model race”) ^clm-db5b5dde3ae522f54eb5
- At Google I/O in May 2026, Sundar Pichai said Gemini 3.5 Pro would come in June, but it never shipped; Google focused on cheaper Flash models instead. (§ How Google got here: “Sundar Pichai said a major update, Gemini 3.5 Pro, would come in June”) ^clm-894de43f68de903889a8
- Anthropic launched its Mythos model family in spring 2026 and upgraded it in September; OpenAI began launching GPT-6 models in early September 2026. (§ The competition: “Anthropic launched its Mythos model family this spring and upgraded it this month”) ^clm-568c39e9ea1e2c3c2531
- Gemini 3 was such a jump that OpenAI declared a code red to catch up. (§ The competition: “Gemini 3 was such a jump that OpenAI declared a "code red" to catch up”) ^clm-ec806a6fa0cdab5e4077
- Kavukcuoglu took over Google DeepMind in August 2026, moving from CTO to senior vice president; Demis Hassabis became chairman of the unit and chief scientist of Alphabet. (§ Leadership change: “Demis Hassabis left the DeepMind CEO role to become chairman of the unit”) ^clm-54dc0e355695e20f7730
- Kavukcuoglu has no strong view on whether AGI has arrived and says the better question is whether we can build intelligent agents people can trust. (§ On AGI: “the better question is whether we can build intelligent agents people can trust”) ^clm-ef34dbf527414da4298c
- In 2026 Google began selling TPUs directly to customers, not only renting access through Google Cloud, as it competes harder with Nvidia. (§ Chips tie-in: “Google began selling TPUs directly to customers, not only renting access through Google Cloud”) ^clm-22c8490997fb7940f00b
