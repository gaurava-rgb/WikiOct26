# Applied Intuition Fields Offers at $30 Billion Valuation

Stephanie Palazzolo, The Information, published 2026-10-13.

## Summary

Applied Intuition, which sells software to build and test self-driving vehicles, has received investment offers valuing it at $30 billion or more, double its June 2026 valuation, and may raise at least $1 billion. The deal would back its bet on selling self-driving software rather than building and running cars, a contrast to Waymo. It joins a growing set of AI application startups, such as Cognition and Perplexity, worth $20 billion or more. It is also expanding into military vehicles and signed operating system deals with Honda and Nissan.
