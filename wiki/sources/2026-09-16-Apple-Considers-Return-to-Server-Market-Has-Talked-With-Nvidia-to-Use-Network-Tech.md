# Apple Considers Return to Server Market, Has Talked With Nvidia to Use Network Tech

Aaron Tilley, Qianer Liu and Phoebe Liu, The Information, published 2026-09-16.

## Summary

Apple is planning an enterprise server built on its own M8 Ultra chips, possibly linked with Nvidia's NVLink Fusion networking, aimed at AI developers, businesses and governments that want to run inference on their own machines. New CEO John Ternus has backed the project from the start, but it is not expected on sale until 2029 and could be cancelled. Demand is real: AI developers bought Mac minis and Mac Studios by the tens of thousands, causing shortages. Apple's hurdles include a failed Xserve history, no enterprise engineering team, and weak developer software next to Nvidia's CUDA.

## Key claims

- Apple is planning an enterprise server built on its own chips, possibly with Nvidia networking gear inside, for AI developers, other businesses and governments, per people familiar with the project. (§ The core story: “Apple is planning an enterprise server built on its own chips, possibly with Nvidia networking gear”) ^clm-7603fd771fc75ecedb45
- Apple is considering two server versions: a smaller one with two linked M8 Ultra chips and a bigger one with four. (§ The core story: “a smaller one with two planned M8 Ultra chips linked together, and a bigger one with four”) ^clm-1355334dc126d2a4335e
- Prediction: The Apple server is not expected on sale until 2029 and could still be cancelled or ship without Nvidia technology. (§ The core story: “Not expected on sale until 2029. It could still be cancelled”) ^clm-96d52e53c8806b0fe17b
- New CEO John Ternus, who took over in September 2026, backed the server project from its start about a year earlier when he ran hardware engineering. (§ The core story: “backed the project from its start about a year ago when he ran hardware engineering”) ^clm-f762365df078353aec8e
- Apple has looked at linking its M8 chips with NVLink Fusion, an Nvidia package of switches, chiplets and software that Nvidia has opened to other companies' chips. (§ The Nvidia piece: “Apple has looked at linking the M8 chips with **NVLink Fusion**”) ^clm-49d5c195739627120cf6
- Apple's current in-house connection technology for its servers is too slow and costly at large scale, per the people. (§ The Nvidia piece: “too slow and costly at large scale”) ^clm-c21fa59e0aa1aa76fd0e
- Networking gear can be 10% to 15% of AI data center hardware costs and was a fifth of Nvidia's data center revenue in the April quarter. (§ The Nvidia piece: “was a fifth of Nvidia's data center revenue in the April quarter”) ^clm-e0794a1a16da1267eaec
- In August 2026 Amazon's cloud unit said it will expand use of NVLink Fusion in next-generation AI servers. (§ The Nvidia piece: “Amazon's cloud unit said it will expand use of NVLink Fusion”) ^clm-43c49b2b15c0188856de
- Part of Apple's rebuilt Siri runs on Nvidia chips in Google data centers, a thaw after years of tension between Apple and Nvidia. (§ A long cold relationship, thawing: “part of Apple's rebuilt Siri runs on Nvidia chips in Google data centers”) ^clm-f4e029800b6bf76dd1a1
- Over the past year large AI labs bought tens of thousands of Mac minis and Mac Studios, causing an extreme shortage. (§ Why Apple sees demand: “Large AI labs bought tens of thousands”) ^clm-96f74139d5a6f7903ebb
- Mac was Apple's fastest-growing segment last quarter, up nearly 29% to $10.4 billion. (§ Why Apple sees demand: “up nearly 29% to $10.4 billion”) ^clm-5c62a9bbef6f5e1a0d22
- Apple already builds servers for Private Cloud Compute, its internal system for AI tasks too big for phones and Macs, and has refused partners' requests to use them. (§ Why Apple sees demand: “It has refused partners' requests to use them.”) ^clm-1aa7c176f6908df8c66d
- Apple sold Xserve servers from 2002 to 2011, and the line failed largely from Apple's indifference to enterprise and weak customer support, per former staff. (§ Hurdles: “It failed largely from Apple's indifference to enterprise and weak customer support”) ^clm-0b43158a3f190cd4be19
- Apple would need developer software like Nvidia's CUDA, but its own tool MLX has a small team and its leader Awni Hannun left for Anthropic this year. (§ Hurdles: “Its own tool, MLX, has a small team, and its leader Awni Hannun left for Anthropic”) ^clm-422bb74a6c8563615f11
- Prediction: OpenAI plans to deploy its custom chip, Jalapeno, later in 2026. (§ Hurdles: “OpenAI plans to deploy its custom chip, Jalapeno, later this year”) ^clm-d8505865972c9eef03fb
