# SpaceX Shakes Up Data Center Leadership After Aggressive Build-Out

Grace Kay, The Information, published 2026-09-01.

## Summary

Elon Musk replaced several leaders of SpaceX's AI data center team with rocket and Starlink veterans after reliability problems in Tennessee and Mississippi, where sites ran for months without backup cooling or power. The shakeup clouds the timing of new sites outside Memphis. SpaceX rents compute to Anthropic and Google, which lifted its AI revenue to $2.6 billion in Q2 2026, and it keeps the right to take that capacity back. Musk now warns that power, not chips, will be the bottleneck, with a shortfall of at least 15 gigawatts by 2027.

## Key claims

- In August 2026 Elon Musk replaced several leaders of SpaceX's data center team with people from its rocket and Starlink businesses, per people familiar. (§ The core story: “The new leaders come from SpaceX's rocket and satellite internet businesses”) ^clm-6513b1c0ec5fe25ee5e9
- The reshuffle followed civil engineering concerns and steady reliability problems at SpaceX's AI data centers in Tennessee and Mississippi, per two people. (§ The core story: “steady reliability problems at SpaceXAI's existing data centers in Tennessee and Mississippi”) ^clm-ea257d4fe0cb7415e024
- In its early rush, SpaceX's AI unit ran its data centers for months without backup cooling and backup power, raising outage risk, per four people. (§ The core story: “ran those facilities for months without backup cooling and backup power in place”) ^clm-2353284d4241ca753560
- SpaceX has pushed back several times its plan to start building data centers outside its Memphis hub, including in Texas, per two people. (§ The core story: “has pushed back, several times, its plan to start building outside its Memphis”) ^clm-81673a57054571bf1708
- Prediction: Musk warned at a G20-related gathering that a power crisis of at least 15 gigawatts will arrive by 2027, leaving many AI chips unable to switch on. (§ Musk's power warning: “Musk warned that a "crisis of power" will arrive by next year”) ^clm-93f3036188cea3c569d4
- Wesley Salandro, former SpaceX vice president of production for the Falcon and Dragon rockets, joined the data center leadership, per one person. (§ Who came in: “Wesley Salandro, former SpaceX vice president of production for the Falcon and Dragon rockets”) ^clm-f34c0e5767e19e9479ec
- Jake Palmer, who led physical infrastructure for SpaceX's AI unit after joining from Hut 8 in September 2025, left in late July 2026. (§ Who left: “Jake Palmer, who led physical infrastructure for SpaceXAI, left in late July”) ^clm-60389efc5b635481a2a5
- Earlier departures included site-build manager Brent Mayo, who left for OpenAI, and Liz Balke, who now works on Anthropic's data center infrastructure team. (§ Who left: “now works on Anthropic's data center infrastructure team”) ^clm-b7aaeee11588dbc22a63
- SpaceX pitched fast data center construction as a key edge ahead of its June 2026 IPO. (§ The business backdrop: “SpaceX pitched fast data center construction as a key edge ahead of its large IPO”) ^clm-35d396c6dd3b9dfd0e86
- SpaceX had about 1.4 gigawatts of compute capacity online as of June 2026 and expects more than 2 gigawatts by year end. (§ The business backdrop: “about 1.4 GW of compute capacity online as of June and expects more than 2 GW”) ^clm-6c9c0aa9d98088ce3270
- SpaceX signed major compute rental deals with outside firms including Anthropic and Google. (§ The business backdrop: “signed major compute rental deals with outside firms including Anthropic and Google”) ^clm-41c7b0db8d01c016032f
- Compute rentals drove SpaceX's AI revenue to $2.6 billion in Q2 2026, up from $737 million a year earlier. (§ The business backdrop: “drove AI revenue to $2.6 billion in Q2 2026, up from $737 million”) ^clm-aee8c0e35f8d0816e424
- Musk has said the rental deals are flexible and SpaceX can take capacity back later for its own AI if needed. (§ The business backdrop: “SpaceX can take capacity back later if needed”) ^clm-768408c770a237952a71
- In August 2026 SpaceX closed its $60 billion all-stock purchase of coding startup Cursor to boost sales of its own AI products. (§ The business backdrop: “closed its $60 billion all-stock purchase of coding startup Cursor”) ^clm-9d75242ee8425ae570a3
