# Meta to Launch Camera-Free Smart Glasses Amid Mounting Privacy Concerns

Jyoti Mann, The Information, published 2026-09-15.

## Summary

Meta plans to release camera-free smart glasses, code-named Luna, this fall, built around voice access to Meta AI and its Muse agent. The move widens its lineup while its camera-equipped Ray-Ban glasses face criticism after some users secretly recorded strangers. Meta also plans to show AR glasses code-named Phoenix, which slipped to 2027, with a hologram call demo at Connect. All this comes as Reality Labs lost $4.62 billion in a single quarter.

- Meta plans to release smart glasses with no camera, code-named Luna, this fall, per people familiar with the matter, to widen its lineup while camera glasses face privacy criticism. (§ The core story: “Meta plans to release smart glasses with no camera this fall”) ^clm-1b4dbcd228b9ec5d180e
