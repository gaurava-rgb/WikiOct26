# Zuckerberg’s Big Biotech Moment

Amy Dockser Marcus, The Information, published 2026-10-10.

## Summary

Biohub, the philanthropy of Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan, announced a $1.8 billion partnership with the NIH and the Department of Energy to build infrastructure for AI-powered biology, framed as a successor to the Human Genome Project. Meta, Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs agreed to add $300 million. The writer argues the event is one of several signs that big tech, not universities, now leads big science: tech CEOs fill the White House science council, Brin and Huang got National Medals of Science, and Anthropic gave $150 million in AI credits to the Genesis Mission. A book pick on Kevin Roose's The AGI Chronicles adds small naming details about Stargate and Gemini.

## Key claims

- Biohub is partnering with the NIH and the Department of Energy in a $1.8 billion investment to build technologies and scientific infrastructure for AI-powered biology, announced at an October 2026 reception. - Biohub launched its Virtual Biology Initiative in April 2026 with a $500 million commitment. (§ The Biohub reception: “Biohub launched its "Virtual Biology Initiative" in April 2026 with a $500 million commitment”) ^clm-080d3f9234dac1b1a0dd
- Mark Zuckerberg said he and Priscilla Chan started the first version of Biohub almost 10 years earlier aiming to cure and prevent all diseases by 2100, and that thanks to AI this no longer seems so crazy.
