# Inside a TPG-Backed Accounting Firm’s Plans for AI and More Acquisitions

Alex Eichenstein, The Information, published 2026-10-14.

## Summary

Smith + Howard, an accounting firm backed by private equity firm TPG, is preparing acquisitions while rolling out Anthropic's Claude to automate routine tasks. TPG argues accounting firms can retain AI cost savings as profit rather than passing them to clients, avoiding a race to the bottom because audit fees historically rose even after offshoring. TPG uses revenue per employee as its key measure of AI return on investment, while noting AI requires upfront capital and multiple years to pay off.

## Key claims

- Smith + Howard, backed by private equity firm TPG, is preparing a new round of acquisitions, including two deals expected to close by the end of 2026. (§ The core story: “Smith + Howard, an accounting firm backed by private equity giant TPG, is preparing a new round of acquisitions”) ^clm-91f5615c9b269d58da49
- Smith + Howard plans to use artificial intelligence to expand its business without hiring accountants at its previous pace. (§ The core story: “Smith + Howard hopes AI will allow it to expand without hiring accountants at the same pace as in the past.”) ^clm-a5166901496942f8b039
- Smith + Howard employs over 800 people across seven states and projects roughly $175 million in revenue in 2026, growing 10% to 15% annually excluding acquisitions. (§ Growth and acquisition targets: “Smith + Howard employs more than 800 workers across seven states and expects roughly $175 million in revenue in 2026.”) ^clm-0bf9ef721ab77cccbcd1
- Smith + Howard aims to add 20% to 25% of forward revenue through acquisitions, lifting its target purchase size to firms with $5 million to $50 million in annual revenue. (§ Growth and acquisition targets: “The firm aims to add 20% to 25% of forward revenue through acquisitions”) ^clm-1115d4ceab121ac722ae
- Smith + Howard increased its acquisition target size to companies generating between $5 million and $50 million in annual revenue, up from $2 million to $20 million previously. (§ Growth and acquisition targets: “lifting target purchase size to between $5 million and $50 million in annual revenue”) ^clm-6fa4557c5ab13167c9c7
- OpenAI launched its majority-owned Deployment Co. in May 2026 with backing from TPG and others to embed engineers inside corporate clients. (§ Passing savings versus retaining profits: “OpenAI’s majority-owned Deployment Co., which launched in May 2026 with backing from TPG.”) ^clm-45637ad326a8dd644816
- Thrive Capital created Thrive Holdings to acquire and modernize professional service firms like accountants, while General Catalyst pursued AI-focused rollups. (§ Passing savings versus retaining profits: “Thrive Capital launched Thrive Holdings to buy and modernize firms like accountants, while General Catalyst is pursuing AI rollups.”) ^clm-c4561424fdf786351c61
- TPG managing director Brian Goffman argued accounting firms retain AI cost savings rather than passing them on to customers, protecting profit margins. (§ Passing savings versus retaining profits: “Brian Goffman, managing director of AI at TPG, argued that accounting firms hold onto savings rather than passing them on”) ^clm-c6776a62b2dfa2bda168
- Audit fees in the accounting industry continued climbing even after major cost-cutting moves such as offshoring reduced labor expenses. (§ Passing savings versus retaining profits: “audit fees continued climbing after offshoring reduced labor costs.”) ^clm-bde5f610b3e4556f9233
- Private equity investors in accounting divided between those excited about AI productivity and those fearing automation would shrink billable hours and revenue. (§ Passing savings versus retaining profits: “Taylor noted private equity investors were previously divided between those excited about AI productivity and those scared it would reduce billable hours and revenue.”) ^clm-42a5e3e3836bb60b0fcf
- Smith + Howard rolled out Anthropic's Claude to all employees to experiment with automating basic tasks like industry-specific audits. (§ Companywide Claude deployment: “Smith + Howard rolled Anthropic’s Claude out to all employees to experiment and propose automation uses.”) ^clm-f207d00bd58d95d082c7
- Smith + Howard engaged external AI trainers partnering with labs like Anthropic, receiving weeks of employee training in exchange for minimum spending commitments. (§ Companywide Claude deployment: “brought in external AI trainers partnering with labs like Anthropic, providing several weeks of employee training in exchange for minimum spending commitments.”) ^clm-4930d454444507985f9e
- Accounting firms with stable client rosters but few technology capabilities trade at valuations of five to six times EBITDA. (§ Valuation multiples and revenue per employee: “Accounting firms with stable client rosters but little technology trade at low valuations of five to six times EBITDA”) ^clm-43d6594c2d3690dbb015
- Established accounting firms receiving direct private equity backing command valuations of 12 times EBITDA or higher. (§ Valuation multiples and revenue per employee: “Established accounting firms receiving direct private equity backing command valuations of 12 times EBITDA or higher.”) ^clm-677bc22303e903c4a3aa
- Goffman stated that unlike offshoring which cuts costs quickly, AI requires upfront capital investment and takes several years to pay off. (§ Valuation multiples and revenue per employee: “Unlike offshoring which delivers immediate cost savings, AI requires upfront capital spending and several years to pay off.”) ^clm-da8ad976b69231f0c725
