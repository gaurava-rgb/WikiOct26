# How Microsoft’s New Office Chief Fixed Copilot and Won Over Nadella

Aaron Holmes, The Information, published 2026-10-12.

## Summary

Charles Lamanna turned around Microsoft's Copilot, starting with a 2025 pitch to Satya Nadella to add Anthropic models and end Copilot's dependence on OpenAI alone. Nadella then unified the scattered Copilot teams under him and in October 2026 also gave him Office, making him a possible CEO successor. Copilot corporate users more than doubled in 2026 to over 30 million. Lamanna now works on margins: a model router and a shift of agent mode back to free OpenAI models temper Microsoft's growing Anthropic bill of over $2 billion a year.

## Key claims

- In summer 2025 Charles Lamanna proposed to Satya Nadella that Microsoft pay for Anthropic models to fix Copilot, which customers called buggy and weak at automating Excel and PowerPoint work. (§ The Anthropic bet: “consider paying for Anthropic models to make Copilot better”) ^clm-54e1da4c6315bfaa6136
- Microsoft commercial chief Judson Althoff was skeptical of adding Anthropic models because they cost far more than OpenAI's, which Microsoft can use at no extra cost under its partnership. (§ The Anthropic bet: “which Microsoft can use at no extra cost under its OpenAI partnership”) ^clm-042198972950ca7e393d
- Lamanna won Nadella over with demos showing Anthropic models were then better than OpenAI's at the math Excel needs and at making good-looking PowerPoints. (§ The Anthropic bet: “better at the math Excel needs and at making good-looking PowerPoints”) ^clm-2c2e4a6dd2aeabf81b98
- Lamanna argued Copilot had been built assuming one model maker, OpenAI, and that this model monogamy would bake in dependence on one model's behavior and make adding new models very hard. (§ The Anthropic bet: “assuming "model monogamy" too long would bake in dependence on one model's behavior”) ^clm-674e68ee36442ab74b53
- Microsoft Copilot's corporate users more than doubled from the start of 2026 to over 30 million by October 2026. (§ Results so far: “Copilot's corporate users have more than doubled since the start of 2026 to over 30 million”) ^clm-37a9a908376e5c604466
- In March 2026 Nadella named Lamanna executive vice president over Copilot engineering reporting directly to him, unifying the separate Copilot engineering groups; Jacob Andreou shares Copilot oversight. (§ Lamanna's growing role: “executive vice president over Copilot engineering, reporting directly to him”) ^clm-ecd00f2c82882749c5cc
- In October 2026 Nadella put Lamanna in charge of Office as well, after the July 2026 retirement of Rajesh Jha, who had run Office and Windows. (§ Lamanna's growing role: “Nadella also put him in charge of Office, after the July 2026 retirement of Rajesh Jha”) ^clm-c96f2e881844cdec6868
- Lamanna's organization, including Office, Copilot and Teams, is about 25,000 people, more than triple a year earlier; Microsoft has over 220,000 employees, with sales and Azure each over 60,000. (§ Lamanna's growing role: “His organization is about 25,000 people, more than triple what he oversaw a year earlier”) ^clm-0bad5319405b1e637d81
- Multiple senior Microsoft executives see Lamanna as a possible successor to Nadella, and some read Nadella bringing him, Andreou, Asha Sharma, Hayete Gallot and Arun Ulag to board meetings as introducing successor candidates. (§ Succession talk: “a deliberate effort to introduce the board to a range of possible successors”) ^clm-95136aa939f3370ce4fa
- Before Lamanna took over, separate Windows and Office groups built their own Copilot features and Rajesh Jha rewarded fast-adopted ones by adding them to every Copilot, which employees called a tangled mess that caused infighting. (§ Why Copilot was a mess: “Employees called it a tangled mess; some executives said it caused infighting”) ^clm-7c243aa83b678c27ffa0
- Microsoft's Office engineers built Copilot agent mode, which makes OpenAI and Anthropic models reason longer on Office tasks; Lamanna made it the default by spring 2026, and internal customer satisfaction then rose sharply. (§ Agent mode and customers: “Lamanna found it worked better for nearly every task and by spring 2026 made it the default”) ^clm-ac605a7b0280cb3b77ed
- AT&T says its staff use Copilot for most personal productivity automation and it grew its Copilot licenses over the past year to about 18,000 seats. (§ Agent mode and customers: “AT&T grew its Copilot licenses over the past year to about 18,000 seats”) ^clm-36e18358183050b7ba84
- National Life Group's CIO said Copilot was essentially useless in 2023 and much improved in spring 2026, but most of his staff use ChatGPT and will not switch back. (§ Agent mode and customers: “most of his employees use ChatGPT, and he has not switched back”) ^clm-05e8d3285703b0c3e7d8
- Over the six months to October 2026, as OpenAI models caught back up to Anthropic's, Lamanna had Copilot agent mode rely more on OpenAI models, improving margins because Microsoft runs OpenAI models for free. (§ Cost, margins and the model router: “That improved margins because Microsoft runs OpenAI models for free”) ^clm-fff4d53fdb3947acf1fe
- Microsoft engineers built a Copilot model router using a homegrown LLM that picks the cheapest model able to do a task, and the vast majority of Office Copilot users use its auto mode. (§ Cost, margins and the model router: “The vast majority of Office Copilot users use "auto" mode, letting the router choose”) ^clm-f7eb61b95b8a1e9ed002
- Microsoft's payments to Anthropic for customer Copilot use grew in 2026 and are on track to exceed $2 billion a year, though swapping in OpenAI models has slowed that growth. (§ Cost, margins and the model router: “are on track to exceed $2 billion a year”) ^clm-ee8150521a3f48dc09b6
- Lamanna has told staff Microsoft should be the Costco of AI, offering the best value at the best price to everyone rather than a high-priced boutique for top coders. (§ "The Costco of AI": “Lamanna has told staff Microsoft should be "the Costco of AI,"”) ^clm-cdf888eb53ef6b98aea5
- Lamanna led Copilot Cowork, which resembles Anthropic's Claude Cowork, and the Autopilot agent, inspired by OpenClaw, which aims to be an always-on agent using other vendors' tools like Workday. (§ New products: “He also led the Autopilot AI agent, inspired by the viral success of OpenClaw”) ^clm-a70bb2789ddc6836e406
- In early October 2026 Microsoft launched a revamped Copilot super-app combining 365 Copilot with Autopilot and Copilot Cowork, which cost extra based on usage. (§ New products: “which cost extra based on usage”) ^clm-c25fa2ccaf136fb2a7a7
