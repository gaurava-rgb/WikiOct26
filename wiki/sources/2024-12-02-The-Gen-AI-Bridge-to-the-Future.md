# The Gen AI Bridge to the Future

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, published 2024-12-02.

## Summary

Thompson lays out three computing paradigms so far (mainframe, PC, smartphone) and names wearables as the next, defined by the lack of a mechanical input. He argues the application layer on one paradigm is the bridge to the next: interactive Applications led from mainframes to PCs, and the Internet led from PCs to the iPhone. Generative AI, and in particular UI generated on demand, is the new bridge to wearables as general purpose computers, which is why Apple's app-centric Vision Pro is stuck in the old paradigm and why Meta's Orion is not shipping yet. He closes optimistic despite 2024 having no GPT-5 level model, saying the product overhang from current AI is massive.

## Key claims

- In 1945 the U.S. government built ENIAC for ballistics calculations, but its first major job was calculations behind the development of the hydrogen bomb. (“first major job was to do calculations that undergirded the development of the hydrogen bomb”) ^clm-846454f3ad1de60b8ac1
- IBM's first System/360, the Model 30, ran batch jobs; two years later IBM's Model 67 with the TSS/360 operating system let users interact with a program through a terminal, which Thompson calls a new Applications paradigm. (§ Bridges to the Future: “Two years later, however, IBM came out with the Model 67 and the TSS/360 operating system”) ^clm-f8954ce3c253b42fd38d
- The WIMP interface (windows, icons, menus, pointer) that made PCs explode in popularity was invented by Xerox PARC, commercialized by Apple, and spread by Microsoft. (§ Bridges to the Future: “which was invented by Xerox PARC, commercialized by Apple, and disseminated by Microsoft”) ^clm-fc54c07e229e0efeb5cb
- Prediction: Thompson predicts wearables (watches, earpieces, glasses) are the next computing paradigm after mainframes, PCs, and smartphones, defined by having no direct mechanical input, leaving speech, gestures, and thought. (“the absence of a direct mechanical input mechanism; that leaves speech, gestures, and at the most primitive level, thought”) ^clm-912244b7656b27805ed0
- As of 2024, Meta's Orion wristband used electromyography (EMG) to read hand gestures with no cameras at all, one sign of progress on inputs for wearables. (“wristband uses electromyography (EMG) to interpret gestures without any cameras at all”) ^clm-32ec2486de274f9582fe
- Thompson argues the application layer on one computing paradigm is the bridge to the next one, and that this is why generative AI is a big deal for realizing the future. (“the application layer on one paradigm provides the bridge to the next one”) ^clm-86db8ce41b7c8f8b0d7a
- Thompson argues interactive Applications came first on mainframes and drove demand for easier access to computers, creating the bridge from mainframes to PCs. (§ Bridges to the Future: “Applications came first: the concept created the bridge from mainframes to PCs”) ^clm-a2e066c850d3fcbf739b
- Thompson argues the Internet was the application-layer innovation of the PC era, and because it ran on every PC, linked PCs, and did not care which device reached it, it became the bridge to the smartphone. (§ Bridges to the Future: “by being agnostic to the actual device it was accessed on”) ^clm-435dae099e32166a2a96
- Thompson argues Microsoft did not miss mobile; its error was extending the PC paradigm to phones, with the wrong interface (stylus and keyboard) and the assumption that Windows' application layer would be the differentiator. (§ Bridges to the Future: “their error was in trying to extend the PC paradigm to mobile”) ^clm-e26152286c9f40ca5984
- Thompson argues Apple built a new touch operating system for the iPhone, but because iOS was based on macOS it was far more capable than BlackBerry OS or PalmOS, above all in reaching the real Internet. (§ Bridges to the Future: “because iOS was based on macOS, it was far more capable than smartphone-only alternatives”) ^clm-9e3a7596a4722239fb85
- Steve Jobs introduced the iPhone in January 2007 as three products in one: a widescreen iPod with touch controls, a revolutionary mobile phone, and a breakthrough Internet communications device. (§ Bridges to the Future: “The third is a breakthrough Internet communications device”) ^clm-d5dc197b5bc7cb285a3a
- Thompson argues wearables in 2024 are in their pre-iPhone era: Oculus is a standalone platform like a game console, while watches, AirPods, and smart glasses are phone accessories like the iPod. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “Wearables today, however, are very much in the pre-iPhone era”) ^clm-d324ab1ad7e34e4a6c05
- Apple's Vision Pro has a PC-class M2 chip, its own visionOS operating system and apps, and can also run iPad apps. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “which not only has a PC-class M2 chip, along with its own visionOS operating system and apps”) ^clm-225812a33b42ef7a2058
- Thompson argues the Vision Pro is like Windows Mobile: a capable device stuck in the previous paradigm Apple dominated, and its iPad app compatibility is a long-term limitation. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “this makes the Vision Pro akin to Microsoft Mobile: yes, it is a capable device, but”) ^clm-fb35314c94fc0a16297c
- Thompson argues apps are not the bridge from mobile to wearables, since apps are just how people reach the Internet on phones, and the Internet was the old bridge, not the new one. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “apps are just the way we access the Internet on mobile, and the Internet was the old bridge”) ^clm-6eea157b116c140f7826
- Thompson says the best thing about Meta's Orion AR glasses is that you see the real world directly, so its 70 degree field of view felt massive even though the Vision Pro offers 100 degrees. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “70 degrees seemed massive on Orion, even though that is less than the Vision Pro”) ^clm-f3c0925125f7d16ba984
- Thompson argues the least impressive part of the Orion demo was its app launcher and Instagram prototype, because it just put the mobile paradigm in front of his face. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “it was simply taking the mobile paradigm and putting it in front of my face”) ^clm-b38711c2b80a4e72fb6c
- Prediction: Thompson predicts the future interface is the exact UI you need exactly when you need it, with glasses generating UI on the fly from your request, surroundings, and state. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “the glasses are smart enough to generate UI on the fly based on the context”) ^clm-2159ca9e928e6f27d57c
- Thompson argues generative UI would also help devices that already exist; a watch app would be much more usable if it showed only the choices needed at that moment instead of acting like a small iPhone. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “A watch application, for example, would be much more usable if”) ^clm-38ebd752bdb46b5eceed
- Prediction: Thompson predicts most of the early exploration of generative UI will happen on the phone. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “that is where I expect most of the exploration around generative UI to happen for now”) ^clm-064aad0736a25858bcb1
- Prediction: Thompson predicts generative AI is building a bridge to wearables that are general purpose computers in their own right, not phone accessories, which he calls bullish for Meta. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “building a bridge to wearables that are not accessories, but general purpose computers in their own right”) ^clm-3e75258328653374f736
- Thompson argues Orion was not shipping in 2024 both because the hardware was not production-ready, especially on cost, and because the whole application layer must first be built on today's devices. (§ The Generative AI Bridge: “It also helps explain why Orion isn”) ^clm-2eb901aed17c91b0284f
- In 2024 no GPT-5 level model was released; the bigger developments were far more efficient, smaller GPT-4 level models and the inference scaling of OpenAI's o1. (§ Survey Complete: “there hasn’t been a GPT-5 level model released”) ^clm-96e16ed100214a1773c2
- At the end of 2024 concerns were rising that AI had hit a data wall and that more intelligent models would need new breakthroughs in architecture. (§ Survey Complete: “Concerns are rising that we may have hit a data wall”) ^clm-9d165ea95f71f01f876f
- Thompson argues the product overhang from 2024's generative AI capabilities is massive, with new application-layer paradigms at the top of the list of things to build. (§ Survey Complete: “The product overhang from the generative AI capabilities we have today are absolutely massive”) ^clm-18f24ec5d454e22fc214
