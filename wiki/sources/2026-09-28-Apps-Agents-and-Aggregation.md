# Apps, Agents, and Aggregation

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, published 2026-09-28.

## Summary

Thompson argues that AI agents, which are AI with a computer of their own, will make most apps and enterprise screens unneeded. Meta's Muse gives every US user a small cloud computer and can build a custom app on request, as it did for his saved Instagram recipes. When doing things becomes easy, the scarce thing is what people want to do, so the company that owns the agent becomes the ultimate Aggregator. Meta (through Muse) and Microsoft (through the new Copilot) lead because they already reach nearly every person and every worker.

## Key claims

- Thompson published 'Messaging was Mobile's Killer App' on February 18, 2014, the day before Facebook agreed to buy WhatsApp. (§ Messaging and Jobs to Be Done: “the day before Facebook acquired WhatsApp”) ^clm-2227383f9382b38fe29a
- In 2013 Facebook launched Facebook Home for Android, and Mark Zuckerberg said apps are not the center of the world, people are. (§ Messaging and Jobs to Be Done: “a year earlier the company had launched its own interface for Android called Facebook Home”) ^clm-e96922c827890cae1d2f
- Thompson's phone held 151 apps in 2013 and holds 689 today, though he rarely opens most of them. (§ Messaging and Jobs to Be Done: “Those screenshots show 151 apps; today my phone has 689”) ^clm-ddbacf136e00ae05207f
- Thompson says his core set of apps has stayed stable: messaging such as WhatsApp, social media mainly X, and now ChatGPT and Claude. (§ Messaging and Jobs to Be Done: “messaging apps, including WhatsApp, social media, primarily X, and now ChatGPT and Claude”) ^clm-c7a816bef5bf8ac9be24
- Thompson argues that an agent is not just AI but an AI that has access to a computer, using deterministic computers to do computer things. (§ A Computer for AI: “they are not just AI: they are an AI that has access to a computer”) ^clm-a3b4b0b0a7f1cef23818
- Thompson argues that to give agents to people who cannot set up a computer, a company must supply both the agent and a computer for it. (§ A Computer for AI: “you have to give people not just an agent but also a computer for their agent”) ^clm-b8f7030b0485c4d048a9
- With Muse, Meta gives every US user a cloud virtual machine with a 2-core processor, 8GB of RAM, and 8GB of storage. (§ A Computer for AI: “a virtual machine with a 2-core processor, 8GB of RAM, and 8GB of storage”) ^clm-4a91c856fc4e7e3c98dc
- Muse built Thompson a custom Recipe Box app from his saved Instagram posts in 5 minutes, while he walked his dog. (§ A Computer for AI: “It took 5 minutes, and the entire conversation took place while I was walking my dog”) ^clm-5a2239c06b60c3ef5640
- Thompson says command-line apps have long been usable by agents, every app with a user interface became usable starting with GPT-5.6 Sol, and Astra made it fast. (§ A Computer for AI: “starting with GPT-5.6 Sol, every app with a user interface was usable too, albeit slowly”) ^clm-e71baadda821f44a82a0
- Thompson argues an agent can create any app on command, even one just for one user, which makes custom UI effectively disposable because it is infinite. (§ From Discovery to Inspiration: “It's effectively disposable, because it's infinite”) ^clm-c796af2fe89fb68ad52e
- Thompson argues that at Meta Connect, Meta's glasses and other devices are best seen as delivery mechanisms for Muse, not as AR, VR, or AI glasses. (§ From Discovery to Inspiration: “Meta's devices are not AR or VR devices, or even AI glasses: they are Muse delivery mechanisms”) ^clm-4ddb49478dc5cc759863
- Thompson argues agents make doing things abundant, so the scarce thing becomes volition, and the problem shifts from discovery to inspiration. (§ From Discovery to Inspiration: “the ability to do stuff is becoming abundant; what is scarce is volition”) ^clm-c5940ff50d3e6b985175
- Thompson argues that apps and services become mere suppliers to the agent, which becomes the ultimate gatekeeper of user demand and desire. (§ From Discovery to Inspiration: “the Agent, the ultimate gatekeeper of not just user demand, but desire”) ^clm-ee82ed5b824e7f9ccfd9
- Microsoft unveiled a redesigned Copilot super app that bundles chat, coding, and agents in one interface. (§ A Platform for Aggregation: “bundles three AI capabilities into a single interface of chat, coding, and agents”) ^clm-5e28e0d914d9c1ba4009
- Microsoft's Autopilot, previously called Scout, is an enterprise agent with its own cloud computer instance that keeps working while the user sleeps. (§ A Platform for Aggregation: “Autopilot has its own cloud computer instance that can be tasked to do things”) ^clm-1ef6e4f30cf982e17d5f
- Thompson argues the new Copilot continues Microsoft's long strategy of being the OS for work, as Teams was its OS for SaaS. (§ A Platform for Aggregation: “the best way to understand Teams was as Microsoft's OS for SaaS”) ^clm-408de1a974e93bfc2811
- Thompson argues Muse and Copilot bid to be both a platform like Windows and an Aggregator like Facebook, the ultimate prize in tech. (§ A Platform for Aggregation: “not just a platform like Windows, or an Aggregator like Facebook”) ^clm-0ffaa8015e93c8705628
- Prediction: Thompson predicts that people will eventually go straight to the job to be done through agents, and using apps will seem odd. (§ The Distributors: “once people realize they can get straight to the job to be done”) ^clm-d23c5703c873992707a1
- Prediction: Thompson predicts computer use will mean humans no longer touch enterprise UI, even as Salesforce plans a three-digit premium to make its product a Claude plugin. (§ The Distributors: “Salesforce is going to try and charge a three-digit premium to make their flagship product a Claude plugin”) ^clm-3d9bc71b823bc877549d
- Thompson argues models are fairly substitutable but agents get stickier with more context and access, so most people and companies will have only one agent. (§ The Distributors: “most people and companies will only have one agent, not multiple”) ^clm-a8aa51af2db45b5fb490
- Thompson argues Meta and Microsoft lead the agent race because they have distribution: Meta reaches nearly every person and Microsoft nearly every employee. (§ The Distributors: “Meta reaches nearly every person on earth; Microsoft reaches nearly every employee”) ^clm-0b38f7923f228065cc49
