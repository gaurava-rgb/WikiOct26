# How Anthropic Clinched $517 Billion in Compute Deals in 11 Months

Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-09-06.

## Summary

The Information estimates that since October 2025 Anthropic has signed at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute worth up to $517 billion, driven by surprise demand for Claude Code and Cowork. Its suppliers now span Amazon, Google, Microsoft, SpaceX and neoclouds like Lambda and Nscale, plus its own Fluidstack-built data centers and chip purchases from Google and AMD. Even so, its planned capacity is far smaller than OpenAI's 30 gigawatt goal, though Anthropic's revenue run rate is higher. Much of the capacity may not arrive because of local pushback, shortages and contracts that can be cancelled on 90 days' notice.

## Key claims

- Since October 2025 Anthropic has agreed to at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute it could use over several years, up from 1 to 2 gigawatts before, per The Information's estimate. (§ The core story: “Anthropic has agreed to at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute”) ^clm-537cafc6f534ef0d5bb6
- The total potential cost of Anthropic's compute deals is up to $517 billion, mostly over the next decade. (§ The core story: “Total potential cost: up to $517 billion, mostly over the next decade”) ^clm-93922d4e31933fe411da
- The $517 billion is far above the $180 billion Anthropic told investors in December 2025 it would spend renting servers through 2029. (§ The core story: “far above the $180 billion it told investors in December 2025”) ^clm-71e0ff2c405c97388615
- Anthropic rushed to sign compute deals to keep up with surprise demand for Claude Code and Cowork. (§ The core story: “to keep up with demand for Claude Code and Cowork”) ^clm-97efca683d49ae8bd454
- Anthropic's annualized revenue passed $65 billion per Bloomberg, above OpenAI's more than $40 billion as of July 2026, yet its planned capacity is much smaller. (§ Versus OpenAI: “passed $65 billion per Bloomberg, above OpenAI's more than $40 billion as of July”) ^clm-c7e4c1cae1b5d6b987b7
- OpenAI told investors in April 2026 it plans 30 gigawatts by 2030 and in August projected about $750 billion of compute spending through 2030, up from $665 billion. (§ Versus OpenAI: “OpenAI told investors in April it plans 30 GW by 2030”) ^clm-ed1c8293f59f2acf27d3
- Amazon and Google remain Anthropic's biggest suppliers, with 11 gigawatts costing more than $300 billion over about 10 years. (§ Who supplies Anthropic: “11 GW costing more than $300 billion over about 10 years”) ^clm-452b64e03dbe4f90dba5
- In November 2025 Anthropic agreed to spend at least $30 billion for about 1 gigawatt of Azure servers running Nvidia chips, notable since Microsoft is OpenAI's longtime backer. (§ Who supplies Anthropic: “at least $30 billion for roughly 1 GW of Azure servers running Nvidia chips”) ^clm-ea6d720b817ab8b50804
- In May 2026 Anthropic agreed to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion a month through May 2029 for AI servers, up to $45 billion total. (§ Who supplies Anthropic: “$1.25 billion a month through May 2029 for AI servers”) ^clm-ac0875e378a4e722820e
- In August 2026 Anthropic signed $80 billion of contracts with neoclouds Lambda and Nscale for at least 460 megawatts over six years. (§ Who supplies Anthropic: “$80 billion of contracts with Lambda and U.K.-based Nscale”) ^clm-e2fce712bd2ea3879f1f
- In November 2025 Anthropic planned to invest $50 billion with Fluidstack to build its own data centers in Texas and New York. (§ Leasing and building its own: “invest $50 billion with cloud startup Fluidstack to build its own data centers”) ^clm-a7a6a9ad3f27bb055ef9
- Anthropic plans to buy multiple gigawatts of Google TPUs, designed with Broadcom (agreed April 2026), and 2 gigawatts of AMD chips (agreed July 2026). (§ Leasing and building its own: “multiple gigawatts of Google TPUs”) ^clm-86766bdb69f28c5ac2f4
- Signed capacity may not show up because towns push back over noise and energy strain and there are shortages of components and grid connections. (§ Why the capacity may not show up: “Towns push back over noise and energy strain”) ^clm-10d1cdfb48e1874ef233
- Compute contracts can often be torn up: in the SpaceX deal, either side can cancel with 90 days' notice. (§ Why the capacity may not show up: “either side can cancel with 90 days' notice”) ^clm-6d6ee9aa61174afda74a
- Anthropic CFO Krishna Rao has made compute deals a priority. (§ Who supplies Anthropic: “CFO Krishna Rao has made compute deals a priority”) ^clm-e9621f91279f6bdcd61a
