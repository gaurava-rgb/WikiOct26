# Meta Reaches AI’s Crowded Frontier—Will Users Care?

Martin Peers, The Information, published 2026-10-12.

## Summary

Meta unveiled Muse Wonder, a model it says delivers state of the art performance on certain tasks, after a 14-month hiring binge to fix its AI slump. Martin Peers argues the claim is lost among identical frontier claims from Google, OpenAI and Anthropic, while buyers shift to cheaper good enough models: only 14% of OpenRouter usage ran through the top 10 models in Q3 2026, down from 55% in Q1 2025. He argues consumers using the Muse agent do not need a frontier model, so Muse Wonder may mostly raise Meta's costs. The column also covers Bumble's talks to go private, Meta's ban on ByteDance ads, and Microsoft's Maia 300 chip.
