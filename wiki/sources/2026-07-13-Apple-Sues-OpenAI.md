# Apple Sues OpenAI, Apple’s Real Problem

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, published 2026-07-13.

## Summary

Apple sued OpenAI and two former Apple workers, saying they stole trade secrets to help OpenAI build devices. Thompson thinks one of them, Chang Liu, is clearly guilty, but that Apple is using his case to dig into all of OpenAI's hardware work. He argues Apple's real anger is about betrayal: Jony Ive and Tang Tan left, and about 400 Apple workers followed them to OpenAI. Beneath that, he says, Apple hates what AI has done to its once safe business, and the suit feels like lashing out.

## Key claims

- Apple sued OpenAI in federal court in the Northern District of California, alleging OpenAI stole trade secrets to build competing devices. A trade secret is private business know-how a company keeps hidden to stay ahead. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “Apple sued OpenAI and one of its top executives Friday alleging the AI company stole trade secrets”) ^clm-1e8466e9b9970bcc13bc
- The suit names OpenAI chief hardware officer Tang Tan and technical staff member Chang Liu, both former Apple employees. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “accuses OpenAI’s chief hardware officer, Tang Tan, and Chang Liu, a member of its technical staff”) ^clm-f0acbbea144c2386b276
- In 2024 Apple and OpenAI signed a deal to have ChatGPT work with Siri, but Apple's new Siri AI, announced in June 2026, runs on Google's Gemini. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “Apple’s new Siri AI, announced in June, is powered by Google’s Gemini technology”) ^clm-e56a9d8a81f2691a1199
- Tang Tan leads OpenAI's device effort, which is meant to let OpenAI reach consumers directly instead of through other companies' devices like the iPhone. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “Tan is leading OpenAI’s efforts to develop its own devices, a crucial strategic effort”) ^clm-406d6e9d367db691e9bf
- OpenAI said it has no interest in other companies' trade secrets. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “We have no interest in other companies’ trade secrets”) ^clm-83a849c4f486031fcd00
- Thompson says Chang Liu is almost certainly guilty: he downloaded dozens of Apple hardware files on his Apple-issued laptop and kept logging onto Apple servers for weeks after he left. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “logged onto Apple’s servers for weeks after he had already left the company”) ^clm-54034be24a95824bbbb5
- Thompson argues Apple's security, as the case shows it, was shockingly poor: Apple did not notice Liu kept a laptop, and a claimed bug let him reach internal servers for weeks. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “Apple’s security, as depicted in this case, is shockingly poor”) ^clm-faaae6b902818d9edf13
- Thompson argues Liu is not the point; he is a fishing license for Apple to use discovery (the court process that forces the other side to hand over documents) to see everything OpenAI is doing. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “What he is, from Apple’s perspective, is a fishing license”) ^clm-c636a1f7ef29e86a9a58
- Apple's filing claims OpenAI's young hardware business rests on stolen trade secrets and is rotten to its core. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “OpenAI’s nascent hardware business now rests on the shakiest of foundations, rotten to its core”) ^clm-aa7d6b2f09c6d057c62d
- Thompson argues nearly every allegation against Tang Tan could be restated benignly, since job interviews naturally touch on past work and Chinese suppliers already reuse what Apple taught them. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “almost every allegation Apple makes against him could easily be restated far more benignly”) ^clm-bb27d0a3820f1a16f258
- Apple says over 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI; Thompson says Tan is the point person for that hiring. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “With over four hundred former Apple employees now working at OpenAI”) ^clm-3103c0e4712bebd50b35
- Thompson argues Apple's trade secret claims are impossibly broad, treating any ex-Apple employee at OpenAI as a walking trade secret; defining what counts would be a major fight at any trial. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “Apple’s trade secret claims in this case are impossibly broad”) ^clm-49fd9a42a319e56b4794
- Thompson argues Apple feels betrayed by Jony Ive, who framed his OpenAI partnership as undoing the harms of the smartphone; since Apple cannot attack Ive directly, Tan bears the brunt. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “Apple can’t bring itself to attack Ive directly, which leaves Tan to bear the brunt”) ^clm-02bf8d0b305d751dc88c
- Thompson says Apple has never before faced a mass exodus of its best hardware and operations people to a competitor; Meta headsets and Google phones did not cause one. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “Apple hasn’t really had competition for its best hardware and operations people until now”) ^clm-143ae0d855fe7901bdef
- Thompson argues that in 2022 Apple had it all figured out: a lock on the premium smartphone market plus Services revenue from every iPhone user. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “Go back to 2022 and Apple had it all figured out”) ^clm-521e0703c6084cd9bdd5
- Thompson lists what AI cost Apple: an embarrassing 2024 new Siri launch, an overhaul of its software and services teams, and loss of its spot as TSMC's most favored customer. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “lost their position as kings of the supply chain and most favored customer of TSMC”) ^clm-b4c18d6d3eaf8ef28252
- Thompson says Apple had to make an emergency mid-cycle price raise after losing supply chain power in the AI era. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “to the point where they had to enact an emergency mid-cycle price raise”) ^clm-7d1cf43a2c1676491695
- Thompson argues Apple must now wrestle with the chance that AI is a paradigm shift that could threaten the iPhone. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “AI is such a paradigm shift that it actually might threaten the iPhone”) ^clm-93ee3cb7c3668774a9fd
- Thompson argues that even if Apple is not doomed and the relaunched Siri works, its position is more fragile than before. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “their position is still more fragile than it was, and everything about life is worse”) ^clm-7c53036debcc0ed8a455
- Thompson argues the lawsuit is Apple using a dumb employee's theft as a cudgel for revenge on the company that stands for everything making its life miserable. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “use it as a cudgel to exact revenge on the company that represents everything”) ^clm-1dfedabe48f5eaa1ef27
- Thompson argues Apple's AI contribution is two-year-old technology set apart only by exclusive access to data it will not share, and that it has no interest in a new AI paradigm that endangers the iPhone. (§ Apple’s Real Problem: “Apple’s contribution is to deliver two year-old technology differentiated by exclusive access to data”) ^clm-2907b4cf4584bd46a152
- Thompson says that at Apple it has long been known the company could and would go through everything on company hardware if you crossed it. (§ Apple Sues OpenAI: “the company could and would go through everything if you ever crossed them”) ^clm-ac424e28fb70daf62019
