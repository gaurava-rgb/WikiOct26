# What Amazon and Microsoft Will Win or Lose From OpenAI's New Deals

Aaron Holmes, Catherine Perloff, Stephanie Palazzolo, Anissa Gardizy, Valida Pau and Shane Burke, The Information, published 2026-02-27.

## Summary

OpenAI's February 2026 funding round values it at $730 billion before new money and brings in Amazon ($15 billion now, $35 billion later), Nvidia ($30 billion) and SoftBank. Amazon gains the most: a bigger $138 billion AWS deal, a 2 gigawatt Trainium commitment, sole outside distribution of OpenAI's Frontier agent tool and custom models for its apps. Microsoft loses Azure's exclusivity but keeps exclusive hosting of stateless OpenAI models. The Amazon terms hint at an OpenAI IPO as soon as Q4 2026, though bankers think 2027 is more likely.

## Key claims

- On February 27, 2026 OpenAI announced a wide partnership with Amazon as part of a funding round that values OpenAI at $730 billion before the new money. (§ The core story: “The round values OpenAI at $730 billion before the new money”) ^clm-4953f4a0964111c02810
- Amazon invests $15 billion in OpenAI up front, due by end of March, and commits another $35 billion when OpenAI goes public or hits unspecified milestones. (§ Amazon: gains the most, pays the most: “Amazon invests $15 billion up front, due by end of March”) ^clm-46639ec9d714809ecaf3
- OpenAI's cloud deal with AWS, announced in fall 2025 at $38 billion over seven years, grows to $138 billion over eight years. (§ Amazon: gains the most, pays the most: “grows to $138 billion over eight years”) ^clm-653b43cca4eda2eff26a
- OpenAI commits to Amazon's Trainium3 and Trainium4 AI chips, about 2 gigawatts of power, when it had about 2 gigawatts total across all cloud providers the year before. (§ Amazon: gains the most, pays the most: “That commitment uses about 2 gigawatts of power”) ^clm-5926571819a7fcb8648f
- Amazon becomes the only outside provider of OpenAI's Frontier agent management tool, and the two will jointly build customized models for Amazon's customer apps. (§ Amazon: gains the most, pays the most: “Amazon becomes the only outside provider of OpenAI's Frontier AI agent management tool”) ^clm-27c0a9808f5f3bc50677
- Amazon relies on Anthropic models for some products, but Anthropic limits how much customers like Amazon can customize them; OpenAI models could power Rufus or Alexa. (§ Amazon: gains the most, pays the most: “Anthropic limits how much customers like Amazon can customize them”) ^clm-a1eaf1fae864edfe3440
- Amazon raised its capital spending by 50% to $200 billion for 2026, mostly for AWS capacity. (§ Amazon: gains the most, pays the most: “by 50% to $200 billion this year, mostly for AWS capacity”) ^clm-4491144b7f4520f9c8ed
- Prediction: OpenAI aims to be ready for an IPO by Q4 2026, per a person with knowledge, but bankers and lawyers who talk with its executives say 2027 is more likely. (§ OpenAI: an IPO may be closer: “only early steps have been discussed, making 2027 more likely”) ^clm-e113eafb9b62a8942ed7
- SoftBank will invest $30 billion in three installments, bringing its total OpenAI investment to $64.6 billion and about a 13% stake. (§ SoftBank: “bringing its total to $64.6 billion and about a 13% stake”) ^clm-358a6a62af5dfdd3231e
- Microsoft loses Azure's OpenAI exclusivity but keeps exclusive rights to host stateless versions of OpenAI models, so some OpenAI agents on AWS still earn Microsoft revenue. (§ Microsoft: keeps the money, loses exclusivity: “Microsoft still has exclusive rights to host stateless”) ^clm-d2ed4708f3c75b1ffc05
- Microsoft will likely still capture most revenue from developers using OpenAI models, a business worth several billion dollars a year, per prior reporting. (§ Microsoft: keeps the money, loses exclusivity: “a business worth several billion dollars a year”) ^clm-34d4c30cfcd64982c85f
- Nvidia's $30 billion investment appears to replace an earlier, never-finalized plan to invest $100 billion tied to OpenAI building 10 gigawatts of Nvidia data centers. (§ Nvidia: “Nvidia's $30 billion appears to replace an earlier plan to invest $100 billion”) ^clm-e098cd2e006eb74dd212
- OpenAI will use an extra 5 gigawatts of Nvidia Vera Rubin chips: 3 gigawatts for inference and 2 gigawatts for training. (§ Nvidia: “OpenAI will use an extra 5 GW of Nvidia's Vera Rubin chips”) ^clm-1e5d1b1096724f953371
- Nvidia CFO Colette Kress said in February 2026 that nearly 9 gigawatts of Blackwell chips have been deployed. (§ Nvidia: “nearly 9 GW of Blackwell chips have been deployed”) ^clm-438227a4ff5f700c307d
- Sam Altman told CNBC that OpenAI sees AGI as near-term and is no longer signing deals that end when AGI arrives. (§ OpenAI: an IPO may be closer: “is no longer signing deals that end when AGI arrives”) ^clm-e73b910cdf96987fd005
