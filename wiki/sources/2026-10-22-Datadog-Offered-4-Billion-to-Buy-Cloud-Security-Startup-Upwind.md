# Datadog Offered $4 Billion to Buy Cloud Security Startup ‘Upwind’

Aaron Holmes, Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-10-22.

## Summary

Observability provider Datadog offered nearly $4 billion to acquire Israeli cloud cybersecurity startup Upwind Security, representing roughly 80 times ARR. Upwind has resisted acquisition offers as it finalizes a $300 million funding round at a $3.8 billion valuation. The startup's ARR grew from $20 million at the end of 2025 to over $50 million, fueled by surging enterprise demand for tools that monitor autonomous AI agents accessing cloud systems.

## Key claims

- Datadog made an offer to acquire Israeli cloud cybersecurity startup Upwind Security for nearly $4 billion. (§ Datadog's buyout offer: “offer to acquire Israeli cloud cybersecurity startup Upwind Security for nearly $4 billion”) ^clm-e1ab51e3f047ce7a442a
- Datadog carries a market capitalization of $90 billion and sells observability software that tracks applications across corporate cloud infrastructure. (§ Datadog's buyout offer: “carries a market capitalization of $90 billion and sells observability software that tracks applications”) ^clm-531cfc65dd09e2935292
- Datadog previously attempted to acquire Upwind Security for roughly $1 billion in 2025 before talks ended. (§ Datadog's buyout offer: “attempted to acquire Upwind for roughly $1 billion in 2025”) ^clm-957f79973baf2d1c97e0
- Upwind Security is finalizing a $300 million funding round at a private valuation of roughly $3.8 billion, fielding multiple takeover bids without accepting an offer. (§ Upwind's growth and fundraising: “finalizing a $300 million funding round at a private valuation of roughly $3.8 billion”) ^clm-5e09a93c2de42cea4c91
- Upwind Security develops cybersecurity software to monitor cloud workloads and AI agents, automatically detecting and responding to breaches. (§ Upwind's growth and fundraising: “develops cybersecurity software designed to monitor cloud-hosted workloads, specifically tracking AI agents”) ^clm-78c436ec3ecd02b62b6c
- Enterprise customers using Upwind Security software include streaming provider Roku and Brazilian digital bank Nubank. (§ Upwind's growth and fundraising: “using Upwind software include streaming provider Roku and Brazilian digital bank Nubank”) ^clm-042a974ec9f24466f506
- Upwind Security's annual recurring revenue crossed $50 million, up from about $20 million in ARR at the end of 2025. (§ Upwind's growth and fundraising: “annual recurring revenue recently crossed $50 million (approximately $4 million monthly), up from about $20 million”) ^clm-4365a610f6158247fee9
- Datadog's nearly $4 billion buyout offer valued Upwind Security at approximately 80 times annual recurring revenue. (§ Upwind's growth and fundraising: “value Upwind at approximately 80 times ARR, exceeding the revenue multiple Alphabet paid for Wiz”) ^clm-28fe08f7213055faa5c1
- Upwind Security was founded in 2022 by Chief Executive Amiram Shachar and raised a $250 million round in January 2026 at a $1.5 billion valuation. (§ Upwind's growth and fundraising: “founded in 2022 by Chief Executive Amiram Shachar and had previously raised more than $400 million”) ^clm-cd9a447371b9e31cfe38
- The proliferation of AI agents independently accessing corporate databases and cloud applications has driven rapid demand for runtime agent monitoring tools. (§ Industry consolidation: “proliferation of AI agents that independently access corporate databases and cloud applications has driven rapid demand”) ^clm-d983eb078bb48f097f3f
