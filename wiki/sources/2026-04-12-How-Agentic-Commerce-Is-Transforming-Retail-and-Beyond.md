# How Agentic Commerce Is Transforming Retail and Beyond

Dolapo Amusan, commercetools, published 2026-04-12.

## Summary

A vendor blog post from commercetools, a commerce software company, that lists eight retail uses for AI shopping agents: discovery, checkout, merchandising, inventory and pricing, loyalty, in-store help, and post-purchase service. It cites surveys showing shoppers are ready to use agents and points to ChatGPT shopping via OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol, built with Stripe and commercetools. It argues that brand loyalty will matter less to agents than clean, machine-readable product data, clear prices, and encoded trust signals. It names the risks for retailers (being bypassed by AI platforms, dependence on them for traffic) and advises agent-ready data, owned brand agents, and a modular API-first setup. The piece is marketing for the author's own products, so its claims favor that view.

## Key claims

- The post describes agentic commerce as AI agents that research, pick a bundle, apply promotions, complete checkout, and trigger follow-ups on a shopper's behalf, learning from each outcome. (§ A quick primer on agentic commerce: “completes checkout (and even starts a subscription, if needed) and triggers follow-ups”) ^clm-e79ad2812526db2fefaa
- Citing McKinsey, the post says 44% of users who have tried AI-powered search call it their primary and preferred source for internet searching. (§ A quick primer on agentic commerce: “who have tried AI-powered search say that it has become their”) ^clm-86d11cc1a68fc35f0ede
- Prediction: Citing a MarTech survey, the post says 60% of shoppers expect to use AI agents within the next 12 months and 73% are familiar with AI tools. (§ A quick primer on agentic commerce: “expect to use AI agents within the next 12 months and 73% are familiar with AI tools”) ^clm-d5855e6e12098fff4970
- The post argues retailers should use agentic AI in their own sites and apps too, quoting Forrester that guided selling with generative AI is the future of owned digital shopping. (§ A quick primer on agentic commerce: “are the future of digital shopping in owned environments”) ^clm-923fbe17f484c57a2a1a
- The post argues agentic product discovery replaces a generic search results page with the single best product or a curated bundle, combining search, personalization, recommendations, and bundling. (§ 1\. Agentic commerce for product discovery: “proactively* surfaces the single best product or curated bundle instead of a generic search results page”) ^clm-a6ee7542ec04e3d3ca86
- The post says rich, machine-readable product data is crucial for retailers to benefit from agent-driven discovery and bundling. (§ 1\. Agentic commerce for product discovery: “enhancing product data and making it machine-readable are crucial”) ^clm-5f338d76d6f1dcc832be
- As of April 2026 the post says consumers are already buying directly inside ChatGPT, made real by OpenAI's Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP), launched with Stripe and commercetools. (§ 1\. - UK retailer Frasers Group and Mexican department store chain Liverpool use commercetools AI Hub and Agent Gateway so shoppers can find and buy their products inside ChatGPT. (§ 1\. Agentic commerce for product discovery: “to enable shoppers to discover and purchase products directly within ChatGPT”) ^clm-81b45b7fee8423969865
- Visa is preparing a Trusted Agent Protocol to help retailers tell AI shopping agents apart from malicious bots. (§ 2\. Agentic commerce for frictionless checkout: “helps retailers distinguish between AI shopping agents and malicious bots”) ^clm-bd93fb51fe3fecd633d8
- The post says agents can handle checkout for repeat consumables, noticing low supply and reordering by push confirmation or auto-checkout under pre-approved rules. (§ 2\. Agentic commerce for frictionless checkout: “auto-checkout under pre-approved rules”) ^clm-0e805d61b60c6079b2f9
- The post says agents can recommend dynamic markdowns to clear seasonal stock while keeping margin, with a human able to override for brand or legal limits. (§ 4\. - commercetools CMO Jen Jones argues emotional brand resonance will not be enough to win placement, because an agent returns a ranked list built on data clarity, not a brand story. (§ 5\. Agentic commerce for loyalty & lifecycle automation: “It’s returning a ranked list built on data clarity”) ^clm-4e821a050dbf8020638d
- The post argues loyalty in an agentic world is won by structured product data, transparent pricing and stock, and algorithmic trust signals such as return policy and ratings encoded for machines. (§ 5\. Agentic commerce for loyalty & lifecycle automation: “Algorithmic trust signals. Return policy, ratings, brand integrity”) ^clm-34c64f73e5d63df76da7
- The post argues agentic AI needs real-time, reliable data first, which unified commerce supplies by connecting fragmented systems into one view. (§ 7\. - Prediction: The post predicts agentic commerce will spread beyond retail wherever transactions are repetitive and data-rich, naming CPG, fashion, electronics, groceries, B2B, pharmacy, and travel. (§ What industries are likely to adopt agentic commerce next?: “wherever transactions are repetitive, data-rich and benefit from intelligent automation”) ^clm-d0e9f47b121203f33f46

(§ What industries are likely to adopt agentic commerce next?: “70% of consumers are willing to trust agents to find lower-cost alternatives”) ^clm-76d6015d8c3603e682e4
