# OpenAI, Anthropic Should Cap Compute for R&D, Say AI Safety Advocates

Tiffany Li, The Information, published 2026-10-21.

## Summary

AI safety researchers led by the AI Futures Project are advocating for strict compute caps on frontier AI research and development, proposing that labs dedicate 70 percent of compute to inference, 25 percent to safety, and only 5 percent to R&D. While OpenAI redirected 10 percent of total compute to alignment and Anthropic committed 6 percent of R&D compute to safety, labs heavily resist hard caps because raw compute drives model capabilities and competitive hiring. In parallel disclosures, Anthropic projected up to $750 billion in future compute spending, while SpaceX logged $14.6 billion in Q3 revenue.

## Key claims

- Safety advocates are pushing AI laboratories to impose binding caps on compute allocated to research and development to slow capability progress. (§ The proposal to cap research compute: “AI safety advocates and researchers are urging leading AI labs to place explicit caps on the proportion of compute dedicated to research and development”) ^clm-ad4790784e595130470f
- The AI Futures Project published a policy proposal titled How to Pace The US Frontier, advocating input-based compute rationing on frontier developers. (§ The proposal to cap research compute: “AI Futures Project, an AI forecasting nonprofit, published a framework titled "How to Pace The US Frontier"”) ^clm-764a707aefe27f27661f
- The AI Futures Project framework proposes allocating 70 percent of lab compute to commercial inference, 25 percent to safety and alignment, and restricting R&D compute to 5 percent. (§ The proposal to cap research compute: “suggests allocating 70 percent of compute to customer inference, 25 percent to alignment and safety monitoring, and capping research and development at just 5 percent.”) ^clm-7c2372088ce5b8e457e0
- Advocates argue compute allocation caps are superior to capability benchmarks because hardware compute distribution is verifiable and resistant to manipulation. (§ The proposal to cap research compute: “Advocates argue that compute allocation is concrete, verifiable, and difficult to manipulate”) ^clm-d633c3da37bece49f54a
- Frontier AI labs resist compute restrictions because leadership, including Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, considers raw compute volume the decisive factor driving AI intelligence gains. (§ The proposal to cap research compute: “Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei previously identified raw compute as the primary driver of capability gains”) ^clm-f3c04d5d14e577138943
- OpenAI redirected roughly 10 percent of its total computing power toward alignment and safety monitoring in late 2026 following internal agent security incidents. (§ Current lab compute splits and geopolitical challenges: “shifted approximately 10 percent of its total compute capacity toward alignment and safety tasks”) ^clm-86eeb070528e8e502121
- Anthropic dedicated approximately 6 percent of its AI research compute to safety work as of July 2026, alongside an equivalent share dedicated to safeguard systems. (§ Current lab compute splits and geopolitical challenges: “Anthropic allocated about 6 percent of its AI R&D compute to safety research, with a comparable share devoted to safeguards”) ^clm-bdc3ea3514f117cfb1ea
- Prediction: Anthropic's draft registration statement indicated the company could commit up to $750 billion toward compute infrastructure over the coming years. (§ Current lab compute splits and geopolitical challenges: “indicated it could spend up to $750 billion on computing over the next several years”) ^clm-4d03a17a152fcbbda616
- SpaceX reported overall third-quarter 2026 revenue of $14.6 billion, including $7.5 billion from its Super Intelligence business spanning Cursor, Grok Bot, and cloud compute. (§ Current lab compute splits and geopolitical challenges: “SpaceX reported third-quarter revenue of $14.6 billion, with $7.5 billion from its Super Intelligence division”) ^clm-3c29331bb81c63e900ca
- Former AWS enterprise sales chief Mike Clayville returned to Amazon as vice president in October 2026 after six years leading sales at Stripe. (§ Current lab compute splits and geopolitical challenges: “AWS enterprise sales leader Mike Clayville rejoined Amazon as vice president after six years at Stripe.”) ^clm-dbd03c37472115310788
