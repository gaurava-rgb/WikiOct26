# DeepSeek's Annualized Revenue Hits $1 Billion as Startup Finalizes $7.5 Billion Fundraising

Juro Osawa and Qianer Liu, The Information, published 2026-09-23.

## Summary

DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate hit $1 billion, more than double a few months earlier, as it finalizes a $7.5 billion round and prepares a Shanghai listing. Growth came even after a price hike of 2.3 to 4.5 times, because demand for its cheap agent-capable Flash models stays strong. Its gross margin on API sales was 82.9% in early 2026, beating Anthropic and OpenAI, thanks to efficient infrastructure. CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors revenue is not a priority; over 70% of compute goes to training, and getting enough chips under US export limits is the biggest challenge.

## Key claims

- DeepSeek's annualized revenue run rate hit $1 billion, more than double a few months earlier, per two people with direct knowledge. (§ The core story: “hit $1 billion, more than double a few months earlier”) ^clm-e97a19a2235d9d92959c
- DeepSeek aims to close its second round of 50 billion yuan ($7.5 billion) at a valuation of about $75 billion by the end of October, and is preparing to go public on the Shanghai Stock Exchange. (§ The core story: “It is also preparing to go public on the Shanghai Stock Exchange”) ^clm-c04a9c3d73b67dde8cb5

- Liang Wenfeng said the price hike did not shrink DeepSeek's customer base because demand stays strong. (§ What drove growth: “the hike did not shrink the customer base because demand stays strong”) ^clm-9873e342e66383a28033
- DeepSeek's V4.1-Flash and V4-Flash perform like much larger models, and customers worldwide use them to power autonomous agents cheaply. (§ What drove growth: “Customers worldwide use them to power autonomous agents”) ^clm-6415ff92c8b0ac8c6683

- DeepSeek's healthy margins come from low running costs, by making infrastructure efficient so tasks need fewer chips. (§ Margins: “making infrastructure efficient so tasks need fewer chips”) ^clm-6cf7a36b6f401073b2ba

- DeepSeek devotes more than 70% of its computing capacity to training new models and under 30% to inference. (§ Revenue is not the priority: “More than 70% of computing capacity goes to training new models”) ^clm-b427f9060caa81e7e184
- Internal tests show DeepSeek's smaller models run well on gaming graphics chips and can handle most everyday user tasks, which could ease the chip shortage. (§ Gaming chips for everyday tasks: “DeepSeek's smaller models run well on gaming graphics chips”) ^clm-aacebebfdbcbe84b987c
