# Why Apple Is Betting on Foldable iPhones

Tiffany Li, The Information, published 2026-09-20.

## Summary

New Apple CEO John Ternus unveiled the iPhone Duo, Apple's first foldable, at $1,999. Apple is betting that foldable demand, which has grown fast in Asia for Huawei and Samsung, will lift iPhone revenue that was mostly flat from 2022 to 2025. IDC expects 10 million Duo units and $27 billion in revenue in the first year. Analysts argue Apple's high-paying customers, trade-ins and installment plans give the US market room to grow, with Apple entering late and expanding the category rather than inventing it.

## Key claims

- New Apple CEO John Ternus showed the first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, at an event in September 2026. (§ The core story: “showed the first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, at an event earlier in September 2026”) ^clm-a2274ab79590210da364
- Apple is betting that the demand which drove double-digit foldable growth outside North America for Huawei, Samsung and others will lift its flagship iPhone sales. (§ The core story: “double-digit foldable growth outside North America for Huawei, Samsung and others”) ^clm-841e0c34a8d5ec7469e6
- The iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 with 256 gigabytes of storage, far pricier than any earlier iPhone. (§ The forecasts: “the iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 with 256 gigabytes of storage”) ^clm-2973b6a7f3fd099f3797
- Prediction: IDC expects 10 million iPhone Duo units sold in the first 12 months after launch, bringing in $27 billion, about 6% of Apple's expected fiscal 2026 revenue. (§ The forecasts: “IDC projects those 10 million units will bring in $27 billion”) ^clm-299a27a4fb5f593b8559
- Prediction: Linda Sui of Smart Analytics Global projects 6 million iPhone Duo units sold in 2026 alone. (§ The forecasts: “projects 6 million Duo units sold in 2026 alone”) ^clm-355c12ad86567ac7b85f
- iPhone revenue was mostly flat from 2022 to 2025, rising just 4% in the fiscal year ending September 2025 to nearly $210 billion. (§ Why Apple needs it: “It rose just 4% in the fiscal year ending September 2025, to nearly $210 billion”) ^clm-813232489b65ec881473
- iPhone sales jumped 22% in the June 2026 quarter on the iPhone 17 line, partly from buyers who last upgraded four years ago. (§ Why Apple needs it: “iPhone sales jumped 22% in the June quarter on the iPhone 17 line”) ^clm-5969c3bbbe4b6faf2cc2
- IDC's Francisco Jeronimo argues the foldable brings novelty, can attract high-end buyers, and could get people upgrading more often again. (§ Why Apple needs it: “the foldable brings novelty and can attract high-end buyers”) ^clm-797e3cc0bfb7d58818ea
- Samsung launched the first mainstream foldable phone in 2019 and sells foldables such as the $1,900 Galaxy Z Fold8. (§ The foldable market: “Samsung launched the first mainstream foldable in 2019”) ^clm-f982a8df5ff901bb6c96
- Per IDC, global foldable revenue rose 16% in 2025 to $27 billion, while non-foldable smartphone sales rose 7% to $550 billion. (§ The foldable market: “global foldable revenue rose 16% last year to $27 billion”) ^clm-8780dc42546023f6f2f6
- Foldables were just 2% of North American smartphone sales in 2025, with most growth in Asian markets like China and South Korea. (§ The foldable market: “Foldables were just 2% of North American smartphone sales last year”) ^clm-62cb61c83b48c7b45c98
- Trade-ins of some recent iPhones could cut almost $900 off the iPhone Duo's sticker price, and installment plans could help buyers justify the price. (§ Why the U.S. could grow: “Trade-ins of some recent iPhones could cut almost $900 off the sticker price”) ^clm-0f110e7d9e5d579deaf6
- Linda Sui argues Apple will educate the market and drive up foldable adoption in the US, including for cheaper rivals. (§ Why the U.S. could grow: “Apple is going to educate the market and drive up the market adoption rate.”) ^clm-83276c425ce34ebd09ae
- The notes argue Apple is again entering a category late, after Samsung went first in 2019, and is expected to expand it rather than invent it. (§ Why it matters for the wiki: “Apple again enters a category late (Samsung went first in 2019)”) ^clm-5a62db3a884a9db6ad17
