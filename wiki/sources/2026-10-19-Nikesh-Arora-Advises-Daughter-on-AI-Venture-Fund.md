# Palo Alto Networks CEO Advises Daughter on New AI-Focused Venture Capital Fund

Julia Hornstein, Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-10-19.

## Summary

Ayesha Arora-Hughes, daughter of Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora, is raising Permanent Partners Collective Fund I with a target above $100 million to back AI applications and infrastructure. Nikesh Arora serves as an adviser to the firm, drawing on his past leadership at SoftBank and role as lead independent director at General Catalyst. Meanwhile, Palo Alto Networks is actively seeking cybersecurity acquisitions as autonomous AI agents transform enterprise security.

- Ayesha Arora-Hughes is raising a new venture capital fund called Permanent Partners Collective Fund I with her father Nikesh Arora advising.
