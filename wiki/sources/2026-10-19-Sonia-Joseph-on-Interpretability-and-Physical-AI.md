# Interpretability Is the Key to Physical AI, Says World Mechanics CEO Sonia Joseph

Rocket Drew, The Information, published 2026-10-19.

## Summary

In an interview with The Information, World Mechanics CEO and former Meta researcher Sonia Joseph argues that world models and mechanistic interpretability are inextricably linked. Building reliable physical AI systems for robotics, medicine, and manufacturing requires disentangled internal representations that directly map to physical laws, rather than relying on black-box heuristics or text-based reasoning. She contends that interpretability-guided training cuts data needs and speeds up development compared to brute-force compute.

## Key claims

- Sonia Joseph, co-founder and CEO of startup World Mechanics, previously led Meta's research forum on interpretability. (§ The connection between world models and mechanistic interpretability: “Sonia Joseph, co-founder and CEO of startup World Mechanics and former head of Meta's interpretability forum”) ^clm-8e3502fb724ba161a8c0
- Joseph contrasts an East Coast definition of world models centered on neuroscience and internal physical representations against a West Coast definition centered on photorealistic video generation and robot control. (§ The connection between world models and mechanistic interpretability: “distinguishes the East Coast definition of world models”) ^clm-347df0685ca2a4d51d6c
- Joseph argues accurate and safe world models require internal neural net representations that are disentangled, causal, and map functionally to physical mechanics. (§ The connection between world models and mechanistic interpretability: “effective world models require internal representations that are disentangled, causal, and map directly to physical laws”) ^clm-9870e3c983358df90583
- Without white-box interpretability, physical AI models can rely on spurious cues, such as the color of a surgeon's mask or finger rings during robotic surgery. (§ The connection between world models and mechanistic interpretability: “Without white-box interpretability, physical AI models can rely on spurious cues, like a surgeon's mask color”) ^clm-6fc4b60103e2b2aeba84
- Interpretability-guided training probes whether a neural network has internalized physical concepts like object permanence at intermediate steps, feeding corrective data to cut training data needs. (§ Interpretability-guided training versus raw scale: “checks whether a model has learned a concept like object permanence and feeds data dynamically”) ^clm-e6ed11568e79a6aedd83
- Joseph contends interpretability and inductive biases are compatible with the bitter lesson because structured verification can accelerate development cycles from years to weeks. (§ Interpretability-guided training versus raw scale: “compatible with the bitter lesson: while brute-force compute may eventually find physical representations”) ^clm-1a008bc9274ccbd83926
- Joseph proposes a training method that treats unsupervised video model latent space like an environment, using reinforcement learning to identify interpretable physical primitives and distill them into efficient architectures. (§ Interpretability-guided training versus raw scale: “distilling large unsupervised video models by using reinforcement learning to identify interpretable, causally relevant primitives”) ^clm-b23f47cb491cb332ff28
- Prediction: Joseph predicts physical AI will diverge from language models because multi-sensor environments require diverse, specialized models rather than a single dominant foundation model. (§ Interpretability-guided training versus raw scale: “physical AI will not follow language models with one foundation model ruling everything”) ^clm-3a2ffc172e9bd3c0ea07
- Physical AI differs fundamentally from language AI because the physical world possesses mathematical and physical ground truth like Newtonian mechanics. (§ Language limits and physical ground truth: “Unlike language, which has no single mathematical ground truth, the physical world has objective laws”) ^clm-42043d39c9b109853573
- Joseph dismisses the idea that recursive self-improvement in language models will solve physical AI, citing real-world temporal and physical constraints. (§ Language limits and physical ground truth: “doubts recursive self-improvement from language models will solve physical AI, calling it a fairy tale”) ^clm-65cee27d3cc6499dc1a6
- Meta developed the Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA) with Yann LeCun to predict abstract object representations in latent space instead of predicting raw pixels. (§ Language limits and physical ground truth: “Meta's Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA), pioneered with Yann LeCun”) ^clm-dc8609ae59941df3b25a
- Joseph departs from Yann LeCun by advocating an interpretability-first approach that directly verifies what latent spaces encode at each stage of network training. (§ Language limits and physical ground truth: “differs by insisting on interpretability first, verifying what latent spaces actually represent at each layer”) ^clm-daada72a7192b22795bf
- Deploying physical AI into high-liability industries like medicine and steel production requires translating internal model reasoning into causal chains that workers can understand and verify. (§ Language limits and physical ground truth: “Making AI interpretable to blue-collar workers and doctors in high-liability industries”) ^clm-93c9a36812c49e2d63fe
