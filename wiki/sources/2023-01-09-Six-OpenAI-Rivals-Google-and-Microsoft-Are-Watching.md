# Six OpenAI Rivals Google and Microsoft Are Watching

Jon Victor, The Information, published 2023-01-09.

## Summary

A January 2023 snapshot of six AI startups (Adept, AI21 Labs, Anthropic, Character, Cohere and Inflection) built by researchers who left OpenAI, DeepMind and Google. Together they had raised more than $1 billion to build their own large language models and planned to license them or build chatbot apps. The piece frames them as possible partners or acquisition targets for big tech, while warning that models may become a commodity where the cheapest good model wins. Anthropic at the time had 80 staff, nominal revenue and a $4 billion valuation.

## Key claims

- In January 2023 Microsoft planned to use OpenAI's technology to improve Bing search and to help Word and Outlook users draft documents and emails. (§ The setup: “Microsoft planned to use OpenAI's tech to improve Bing search”) ^clm-0f71d48bfea31cb2ae35
- Top researchers from OpenAI, DeepMind and Google had left to start or join six rival model startups, which together had raised more than $1 billion in equity funding. (§ The setup: “Together they had raised more than $1 billion in equity funding”) ^clm-6f0cd179930186bf0775
- Stanford's Center for Research on Foundation Models found that models from AI21 Labs, Cohere and Anthropic were less likely to produce toxic answers than OpenAI's GPT-3. (§ Who might buy, and why: “were less likely to produce toxic answers than OpenAI's GPT-3”) ^clm-65d9e61404345301fb40
- Runway ML CEO Cristóbal Valenzuela argued that if language models become a commodity, winning comes down to running the cheapest effective model. (§ Who might buy, and why: “Whoever can run the cheapest and most effective model will win.”) ^clm-50d6c2a9597cd28801b3
- AI consultant Vin Vashishta argued that Salesforce or VMware could buy one of the startups to sell AI apps, and Netflix or Disney could buy one to train models on their film and TV libraries. (§ Who might buy, and why: “Netflix or Disney could buy one and train models on their film and TV libraries”) ^clm-1d7c60349606a7b52300
- Adept AI Labs, led by former OpenAI and Google engineer David Luan, built a browser extension that does tasks from plain-language instructions; it had raised $66 million, had 20 staff and nominal revenue. (§ The six startups: “$66M raised (Greylock, Addition), 20 staff, nominal revenue”) ^clm-e376cefcc89594b403c6
- AI21 Labs was valued at $750 million in July 2022, had 160 staff and more than $20 million in annual recurring revenue. (§ The six startups: “Valued at $750M in July 2022. 160 staff. Over $20M in annual recurring revenue”) ^clm-fcbe422a551c3809da7e
- Anthropic had raised $704 million, was valued at $4 billion in April 2022 per PitchBook, and had 80 staff and nominal revenue. (§ The six startups: “Valued at $4B in April 2022 (per PitchBook). 80 staff, nominal revenue”) ^clm-7758453807e8271537d9
- Anthropic's backers included Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison, and the company would not say how FTX's collapse affected those investments. (§ The six startups: “Would not say how FTX's collapse affected those investments”) ^clm-d2949007e726b629986e
- Anthropic was founded by siblings Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President), both OpenAI vice presidents during GPT-3, and is registered as a public benefit corporation. (§ The six startups: “Siblings Dario Amodei (CEO) and Daniela Amodei (President) were OpenAI VPs during GPT-3”) ^clm-4c6c74434f032e1612b6
- Anthropic planned to open API access to a small set of partners in 2023 and was looking to raise hundreds of millions more, per one person. (§ The six startups: “a small set of partners" in 2023, and was looking to raise hundreds of millions more”) ^clm-1ee168154ce0344762e6
- Character.AI founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas built Google's LaMDA and left Google over red tape; the startup had 18 staff and no revenue. (§ The six startups: “Founders Noam Shazeer and Daniel De Freitas built Google's LaMDA and left over red tape”) ^clm-63727b31835d4f0a1f09
- Cohere had a strategic partnership with Google and reportedly talked with Google and Nvidia about investment, per The Wall Street Journal. (§ The six startups: “Has a strategic partnership with Google and reportedly talked with Google and Nvidia about investment”) ^clm-36f72d73e4780a7174d8
- Inflection, led by DeepMind co-founder Mustafa Suleyman with co-founder Reid Hoffman, had raised $225 million, was valued at $1.23 billion in April 2022, and had 20 staff and no product. (§ The six startups: “Valued at $1.23B in April 2022 (per PitchBook). 20 staff, no product yet.”) ^clm-06d1a2e9bb441c6a074f
