# How CoreWeave’s Debt Binge Brought a Moment of Reckoning

Dakin Campbell, The Information, published 2026-10-20.

## Summary

CoreWeave faced an acute market reckoning as its stock dropped 20 percent in a week to $61 and bond yields climbed above 15 percent amid rising interest rates and inflation data. The neocloud, which originated as crypto miner Atlantic Crypto in 2017, pioneered debt financing collateralized by AI chips through a $2.3 billion Blackstone loan in 2023 anchored by Microsoft. While CoreWeave grew to $5.1 billion in revenue in 2025 and secured insurance company debt, critics like Ben Horowitz characterize its business as a fragile financial machine under rising capital costs.

## Key claims

- CoreWeave shares fell approximately 20 percent in mid-October 2026, falling from near $80 to $61 and leaving the company valued at $35 billion. (§ The market selloff and rising debt yields: “CoreWeave shares fell roughly 20 percent in one week, dropping from nearly $80 to just over $61”) ^clm-6ffa1ff93280219d71c5
- Yields on CoreWeave debt climbed above 15 percent as rising benchmark yields and inflation figures raised expectations of higher borrowing costs. (§ The market selloff and rising debt yields: “Yields on CoreWeave's existing debt climbed above 15 percent, reflecting sharp investor anxiety”) ^clm-a3ac686906824c0fcd8c
- Ben Horowitz criticized CoreWeave's debt-heavy structure as a Rube Goldberg financial machine, stating Andreessen Horowitz avoided investing in such models. (§ The market selloff and rising debt yields: “Venture capitalist Ben Horowitz called CoreWeave a Rube Goldberg financial machine on the All-In podcast”) ^clm-59c8144f9535fdf2e694

(§ The market selloff and rising debt yields: “Investors have used CoreWeave stock and credit default swaps to hedge broad exposure to private AI foundation labs”) ^clm-35f469178fff7aa57b9d
- CoreWeave was founded in 2017 as Atlantic Crypto by commodities traders mining digital assets with Nvidia GPUs, before pivoting to cloud computing after the 2018 cryptocurrency crash. - Blackstone CEO Steve Schwarzman approved a $2.3 billion loan to CoreWeave in August 2023 despite internal misgivings about backing a firm that made only $16 million the prior year. - CoreWeave structures project financing by borrowing against customer contracts, estimating it takes two and a half years of contract cash flow to pay off project debt.
