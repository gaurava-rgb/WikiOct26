# DeepSeek Bets Big on Huawei Chips to Bypass U.S. Export Controls

Juro Osawa and Qianer Liu, The Information, published 2026-09-21.

## Summary

DeepSeek CEO Liang Wenfeng told investors that training models on Chinese chips is a major priority and that Huawei could start delivering training chips as early as Q4 2026. He called it one of DeepSeek's biggest bets, made to escape US export controls, though DeepSeek still trains on Nvidia chips likely from the black market. Liang estimated a frontier model needs about 200,000 Huawei Ascend 950 chips versus 50,000 Nvidia GB300s. Huawei's supply is capped by limited access to advanced memory, while DeepSeek trains ever bigger models.
