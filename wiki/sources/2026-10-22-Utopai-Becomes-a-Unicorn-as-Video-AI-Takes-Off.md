# Utopai Becomes a Unicorn as Video AI Takes Off

Rocket Drew, The Information, published 2026-10-22.

## Summary

Video AI startup Utopai Studios attained unicorn status at a $1 billion valuation, using fine-tuned open-source MiniMax models to cut film planning and CGI costs by 80-90% across 20 upcoming film and TV projects. Peer Higgsfield reached $1 billion in annualized revenue, while Kling AI raised nearly $3 billion at a $15 billion valuation. In broader tech news, DeepSeek is closing a $15 billion funding round led by CATL and Tencent, Nebius secured a $3.5 billion credit facility, Nvidia expanded chip substrate capacity 2.5x with suppliers like Ibiden, and OpenAI launched models on Google Cloud.

## Key claims

- Utopai Studios secured a $60 million funding round at a $1 billion valuation, and received term sheets for a follow-on round valuing the company at $2 billion to $3 billion. (§ Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios: “secured a $60 million funding round at a $1 billion valuation, attaining unicorn status, with term sheets received for a follow-on round valuing the business between $2 billion and $3 billion”) ^clm-439b857fc59c435cf651
- Utopai Studios Chief Executive Cecilia Shen, formerly an AI researcher on Google's X moonshot team, founded the company in 2022. (§ Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios: “Chief Executive Cecilia Shen, a former AI researcher on Google's X moonshot team, founded Utopai in 2022”) ^clm-9f8b00c1b758769f2daf
- Utopai Studios operates as an independent film studio rather than a software seller, arguing that selling software alone offers negligible profit margins in entertainment. (§ Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios: “operates as an independent film studio, arguing that selling software alone offers negligible profit margins”) ^clm-5afc1c29e6d2fdbd4566
- Utopai Studios fine-tuned the open-source MiniMax H3 model to create a video engine that ranks second on Artificial Analysis text-to-video benchmarks. (§ Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios: “fine-tuned open-source model MiniMax H3 to build its own video engine, ranked second on Artificial Analysis”) ^clm-022f146898813350082c
- Deploying AI tools cuts scene planning and computer-generated imagery costs for films by 80% to 90%. (§ Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios: “cuts scene planning and computer-generated imagery costs by 80% to 90%”) ^clm-691378f285ba5c442b2c
- Utopai Studios has more than 20 projects in development, including two television series and three feature films planned for release in approximately 3,000 North American theaters in 2027. (§ Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios: “three feature films planned for release in approximately 3,000 North American movie theaters in 2027”) ^clm-f750788f6ee920c3aba2
- Video AI startup Higgsfield announced it crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue in September 2026, after quadrupling its valuation to $5.4 billion in August. (§ Video AI peers: “Higgsfield announced it crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue in September 2026, after quadrupling its valuation to $5.4 billion”) ^clm-884454e68958f837da43

- Media executive Jeffrey Katzenberg partnered with the former head of OpenAI's Sora app to establish an AI film production company in discussions to license and customize Sora. (§ Video AI peers: “partnered with the former head of OpenAI's Sora app to establish an AI film production company in discussions to license and customize Sora”) ^clm-d72e494e9843cd16808e
