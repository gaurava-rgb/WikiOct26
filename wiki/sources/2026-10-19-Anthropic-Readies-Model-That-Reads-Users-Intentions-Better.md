# Anthropic Readies Model That Reads Users' Intentions Better

Stephanie Palazzolo, The Information, published 2026-10-19.

## Summary

Anthropic is preparing an unreleased model with superior intuition and intention-reading capabilities, while assembling a $60 billion debt package for Google TPUs with Blackstone anchoring a junior slice. Google is building Gemini 4 Carbon with visual advantages from YouTube, while OpenAI prepares mid-tier Terra models to undercut Anthropic on API cost. Major deals include Proofpoint acquiring Varonis Systems for $6.3 billion and private equity buying CCC Intelligent Solutions for $6.1 billion.

## Key claims

- Anthropic is preparing an unreleased model with superior reasoning and intuition that discerns underlying user intentions without explicit prompting. (§ Frontier model cadence and Anthropic intention-reading model: “Anthropic is preparing to release an advanced model with superior reasoning and intuition”) ^clm-5c31046fdc116c4f77bb
- Google is developing Gemini 4 Carbon as a successor to Gemini 4 Argon, leveraging YouTube video training data for visual tasks. (§ Rival model moves from Google and OpenAI: “Google is developing a model codenamed Gemini 4 Carbon, following its release of Gemini 4 Argon”) ^clm-e01abe7cb4bc5132dfdc
- OpenAI is developing a new iteration in its mid-tier Terra model family to undercut market leader Anthropic on developer API costs. (§ Rival model moves from Google and OpenAI: “OpenAI is preparing a new iteration in its mid-tier Terra model family”) ^clm-6889c474a714b018a8d1
- OpenAI is preparing a major unreleased model featuring looping transformers, while working through safety and security risks before release. (§ Rival model moves from Google and OpenAI: “developing a major unreleased model featuring looping transformer architectures, but is in an all-hands sprint”) ^clm-5b28c480687279088163
