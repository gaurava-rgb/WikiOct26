# Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch09 Shein and Temu - E-commerce Giants from Asia

John Gallaugher, FlatWorld, published 2025.

## Summary

Gallaugher's chapter on Shein and Temu, two China-born online retailers that grew into global giants in a few years. Shein beat Zara at its own game by mining app, ad, and social media data with AI, testing tiny batches from thousands of contract factories, and shipping each order straight to consumers under the U.S. de minimis import rule. The chapter details the heavy criticism both firms face over labor conditions, Xinjiang cotton, copied designs, dangerous products, and environmental harm, plus their lawsuits against each other. It closes on Shein's marketplace push against Amazon, its Forever 21 deal, Temu's money-losing growth, and whether either firm has a sustainable advantage.
