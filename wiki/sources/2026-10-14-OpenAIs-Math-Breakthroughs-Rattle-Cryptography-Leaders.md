# OpenAI’s Math Breakthroughs Rattle Cryptography Leaders

Tiffany Li, The Information, published 2026-10-14.

## Summary

OpenAI's release of over 300 math solutions from an internal model sparked concerns among leading cryptographers about AI cracking public key cryptography that secures internet payments and messaging. Pioneers Whitfield Diffie and Martin Hellman along with computer scientists warned that advances could threaten elliptic curve cryptography within years. The piece also reports on the FBI arrest of an OpenAI employee for planting a recording device in an executive conference room, cost-cutting targets at Microsoft Azure, JPMorgan blocking external agents from client accounts, and Character.ai's bankruptcy filing.
