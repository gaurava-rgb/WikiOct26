# SB Energy Considers Waiting Until December or Later for an IPO

Phoebe Liu, Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-10-22.

## Summary

SoftBank-controlled data center builder SB Energy is considering delaying its initial public offering to December 2026 or into 2027 after previously aiming for September. Bankers floated a $60 billion valuation on $139 million in first-half revenue, but investors balked at the 200x multiple despite $430 billion in contracted revenue for OpenAI's Ohio data center campus. The pause mirrors broader delays across data center developers, including DayOne and AirTrunk, while Nscale presses ahead targeting a $30 billion listing.

## Key claims

- SB Energy is evaluating whether to delay its initial public offering to December 2026 or into 2027, later than its earlier September target. (§ Delayed IPO timeline: “SB Energy is evaluating whether to delay its initial public offering to December 2026 or into 2027”) ^clm-0418b97c74a3e573aa68
- SB Energy leaders are tracking Anthropic's expected public offering ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday in November 2026 to gauge investor sentiment. (§ Delayed IPO timeline: “tracking Anthropic's expected public offering ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday in November 2026”) ^clm-9e9dfa847c1221307b8b
- SB Energy sought to raise $5 billion to $7 billion from its IPO to fund the massive Ohio data center campus being built for OpenAI. (§ Delayed IPO timeline: “had aimed to raise between $5 billion and $7 billion from the offering to fund the massive Ohio data center”) ^clm-e3fc6e1b344a685cd9f0
- OpenAI's planned Ohio data center is designed for 8.5 gigawatts of power capacity, roughly $600 billion in Nvidia chips, and a $105 billion credit guarantee from Nvidia. (§ Delayed IPO timeline: “Ohio campus is designed to reach 8.5 gigawatts of power capacity, house approximately $600 billion in Nvidia chips”) ^clm-bc8d822d38a5a1e3412e
- Building the full Ohio data center campus will require more than $100 billion in total capital. (§ Delayed IPO timeline: “Building the full Ohio campus will require more than $100 billion in total capital”) ^clm-050e21f8237979075b26
- SB Energy reported $1.2 billion in cash and cash equivalents as of June 30, 2026, alongside a $1.5 billion Nvidia equity investment received in August. (§ Balance sheet and valuation hurdles: “supported by a $1.5 billion investment from Nvidia in mid-August 2026 alongside $1.2 billion in cash”) ^clm-7570b796eb12895fed8c
- Prospective IPO investors balked at a proposed $60 billion valuation for SB Energy, which represents more than 200 times its annualized first-half 2026 revenue of $139 million. (§ Balance sheet and valuation hurdles: “proposed a valuation around $60 billion for SB Energy, but prospective investors have balked at the price”) ^clm-cb506998b06eeb7c95fd
- SB Energy holds $430 billion in contracted data center lease revenue, tied entirely to OpenAI's Ohio campus over 15 to 20 year terms starting in 2028. (§ Balance sheet and valuation hurdles: “holds $430 billion in contracted data center lease revenue, but that revenue is tied entirely to the OpenAI Ohio facility”) ^clm-ad78dc30879cc13a89e5
- Nscale is attempting to proceed with a fall 2026 public listing at a valuation around $30 billion, aided by talks with Anthropic over a $45 billion cloud contract. (§ Headwinds across data center developers: “Nscale is attempting to proceed with a fall 2026 public listing at a valuation around $30 billion”) ^clm-43892ad2fc7a05a392f9
- Singapore-based data center operator DayOne is considering delaying its initial public offering or downsizing the capital raised. (§ Headwinds across data center developers: “DayOne is considering delaying its initial public offering or downsizing the capital raised”) ^clm-c7153369eb9ae09b95e1
- Blackstone-backed data center operator AirTrunk postponed a planned $1.5 billion real estate investment trust listing on the Singapore Exchange. (§ Headwinds across data center developers: “AirTrunk postponed a planned $1.5 billion real estate investment trust listing on the Singapore Exchange”) ^clm-180ee3f9570b93b07915
