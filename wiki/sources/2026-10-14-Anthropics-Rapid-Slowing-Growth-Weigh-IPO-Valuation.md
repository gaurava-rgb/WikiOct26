# Anthropic’s Rapid But Slowing Growth Could Weigh on IPO Valuation

Cory Weinberg, The Information, published 2026-10-14.

## Summary

Anthropic reported revenue in a private San Francisco meeting with prospective IPO investors, showing third-quarter 2026 revenue of $16.8 billion, up about 45% from the second quarter. The 45% quarterly growth slowed from the prior period, when Claude Code lifted revenue by more than 140%. Anthropic reported an 11% adjusted operating margin through the quarter, but disclosed that distillation contributes 5% to 7% of revenue and is expected to fall. Slower expansion and debt market volatility led some attendees to expect bankers to lower Anthropic's planned November 2026 IPO valuation below $1.5 trillion.

## Key claims

- Anthropic's revenue reached $16.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, rising about 45% from the second quarter, per people briefed on the numbers. (§ The core story: “Revenue reached $16.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, rising approximately 45% from the second quarter”) ^clm-ed2eee7102d53db2be7f
- Anthropic's third-quarter revenue of $16.8 billion was more than 3.6 times its total revenue for all of 2025. (§ The core story: “revenue was more than 3.6 times Anthropic's total revenue for the entire year of 2025”) ^clm-090d16732cf8de245cd0
- Anthropic's 45% quarterly revenue growth in the third quarter marked a slowdown from the second quarter, when Claude Code drove revenue up over 140% from the first quarter. (§ The core story: “slowdown from the second quarter, when demand for Claude Code propelled revenue up more than 140%”) ^clm-e3bd260d987a621a092b
- Anthropic reported an adjusted operating margin of 11% for 2026 through the third quarter, excluding stock-based compensation, indicating progress toward profitability. (§ Financial margins and distillation revenue: “adjusted operating margin of 11% for 2026 through the third quarter, per one person briefed on the figures”) ^clm-050ac743ad4f435b812b
- Anthropic disclosed that 5% to 7% of its revenue comes from model distillation, in which third-party firms train models using Anthropic outputs. - Anthropic warned investors that distillation revenue will decline and weigh on results in the current quarter as the company combats unauthorized distillation by Chinese labs. - Anthropic's private market valuation reached $965 billion in May 2026, rising from less than $20 billion two years earlier. (§ IPO timing and data center headwinds: “valuation rose to $965 billion in May 2026, compared with less than $20 billion two years earlier”) ^clm-069a4fc13787763bfa68
- Decelerating growth and tighter market conditions led some investor attendees to expect bankers to lower Anthropic's IPO valuation target to less than $1.5 trillion, down from private discussions of $2 trillion.
