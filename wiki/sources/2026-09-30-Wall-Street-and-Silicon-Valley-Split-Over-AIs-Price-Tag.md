# Wall Street and Silicon Valley Split Over AI's Price Tag

Cory Weinberg and Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-09-30.

## Summary

Venture investors keep paying ever higher prices for AI companies while public market investors are pulling back amid rising rates. Several mid-sized IPOs have stalled, and some public investors say Anthropic should be valued nearer $1.5 trillion than the $2 trillion floated. OpenAI and Anthropic will need steady financing after their IPOs to cover huge data center commitments, and data center firms like SB Energy and Nscale face doubts over debt and customer concentration. SpaceX's successful IPO is the comparison point.

## Key claims

- Two large public investors said bankers should value Anthropic nearer $1.5 trillion, not the $2 trillion some bankers floated early on. (§ The core story: “bankers should value Anthropic nearer $1.5 trillion, not the $2 trillion”) ^clm-ba5f8ee9743727dcd2d9
- Anthropic's IPO is coming later in 2026 than investors expected and is likely weeks away from its roadshow. (§ The core story: “Sentiment on Anthropic's giant IPO is cooling”) ^clm-7f6851b98b751f9f325b
- Anthropic has committed to at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute that could cost well over $500 billion over the next decade. (§ Why public investors are wary: “Anthropic has committed to at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute”) ^clm-63d7ec59d8a8e7c9874a
- OpenAI is in early talks to raise about $30 billion before its IPO. (§ Why public investors are wary: “in early talks to raise about $30 billion pre-IPO”) ^clm-86689254e577ca50c8fb
- AllianceBernstein's Samantha Lau said new listings must be priced conservatively to open the market, and asked who pays for agent software before the payoff. (§ Why public investors are wary: “the only way to open the market is to be conservative”) ^clm-456f51e079cc0b981814
- Oura delayed its IPO the day before final pricing because investors said its valuation ask was too high, per people familiar. (§ Deals stalling: “Oura (smart ring) delayed its IPO Tuesday, the day before final pricing”) ^clm-cb2b3e671798615493f8
- SB Energy, which is building a big Ohio facility for OpenAI, has not started marketing its IPO nearly a month after filing, partly over its dependence on OpenAI for revenue. (§ Deals stalling: “investors balk, partly over its dependence on OpenAI for revenue”) ^clm-d81bd65e738416570d41
- Data center firms Nscale and Switch plan IPOs, but investors dislike their heavy debt and need for more funding as rates rise. (§ Deals stalling: “Investors dislike their heavy debt and need for more funding as rates rise”) ^clm-8aa6c2af3ce25f21874c
- AI consumer assistant Instinct raised $1 billion at a $10 billion valuation a year after founding, backed by Sequoia, Benchmark and Coatue. (§ Venture froth continues: “raised $1 billion at a $10 billion valuation a year after founding”) ^clm-753e0086cfa6580ec8a2
- Sequoia's Pat Grady told limited partners that VCs now broadly believe AI has reached AGI, and that valuations are totally insane and emblematic of the froth. (§ Venture froth continues: “valuations are "totally insane" and "emblematic of the froth."”) ^clm-fc6c7a0cbf5ab7bb6dae
- SpaceX raised nearly $86 billion in June 2026, the largest IPO ever, and its stock is up about 10%, valuing it around $2 trillion. (§ The SpaceX comparison and Anthropic's advantages: “SpaceX raised nearly $86 billion in June, the largest IPO ever”) ^clm-f33e1a833df15439c11a
- Prediction: Possible boosts for Anthropic's IPO include mutual funds that already bought shares privately and potential cash from Nvidia, Alphabet and Amazon. (§ The SpaceX comparison and Anthropic's advantages: “Nvidia, Alphabet and Amazon could add cash”) ^clm-122a848fbff1c791b398
