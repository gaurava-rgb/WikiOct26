# Cognition’s Plan to Limit Tokenmaxxing to the Most Efficient Engineers

Rocket Drew, The Information, published 2026-10-15.

## Summary

Cognition introduced dynamic token allocations for its AI software engineer Devin, measuring human work hours against session compute costs to allocate higher limits to efficient engineers. The company also launched Devin Coach to intercept common prompting mistakes and detect non-work usage. In corporate finance, Anduril launched a $1.3 billion tender at a $90 billion valuation, Modal raised $750 million at $15.75 billion, and OpenAI compute head Sachin Katti announced his departure.

## Key claims

- Cognition introduced dynamic token allocations for Devin that adjust employee usage caps based on calculated productivity and compute cost efficiency. (§ Dynamic token limits and efficiency grading: “Cognition, developer of AI software engineer Devin, introduced dynamic resource allocations that adjust employee token limits based on efficiency ratings”) ^clm-1bc9292e11db366f6e16
- Cognition calculates engineer efficiency by dividing estimated human equivalent hours of coding work by the dollar cost of the Devin session. (§ Dynamic token limits and efficiency grading: “calculating the human-hour equivalent of work produced during a coding session and dividing that by the underlying compute cost”) ^clm-97200c64a8e73a3a2b66
- Cognition customer engineering head Calvin Giroud compared dynamic AI resource caps to credit card limit increases earned through reliable stewardship. (§ Dynamic token limits and efficiency grading: “Calvin Giroud, Cognition vice president of customer engineering, compared the dynamic limit mechanism to credit cards that grant higher borrowing ceilings”) ^clm-65c78f3ffe67b1035846
- Cognition introduced Devin Coach to identify common prompting mistakes, such as underspecified requests or selecting unnecessarily expensive models. (§ Devin Coach for workflow optimization: “identifies roughly 20 to 30 common user errors, including underspecified task prompts and selecting unnecessarily expensive AI models”) ^clm-738cf05937abdb9191d2
- Devin Coach instructs developers to break multi-part requests into concurrent parallel tasks to reduce processing time. (§ Devin Coach for workflow optimization: “prompts developers to split complex multi-part requests into concurrent parallel tasks to save time”) ^clm-0e434a947e9435b04e90
- Anduril Industries opened an employee share tender valuing the autonomous defense contractor at approximately $90 billion. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “Defense technology maker Anduril Industries opened tender discussions to allow employee share sales at a valuation of approximately $90 billion”) ^clm-c3def765d174e752c63f
- Anduril's employee share tender is capped at $1.3 billion at a price of $94.80 per share with closing targeted for early November 2026. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “tender is capped at $1.3 billion at a price of $94.80 per share, scheduled to conclude in early November 2026”) ^clm-3f01646253f6e3ab889e
- Sachin Katti, vice president of compute strategy at OpenAI, announced he will step down on November 15, 2026 to start a new company. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “Sachin Katti, vice president of compute strategy at OpenAI and former Intel executive, announced plans to leave OpenAI on November 15, 2026”) ^clm-6700d231ec147044798f
- AI infrastructure provider Modal closed a $750 million Series D funding round led by Accel at a $15.75 billion valuation. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “Modal closed a $750 million Series D funding round led by Accel at a $15.75 billion post-money valuation”) ^clm-413eba966842723d4907
- Berkshire Hathaway agreed to invest $330 million in Italian software company Bending Spoons via a private placement of common stock. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “Berkshire Hathaway agreed to invest $330 million in Italian software firm Bending Spoons through a private placement of common stock”) ^clm-236f2dc937febf514689
- Nvidia committed $250 million in compute credits over five years to support agentic AI safety and security research in the United Kingdom. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “Nvidia announced a $250 million five-year compute commitment for agentic security research in the United Kingdom”) ^clm-01e156c98bd0dac7cc2c
- European payments company Mollie committed 50 million euros to establish internal banking and payment rails for autonomous AI agents. (§ Executive moves and corporate funding: “Mollie committed 50 million euros, approximately $54 million, to build internal banking and payment infrastructure for autonomous AI agents”) ^clm-b291b4c5e43067f6df08
