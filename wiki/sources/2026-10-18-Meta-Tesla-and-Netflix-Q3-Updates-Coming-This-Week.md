# Meta, Tesla and Netflix Q3 Updates Coming This Week

Martin Peers, The Information, published 2026-10-18.

## Summary

Meta digital ad revenue grew 30% in early 2026 to $59 billion in Q2, but analysts project third-quarter capital spending will double to $40.5 billion, causing negative free cash flow of $10.3 billion. Tesla faces slowing vehicle deliveries and a delayed Roadster to 2028 as its valuation lags SpaceX. Netflix growth slowed to 11.7% amid executive churn, while Microsoft canceled a planned UK data center with neocloud Nscale over power connection delays.

## Key claims

- In the first half of 2026, Meta digital ad revenue grew 30%, double Google growth rate, with second-quarter ad revenue reaching $59 billion. (§ Meta AI advertising boom and surging capital expenditure: “In the first half of 2026, Meta digital advertising revenue grew 30%, double Google growth rate”) ^clm-6a729ebe78d85e14bb50
- Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg stated that 9 million small businesses use at least one Meta AI creative tool to build ads. (§ Meta AI advertising boom and surging capital expenditure: “9 million small businesses use at least one Meta AI creative tool to build ads”) ^clm-7405b0353e0c8f03d074
- Prediction: Analysts project Meta third-quarter 2026 capital expenditures of $40.5 billion, double the year-earlier level. (§ Meta AI advertising boom and surging capital expenditure: “Analysts project Meta third-quarter capital expenditures of $40.5 billion, double the year-earlier figure”) ^clm-d1eb35b8c4a8bf76459b
- Prediction: Meta third-quarter capex is projected to drive negative free cash flow of $10.3 billion, compared to positive $10.6 billion a year earlier. (§ Meta AI advertising boom and surging capital expenditure: “drive negative free cash flow of $10.3 billion for the quarter, compared to positive free cash flow of $10.6 billion”) ^clm-9a0ce83bda1f59882d94
- Meta held $90 billion in cash and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026. (§ Meta AI advertising boom and surging capital expenditure: “Meta held $90 billion in cash and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026”) ^clm-c2dee90eadd6d07b98e1
- Tesla market capitalization stands at $1.4 trillion, approximately $600 billion below SpaceX valuation of roughly $2 trillion. (§ Tesla sales plateau and robotics pivot: “Tesla market capitalization stands at $1.4 trillion, about $600 billion below SpaceX valuation”) ^clm-e41b833b1dc0903c4cbe
- Tesla third-quarter deliveries fell 2% year-over-year, leading analysts to estimate revenue grew only 0.6% in the quarter. (§ Tesla sales plateau and robotics pivot: “Third-quarter deliveries fell 2% year-over-year, leading analysts to project a meager 0.6% revenue increase”) ^clm-1263f6679deefddf17ca
- Tesla unveiled its upcoming Roadster but scheduled production to begin in 2028. (§ Tesla sales plateau and robotics pivot: “Tesla revealed its upcoming Roadster, but production is not scheduled to begin until 2028”) ^clm-e6b30dc1a0dd016c8bfe
- Prediction: Netflix projected third-quarter revenue growth of 11.7%, slowing from 16.2% in the first quarter of 2026. (§ Netflix Hollywood maturation and executive turnover: “Netflix projected third-quarter revenue growth of 11.7%, down 4.5 percentage points from 16.2%”) ^clm-c571ee37385500fb0701
- Netflix shares fell 25% year-to-date in 2026, trading at 5.6 times forward revenue compared to 2.2 times for Walt Disney Co. (§ Netflix Hollywood maturation and executive turnover: “Netflix shares trade at 5.6 times forward revenue, compared to 2.2 times for Walt Disney Co”) ^clm-5483dd628f6e17b2a981

(§ Infrastructure and regulatory developments: “Microsoft withdrew from a planned United Kingdom data center that neocloud Nscale was constructing”) ^clm-63e73267d2e1b380f630
