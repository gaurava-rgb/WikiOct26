# Where Nvidia's $100 Billion Dealmaking Juggernaut Will Go Next

Valida Pau, Phoebe Liu, The Information, published 2026-10-07.

## Summary

Nvidia has signed a record run of deals and is expected to keep going into robotics, self-driving, and AI models that run locally on phones and home computers. The reporters say Jensen Huang wants thousands of thriving AI models so OpenAI and a few other big buyers do not hold too much power over Nvidia's sales. Deals include the $12.9 billion Hugging Face purchase, a $6 billion Poolside license-and-hire, a Perplexity partnership, and a $105 billion credit guarantee for an OpenAI data center. Widening credit default swap spreads in August 2026 now act as a limit on how much risk Nvidia takes.

## Key claims

- Prediction: Nvidia's next deal targets are expected to be robotics, self-driving technology, and AI models that run locally on phones and home computers, per bankers, lawyers and investors who have worked with it. (§ The core story: “robotics, self-driving technology, and AI models that run locally on phones and home computers”) ^clm-afea99a0c5c8bc59b578
- The reporters say Jensen Huang wants a world with thousands of thriving AI models, so that OpenAI and a few other big buyers do not hold too much power over Nvidia's sales. (§ The core story: “Jensen Huang wants a world with thousands of thriving AI models, not a few”) ^clm-61514f3c8dbb32c46270
- In July 2026 Nvidia was interested in buying AI model marketplace OpenRouter but asked for more time; the founders would not wait and Stripe won with an $8 billion bid, per a person with knowledge. (§ The core story: “Stripe won with an $8 billion bid”) ^clm-f6164c5f5b50d1b7bd14
- Over roughly two months after July 2026, Nvidia signed more than $140 billion in deals, including a $105 billion credit guarantee, by the reporters' tally. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Nvidia signed more than $140 billion in deals, including a $105 billion credit guarantee”) ^clm-583c872a7aebf1c15581
- As of late July 2026 Nvidia had nearly $100 billion in equity investments plus $25 billion in future commitments, and $99 billion in cash and securities. (§ Key facts and numbers: “nearly $100 billion in equity investments plus $25 billion in future commitments”) ^clm-cc9f0c875bb0c0c8d238
- Three customers made up 44% of Nvidia's sales in the six months to July 2026, per Nvidia's disclosure. (§ Key facts and numbers: “three customers made up 44% of sales in the six months to July 2026”) ^clm-49e700b30551eaf659e9
- Nvidia bought Hugging Face for $12.9 billion, more than 80 times its $150 million annualized revenue, per people close to the deal. (§ Key facts and numbers: “$12.9 billion, over 80 times its $150 million annualized revenue”) ^clm-90a96ad5e299ad8b3ac5
- Nvidia signed a $6 billion license-and-hire deal with Poolside, partly to help its own Nemotron open models. (§ Key facts and numbers: “$6 billion license-and-hire deal, partly to help Nvidia's own Nemotron open models”) ^clm-3a4f05d1c2bb646cd316
- Nvidia has discussed investing another $1 billion in humanoid robot maker Figure, which seeks a valuation around $38 billion, per one person. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Nvidia has discussed investing another $1 billion”) ^clm-9d3186c08337fcda3f58
- Nvidia's talks with Perplexity ranged from an outright purchase to a license deal of at least $20 billion, and ended as a partnership plus a planned roughly $3 billion investment at a $35 billion pre-money valuation. (§ Key facts and numbers: “ended as a partnership (late August 2026) plus a planned roughly $3 billion investment”) ^clm-ba28062190adcb59f3f7
- For OpenAI's Ohio data center, Nvidia first discussed $250 billion in credit support, then guaranteed $105 billion for phase one only, plus a $3 billion investment in SB Energy. (§ Key facts and numbers: “guaranteed $105 billion for phase one only, plus a $3 billion investment in SB Energy”) ^clm-89f123836c3cf166975f
- Nvidia invested $30 billion in OpenAI's recent funding round; the final $10 billion closed October 1, 2026. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Nvidia invested $30 billion in OpenAI's recent round”) ^clm-0cf15556de2ac669fb95
- In early August 2026 Huang gathered Blackstone, Apollo and Goldman Sachs to finance $500 billion of Nvidia hardware, and Nvidia may backstop up to 25% in some deals. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Nvidia may backstop up to 25% in some deals”) ^clm-3612e6a34df64983e50c
- Nvidia's credit default swap spreads widened in August 2026, signaling investor worry; Huang watched them closely and the OpenAI guarantee shrank to less than half the size first discussed. (§ Change versus earlier views: “the OpenAI guarantee shrank to less than half the size first discussed”) ^clm-c6e065cb5277de70ba37
- Huang publicly downplays the risk, telling a Goldman Sachs conference in September 2026: I'm not taking any risks. (§ Change versus earlier views: “I'm not taking any risks”) ^clm-bab5d68dff2841fd7466
