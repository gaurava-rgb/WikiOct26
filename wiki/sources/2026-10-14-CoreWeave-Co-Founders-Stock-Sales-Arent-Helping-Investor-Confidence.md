# CoreWeave Co-Founders’ Stock Sales Aren’t Helping Investor Confidence

Martin Peers, The Information, published 2026-10-14.

## Summary

CoreWeave shares fell 15.5% to $65 amid an inflation-driven sell-off in AI neocloud stocks and rising worries over debt-funded expansion, after issuing $4.2 billion in convertible notes in September. Filings showed CEO Michael Intrator sold stock every week from April through late September 2026. Smaller neoclouds Nscale and SB Energy struggled with public offerings, while Singapore data center operator AirTrunk postponed its REIT listing. Elsewhere, Berkshire Hathaway invested $330 million in Bending Spoons, Anthropic agreed to license security tech from Opaque Systems, and OpenAI compute strategy VP Sachin Katti announced his departure.
