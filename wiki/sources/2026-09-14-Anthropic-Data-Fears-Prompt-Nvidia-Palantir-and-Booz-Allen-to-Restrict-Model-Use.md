# Anthropic Data Fears Prompt Nvidia, Palantir and Booz Allen to Restrict Model Use

Laura Bratton and Aaron Holmes, The Information, published 2026-09-14.

## Summary

Firms doing sensitive work, including Palantir, Nvidia and Booz Allen Hamilton, are limiting use of Anthropic's flagship Fable model after Anthropic gave itself the right in June to keep 30 days of usage logs. Before that, some business customers had zero data retention. OpenAI faces similar suspicion after its research chief suggested customer data helped a math breakthrough. Customers are moving sensitive work to their own or open models, and Microsoft and Palantir are using the fears to position themselves between enterprises and the labs.

## Key claims

- In June 2026 Anthropic changed its policy for its flagship Fable model to give itself the right to keep logs of customer use on a rolling 30-day basis, saying this helps defend against complex, novel attacks. (§ The core story: “the right to keep logs of customer use on a rolling 30-day basis”) ^clm-9119ddf4f1357b57d17c
- Before the June change, Anthropic had given some business customers zero data retention, meaning it did not store their data at all. (§ The core story: “Anthropic had given some business customers zero data retention (ZDR)”) ^clm-35cb31591bc49d60b61f
- Both Anthropic and OpenAI say that by default they do not train on enterprise customers' inputs or outputs unless customers opt in, while individual users must opt out manually. (§ What the labs say they collect: “by default they do not train on enterprise customers' inputs or outputs unless customers opt in”) ^clm-732681379d05e89f1f40
- OpenAI Chief Research Officer Mark Chen posted that such data helps improve models, including the AI that solved the Navier-Stokes problem; a spokesperson later said he meant data customers chose to share. (§ What the labs say they collect: “including the AI that solved the Navier-Stokes math problem”) ^clm-0ee3b39a8dc0ff574d43
- OpenAI let customers store safety logs from GPT-5.6-Cyber users on their own servers in August 2026, and after customer blowback Anthropic launched a similar self-storage option in September, built with more than 100 customers. (§ Retention policy back and forth: “Anthropic this month launched a similar self-storage option, rolling out in phases this fall”) ^clm-218eb3774e5c59ef832e
- Palantir, which resells Anthropic models, will not offer Fable to its clients until Anthropic gives zero data retention it cannot later revoke. (§ Customer by customer: “Palantir will not offer Fable to clients until Anthropic gives ZDR it cannot later revoke”) ^clm-1fefa158949545de3016
- Palantir CEO Alex Karp said enterprises are tired of AI labs abusing them, and Palantir handed out a booklet on negotiating zero data retention. (§ Customer by customer: “enterprises are tired of AI labs abusing them”) ^clm-dd4e22a1eae6e23f5fa1
- Palantir and some others say they have zero data retention access to OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, which Palantir now offers. (§ Customer by customer: “they have ZDR access to OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, which Palantir now offers”) ^clm-d258b7d99aabeeb8d652
- The article notes Palantir's warnings are self-serving because it wants to be the buffer between businesses and the labs, a pitch Microsoft and Salesforce also make. (§ Customer by customer: “it wants to be the buffer between businesses and the labs”) ^clm-5d0098f9eac45dd3924a
- Nvidia uses Fable only for less sensitive work such as open-source projects and runs sensitive work, like AI supply chain monitoring, on its own Nemotron models. (§ Customer by customer: “Sensitive work, like AI supply chain monitoring, runs on Nvidia's own Nemotron models”) ^clm-3aafd5013488f2437bc6
- Booz Allen Hamilton bars staff from using Fable on its proprietary cybersecurity software because its CTO worries the model might learn from its code. (§ Customer by customer: “"might be learning from some of our code,"”) ^clm-4a52d3fcf600c3d2ebb7
- A large US utility dropped plans to test Fable for running core power infrastructure after Anthropic refused nonrevocable zero data retention. (§ Customer by customer: “after Anthropic refused nonrevocable ZDR”) ^clm-410fcedc16acddf244f5
- Northrop Grumman runs open-source models on air-gapped servers for coding and research, using models from Poolside, Luminary and Nvidia. (§ Customer by customer: “runs open-source models on air-gapped servers (machines cut off from the internet)”) ^clm-f27035b360b75fadae28
- Microsoft is using the data fears to lure OpenAI and Anthropic clients, per people involved, and offered one large client privately managed servers that send no data to outside AI providers. (§ Microsoft pounces: “Microsoft is using the fears to lure OpenAI and Anthropic clients”) ^clm-af4f196a11986fe9840b
