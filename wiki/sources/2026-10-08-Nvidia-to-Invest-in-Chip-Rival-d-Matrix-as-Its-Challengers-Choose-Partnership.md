# Nvidia to Invest in Chip Rival d-Matrix as Its Challengers Choose Partnership

Phoebe Liu, Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-10-08.

## Summary

Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, an AI inference chip startup that competes with it, as part of a push to make rival chips work alongside Nvidia GPUs inside its ecosystem. Rivals that could not dent Nvidia's lead now pair their chips with its GPUs, including Intel, Marvell, Amazon's Trainium and SambaNova. The approach, called disaggregated inference, could help Nvidia's networking business. The DOJ is probing whether Nvidia's $20 billion Groq deal was structured to avoid review.

## Key claims

- Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, a seven-year-old AI server chip maker that aims to compete with Nvidia, per three people with knowledge of the deal. (§ The core story: “Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, a seven-year-old maker of AI server chips”) ^clm-5d6003d8bb5de6f9aec5
- The d-Matrix investment is part of Nvidia's effort to make its technology work with rival chip designers and pull them into its dominant hardware ecosystem. (§ The core story: “pull them into its dominant hardware ecosystem”) ^clm-043641192d7e96997871
- It is Nvidia's first deal with an AI inference chip startup since its $20 billion licensing agreement with Groq in December 2025. (§ The core story: “first deal with an AI inference chip startup since its $20 billion licensing agreement with Groq”) ^clm-66a5bc8a67abd17ebfd7
- Rivals that found it hard to dent Nvidia's lead increasingly try to work alongside Nvidia GPUs instead of replacing them, which could help Nvidia's networking hardware. (§ Rivals choose partnership: “Bringing rivals into its ecosystem could help Nvidia's networking hardware”) ^clm-381c4ac7c29bb0c4c831
- In the past 12 months Nvidia took multibillion-dollar stakes in Intel and Marvell, partly to support integrating its GPUs with their chips. (§ Rivals choose partnership: “Nvidia took multibillion-dollar stakes in more established rivals Intel and Marvell”) ^clm-13df1b72f75719e225ee
- In August 2026 Amazon said Nvidia GPUs and Trainium chips could run in the same data center with similar racks, and Trainium would use Nvidia networking and memory hardware. (§ Rivals choose partnership: “Amazon also said Trainium would use Nvidia's networking and memory hardware”) ^clm-ce539863f6b1ec8d2bde
- In June 2026 SambaNova Systems showed a way to connect its chips with Nvidia GPUs to run AI models together. (§ Rivals choose partnership: “SambaNova Systems showed a way to connect its chips with Nvidia GPUs”) ^clm-50bd4ce4396df68c3a0e
- Nvidia is building a system combining its GPUs with Groq's inference chips; it hired Groq's founders and staff in the $20 billion licensing deal. (§ Rivals choose partnership: “Nvidia hired Groq's founders and other staff in the $20 billion licensing deal”) ^clm-04d8907f2dd2b7e4a027
- The Department of Justice is investigating whether Nvidia structured the Groq deal to avoid a formal antitrust review, per people familiar; the New York Times first reported it. (§ Antitrust probe: “The Department of Justice is investigating whether Nvidia structured the Groq deal”) ^clm-e1d4304faf713e9a777b
- Nvidia still says publicly that its own chips alone are the most efficient for nearly all AI workloads. (§ Disaggregated inference: “its own chips, alone, are the most efficient for nearly all AI workloads”) ^clm-b2a37ad463061caea891
- Anthropic and OpenAI are each developing their own inference chips and using other firms' chips to rely less on Nvidia. (§ Disaggregated inference: “Anthropic and OpenAI are each developing their own inference chips”) ^clm-67fe51c293043a92e3af
- In the planned system, d-Matrix chips run a small model that guesses a bigger model's answer, which an Nvidia GPU checks, a technique called speculative decoding. (§ What d-Matrix does: “d-Matrix chips run a small AI model that guesses a bigger model's answer”) ^clm-9c536b1053160ec307fe
- d-Matrix last raised $275 million at a $2 billion valuation in November 2025 and was reported to be seeking funding at a $5 billion valuation. (§ What d-Matrix does: “d-Matrix last raised $275 million at a $2 billion valuation in November 2025”) ^clm-150921d439804859a1fb
