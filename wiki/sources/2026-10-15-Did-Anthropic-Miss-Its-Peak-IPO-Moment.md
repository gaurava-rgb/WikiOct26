# Did Anthropic Miss Its Peak IPO Moment?

Martin Peers, The Information, published 2026-10-15.

## Summary

Anthropic's slowing third-quarter revenue growth has sparked investor debate over whether the AI lab delayed its initial public offering past its peak valuation moment. Manhattan Venture Partners projected an IPO valuation of $1.4 trillion to $1.5 trillion, below earlier $2 trillion expectations. Doubts persist around Anthropic's competitive moat as enterprises turn to cheaper models and the company carries over $500 billion in compute commitments. In contrast, Apple's capital-light strategy helped its shares rise 26% year to date as the only big tech firm with valuation multiple expansion.

## Key claims

- The Information argues Anthropic may have missed its peak valuation window by delaying its initial public offering past June 2026. (§ Slowing growth and IPO valuation ceiling: “Anthropic may have experienced its peak valuation moment around June 2026, when SpaceX completed its own public listing”) ^clm-46d9539dda1c4a27ccbc
