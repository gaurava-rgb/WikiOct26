# Why Upstart Banks May Have to Keep Raising Money

Yueqi Yang, The Information, published 2026-10-18.

## Summary

New venture-backed de novo banks chartered under the Trump administration face strict regulatory capital cushions, requiring 10% to 12% tier 1 leverage ratios for their first three years. Because newer banks lack retained earnings, surging deposits force repeated equity raises. Palmer Luckey's Erebor Bank is raising $1.5 billion at an $8 billion valuation as deposits reached $7 billion, subject to an FDIC capital-call requirement, while crypto-focused upstarts Openreserve and Augustus also prepare launches.

## Key claims

- De novo banks face 10% to 12% minimum tier 1 leverage ratios for their first three years, compared to 5% to 6% for established banks. (§ The regulatory capital treadmill for de novo banks: “unprofitable new banks (de novo banks) must maintain 10% to 12% tier 1 leverage ratios for their first three years”) ^clm-817213633458a5c3bad7
- Unprofitable startup banks must repeatedly raise equity from investors to fund regulatory capital cushions as deposits and assets expand. (§ The regulatory capital treadmill for de novo banks: “forcing them to raise fresh equity as deposits and assets expand”) ^clm-ea292a7e2d7bbdb51008
- Palmer Luckey's Erebor Bank is set to raise $1.5 billion at an $8 billion valuation after launching with $625 million in February 2026. (§ Erebor Bank rapid expansion and unusual FDIC conditions: “Erebor Bank is raising $1.5 billion in fresh equity at an $8 billion pre-money valuation”) ^clm-34fd969fd391db053710
- Erebor Bank deposits surged from $4 billion in June 2026 to $7 billion at the end of September 2026. (§ Erebor Bank rapid expansion and unusual FDIC conditions: “deposits surged to $7 billion at the end of September 2026, up from $4 billion the prior quarter”) ^clm-7b0fc706687207f31ef5
- The FDIC imposed a capital-call condition on Erebor Bank requiring investors to contribute additional equity if regulatory capital drops below minimums. (§ Erebor Bank rapid expansion and unusual FDIC conditions: “Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation imposed an unusual capital-call condition on Erebor, requiring backstop investors to contribute additional equity”) ^clm-d57fcb71f0dc4659e6aa
- Erebor Bank focuses on customers traditional banks avoid, including defense contractors, crypto firms, and businesses operating in Venezuela and Syria. (§ Erebor Bank rapid expansion and unusual FDIC conditions: “Erebor focuses on customers traditional banks avoid, including defense contractors, crypto firms, and businesses operating in Venezuela and Syria”) ^clm-de1442880067a14941ec
- Openreserve raised $25 million in September 2026 and expects another equity raise before launching in 2027 to tokenize trade receivables and issue stablecoins. (§ Digital asset upstarts Openreserve and Augustus: “Openreserve, an a16z crypto-backed digital bank led by founder Dee Choubey, raised $25 million in September 2026”) ^clm-44ca05fe59ade2a5d6bd
- Augustus raised $180 million led by Tiger Global at a $1 billion valuation in July 2026, focusing on international dollar clearing for fintech and crypto firms. (§ Digital asset upstarts Openreserve and Augustus: “Augustus, led by chief executive Ferdinand Dabitz, raised $180 million in a round led by Tiger Global Management at a $1 billion valuation”) ^clm-bf5d9a63e3c3a486b217
