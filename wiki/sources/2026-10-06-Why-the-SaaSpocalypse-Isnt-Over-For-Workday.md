# Why the SaaSpocalypse Isn't Over For Workday

Laura Bratton, Anita Ramaswamy, The Information, published 2026-10-06.

## Summary

AI is unlikely to replace Workday's core HR and finance records, but it may cap Workday's growth. Some customers use outside agents from Anthropic, Microsoft and others to pull data out of Workday, and many who bought Workday's own AI tools barely use them. The reporters frame the risk as Workday becoming a trusted database that agents call, while the valuable work happens above it. Workday plans to charge when outside AI touches its data but has waived those fees until 2027, and revenue growth is slowing.

## Key claims

- Some Workday customers now use outside agents from Anthropic, Microsoft and others to pull data out of Workday, so nobody visits the app. (§ The core story: “use outside agents (Anthropic, Microsoft and others) to pull data out of Workday”) ^clm-22eeed44c734f9d00b91
- The reporters argue the risk is that Workday becomes dumb infrastructure, a trusted database agents call, while the valuable work happens above it. (§ The core story: “a trusted database that agents call, while the valuable work happens above it”) ^clm-ab7c93341ddd41acb1b5
- At one consultancy with more than 400 Workday customers, about half signed deals with usage-based AI access but only 10% to 20% actively use Workday AI, and none use Sana Enterprise. (§ Key facts and numbers: “only 10% to 20% actively use Workday AI; none use Sana Enterprise”) ^clm-1462478c222ad2b8769e
- Workday will charge when outside AI reads or changes its data, but it waived API overage fees from May 2026 to February 2027. (§ Key facts and numbers: “waived API overage fees from May 2026 to February 2027”) ^clm-38585545f942c563a8ac
- 5,500 of Workday's about 11,500 customers use its agents, up 35% quarter on quarter in the July 2026 quarter, per the company; it is unclear how many pay. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Customers using Workday's agents: 5,500 of about 11,500, up 35% quarter on quarter”) ^clm-cdf35f7fa78fb5c6614d
- Workday said in August 2026 its AI agents plus Sana are on pace for $600 million a year, three times a year earlier. (§ Key facts and numbers: “AI agents plus Sana on pace for $600 million a year”) ^clm-ed985aa026dc2881504a
- Workday's revenue growth slowed to 12.8% in the July 2026 quarter, analysts expect 9.7% in the January 2027 quarter, and its market cap is about $45 billion. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Overall revenue growth slowed to 12.8% in the July 2026 quarter”) ^clm-b4109106c78c5f350531
- Prediction: Analysts expect Workday's revenue growth to slow to 9.7% in the January 2027 quarter, per Koyfin. (§ Key facts and numbers: “analysts expect 9.7% in the January 2027 quarter”) ^clm-a061a8a34a88cfa3c7a8
- Reuters reported in August 2026 that Silver Lake considered taking Workday private. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Silver Lake considered taking Workday private”) ^clm-8ff55f7f56d773aa3ea3
- Workday founders Duffield and Bhusri hold 69% of voting power; the company laid off about 400 people in February 2026 and announced another 500 in late September. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Founders Duffield and Bhusri hold 69% of voting power”) ^clm-8d063ee44a772a882c8b
- Co-founder Aneel Bhusri has been back as Workday CEO since February 2026. (§ Named people and roles: “Aneel Bhusri, co-founder, back as CEO since February 2026”) ^clm-f19fd9fc20ce8bf2cbca
- Workday CTO Gabe Monroy says the focus is adoption, and he is leading work to make its in-house XpressO code language usable by AI coding tools. (§ Named people and roles: “make its in-house XpressO code language usable by AI coding tools”) ^clm-2b6e59c3ba999b193a53
- Adviser Raphaelle D'Ornano summed up the situation: Workday is called, not visited, and the toll is waived. (§ Named people and roles: “Workday is called, not visited, and the toll is waived”) ^clm-a190c2da765eb557fe54
- The earlier fear that AI kills enterprise apps outright has faded and Workday shares partly recovered; the new worry is slower growth and lost pricing power. (§ Change versus earlier views: “The new worry is slower growth and lost pricing power”) ^clm-5c9debb06e561fdd11da
