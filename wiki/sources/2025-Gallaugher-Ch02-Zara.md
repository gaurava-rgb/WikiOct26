# Gallaugher, Information Systems v10 (2025): Ch02 Zara - Fast Fashion from Savvy Systems

John Gallaugher, FlatWorld, published 2025.

## Summary

Gallaugher's chapter explains how Inditex, founded by Amancio Ortega, used targeted information systems to make Zara the world's largest fashion retailer. Store managers gather customer wishes on handheld devices, designers at The Cube turn that data into small batches, and a vertically integrated, Spain-centered supply chain with RFID tagging gets new styles to stores in about two weeks. The chapter contrasts this with Gap's inventory collapse, H&M's outsourced model, and Prada's failed high-tech store, and shows how the model yields fewer markdowns, higher margins, and almost no advertising. It closes with Zara's omnichannel push and COVID-19 response, its risks (two Spanish hubs, currency, environment), and new threats from rivals, especially Shein.
