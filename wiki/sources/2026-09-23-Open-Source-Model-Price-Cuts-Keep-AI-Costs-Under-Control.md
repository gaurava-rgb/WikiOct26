# Open Source, Model Price Cuts Keep AI Costs Under Control

The Information Staff, The Information, published 2026-09-23.

## Summary

Session write-ups from The Information's AI Agenda Live conference say open source models and steep price cuts from OpenAI and Anthropic are helping companies control AI costs. Uber held token spend flat after blowing through its AI budget, and firms are said to be moving from token-maxxing to value-maxxing, though EY says only one in ten companies can show AI return on investment. Speakers also covered AI-driven layoffs at Block and Cloudflare, agent reliability, Blackstone's appetite for lab debt, and lab economics such as compute contract sizes and data center costs.

## Key claims

- EY's Dan Diasio said only one in ten companies can show AI return on investment in its profit and loss statement, and most scaling has run on enthusiasm rather than evidence. (§ The core story: “only one in ten companies can show AI return on investment”) ^clm-2b3ad75841a9bf1c970e
- Uber held its AI token spending mostly flat despite wider use, after AI coding tools blew through its annual AI budget within months. (§ Uber holds token spend flat (Tiffany Li): “AI coding tools blew through its annual AI budget within months”) ^clm-94dd5373b364ac8081e8
- Replit CEO Amjad Masad said Replit uses open-source models probably less than in January 2026 because OpenAI cut prices hard. (§ Replit: OpenAI price cuts slow open-source use (Kevin McLaughlin): “Replit uses open-source models "probably less" now than in January because OpenAI cut prices hard”) ^clm-8d8da5cb076eef593bc3
- OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and Luna, priced 50% below the same tiers of its prior model. (§ Replit: OpenAI price cuts slow open-source use (Kevin McLaughlin): “priced 50% below the same tiers of the prior model”) ^clm-59ecb850f35ac08c508d
- Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, which it says costs 40% less to run than its prior top models while matching or beating them. (§ Replit: OpenAI price cuts slow open-source use (Kevin McLaughlin): “Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5, which it says costs 40% less to run”) ^clm-468afc4edfa185365844
- Atlassian's Tamar Yehoshua warned that open-source models carry serving costs plus hidden costs in the in-house expertise needed to run them. (§ Replit: OpenAI price cuts slow open-source use (Kevin McLaughlin): “hidden costs in the in-house expertise needed to run them”) ^clm-4fccae2f6791c1ee1f65
- Nebius said firms are moving from token-maxxing to value-maxxing, and Nebius is open to charging by outcome instead of per GPU hour. (§ Uber holds token spend flat (Tiffany Li): “Nebius is open to charging by outcome instead of per GPU hour”) ^clm-29413f573a1b6110c9bb
- Blackstone's venture that buys Google TPUs and leases them to developers plans to buy more given the chip shortage, and Blackstone may buy AI lab debt at the right price. (§ Blackstone may buy AI labs' debt (Dakin Campbell): “Blackstone's venture that buys Google TPUs (Google-designed AI chips) and leases them to developers plans to buy more”) ^clm-26cfa872882d12d9a3e2
- Block cut nearly half its staff in February 2026; its agent Builderbot can build up to 80% of a new feature, making code review the new bottleneck. (§ Block and Cloudflare on AI-driven layoffs (Michael Roddan): “Block cut nearly half its staff in February”) ^clm-0212839b085f855210f3
- Cloudflare cut about 20% of staff in May 2026; month-end finance decks went from nearly a week to an hour. (§ Block and Cloudflare on AI-driven layoffs (Michael Roddan): “Cloudflare cut about 20% of staff in May”) ^clm-ae494759c0e2137f322a
- Core Automation CEO Jerry Tworek said models ace math and coding contests but are not much more reliable at everyday work, so people do not trust agents. (§ Neolabs: AI needs reliability (Rocket Drew): “not much more reliable at everyday work, so people do not trust agents”) ^clm-2ee09a57b995ae89473a
- OpenAI and Anthropic are racing to lock in more than $1 trillion of compute contracts. (§ Lab economics (Phoebe Liu): “OpenAI and Anthropic are racing to lock in more than $1 trillion of compute contracts”) ^clm-babb946b683942fafd1a
- Volta CEO Ricard Boada said frontier labs earn $75 million per megawatt a year against a levelized compute cost of $15 million to $20 million. (§ Lab economics (Phoebe Liu): “frontier labs earn $75 million per megawatt a year”) ^clm-a9f525e551892f369283
- Volta signed a $10 billion Norway compute deal with a frontier lab, reportedly Anthropic. (§ Lab economics (Phoebe Liu): “Volta signed a $10 billion Norway compute deal with a frontier lab, reportedly Anthropic”) ^clm-1bf83d03025ff9f82c49
- Crusoe's Chris Dolan said a gigawatt data center site costs $15 billion to $20 billion to build plus $30 billion to $40 billion in chips. (§ Lab economics (Phoebe Liu): “a gigawatt site costs $15 billion to $20 billion to build”) ^clm-367d100e322dbbcc9adf
