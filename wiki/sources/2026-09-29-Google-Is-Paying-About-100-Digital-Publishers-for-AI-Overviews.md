# Google Is Paying About 100 Digital Publishers for AI Overviews

Alix Coutures and Catherine Perloff, The Information, published 2026-09-29.

## Summary

Google has been paying about 100 digital publishers in a pilot based on how much their content contributes to AI answers in AI Overviews, AI Mode and Gemini. This reverses Google's long stance that search traffic was payment enough, after AI Overviews sharply cut that traffic. Payments are mostly tiny, often under a tenth of 1% of ad revenue, though one early joiner earns over $1 million a year. Lawsuits from Penske Media and regulators in the U.K. and EU have pushed Google toward licensing deals and an opt-out from AI features.

## Key claims

- Google has been paying roughly 100 digital publishers based on how much their content contributes to AI answers in search and other products, per people familiar. (§ The core story: “Google has been paying roughly 100 digital publishers based on how much their content contributes”) ^clm-b11dfd166d318690a9b8
- The pilot covers AI Overviews, AI Mode and the Gemini chatbot and started less than a year ago. (§ The core story: “The pilot covers AI Overviews (AI summaries at the top of search results), AI Mode and the Gemini chatbot”) ^clm-927830ada2c8baa26383
- Google long argued it should not pay publishers whose content answers queries because search sends them traffic, but AI Overviews sharply cut that traffic. (§ The core story: “Google long argued it should not pay publishers whose content answers queries”) ^clm-5d252025fb9b98068fba
- For several small and midsize sites, Google's payments are less than one-tenth of 1% of their ad revenue; an early joiner earns more than $1 million a year. (§ How much it pays: “payments are less than one-tenth of 1% of their ad revenue”) ^clm-d04a1b1d61457f95c0fa
- Some larger publishers are staying out of the pilot because payouts are small, hoping to pressure Google and others to pay more, per three people. (§ How much it pays: “Some larger publishers are staying out because payouts are small”) ^clm-cf94629150396b3ec266
- Prediction: If enough publishers stay, the pilot could become the model for how other AI companies pay websites. (§ How much it pays: “this could become the model for how other AI companies pay websites”) ^clm-94ab05e7398bc7cf99bb
- Only a few sources meaningfully improve an answer: if the model checked 100 sites, perhaps only five would count, and topics like anime and gaming seem to earn more. (§ How value is judged: “If the model checked 100 sites for an answer, perhaps only five (5%) would meaningfully improve it”) ^clm-0bc143e233f4ca6c2c3b
- Google checks AI answers against fresh search content to cut hallucinations, because models trained on old internet content go stale. (§ How value is judged: “Google checks answers against fresh search content to cut hallucinations”) ^clm-184a15678bc980761fa8
- Google licensed Reddit data to train Gemini in 2024 and signed a January 2025 deal with the Associated Press for a real-time news feed in the Gemini app. (§ Google's evolving stance: “2024: licensed Reddit data to train Gemini”) ^clm-0ee61fb3d55ed74f7b84
- World History Encyclopedia says its Google traffic fell 25% to 35% in each of the past two years and is down about 50% from 2024. (§ The traffic damage: “Google traffic fell 25% to 35% in each of the past two years”) ^clm-04b0939622d47a16853a
- Penske Media sued Google in September 2025 claiming AI Overviews drained traffic and affiliate revenue and illegally tied AI Overview inclusion to search indexing. (§ Legal and regulatory pressure: “claimed AI Overviews drained traffic and cut affiliate revenue”) ^clm-8b63a2211d080d8988b0
- In June 2026 U.K. regulators ordered Google to let publishers opt out of AI search features without losing regular search ranking. (§ Legal and regulatory pressure: “U.K. regulators ordered Google to let publishers opt out of AI search features”) ^clm-86c7983906ff8395de82
- In December 2025 the European Commission opened an antitrust probe into whether Google uses publisher content for AI without fair pay. (§ Legal and regulatory pressure: “the European Commission opened an antitrust probe into whether Google uses publisher content”) ^clm-ec31a5ec50407beead90
- In August 2026 Google finished an opt-out letting publishers refuse use in AI Overviews, AI Mode or Google Discover without losing search visibility. (§ Legal and regulatory pressure: “Google finished an opt-out letting publishers refuse use in AI Overviews, AI Mode or Google Discover”) ^clm-68a80505faa4bf79ac5a
