# Andreessen Horowitz to Drop ‘Crypto’ From Name of Flagship Blockchain Fund

Leo Schwartz, The Information, published 2026-10-22.

## Summary

Andreessen Horowitz is rebranding its dedicated blockchain unit from a16z crypto to a16z frontier, broadening its mandate to artificial intelligence, compute infrastructure, and robotics. Across five funds the group raised nearly $10 billion, but its May 2026 vehicle of $2.2 billion fell to less than half its 2022 predecessor. Founding partner Ali Yahya is leaving, while general partner Chris Dixon acknowledged consumer Web3 shortfalls and repositioned the sector around stablecoins and agentic payments.

## Key claims

- Andreessen Horowitz informed limited partners that its dedicated blockchain unit, a16z crypto, will change its name to a16z frontier and broaden beyond crypto into emerging technologies.
