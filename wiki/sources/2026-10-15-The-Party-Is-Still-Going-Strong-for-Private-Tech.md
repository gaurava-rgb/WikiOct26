# The Party Is Still Going Strong for Private Tech

Valida Pau, The Information, published 2026-10-15.

## Summary

Investors at Morgan Stanley's private tech conference exhibited a stark disconnect between troubled public tech markets and buoyant private startup fundraising. While public investors dumped shares of CoreWeave and Nebius over mounting infrastructure debt and Australian operator Firmus yanked its IPO, private tech investors eagerly backed inference providers Fireworks AI, Baseten, and Fal. Meanwhile, data center builder Nscale sought to raise up to $3 billion in a public listing at an ambitious $35 billion valuation.

## Key claims

- Morgan Stanley technology equity head Diana Doyle observed a stark sentiment disconnect as private markets reward growth while public markets enforce strict financial discipline. (§ Public versus private market sentiment divergence: “Diana Doyle, head of technology equity capital markets at Morgan Stanley, noted that private markets still prioritize and reward growth above all else”) ^clm-94964eaedf3ea0257784
- Australian data center operator Firmus withdrew its planned initial public offering amid rising public market skepticism over AI infrastructure debt. (§ Public versus private market sentiment divergence: “Australian data center developer Firmus pulled its planned initial public offering amid rising public market skepticism”) ^clm-9c2e549c35e21cf8f740
- Public market investors sold off shares of CoreWeave and Nebius amid escalating bond yields and concerns over debt-heavy AI infrastructure financing. (§ Public versus private market sentiment divergence: “surging yields on CoreWeave debt, a broad semiconductor selloff, and sharp share price drops for CoreWeave and Nebius”) ^clm-26dc2552a4de4d20647f
- Data center operator Nscale sought to raise up to $3 billion in an initial public offering at a valuation exceeding $35 billion, per the Financial Times. (§ AI infrastructure debt and valuation pushback: “Nscale sought to raise up to $3 billion in an initial public offering at a valuation exceeding $35 billion”) ^clm-fa9e2fe2f425c722ec4e
- Mutual fund managers at Morgan Stanley's tech conference questioned whether debt-saddled data center operators can successfully go public at target valuations. (§ AI infrastructure debt and valuation pushback: “Fund managers questioned whether debt-laden data center operators can successfully complete public offerings at requested price targets”) ^clm-3fa01358ccd123898fdc
- Inference startups Fireworks AI, Baseten, and Fal experienced surging developer demand as enterprises adopted open-source models that undercut OpenAI and Anthropic. (§ High demand for private inference and defense startups: “These inference providers are experiencing surging developer demand as enterprises adopt open-source models that undercut pricing from OpenAI and Anthropic”) ^clm-05f42f7e1f9337a09c66
- Defense technology startups Anduril, Hadrian, and Saronic attracted intense private investor demand following multi-billion dollar valuation rounds. (§ High demand for private inference and defense startups: “Defense technology startups including Anduril, Hadrian, and Saronic attracted strong investor interest after closing multi-billion dollar valuation rounds”) ^clm-d122072d5682a7a6425e
- Morgan Stanley tech investment banking chief David Chen argued that public listings provide an essential competitive weapon by unlocking access to convertible debt. (§ High demand for private inference and defense startups: “public market access remains a decisive competitive weapon because public listings unlock flexible capital instruments like convertible bonds”) ^clm-3dcb2372343cab79826a
