# The Attractive Economics Behind Anthropic Data Center Sprint

Ann Davis Vaughan, The Information, published 2026-10-11.

## Summary

Anthropic is pushing partners to speed up $530 billion of data center leases because each server switched on sooner can be very profitable. At a 330 MW Hut 8 site in Louisiana running Google chips from Q2 2027, one month of early operation could add $1.3 billion or more in revenue at an estimated 85% gross margin if used only for inference. Former bitcoin miners with scarce grid rights have become key to Anthropic's race toward 15 GW against OpenAI. The economics are so strong that AI inference could afford power at over 100 times normal grid prices, which could let data centers trade flexible grid use for faster hookups.

## Key claims

- Anthropic is pushing its partners to speed up the $530 billion of data center leasing deals it has signed, because every server plugged in sooner could be very profitable. (§ The core question: “Anthropic is pushing its partners to speed up the $530 billion of data center leasing deals”) ^clm-03c9dd7b7d668f6719dd
- Anthropic will run Google's AI chips at a 330-megawatt Hut 8 site in St. Francisville, Louisiana starting in Q2 2027; Fluidstack will lease and operate it, and Hut 8 wants to grow the site to 1 GW. (§ The Louisiana site: “Anthropic will run Google's AI server chips at a 330-megawatt site, starting in the second quarter of 2027”) ^clm-5cb298e48431540be7c9
- Hut 8 is fairly new to AI infrastructure and runs a legacy bitcoin mining business with Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump. (§ The Louisiana site: “runs a legacy bitcoin mining business with Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump”) ^clm-713abb6162afe37e0051
- Anthropic has committed to rent or lease about 15 GW of capacity in coming years from AWS, Google, SpaceXAI and more than a dozen others, and has an unannounced multiyear cloud deal with IREN, per people familiar. (§ The scale of Anthropic's compute race: “has committed to rent or lease about 15 GW of capacity in the coming years”) ^clm-4ad7c497ef90ad71343d
- Prediction: Measured AI projects Anthropic's compute capacity at 1.4 GW in 2025, 5 GW in 2026, 9.5 GW in 2027 and over 15 GW in 2028; Microsoft's whole Azure cloud ran at about 5 GW at the end of 2023. (§ The scale of Anthropic's compute race: “1.4 GW in 2025, 5 GW in 2026, 9.5 GW in 2027 and 15+ GW in 2028”) ^clm-a1430578fa76dad07f6a
- Anthropic's ability to beat OpenAI in the compute race will depend more on former bitcoin firms such as Hut 8, TeraWulf, IREN and Nscale, some of which struggle to raise public money at the valuations they want. (§ The scale of Anthropic's compute race: “will depend more and more on Hut 8, TeraWulf, IREN, Nscale and other current or former bitcoin firms”) ^clm-4f8d6b9ac8c8aa19d852
- Anthropic's data center partners matter because they hold grid-connected power rights at old aluminum smelters, failed green hydrogen sites and other places bigger cloud providers missed, and such rights are rare given long grid waits. (§ Valuable energy rights: “Such rights are rare and very valuable, given long waits to connect new projects to the grid”) ^clm-de328d1cdff1e66ec842
- Vertiv is redesigning its cooling and power gear for rapid installation, packaging prefabricated bundles in its factories that cut typical installation time in half, CEO Giordano Albertazzi said. (§ Building faster: “cutting typical installation time in half”) ^clm-ff69e64ce7864104dc6e
- Prediction: The Louisiana campus uses 330 MW of power for 245 MW of usable compute, and bringing it online one month early could earn Anthropic an extra $1.3 billion or more in revenue if used entirely for inference, per investor and analyst estimates. (§ Why speed is worth so much: “Anthropic could make an extra $1.3 billion or more in revenue”) ^clm-9ac3ed07c00d2d0411ca
- Christian Okoye of Occam Edge estimates Anthropic might pay about $200 million a month to lease chips for the Louisiana site, implying an 85% gross margin there if used only for inference; the margin excludes training, payroll and other costs. (§ High margin: “That implies an 85% gross profit margin in the Louisiana example”) ^clm-c91441d2d502ea5f4f56
- Anthropic went from spending about $2.30 on operations per $1 of revenue in spring 2025 to a slight profit on that basis in the June 2026 quarter, helped by strong inference economics. (§ High margin: “from spending about $2.30 on operations per $1 of revenue in spring 2025”) ^clm-4182d4bfe2aa9f958d4e
- At the start of 2026 Anthropic projected its model-training costs would be more than twice its inference spending, and its 2026 server rentals from SpaceX and others will depress margins. (§ Caveats: “projected its model-training costs would be more than twice its inference spending”) ^clm-a62d80bd14ff7e9ac38a
- Ann Davis Vaughan argues Anthropic may be the only company with real pricing power, since nobody else running generative AI apps, including OpenAI, seems to earn near its inference gross margin. (§ Caveats: “Anthropic may be the only company with such pricing power”) ^clm-f641f87c71f9aab336ef
- Developers say Anthropic will accept cutting power use when the grid is strained, running on backup power, or building farther from customers to get data center power quickly. (§ Getting power fast: “it focuses on getting power quickly, even if that means cutting electricity use when the grid is strained”) ^clm-484bc22ca47b6cfa1627
- Hans Royal of Royal Powers Energy estimates that in Q3 2026 commercial AI inference facilities could profit even at $5,631 per megawatt-hour of power, over 100 times the typical $50 wholesale price, and top labs like Anthropic at $12,791. (§ Compute Heat Rate: “could make money even at a power cost of $5,631 per megawatt-hour”) ^clm-df7f43045e484dbdac55
- A Royal Powers and Occam Edge paper argues AI data centers could get partial power sooner by agreeing to leave the grid for a few peak hours a year, which could lower power costs for everyone by spreading utility costs over more revenue. (§ A better trade: flexible power: “if they agree to leave the grid for a few peak hours a year”) ^clm-eb8af59ec0cda67dd0bb
- Prediction: Hut 8's tax payments on the Louisiana site are expected to triple West Feliciana Parish's annual revenue to $130 million from $40 million, and the parish passed a law letting it cut residents' property taxes 25%. (§ Local finances in Louisiana: “triple the parish's annual revenue to $130 million from $40 million”) ^clm-1880d8837e3dbb78fdb4
- Google is unlocking about 890 MW by funding upgrades at 11 nuclear reactors and contracting another 2.7 GW in a supply deal with Constellation Energy, easing the Mid-Atlantic grid's AI power shortage. (§ Other news: “by funding upgrades at 11 reactors, plus a contract for another 2.7 GW”) ^clm-5870ba442165a015b953
