# Anthropic in Talks for 1-Gigawatt Data Center Lease With Apollo-Backed Developer

Valida Pau and Amir Efrati, The Information, published 2026-09-22.

## Summary

Anthropic is in early talks to lease up to 1 gigawatt of data center capacity from Stream Data Centers, an Apollo-owned developer, and fill it with Google TPUs, possibly with a Google credit guarantee. The aim is to rely less on cloud providers and skip their profit margin, as OpenAI is also doing. A gigawatt needs at least $40 billion of capital, and Anthropic has signed $531 billion of compute deals in 11 months. The real limit is developers that already hold power and permits.

## Key claims

- Anthropic is in early talks to lease up to 1 gigawatt of compute capacity from Stream Data Centers, a developer majority-owned by Apollo Global Management, to rely less on cloud providers. (§ The core story: “lease up to 1 gigawatt of compute capacity from **Stream Data Centers**”) ^clm-e635d57fa832a7fd6ea1
- Anthropic would be a direct tenant of the Stream sites and fill them with TPUs, AI chips designed by Google and Broadcom, per people with knowledge of the talks. (§ The core story: “Anthropic would be a direct tenant of Stream sites and fill them with TPUs”) ^clm-b5d855443228a4920dd2
- Anthropic has discussed Google giving a credit guarantee for the Stream project, and has sought Google backstops for other leases before. (§ The core story: “Anthropic has also discussed Google giving a credit guarantee for the project”) ^clm-d4dbc2f7d2337b844fdf
- Data center developers say a gigawatt of capacity needs at least $40 billion of capital investment. (§ The core story: “a gigawatt of capacity needs at least $40 billion of capital investment”) ^clm-57448d0d3185c3857fa2
- The Stream deal is months from done and could fall apart; Anthropic would need financial guarantees for the leases and financing for the chips. (§ The core story: “The deal is months from done and could fall apart”) ^clm-ba375f54af6fa78fa540
- Anthropic today gets most of its compute by renting chip servers from AWS, Google and SpaceX. (§ Why leases instead of clouds: “most of its compute by renting chip servers from AWS, Google and SpaceX”) ^clm-b34a0967fb912a3670ec
- Anthropic agreed to lease smaller sites from former bitcoin miners Hut 8 and TeraWulf, giving it direct control over servers and a way to cut costs by skipping cloud providers' profit. (§ Why leases instead of clouds: “a way to cut costs by skipping cloud providers, which want their own profit”) ^clm-0bbf1bee3b7b65cf44b0
- Leasing data centers shifts equipment and staff costs onto the labs; Anthropic is hiring dozens of people for roles like securing power and facility design. (§ Why leases instead of clouds: “the labs carry the cost of the equipment and the staff”) ^clm-fc06bb30c15033d1cf42
- Earlier in 2026 Anthropic struck a deal with AMD for 2 gigawatts worth of chips. (§ Anthropic's other compute deals: “a deal with AMD for 2 GW worth of chips”) ^clm-a618b75631be52f5bb43
- Anthropic has a $35 billion agreement to lease TPUs from a special purpose vehicle set up by Apollo and Blackstone. (§ Anthropic's other compute deals: “A $35 billion agreement to lease TPUs from a special purpose vehicle”) ^clm-ea75846da927e012ae60
- Broadcom said it wants to work with financial firms to finance more than 20 gigawatts of compute, implying about $700 billion in chip purchases. (§ Anthropic's other compute deals: “finance more than 20 GW of compute, implying about $700 billion in chip purchases”) ^clm-06c00b38951c076e8ca0
- Anthropic signed $531 billion of compute deals in the last 11 months, mostly with AWS, Google and Microsoft. (§ Anthropic's other compute deals: “Anthropic signed $531 billion of compute deals in the last 11 months”) ^clm-4bafd89b440ea84b8867
- In August 2026 OpenAI agreed to lease the first phase of a planned 10-gigawatt data center in Ohio, with Nvidia credit support covering up to $105 billion. (§ The race with OpenAI: “with Nvidia credit support covering up to $105 billion”) ^clm-29902c9bfa99368eec47
- Only a limited number of developers have power supplies and permits lined up, so labs compete for them. (§ The race with OpenAI: “Only a limited number of developers have power supplies and permits lined up”) ^clm-154b5cb3bc3c9008735d
