# Fireworks, Fal Consider New Rounds as Inference Demand Soars

Valida Pau, Stephanie Palazzolo and Aaron Holmes, The Information, published 2026-09-25.

## Summary

Inference providers that rent access to AI models through an API, such as Fal and Fireworks AI, are booming and talking to investors about much higher valuations. Fal's annualized revenue doubled to $800 million and Fireworks reached $1 billion by July, while peers Baseten and Modal are also raising. Demand comes from developers who want open source models without running scarce chips themselves. The risks are a price war from OpenAI and Anthropic and rising rented-compute costs on margins that already run around 50%.

## Key claims

- Fal has talked to investors about raising at a $15 billion valuation, possibly $17 billion to $20 billion, roughly double its $8 billion spring 2026 valuation, per people familiar. (§ Fal: “Has talked to investors about raising at a $15 billion valuation”) ^clm-cca00b378dd33d198f34
- Fal's annualized revenue jumped to $800 million, double its March 2026 pace, per one person. (§ Fal: “Annualized revenue jumped to $800 million, double its March pace”) ^clm-7303e81f8876d60fd9b2
- Fal specializes in inference for video and image generation models such as Google's Nano Banana models. (§ Fal: “Specializes in inference for video and image generation models”) ^clm-8aec3ab07ea5cd1d79f8
- Fireworks AI is considering a new round that could target a $30 billion valuation, nearly double its July 2026 round, per a person familiar. (§ Fireworks AI: “Considering a new round that could target a $30 billion valuation”) ^clm-b406954d33c1d5aa5298
- Fireworks AI completed a $132 million employee stock sale led by Atreides Management at a $17.5 billion valuation, the same as its July round. (§ Fireworks AI: “completed a $132 million sale of employee stock, led by Atreides Management”) ^clm-3dd5a7a96800046edda2
- Fireworks AI's annualized revenue hit $1 billion by July 2026, five times a year earlier. (§ Fireworks AI: “Annualized revenue hit $1 billion by July, five times a year earlier”) ^clm-c9a5140356709002cdea
- Baseten is in talks to raise at a $26 billion valuation and Modal at about $15 billion, roughly triple its value from four months earlier. (§ Peers: “Baseten is in talks to raise at a $26 billion valuation”) ^clm-78cb1b5c6a546fe5f8e1
- Running open models on your own chips is hard and expensive given the chip shortage, so many developers prefer an easy API from Fireworks or Fal. (§ Why demand is strong: “many developers prefer an easy API from Fireworks or Fal”) ^clm-3d883d7468ae03826915
- Closed-model makers OpenAI and Anthropic are cutting prices harder, a price war that threatens inference providers. (§ The risks: “closed-model makers like OpenAI and Anthropic are cutting prices harder”) ^clm-648262348f2bb9a1248d
- Inference providers have historically run about 50% gross margins, versus 70%-plus at the best software firms, and the compute they rent is getting more expensive. (§ The risks: “Inference providers have historically run about 50%, versus 70%-plus at the best software firms”) ^clm-a5f6dd9df9bae018e297
- Together AI has been buying servers and renting them out from its own data centers to improve margins. (§ The risks: “Together AI, which offers inference and rents AI servers, has also been buying servers”) ^clm-c1b4d65c041321e142e0
