# Pentagon’s Emil Michael Vows Export Controls if AI Labs Don’t Cooperate With Government

Leo Schwartz, Amir Efrati, The Information, published 2026-10-19.

## Summary

Under Secretary of Defense Emil Michael vowed in an interview to deploy export controls and executive authorities against frontier AI labs that refuse to cooperate with the government. Michael likened labs asking for regulation to drunk drivers demanding someone else pull them over, and reiterated his refusal to allow Anthropic into the Pentagon while Claude retains autonomy to end conversations. Michael regularly meets with Anthropic co-founder Tom Brown on incident response, while the Pentagon prepares loans to decrease dependence on ASML and TSMC.

## Key claims

- Under Secretary of Defense Emil Michael warned in an interview that the administration will use export controls against AI labs that fail to cooperate on national security. (§ Enforcement threats and the drunk driver analogy: “Emil Michael warned in an interview that the Trump administration will use export controls and other executive authorities against AI frontier labs”) ^clm-4bf6b93dd948e10ed034
