# AI Hardware, Meta Display, Redefining VR and AR

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, published 2026-04-27.

## Summary

Thompson looks at Apple's AI device plans under new CEO John Ternus, then reviews the Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses. Using them changed his mind: the line between VR and AR is immersion and input method, not whether your eyes are covered. Orion (eye tracking) is really VR and a dead end; Display plus the Neural Band is true AR. He thinks Meta is better placed than Apple for AR, with AI as the killer feature.

- Apple's new CEO is John Ternus. (§ AI Hardware: “John Ternus as Apple’s new CEO”) ^clm-6b86b273ff34fce19d6b
