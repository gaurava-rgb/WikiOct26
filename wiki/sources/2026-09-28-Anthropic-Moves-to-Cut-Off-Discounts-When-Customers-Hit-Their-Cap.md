# Anthropic Moves to Cut Off Discounts When Customers Hit Their Cap

Kevin McLaughlin, The Information, published 2026-09-28.

## Summary

Anthropic ends a customer's committed-spend discount as soon as the customer uses up the tokens in its contract, a harder line than OpenAI or the big clouds take. Anthropic's discounts run around 15% and it reportedly earns about a 75% gross margin on existing models, while more than 100 firms spent over $10 million each with it. OpenAI, behind on revenue, is competing on price with 50% cuts and more aggressive discounts, winning customers like CodeRabbit. Both labs add share-of-wallet clauses, while some customers worry about the labs learning from their intellectual property.

## Key claims

- Once an Anthropic customer uses up the tokens in its contract, its discount ends and it must sign a new deal or pay higher prices, per managers at three software firms. (§ The core story: “once a customer uses up the tokens (units of AI usage) in its contract, the discount ends”) ^clm-f5917554e2e578344f79
- Amazon, Microsoft and Google usually keep discounted prices on spending above the commitment for the rest of the contract term, per NPI Financial's Jeff Muscarella. (§ How others handle it: “Amazon, Microsoft and Google usually keep discounted prices on spending above the commitment”) ^clm-eb7235455c07341711c6
- When customers hit their commitment, OpenAI gives the rest of the calendar month plus one more month to negotiate before switching to list prices. (§ How others handle it: “OpenAI gives the rest of the calendar month plus one more month to negotiate”) ^clm-99be1acd3e77d72a3851
- Anthropic's discounts can be around 15% off list price, per three software firms. (§ The numbers: “Anthropic's discounts can be around 15% off list price”) ^clm-7ae68adeeb71c8a7102d
- More than 100 firms spent over $10 million each with Anthropic in the 12 months ending June 2026, and more than 1,000 spent over $1 million each. (§ The numbers: “More than 100 firms spent over $10 million each with Anthropic in the 12 months ending June”) ^clm-3a7391ff267a77365067
- Meta and Cursor are big Anthropic customers; Claude Code and Claude Cowork both got pricier as Anthropic changed pricing. (§ The numbers: “Big customers include Meta and Cursor”) ^clm-cc08f6fb83c0304090ff
- Anthropic told at least one partner it earns about a 75% gross margin on its existing models. (§ The numbers: “Anthropic told at least one partner it earns about a 75% gross margin”) ^clm-b342f1fbd10ad8f3db3f
- Anthropic zoomed far ahead of OpenAI in revenue in 2026 and widened that lead between May and August. (§ OpenAI's push to catch up: “Anthropic zoomed far ahead of OpenAI in revenue this year”) ^clm-321ac1798e7bd5d3648a
- OpenAI cut prices of new models 50% for customers buying through intermediaries like OpenRouter and Vercel, and Sam Altman said the new models are half the price per token. (§ OpenAI's push to catch up: “OpenAI cut prices of new models 50% for customers buying through intermediaries”) ^clm-241bf74221f785bc8f66
- CodeRabbit switched its main AI supplier from Anthropic to OpenAI over six months; it spends tens of millions a year on AI. (§ OpenAI's push to catch up: “Six months ago CEO Harjot Gill said Anthropic was its main supplier; now OpenAI is”) ^clm-e242028e213e94e4f3b5
- The executive leading OpenAI's enterprise push left abruptly in August 2026, and OpenAI is merging pre-sale and post-sale roles. (§ OpenAI sales shake-up: “The executive leading OpenAI's enterprise push left abruptly in August”) ^clm-ccff7d4fea4e0522d9e6
- Both labs now add share-of-wallet clauses asking big customers to send most of their AI spend to them; consultants say the labs cannot verify it. (§ Share of wallet and trust: “Both labs now add "share of wallet" clauses”) ^clm-be4109d055bdcb1baa4c
- Worries that the labs could learn from customers' intellectual property have led Palantir, Nvidia and Booz Allen Hamilton to demand guarantees or cut use of the most advanced models. (§ Share of wallet and trust: “have led Palantir, Nvidia and Booz Allen Hamilton to demand new guarantees”) ^clm-5172f1f9fb58f6ade051
