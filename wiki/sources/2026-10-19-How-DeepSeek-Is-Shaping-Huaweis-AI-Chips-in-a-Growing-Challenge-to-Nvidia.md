# How DeepSeek Is Shaping Huawei’s AI Chips in a Growing Challenge to Nvidia

Qianer Liu, Juro Osawa, The Information, published 2026-10-19.

## Summary

DeepSeek is collaborating closely with Huawei Technologies to shape future Ascend AI chips, with Huawei engineers stationed at DeepSeek to adapt hardware for frontier training without Nvidia. DeepSeek plans to use Huawei Ascend 950DT chips for pre-training by late 2026 and receive priority supply of the Ascend 960DT in 2027. While Epoch AI estimates Huawei chips trail Nvidia B300s by 7x in arithmetic performance, DeepSeek is using open-source TileLang to break CUDA lock-in while continuing to access smuggled Blackwell chips.

## Key claims

- DeepSeek is collaborating closely with Huawei Technologies to shape future AI chip designs, with Huawei engineers stationed at DeepSeek offices. (§ DeepSeek and Huawei engineering co-design: “DeepSeek is collaborating closely with Huawei Technologies to shape future AI chip designs”) ^clm-6e2680d565a72fba2462
- DeepSeek plans to begin using Huawei current flagship Ascend 950DT chip for pre-training models as early as late 2026. (§ DeepSeek and Huawei engineering co-design: “DeepSeek plans to begin using Huawei current flagship Ascend 950DT chip for pre-training models”) ^clm-bfe5655d0038748913c6
- Huawei plans to allocate most of the initial supply of its next-generation Ascend 960DT training chip to DeepSeek in the first quarter of 2027. (§ DeepSeek and Huawei engineering co-design: “Huawei plans to allocate most of the initial supply of its next-generation Ascend 960DT training chip to DeepSeek”) ^clm-1b039e26d4e3c30f1542
- Huawei announced an annual Ascend processor roadmap: Ascend 960DT in early 2027, Ascend 970 in 2028, and Ascend 980 in 2029. (§ DeepSeek and Huawei engineering co-design: “Huawei announced an annual release cadence for Ascend processors: Ascend 960DT in the first quarter of 2027”) ^clm-8a534eee7c25dcf031bc
- Epoch AI estimates Nvidia B300 chips deliver approximately seven times the raw arithmetic performance of Huawei Ascend 950. (§ Performance gap and covert access to Nvidia Blackwell: “Epoch AI estimates that Nvidia B300 chips deliver approximately seven times the raw arithmetic performance”) ^clm-b657ef6ebd1e7eaac87d
- Prediction: Epoch AI projects Huawei will remain three to four years behind Nvidia on single-chip performance through 2030. (§ Performance gap and covert access to Nvidia Blackwell: “Epoch AI projects Huawei will remain three to four years behind Nvidia on single-chip performance through 2030”) ^clm-59cecb4a7c89a52a45c6
- DeepSeek obtained barred Nvidia Blackwell chips by disassembling servers bought in permitted countries and shipping the chips to China to train V4 models. (§ Performance gap and covert access to Nvidia Blackwell: “DeepSeek obtained barred Blackwell chips via intermediaries who purchased servers in permitted countries”) ^clm-223ac291c20548ea3a7b
- DeepSeek is bypassing Nvidia CUDA software lock-in using TileLang, an open-source programming language developed by Peking University researchers. (§ Breaking CUDA lock-in with TileLang: “DeepSeek is bypassing Nvidia CUDA software lock-in using TileLang, an open-source programming language”) ^clm-cbc07a8e60cad697ddbc
- DeepSeek released a TileLang version tailored for Huawei Ascend 950 chips with five code suites covering calculations and data transfers. (§ Breaking CUDA lock-in with TileLang: “DeepSeek used TileLang for its V4 models and recently released a TileLang version tailored for Huawei Ascend 950 chips”) ^clm-31f7dc1aa7b0bd6dc2b5
- Huawei Rotating Chairman Eric Xu stated training tests went well and expects widespread adoption by 2027 while domestic supply remains constrained. (§ Breaking CUDA lock-in with TileLang: “Rotating Chairman Eric Xu stated training tests went well and expects widespread adoption by 2027”) ^clm-061c00ff503f9e5bf02f
- Full pre-training of a frontier AI model requires well over 10,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT processors running uninterrupted for weeks. (§ Breaking CUDA lock-in with TileLang: “Full pre-training of a frontier model requires well over 10,000 Ascend 950DT processors running uninterrupted for weeks”) ^clm-30d2c22f4450782cf3a3
