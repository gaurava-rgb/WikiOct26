# The Closing of the ‘Defenders Window’ Is Nigh

Leo Schwartz, Nick Wingfield, The Information, published 2026-10-21.

## Summary

OpenAI led an industry effort to patch critical vulnerabilities in PHP as frontier labs warn the defender's window is closing against unrestricted Chinese open-weight models like Z.ai's GLM-5.3. The Trump administration's Super Intelligence Force stepped in to coordinate remediation in its first major engagement with OpenAI. Meanwhile, Meta reported Q3 revenue up 26% to $64.6 billion and 10 million Muse users, but negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion on heavy AI capex, prompting the creation of non-voting Class C shares to fund compute without diluting Mark Zuckerberg's control.

## Key claims

- Since August 2026, an OpenAI-led industry coalition has worked quietly to fix critical vulnerabilities in PHP, an open-source web language. (§ The narrowing defender's window: “OpenAI-led industry coalition has worked quietly to fix critical vulnerabilities in PHP”) ^clm-d54feccc9bb3cc99d969
- OpenAI warned that the defender's window, the period when defenders armed with frontier AI can detect and patch vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them, is rapidly closing. (§ The narrowing defender's window: “defender's window is shrinking rapidly”) ^clm-f1f549ad1d8f81e4d57f
- Anthropic warned in late September 2026 that Chinese lab Z.ai's GLM-5.3 model trailed United States frontier models by only four months in cyber capabilities. (§ The narrowing defender's window: “Z.ai's GLM-5.3 model trailed United States frontier models by only four months”) ^clm-1fe49104028371c2cf31
- The Trump administration's Super Intelligence Force stepped in to coordinate government communications and push parties to deploy the PHP security patch. (§ The narrowing defender's window: “Super Intelligence Force stepped in to coordinate government communications”) ^clm-9f00e0357bdb214a1221
- In late October 2026, OpenAI briefed National Intelligence Director Jay Clayton and senior defense official Emil Michael in its first major engagement with the Super Intelligence Force. (§ The narrowing defender's window: “briefing National Intelligence Director Jay Clayton and senior defense official Emil Michael”) ^clm-4cad92c4f83889896707
- Meta Platforms reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $64.6 billion, up 26% year over year, while Reality Labs trimmed its operating losses. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “third-quarter 2026 revenue of $64.6 billion, up 26% year over year”) ^clm-808ecee8a2d98f48b69b
- Meta recorded negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion in third-quarter 2026 due to AI infrastructure investments, down from positive $10.63 billion a year earlier. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion, down from positive free cash flow of $10.63 billion”) ^clm-14c306ceb0083d3decd1
- Meta reaffirmed full-year 2026 capital expenditures of $135 billion to $145 billion. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “reaffirmed full-year 2026 capital expenditures of $135 billion to $145 billion”) ^clm-b1dcc921139a0ac1f98e
- Meta Chief Executive Mark Zuckerberg announced that the Muse personal agent app reached 10 million users in the United States and Canada within six weeks of launch. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “Muse personal agent app reached 10 million users in the United States and Canada”) ^clm-68d550a6369839284f99
- Meta plans to monetize Muse through commerce transaction fees paid by businesses and token fees for power users accessing advanced models. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “monetization planned through commerce transaction fees and fees for advanced model tokens”) ^clm-088bf12c677fb7b90d7a
- Meta incurred nearly $10 billion in legal charges in the third quarter of 2026, reducing operating income by 37% to just under $13 billion. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “incurred nearly $10 billion in legal charges in the third quarter”) ^clm-a9d1b575c9e295d552ea
- Meta announced a new class of non-voting Class C common stock, effective January 19, 2027, issued as a 1:1 dividend to existing Class A and B shareholders to fund AI investments without diluting Mark Zuckerberg's voting control. (§ Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock: “new class of non-voting Class C common stock, effective January 19, 2027”) ^clm-42c2f037554b0f24e6ad
- Tesla reported third-quarter 2026 revenue flat at $28 billion, with automotive revenue falling 3% to $20.56 billion. (§ Tesla and Microsoft updates: “flat third-quarter revenue of $28 billion, with automotive revenue falling 3% to $20.56 billion”) ^clm-15b1156c059fc3d0fb32
- Microsoft asked shareholders to approve a 6% cut to Chief Executive Satya Nadella's pay following revenue drops in Xbox gaming. (§ Tesla and Microsoft updates: “6% cut to Chief Executive Satya Nadella's pay following revenue drops in its Xbox”) ^clm-4d16e4a91aa3f3c89343
