# Alibaba's AliExpress Tests Membership Program in Challenge to Amazon

Juro Osawa, The Information, published 2026-09-30.

## Summary

AliExpress, Alibaba's international shopping site, is testing an invitation-only, fee-free membership called Elite for customers who spend over $800 a year, as a challenge to Amazon Prime. The move comes as the direct-from-China parcel model weakens under U.S. tariffs and new EU per-parcel fees, pushing AliExpress, Shein and Temu toward branded goods and better service. AliExpress is also testing next-day delivery from local European warehouses. After heavy losses from its earlier ultra-low-price Choice push, AliExpress posted an operating profit in the quarter through June.
