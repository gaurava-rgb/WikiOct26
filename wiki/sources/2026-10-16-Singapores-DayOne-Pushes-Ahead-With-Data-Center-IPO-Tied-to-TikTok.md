# Singapore’s DayOne Pushes Ahead With Data Center IPO Tied to TikTok

Claudia Chong, Juro Osawa, The Information, published 2026-10-16.

## Summary

Singapore-based data center operator DayOne is pushing ahead with a New York IPO to raise up to $5 billion despite market jitters that derailed listings by AI cloud firms. TikTok parent ByteDance accounts for nearly 70% of DayOne revenue, though that capacity serves conventional workloads rather than AI. DayOne is expanding into AI data centers with Western customers like Oracle, while neocloud Firmus and Blackstone-backed AirTrunk faced IPO setbacks.

## Key claims

- Singapore-based data center operator DayOne is preparing an initial public offering in New York to raise as much as $5 billion. (§ The core story: “DayOne Data Centers, a Singapore-based data center operator, is preparing for an initial public offering in New York to raise as much as $5 billion”) ^clm-d34a23c88ac7ff7562ef
- ByteDance accounts for nearly 70% of DayOne revenue, representing 69.2% of sales in the first half of 2026. (§ The core story: “ByteDance accounts for nearly 70% of DayOne revenue”) ^clm-be1aaf55d4dbfc08c71e
- DayOne revenue from ByteDance supports conventional workloads like video streaming and e-commerce rather than AI training or inference. (§ The core story: “revenue from ByteDance supports conventional uses like video streaming and e-commerce transactions, not AI training or inference”) ^clm-25453dd7d765f0b0e5be
- GDS International spun off DayOne in late 2024 after raising $1.87 billion, leaving Coatue, Hillhouse, and GDS each with about 19% stakes. (§ Corporate origins and international expansion: “In late 2024, GDS International was spun off after raising $1.87 billion in Series A and B rounds”) ^clm-9e88019c90b86ed1d5b3
- As of September 2026, ByteDance committed to 838 megawatts of data center capacity across DayOne sites in Malaysia, Thailand, and Finland. (§ Corporate origins and international expansion: “ByteDance had committed to 838 megawatts in capacity across DayOne sites in Malaysia, Thailand, and Finland”) ^clm-f5d3fba059a1765df445
- TikTok is investing 2 billion euros ($2.2 billion) in two Finnish data centers in Kouvola and Lahti under Project Clover to store European user data locally. (§ Corporate origins and international expansion: “TikTok is investing 2 billion euros ($2.2 billion) under Project Clover to store European user data locally”) ^clm-a0efc43f207f5fe7ca9f
- Oracle is DayOne second-largest customer, generating 15% of DayOne revenue in the first half of 2026. (§ AI ambitions and diversification away from ByteDance: “Oracle is DayOne second-largest customer, representing 15% of first-half 2026 revenue”) ^clm-9011ab03030f1f0c50a4
- Prediction: For contracts starting in 2027, ByteDance represents less than 50% of DayOne total commitments as non-Chinese customers expand. (§ AI ambitions and diversification away from ByteDance: “For contracts starting in 2027, ByteDance represents less than 50% of total commitments”) ^clm-668ce00240fc4baaebd5
- Nvidia-backed neocloud Firmus Technologies scrapped its $5.5 billion IPO in Australia amid investor worries over its $30.6 billion valuation and debt. (§ Market context and rival IPO stumbles: “Firmus Technologies scrapped its $5.5 billion IPO last week due to concerns over its $30.6 billion valuation and debt pile”) ^clm-969d991bc7c4118a1c25
- Blackstone-backed data center operator AirTrunk postponed a $1.5 billion real estate investment trust listing in Singapore. (§ Market context and rival IPO stumbles: “Blackstone-backed data center operator AirTrunk postponed a $1.5 billion real estate investment trust listing in Singapore”) ^clm-539c243406196ed84c94
- In June 2026, Firmus agreed to partner with DayOne on a data center in Batam, Indonesia, with at least 170,000 advanced Nvidia chips, with Meta leasing capacity. (§ Market context and rival IPO stumbles: “Firmus agreed to work with DayOne on a data center in Batam, Indonesia, with at least 170,000 advanced Nvidia server chips”) ^clm-bce3b565b118481ac857
