# SpaceX Overhauls Data Center Build-Out, Potentially Slowing Expansion

Grace Kay, The Information, published 2026-09-10.

## Summary

SpaceX rocket engineers now run xAI's data centers and are trading speed for reliability, adding and testing backup power and cooling before sites go live. This reverses the 2024 Colossus approach, where xAI brought 100,000 GPUs online in 122 days but with little redundancy, temporary gas turbines and mixed equipment. Reliability matters more now that SpaceX rents compute to Anthropic and Google, and a September outage in Memphis hit customers. More than a dozen data center leaders have left, some to OpenAI and Anthropic, and the change may slow new campuses.

- A new team of SpaceX rocket engineers now runs xAI's data centers and is trading speed for reliability, which may slow new campuses, per people familiar. (§ The core story: “They are trading speed for reliability, which may slow new campuses”) ^clm-f0ff32c332045feb9a28
