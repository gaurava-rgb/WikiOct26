# Google Earnings, Meta Earnings

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, published 2026-05-04.

## Summary

Thompson compares Q1 2026 results from Alphabet and Meta. Google's 81% net income jump came mostly from marking up private investments, very likely its Anthropic stake, while operating profit rose 30% and Google Cloud grew 63% with a 33% margin. He asks how much of that Cloud boom is Anthropic buying TPU compute. Meta's core business grew faster, yet its stock fell because Zuckerberg asked investors to trust a long-term AI bet on personal agents and self-improving models. Thompson backs Meta's consumer focus and repeats his view that having no cloud business is an advantage.

## Key claims

- Alphabet reported Q1 2026 revenue of about $110 billion, up 22%, and net income of $62.6 billion, up 81% year over year. (§ Google Earnings: “Alphabet’s sales reached about $110 billion, exceeding analyst expectations. Net income was $62.6 billion”) ^clm-4fb66734e15d91957985
- Google's cloud backlog grew to $460 billion in Q1 2026, up from $240 billion the prior quarter, and Google expects to record about half of it as revenue over the next two years. (§ Google Earnings: “its cloud backlog ballooned to $460 billion, up from $240 billion in the prior quarter”) ^clm-d8779378a425f96443b0
- Alphabet raised its 2026 capital spending forecast to $180 to $190 billion, from $175 to $185 billion. (§ Google Earnings: “revised its estimates for capital expenditures this year to $180 billion to $190 billion”) ^clm-eb61e9d62a4bcf348754
- Thompson argues the more pertinent profit figure is Google's 30% operating profit growth, not the 81% net income jump, though 30% still outpaced 22% revenue growth. (§ Google Earnings: “The more pertinent number, however, is 30%, which is how much Google’s operating profit increased”) ^clm-d3c5872658add5702c76
- Google's other income (expense), net jumped 237% to $37.7 billion in Q1 2026, which explains the rest of the net income increase. (§ Google Earnings: ““Other income (expense), net”, which jumped 237% to $37.7 billion”) ^clm-1d7a566dbdd7e215b82f
- As of March 31, 2026, Google's non-marketable equity securities (stakes in private companies) were carried at $101.3 billion, of which $73.6 billion was remeasured at fair value during the quarter. (§ Google Earnings: “was $101.3 billion, of which $73.6 billion was remeasured at fair value during the three months”) ^clm-781b453943529e8dff74
- Anthropic raised $30 billion at a $380 billion valuation in February 2026, a 108% increase over its $183 billion valuation in September 2025. (§ Google Earnings: “that was a 108% increase over its $183 billion valuation”) ^clm-a06e39cc581a7da9ba62
- Thompson argues the markup of Google's Anthropic stake, about 14% as of March 2025, is almost certainly the biggest driver of Google's jump in other income. (§ Google Earnings: “significant enough to almost certainly be the biggest driver by far of that increase in profit”) ^clm-8264bdab2b36f95e1611
- Google Cloud reported Q1 2026 revenue of $20.0 billion, up 63%, and operating profit of $6.6 billion, a 33% margin, up from 30% in Q4 2025. (§ Google Earnings: “Q1 2026 | $20,028 | 63% | $6,598 | 33%”) ^clm-9b462387a7f2f6ae3c68
- Thompson's table shows Google Cloud turned its first quarterly profit in Q1 2023, $191 million on $7.45 billion of revenue, after losing money every quarter since at least Q4 2019. (§ Google Earnings: “Q1 2023 | $7,454 | 28% | $191 | 3%”) ^clm-bbe2a34738e1e2674044
- In October 2025 Anthropic said it would expand its use of Google Cloud to up to one million TPUs, worth tens of billions of dollars and bringing well over a gigawatt of capacity online in 2026. (§ Google Earnings: “including up to one million TPUs, dramatically increasing our compute resources”) ^clm-f7092ff1b588384d2513
- Sundar Pichai said Enterprise AI solutions became Google Cloud's primary growth driver for the first time, and revenue from products built on Google's gen AI models grew nearly 800% year over year in Q1. (§ Google Earnings: “Our Enterprise AI solutions have become our primary growth driver for Cloud for the first time”) ^clm-9e135056c3c8f65e8203
- Thompson notes Enterprise AI solutions include Vertex AI, which also serves Anthropic models, so it is unclear whether the growth reflects Gemini usage or Anthropic inference running on Google Cloud. (§ Google Earnings: “what is certain is that Gemini usage is a separate question entirely”) ^clm-9dcc121b928b4223aefc
- Thompson suggests the gap between Google's strong results and near-silent industry chatter about Gemini may reflect the split between the startup world and enterprise software, or Anthropic showing up in the results in several ways. (§ Google Earnings: “Anthropic is all over these results in more ways than one”) ^clm-7b29cce21d6e9f0720f5
- Alphabet stock rose 10% after earnings, giving Google its best month since 2004, while Meta shares fell almost 9%, their steepest drop since October 2025. (§ Meta Earnings: “While Alphabet jumped 10% for the day, Meta shares plunged almost 9%”) ^clm-4ceda1aed256e376764b
- Meta reported Q1 2026 revenue of $56.3 billion, up 33%, its biggest jump in nearly five years, and net income of $26.8 billion, of which $8 billion came from a tax benefit. (§ Meta Earnings: “$8 billion of net income was the result of a tax benefit”) ^clm-095d45fcbd5dcd2f81d9
- Meta said it would spend more on AI data centers in 2026 than it had previously forecast. (§ Meta Earnings: “would spend even more money to build out AI data centers this year than it had previously forecast”) ^clm-ccd1eaba5800ad8e197a
- Thompson argues Wall Street rewarded Google because Google Cloud shows an AI infrastructure payoff now, while Zuckerberg spent Meta's call on versions of 'trust me'. (§ Meta Earnings: “on various permutations of “trust me”. This was the most important thing he said”) ^clm-d8f0d9f1d558a3c8c29a
- Zuckerberg said investors should watch whether Meta can build personal agents that just work, grow them to billions of users, and then make them profitable. (§ Meta Earnings: “whether we can build the version [of personal agents] that really like just works”) ^clm-9f1f998d3e7fb3673496
- Zuckerberg said models that cannot improve themselves will not stay leading, so recursive self-improvement is table stakes for Meta's lab. (§ Meta Earnings: “the companies that don’t do that are not going to be leading labs”) ^clm-c51851c279f41c9d441c
- Thompson argues Zuckerberg framed Meta's AI spending as protecting terminal value: without it, Meta could not make compelling new products and would lose its hold on consumer attention. (§ Meta Earnings: “Meta’s AI investment is not just about future growth, but also terminal value”) ^clm-0d7422740c4a0033f73b
- Thompson stands by his view that Meta's lack of a cloud business will prove an advantage, since serving consumers costs it no forgone enterprise sales, while Google must weigh serving compute-hungry customers like Anthropic. (§ Meta Earnings: “Meta’s lack of a cloud business will ultimately prove to be an advantage”) ^clm-1a208b189dc0f2046370
- Zuckerberg argued AI agents get better when the whole stack is optimized, so Meta builds frontier models, agents, and infrastructure top to bottom. (§ Meta Earnings: “AI agents get better when you fully optimize the stack”) ^clm-f2f73d3c83c19bcbe69d
- Zuckerberg said no other lab talks about building an AI that is really good at shopping, which he framed as part of Meta's personal superintelligence vision. (§ Meta Earnings: “I don’t hear any other labs out there talking about how they’re building an AI that’s really good at shopping”) ^clm-1b6fce8cdf9d081c6e3b
- Prediction: Thompson argues there is an opening to build AI for people, especially if OpenAI shifts its attention to fighting Anthropic for the enterprise, and it suits Meta's advertising engine. (§ Meta Earnings: “especially if OpenAI shifts all of its attention to fighting with Anthropic for the enterprise”) ^clm-ad296695d2844cd8fce4
