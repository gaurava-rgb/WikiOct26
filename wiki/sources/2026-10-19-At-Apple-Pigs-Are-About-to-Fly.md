# At Apple, Pigs Are About to Fly

Nick Wingfield, The Information, published 2026-10-19.

## Summary

Apple issued invitations for a product event teasing a touchscreen MacBook Pro, reversing a decade-long refusal under former CEO Tim Cook, who previously compared touchscreen laptops to combining a toaster and refrigerator. The reversal comes under new CEO John Ternus, who has pushed Apple into previously avoided hardware categories including the iPhone Duo foldable and HomeView smart display. Meanwhile, Elon Musk pledged an $11 billion SpaceX equity gift to American children, and the Department of Homeland Security banned DeepSeek from federal civilian systems.

## Key claims

- Apple sent invitations for an October 2026 product event teasing the line 'Can't wait to pro and tell next week', indicating the launch of its first touchscreen MacBook Pro. - A touchscreen MacBook reverses a decade of company resistance under former CEO Tim Cook, who in 2012 rejected touchscreen laptops by comparing them to converging a toaster and refrigerator. (§ Touchscreen MacBooks and the Ternus product era: “reverses a decade of corporate policy under former CEO Tim Cook”) ^clm-1d79b3769ec657640681
- Touchscreen capability has become common in the laptop industry, appearing on approximately one in five notebook computers sold by late 2026. (§ Touchscreen MacBooks and the Ternus product era: “Touchscreens are now standard in the market, present on roughly one in five laptops sold”) ^clm-aa452ff1101aa84a0428
- The hardware pivot reflects the leadership of CEO John Ternus, a longtime hardware engineering executive who is more product-driven than Tim Cook was. (§ Touchscreen MacBooks and the Ternus product era: “leadership changes under CEO John Ternus, a longtime hardware engineering executive”) ^clm-90f34deb7fbd9a67ea31
