# Amazon Earnings, Trainium and Commodity Markets, Additional Amazon Notes

Ben Thompson, Stratechery, published 2026-04-30.

## Summary

Amazon's Q1 2026 results: AWS grew 28%, the fastest since 2022, and Amazon's chip business is now large. Thompson's frame: cloud and AI tokens are commodities, and commodity markets are won on cost, so Trainium's cheaper cost structure should pay off as demand shifts from training to inference and agents. Also: Jassy warms to third-party shopping agents, sees ads thriving in agentic shopping, and uses sports to acquire Prime members.

## Key claims

- AWS revenue grew 28% in Q1 2026. (§ Amazon Earnings: “Amazon Web Services grew 28%, the fastest pace since 2022”) ^clm-e7f6c011776e8db7cd33
- Amazon Q1 2026 total revenue rose 17% to $181.5 billion, and net profit increased 77% to $30.3 billion. (§ Amazon Earnings: “Revenue for the period rose 17% to $181.5 billion, while net profit increased a sharp 77% to $30.3 billion”) ^clm-7902699be42c8a8e46fb
- Amazon Trainium chips annual revenue run rate is over $20 billion, making AWS a top 3 data center chip business. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “our custom silicon business is now one of the top 3 data center chip businesses in the world”) ^clm-2c624232cdd221771294
- AWS has over $225 billion in revenue commitments for Trainium, including from Anthropic and OpenAI. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “we now have over $225 billion in revenue commitments for Trainium”) ^clm-19581e27de7ced00ff1c
- Meta committed to using tens of millions of Graviton CPU cores, which offer up to 40% better price performance than other x86 processors. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “Meta is committed to using tens of millions of Graviton cores”) ^clm-4fc82b26aecb47d2868c
- Sam Altman confirmed OpenAI's Bedrock Managed Agents is currently an AWS-exclusive. (§ Additional Amazon Notes: “Sam Altman was pretty clear that this was an AWS-only thing for now”) ^clm-6b51d431df5d7f141cbe
- Jassy says Amazon's chip business grew nearly 40% quarter over quarter in Q1 2026 and has an annual revenue run rate over $20 billion, growing triple digits year over year. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “our annual revenue run rate is now over $20 billion and growing triple-digit percentages year-over-year”) ^clm-bdf83c9776a9958879c8
- Jassy says that if Amazon's chips business sold to AWS and third parties as a standalone company, its run rate would be $50 billion. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “our annual revenue run rate would be $50 billion”) ^clm-deb009b6bca4ec2da9be
- Trainium2 has about 30% better price performance than comparable GPUs and is largely sold out; Trainium3, shipping since early 2026, has 30% to 40% better price performance than Trainium2. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “Our Trainium2 chip has about 30% better price performance than comparable GPUs and is largely sold out”) ^clm-1b982aadfe863ef4891b
- Amazon Bedrock is used by over 125,000 customers and runs most of its inference on Trainium; almost 80% of the Fortune 100 use Bedrock. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “Amazon Bedrock, which is used expansively by over 125,000 customers, runs most of its inference on Trainium”) ^clm-9ce1bff424b089bbe490
- Graviton is used by 98% of the top 1,000 EC2 customers, according to Jassy. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “now used by 98% of the top 1,000 EC2 customers”) ^clm-6bd6be987cda30a62b34
- Prediction: Jassy expects Trainium to save Amazon tens of billions of dollars of capex each year and give several hundred basis points of operating margin advantage versus relying on others' chips. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “we expect Trainium will save us tens of billions of dollars of CapEx each year”) ^clm-87b4d9d1be3c62f5ca44
- Amazon's Q1 2026 net profit jump was attributed to pretax income from its investment in Anthropic. (§ Amazon Earnings: “which Amazon attributed to pretax income from its investment in Anthropic”) ^clm-2142dcd77db4cc138757
- Thompson argues that in a commodity market the lowest-cost supplier earns the most, so if Trainium delivers tokens more cheaply than Nvidia, AWS profits will be larger; power is the one constraint that would favor Nvidia's tokens-per-watt. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “to the extent that AWS can deliver tokens from Trainium with a meaningfully cheaper cost structure relative to Nvidia”) ^clm-c40b6055bf2baa89bccb
- Thompson judges that for the next few years the US has found enough power and chips are the critical constraint, which makes Trainium look very profitable. (§ Trainium and Commodity Markets: “the U.S. has scrounged up enough power; the more critical constraint is chips”) ^clm-a82950a6ede395c50a31
- Jassy argues AWS will win the agentic era because of its cost structure from its own chips and because it won the commodity cloud era, so customers' data is already there. (§ Amazon Earnings: “Jassy believes AWS is set to benefit from the agentic era of AI”) ^clm-68900ca801eea1bc3ce3
- Jassy said memory price and supply shortages push companies from on-premises servers into the cloud, since suppliers prioritize the largest customers. (§ Additional Amazon Notes: “it is a further impetus pushing companies who have on-premises infrastructure into the cloud”) ^clm-06d0d96824e1f55ece73
- Jassy said Amazon wants to work with third-party horizontal shopping agents, though agentic commerce referrals are a small fraction of search-engine referrals today. (§ Additional Amazon Notes: “we’ll do a lot of work with third-party horizontal agents”) ^clm-45195098beeb7020ff14
- Jassy argues advertising will do well in agentic commerce because multi-turn conversations create many chances to show sponsored products and sponsored prompts; Rufus is Amazon's own shopping agent. (§ Additional Amazon Notes: “I actually believe that advertising will do well in a world of agentic commerce”) ^clm-06b3d9af71385ecd8d9a
- Amazon says Prime Video is now a large and profitable business, and Thompson argues sports rights pay off mainly as a way to acquire Prime members. (§ Additional Amazon Notes: “Prime Video is now a large and profitable business in its own right”) ^clm-6fcfeae2bf55a2304028
