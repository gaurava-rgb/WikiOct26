# Anthropic's In-House Payments Tech Push Could Chip Away at Stripe

Stephanie Palazzolo, The Information, published 2026-09-04.

## Summary

Job postings show Anthropic plans to build more of its own billing, fraud detection and treasury systems, because soaring usage and token-based pricing make collecting money hard. With annualized revenue near $45 billion, payment fees and billing tools are now worth owning, which could reduce its reliance on Stripe. The posts do not say Anthropic will drop outside processors. The move fits a wider drift, as OpenAI added Adyen and Shopify added PayPal, while Stripe buys OpenRouter and Metronome to keep its AI revenue.

## Key claims

- Job postings show Anthropic wants to build more billing, fraud detection and other financial systems itself and is weighing which payment services to build instead of buy. (§ The core story: “Anthropic wants to build more billing, fraud detection and other financial plumbing itself”) ^clm-a5f390b555d22f888c49
- Soaring usage and complex token-based pricing make collecting money hard for Anthropic. (§ The core story: “soaring usage and complex token-based pricing”) ^clm-413c8d450cd192e13401
- Anthropic uses many Stripe products beyond core processing, including Link checkout, Radar fraud scoring, invoicing and usage-based billing. (§ The core story: “Link checkout, Radar (real-time fraud scoring), invoicing and usage-based billing”) ^clm-289ceaa02081fae01e34
- Anthropic's annualized revenue rose nearly fivefold to almost $45 billion in the five months to May 2026. (§ What the job posts say: “rose nearly fivefold to almost $45 billion in the five months to May”) ^clm-7847a3be956ce8d91ec8
- An Anthropic job post says the business is scaling faster than the processes and systems that support it. (§ What the job posts say: “is scaling faster than the processes and systems that support it”) ^clm-78b8262767dc96182c23
- An Anthropic treasury job post says the team is building financial apps no vendor has built yet. (§ What the job posts say: “the team is building financial apps no vendor has built yet”) ^clm-05bb277e3e4b0f2822e2
- Stripe earns most revenue by taking 2.9% of payment volume plus a fixed per-transaction fee, with big customers often paying less. (§ Why build in-house: “Stripe earns most revenue by taking 2.9% of payment volume plus a fixed per-transaction fee”) ^clm-672e6dd6983f0a006749
- The job posts do not say Anthropic plans to drop outside payment processors, and fully replacing Stripe would take a long time. (§ Why build in-house: “The posts do not say Anthropic plans to drop outside processors”) ^clm-8d5e1c03949aef2d8296
- In August 2026 OpenAI, a longtime Stripe customer, added Adyen as an extra payment provider. (§ The wider shift away from Stripe: “OpenAI, a longtime Stripe customer, added Adyen as an extra payment provider”) ^clm-922ce5e11037d3ab830c
- Earlier in 2026 OpenAI moved storage of customer card data from Stripe's vault to an intermediary, making it easier to use several processors. (§ The wider shift away from Stripe: “OpenAI moved storage of customer card data to an intermediary”) ^clm-97c39fb5cdaf259d7616
- Stripe is buying OpenRouter, which sits between developers and AI model providers, for more than $7 billion. (§ The wider shift away from Stripe: “Stripe buying OpenRouter for more than $7 billion”) ^clm-c56f64a418c2a8186851
- Stripe's revenue growth sped up to about 33% in 2025, its fastest since the 2021 e-commerce boom. (§ The wider shift away from Stripe: “Stripe's revenue growth sped up to about 33% last year”) ^clm-d77665422c8a7128a624
- In January 2026 Stripe bought usage-based billing startup Metronome for a reported $1 billion to meter and charge for AI. (§ The wider shift away from Stripe: “Stripe bought usage-based billing startup Metronome for a reported $1 billion”) ^clm-c53ac1ed447f77eff7d0
- Shopify added PayPal for some US transactions in 2024 and is getting state money transmitter licenses, which could cut its need for Stripe. (§ The wider shift away from Stripe: “Shopify added PayPal for some U.S. transactions two years ago”) ^clm-a1c9d86aefe4beeaf8d4
