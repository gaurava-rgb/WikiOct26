# Anthropic's Big Charity Bill for Shareholders

Cory Weinberg, The Information, published 2026-10-04.

## Summary

Financials Anthropic shared with prospective IPO investors show a large non-cash cost from matching employees' stock pledges to charity with new company stock. The match cost more than $660 million from October 2025 to March 2026 and $540 million in 2025, far above any Fortune 500 peer, and it dilutes other shareholders. Anthropic excludes it from adjusted operating profit, which Calcbench's CEO calls uncommon. The reporter argues the expense could grow into the billions after the IPO as employees cash in pledges.

## Key claims

- Anthropic matches stock that employees pledge to charity with its own newly issued stock, a non-cash expense that dilutes other shareholders. (§ The core story: “the company matches stock that staff pledge to charity with its own stock”) ^clm-807f163adcedd65cc34a
- Anthropic recorded more than $660 million in charity-match expense from October 2025 to March 2026, per investors. (§ Key facts and numbers: “More than $660 million in charity-match expense from October 2025 to March 2026”) ^clm-8b3b4239a92dff42d73b
- The charity match cost about $125 million in Q1 2026, 10% of employee expenses and 2% of total operating costs. (§ Key facts and numbers: “About $125 million in Q1 2026: 10% of employee expenses”) ^clm-670c861a6a33455608e2
- Anthropic's 2025 charitable contributions were $540 million; the next highest comparable non-cash donation in Fortune 500 filings was BlackRock at $109 million. (§ Key facts and numbers: “2025 charitable contributions: $540 million”) ^clm-b1467c59bb9a12553602
- The match is 3-to-1 on up to half their stock for employees who joined in 2024 or earlier, and 1-to-1 on up to a quarter of stock for newer hires. (§ Key facts and numbers: “Match terms: 3-to-1 for employees who joined in 2024 or earlier”) ^clm-4af077c8283b9179d3c4
- Anthropic excluded the charity expense, along with stock compensation and a large legal settlement, from its adjusted operating profit.
