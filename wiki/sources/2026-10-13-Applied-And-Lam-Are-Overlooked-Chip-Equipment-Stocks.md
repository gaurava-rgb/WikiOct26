# Applied And Lam Are Overlooked Chip Equipment Stocks

Anita Ramaswamy, The Information, published 2026-10-13.

## Summary

Ramaswamy argues chipmaking toolmakers Applied Materials and Lam Research are still overlooked even after both stocks rose 125% in a year, because their growth could speed up over the next two years as memory makers expand. Lam grows faster and trades at a higher multiple, helped by NAND and memory packaging tools, but that leaves it more exposed to an AI spending pullback. Applied is cheaper, has more exposure to fast-growing DRAM, plans to double its output by 2028, and has a cushion in automotive and industrial chips. Both get about a third of revenue from China and remain cyclical businesses.

## Key claims

- Applied Materials and Lam Research stocks tracked each other closely over the 12 months to October 2026 and both rose 125%. (§ The setup: “are both up 125% in that time”) ^clm-017d56fb78b03917ed12
- As of October 2026 Applied Materials traded at a forward price-earnings multiple of 28.8 versus 33.7 for Lam Research, per S&P Global Market Intelligence. (§ Valuation: Applied is cheaper: “Applied trades at a forward price-earnings multiple of 28.8 times, below Lam at 33.7”) ^clm-a5b3ee1cec10485ae782
- On a revenue multiple, Applied Materials was at 9.1 versus 11.4 for Lam Research as of October 2026. (§ Valuation: Applied is cheaper: “Applied is at 9.1 versus Lam at 11.4”) ^clm-32d36e31815c8efc7173
- Prediction: Lam Research revenue grew 24% in fiscal 2025 and slightly faster in fiscal 2026, and S&P data expects 50% growth over the next 12 months. (§ Valuation: Applied is cheaper: “is expected to grow 50% over the next 12 months, per S&P data”) ^clm-ba4805a296a0f9993cfb
- Prediction: Applied Materials grew revenue only 4% in its last fiscal year and is expected to grow 21% in fiscal 2026 and 35% in fiscal 2027. (§ Valuation: Applied is cheaper: “it is expected to grow 21% in fiscal 2026 and 35% in fiscal 2027”) ^clm-43bfee02e09c8a954dc5
- In their most recent fiscal quarters, DRAM was 14.5% of Lam Research revenue and 20.1% of Applied Materials revenue; DRAM is the fastest-growing memory category. (§ The bull case for Applied: DRAM: “DRAM was 14.5% of Lam's total revenue and 20.1% of Applied's”) ^clm-fbe6e067367840165c9b
- About two-thirds of Applied Materials' business is etch and deposition tools, and newer DRAM, HBM and advanced logic chips stack more layers, so chipmakers need more of those steps. (§ The bull case for Applied: DRAM: “About two-thirds of Applied's business is etch and deposition tools”) ^clm-4e90417e9893fdbfe47a
- Goldman Sachs analyst Jim Schneider said Applied Materials has outsized exposure to the chipmaking steps that are growing fastest. (§ The bull case for Applied: DRAM: “outsized or overweight exposure to that stuff, which is growing fastest”) ^clm-c9274deba2450bf99834
- Applied Materials doubled its manufacturing capacity over several years and plans to double its equipment output again by 2028, CFO Brice Hill said on an August 2026 earnings call. (§ Applied's capacity plan: “plans to double the volume of equipment it produces from current levels by 2028”) ^clm-1ec1211f4e3801b7645a
- Applied Materials based its capacity decision on eight-quarter forecasts from large customers, a lead time that grew as customers signed longer-term deals, which could lower overproduction risk. (§ Applied's capacity plan: “forecasts their large customers give for the next eight quarters”) ^clm-9c59affc78ce1797f23a
- Lam Research said its NAND revenue in the most recent quarter had doubled compared with the March 2026 quarter. (§ Lam: NAND and packaging: “Lam said NAND revenue had doubled compared to the March quarter”) ^clm-d78901e8c3bcd96be06d
- Prediction: HSBC analysts argued in an August 2026 report that NAND supply could reach parity with demand by the end of 2027, which could slow Lam Research's growth. (§ Lam: NAND and packaging: “NAND supply could reach parity with demand by the end of 2027”) ^clm-a6705d2e4d995c862f07
- Lam Research also grows by selling tools that package memory wafers onto chips, which makes it more exposed than Applied Materials if AI spending pulls back. (§ Lam: NAND and packaging: “That makes Lam more exposed if AI spending pulls back”) ^clm-b743789cfb7ed37eafea
- Ramaswamy argues Applied Materials' reliance on slower automotive and industrial markets hurt its recent growth but could shield it if AI spending stumbles. (§ Applied's other cushion: “Applied depends more on slower markets like automotive and industrial chips”) ^clm-476febee09a24924ad47
- Applied Materials and Lam Research each get around a third of their revenue from China and have been hit by trade restrictions limiting what they can sell there. (§ Risks for both: “each company gets around a third of its revenue from China”) ^clm-829153b90016021f8731
- Prediction: Jim Schneider says chip equipment is still cyclical but expects at least one more year of accelerating growth and two more years of growth. (§ Risks for both: “one more year of accelerating growth rate and two more years of growth”) ^clm-c1d3f8a61e9a9e984223
