# OpenAI Accelerates Ad Sprint With Meta Hires, Slashed Prices

Alix Coutures, The Information, published 2026-10-15.

## Summary

OpenAI accelerated its advertising push by hiring dozens of ad specialists from Meta Platforms and cutting ad prices. The advertising group grew to roughly 140 employees under former Meta executive Dave Dugan, though it reorganized seven times since launching in February 2026. Annualized ad revenue reached $1 billion in late August 2026, though the business is likely to miss its earlier $2.4 billion target for the full year. Active advertisers jumped to 60,000 in early October as click costs fell to $5 compared to $30 to $50 on Google search.

## Key claims

- Nearly half of the roughly 140 OpenAI employees working on advertising joined between July and September 2026, with Meta Platforms as the largest source of talent. (§ Rapid hiring and organizational churn: “Nearly half of the roughly 140 OpenAI employees working on ads joined between July and September 2026”) ^clm-253210d9172b243daf7c
- OpenAI ad chief Dave Dugan spent 12 years at Meta Platforms before joining as vice president of global ad solutions. (§ Rapid hiring and organizational churn: “Ad chief Dave Dugan spent 12 years at Meta before joining OpenAI as vice president of global ad solutions”) ^clm-18baf22d3bb7da2b7abf
- OpenAI's advertising division underwent seven reorganizations between its launch in February 2026 and October 2026. (§ Rapid hiring and organizational churn: “ad division has reorganized seven times since its February 2026 debut, according to people familiar with the matter”) ^clm-f8ddce4cc1cd227bb1e6
- Former Facebook executive Fidji Simo stepped back from her full-time role at OpenAI in early July 2026. (§ Rapid hiring and organizational churn: “Former Facebook executive Fidji Simo stepped back from her full-time role in early July 2026”) ^clm-4336960ca56030dc0532
- Annualized revenue from ChatGPT advertising reached $1 billion in late August 2026 after launching in February 2026. (§ Financial performance and advertiser growth: “Annualized revenue from ChatGPT ads reached $1 billion in late August 2026, up from zero when the pilot launched in February”) ^clm-378dc4733abccaf8a474
- OpenAI approached a $50 billion annualized revenue pace across its overall business at the end of September 2026. (§ Financial performance and advertiser growth: “OpenAI neared a $50 billion annualized revenue run rate across its entire business at the end of September 2026”) ^clm-1af30d56a72e0f8a3b33
- OpenAI is projected to fall short of the $2.4 billion in total 2026 ad revenue it projected to investors earlier in the year. (§ Financial performance and advertiser growth: “OpenAI is likely to fall short of the $2.4 billion in total 2026 ad revenue it projected to investors earlier in the year”) ^clm-6b8898eb6e5895d6c433
- Active advertisers on ChatGPT grew to about 60,000 in early October 2026, up from 20,000 in late August 2026. (§ Financial performance and advertiser growth: “Active advertisers on ChatGPT jumped to roughly 60,000 in early October 2026, up threefold from 20,000 in late August”) ^clm-30cfb376938d33a7cb19
- Prediction: U.S. advertising spending on ChatGPT is projected to increase 40% in October 2026 compared to September 2026 across more than 60 countries. (§ Financial performance and advertiser growth: “U.S. ad spending on ChatGPT is projected to grow 40% in October from September, according to Sensor Tower”) ^clm-a9132c774329b1649c42
- A click on a ChatGPT advertisement costs approximately $5, compared to $30 to $50 on Google search for equivalent software buyers. (§ Pricing advantages, SMB shift, and customer acquisition: “click on a ChatGPT ad costs about $5, compared to $30 to $50 on Google for identical business software clients”) ^clm-930d0d8384aaca5b466a
- Roughly 20% of all user queries on ChatGPT express intent to buy a product or service. (§ Product roadmap and targeting limitations: “About 20% of all queries on ChatGPT indicate an explicit intent to purchase a product or service”) ^clm-e393f0dd0d6d5d917220
- A third-party study of 187,000 ChatGPT ad placements found that one in nine ads was unrelated to the prompt query. (§ Product roadmap and targeting limitations: “roughly 187,000 ad placements by research firm Adthena found that one in nine ads was completely unrelated to the user query”) ^clm-88adf06299fe94da6799
