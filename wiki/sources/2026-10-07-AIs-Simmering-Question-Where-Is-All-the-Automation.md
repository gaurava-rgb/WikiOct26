# AI's Simmering Question: "Where the F--- Is All the Automation?"

Cory Weinberg, The Information, published 2026-10-07.

## Summary

Weinberg contrasts the AI labs' sense that an age of automation has arrived with the lack of evidence ordinary people feel it. At a Modal developer conference, Cognition founder Scott Wu pitched companies full of virtual employees, while former OpenAI researcher Diogo Almeida argued today's LLMs are tuned to assist a human, not to run unattended. Anthropic's Cat Wu says much automation stalls at Claude doing 80% and a person 20%, and OpenCode's Dax Raad says the bigger problem is getting people to check AI-written code. Economists do not yet see AI productivity gains in major statistics.
