# Software Firms Discount AI to Keep Customers From Anthropic, OpenAI

Laura Bratton, Aaron Holmes and Catherine Perloff, The Information, published 2026-09-22.

## Summary

Software firms and clouds including Microsoft, Amazon, Figma and Workday are offering new discounts on AI products as customers push back on a shift from per-seat to usage-based pricing. Customers have moved budgets to Anthropic's Claude Code and OpenAI's Codex and are now choosier. Microsoft is giving grace periods, credits and deeper Copilot discounts, while Workday, HubSpot and AWS offer free access and trials. Incumbents are paying the labs for models while discounting to keep customers from going straight to them.

## Key claims

- Software firms and clouds including Amazon, Microsoft, Figma and Workday are offering new AI discounts to clients and consulting partners tired of pricing changes. (§ The core story: “including Amazon, Microsoft, Figma and Workday, are offering new discounts on AI products”) ^clm-427aea2b17ab5348db67
- Many software vendors are moving from per-seat pricing to usage-based pricing, which can push costs up and has customers grumbling. (§ The core story: “Many vendors are moving to usage-based pricing (pay per use or per completed task) instead of per-seat pricing”) ^clm-4f508c15aab090869bcb
- Anthropic and OpenAI are each on pace to make tens of billions of dollars in 2026, while traditional software makers are still working out how to compete with them. (§ The core story: “each on pace to make tens of billions this year”) ^clm-09099e38f448e5e4edb3
- Software makers pay Anthropic and OpenAI to power their AI features, which is costly and part of why their prices changed. (§ The core story: “Those same software makers pay Anthropic and OpenAI to power their AI features”) ^clm-d90f72f3a4e7d8c52d92
- Snowflake shows rising AI revenue growth, while Workday, HubSpot, Salesforce and Adobe have not shown a notable acceleration. (§ The core story: “Workday, HubSpot, Salesforce and Adobe have not shown a notable acceleration”) ^clm-01a07e674199920f00dd
- A global manufacturer got at least a month's grace period to keep paying per seat for GitHub Copilot after Microsoft moved to usage pricing, which would add roughly $150,000 a month to its bill; it is weighing a move to Claude Code and SpaceX's Cursor. (§ Microsoft: “The change would add roughly $150,000 a month to its bill”) ^clm-09c61850c2ceb6538f46
- Microsoft Copilot costs $30 per user per month, plus new usage charges for features like Copilot Cowork. (§ Microsoft: “Copilot costs $30 per user per month, plus new usage charges”) ^clm-b259f70dd4c1a77acbcd
- Microsoft began offering 5% to 10% per-seat discounts to buyers of at least 2,000 seats, which before only giants like KPMG or PwC got, and plans 30% to 50% discounts on corporate Copilot in coming months. (§ Microsoft: “Microsoft plans 30% to 50% discounts on corporate Copilot in coming months”) ^clm-f29501cf1336ace40f4d
- FICO's CIO said dozens of its vendors added AI at no extra cost at renewal in 2026 and he would not sign a contract that does not heavily discount or bundle the AI. (§ Other software firms: “he would not sign a contract that does not heavily discount or bundle the AI”) ^clm-f622f192882b7fda506b
- Workday began a year of unlimited free access to its Sana Enterprise AI platform for about 20 large customers this summer, focusing on adoption over near-term revenue. (§ Other software firms: “our focus is really on adoption, adoption, adoption.”) ^clm-15a62b87bc4ee4303839
- Figma CEO Dylan Field said Figma cut AI costs by as much as half and moved to usage pricing in March 2026, aiming for volume over high margins. (§ Other software firms: “it cut AI costs by as much as half”) ^clm-854f0ed645ac9f4a1787
- Thoughtworks' Shayan Mohanty says vendors now typically give 30% to 35% discounts on software and AI plus longer trials, versus discounts usually under 20% before. (§ Other software firms: “vendors typically give 30% to 35% discounts on software and AI and longer trials”) ^clm-891d22230d0029a85d0a
- HubSpot began free trials of up to 30 days for its AI agents while charging per completed task, and its revenue growth slowed in the June quarter. (§ Other software firms: “HubSpot's revenue growth slowed in the June quarter”) ^clm-a21c39bd09b033aa6f21
- Adobe's freemium approach boosted Firefly use, but executives said it weighs on backlog and annual recurring revenue growth. (§ Other software firms: “it weighs on backlog and annual recurring revenue growth”) ^clm-e74edaecbf502d84f6d6
- AWS offered consultancy Rhythmic Technologies about four months free of Kiro's top tier, but it declined because it already uses Claude Code; AWS says Kiro developer numbers doubled from Q1 to Q2 2026. (§ Amazon (AWS): “He declined because his firm already uses Claude Code”) ^clm-69ad99419ff055fcdd63
