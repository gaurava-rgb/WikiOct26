# SpaceX’s Musk Lauds Its Soaring Cloud Business and Possible Tesla Merger

Nick Wingfield, The Information, published 2026-10-20.

## Summary

SpaceX's Superintelligence division nearly tripled third-quarter revenue to $7.56 billion, becoming its largest operating segment and generating $431 million in operating profit. Elon Musk stated hyperscalers are aggressively seeking SpaceX compute, while CFO Bret Johnsen noted data center investments recoup capital in under twelve months. Musk also confirmed special committees are evaluating a potential SpaceX-Tesla merger. In other developments, Anthropic's draft prospectus disclosed $16.3 billion in revenue and an $80 billion accounting loss in the first half of 2026.

## Key claims

- SpaceX's Superintelligence segment, comprising X, Grok Bot, and cloud infrastructure leasing to clients like Anthropic, expanded rapidly in late 2026. (§ Superintelligence segment and cloud growth: “SI (superintelligence) division, which encompasses X, Grok Bot, and an external cloud infrastructure unit renting compute to customers including Anthropic.”) ^clm-e41d3ec2665415a606f7
- SpaceX's Superintelligence division reached $7.56 billion in third-quarter 2026 revenue, nearly tripling year-over-year to become the company's largest business division. (§ Superintelligence segment and cloud growth: “SI segment revenue nearly tripled in the third quarter to $7.56 billion, becoming SpaceX's single largest business line.”) ^clm-ed8ec35175e673f6c39c
- SpaceX's Superintelligence unit earned an operating profit of $431 million in the third quarter of 2026, recovering from a $1.26 billion loss in the previous quarter. (§ Superintelligence segment and cloud growth: “SI unit achieved an operating profit of $431 million, swinging from an operating loss of $1.26 billion in the second quarter.”) ^clm-fcb7d51e332a355c9971
- Elon Musk stated hyperscalers and startups are relentlessly seeking to rent SpaceX compute capacity, allowing the company to sell out its capacity several times over. (§ Superintelligence segment and cloud growth: “company is overwhelmed by compute requests from hyperscalers and smaller firms, noting SpaceX could rent out its capacity several times over.”) ^clm-624a9cb9ea0437066a74
- SpaceX CFO Bret Johnsen reported that strong cloud compute pricing allows SpaceX to recoup capital on new data center deployments in less than one year. (§ Superintelligence segment and cloud growth: “current pricing yields a payback period of less than one year on new data center deployments.”) ^clm-773b289664fbb0bd82ad
- Tesla and SpaceX formed independent board committees to evaluate a potential merger, with Elon Musk recusing himself from formal deliberations as CEO of both companies. (§ Tesla merger deliberations and other corporate news: “special board committees have formed to examine a potential merger, though he recused himself as CEO of both entities.”) ^clm-3680dad596e0033e9320
- Anthropic's draft IPO prospectus disclosed first-half 2026 revenue of $16.3 billion and an accounting net loss exceeding $80 billion related to financing contracts. (§ Tesla merger deliberations and other corporate news: “Anthropic reported a net loss of more than $80 billion for the first half of 2026, largely stemming from accounting for financing deals, on revenue of $16.3 billion”) ^clm-a207660879059b09ad5c
- Salesforce agreed to purchase customer marketing software company Braze for roughly $5.66 billion including debt in October 2026. (§ Tesla merger deliberations and other corporate news: “Salesforce agreed to acquire customer engagement platform Braze for approximately $5.66 billion including debt.”) ^clm-7f62edd509e4ac38f13b
