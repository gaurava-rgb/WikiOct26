# Notes: OpenAI Eyes Acquisition That Could Boost Cybersecurity Business

The Information, Valida Pau and Amir Efrati, 2026-10-13 (exclusive).

## The deal talks

- OpenAI is in early talks to buy Trail of Bits, a firm that finds and fixes vulnerabilities (security holes) in open-source software, per people familiar with the matter.
- OpenAI is trying to build a substantial cybersecurity business.
- The deal size could not be learned. OpenAI has sometimes paid in the low hundreds of millions of dollars in stock for small startups, and sometimes less.
- Talks are ongoing and the deal could fall apart, the people said.
- Trail of Bits staff could help with one of OpenAI's most promising business opportunities.

## OpenAI vs Anthropic in security AI

- In 2026 OpenAI and its archrival Anthropic have tried to one-up each other building specialized AI that finds software vulnerabilities.
- Working with the White House, OpenAI and Anthropic have let other large businesses use their most advanced models to find and patch holes in their own software.
- Both have neutered those abilities in the models released to the wider public, because bad actors could use them for attacks.
- Security software firms such as Palo Alto Networks have used OpenAI and Anthropic models to improve the products they sell to businesses.
- Another sign of OpenAI's security ambitions: it recently hired Dali Rajic, a former executive at Wiz (the Google-owned cloud security unit), as its chief revenue officer.

## About Trail of Bits

- Founded in 2012. Provides security research and tools and helps maintain open-source projects.
- More than 40 employees, mostly security engineers, per its website.
- It never took typical venture funding. It got a grant of about $10,000 from the Department of Defense in 2015 and a $1 million loan in 2020 from the federal Paycheck Protection Program (a pandemic-era small business loan program).

## Patch the Planet

- In June 2026 Trail of Bits partnered with OpenAI on a software-patching program called Patch the Planet.
- Its staff work on open-source projects such as the Python programming language to find, review and fix bugs, using OpenAI Codex tools, including models specialized for cybersecurity.
- CEO Dan Guido said in a promo video that the firm had earned the open-source community's trust "over more than a decade of work."
- Guido also said Codex let his team "ship things in a day" that would otherwise have taken weeks.

## AI in real attacks

- Security has been a focus for AI labs as models got stronger.
- Hackers have already used publicly available AI in many cyberattacks worldwide, per independent security researchers.
- The week before, CrowdStrike said the attacker behind a recent hack of South Korean financial institutions used an open-source Chinese AI agent and Chinese large language models.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- OpenAI journey: cybersecurity is turning into a real business line for OpenAI, with an acquisition target and a security-world CRO.
- Anthropic journey: the two labs race head to head on vulnerability-finding AI, with gated access for big firms.
- AI shift 2024-2026 story: AI now both powers real attacks and is sold to defenders, echoing the Hugging Face hack and the debate over who may use cyber-capable models.
- Google journey: OpenAI hiring a Wiz executive ties to Google's Wiz bet on AI-speed security.
