# Notes: Datadog Offered $4 Billion to Buy Cloud Security Startup ‘Upwind’

The Information, Aaron Holmes and Valida Pau, 2026-10-22.

## Datadog's buyout offer

- Enterprise software and observability provider Datadog made an offer to acquire Israeli cloud cybersecurity startup Upwind Security for nearly $4 billion.
- Datadog carries a market capitalization of $90 billion and sells observability software that tracks applications and data across corporate cloud infrastructure.
- The buyout offer comes as cybersecurity spending surges in response to artificial intelligence threats, where advanced models can discover critical vulnerabilities across widely used software like PHP.
- Datadog previously attempted to acquire Upwind for roughly $1 billion in 2025, but those discussions ended without a deal.

## Upwind's growth and fundraising

- Upwind has received takeover interest from multiple bidders in recent months but has declined to accept an acquisition offer.
- Upwind is finalizing a $300 million funding round at a private valuation of roughly $3.8 billion, meaning Datadog's buyout offer carried little premium over private market pricing.
- Upwind develops cybersecurity software designed to monitor cloud-hosted workloads, specifically tracking AI agents that interact with cloud applications and automatically detecting and responding to breaches.
- Enterprise customers using Upwind software include streaming provider Roku and Brazilian digital bank Nubank.
- Upwind's annual recurring revenue recently crossed $50 million (approximately $4 million monthly), up from about $20 million in ARR at the end of 2025.
- A buyout transaction near $4 billion would value Upwind at approximately 80 times ARR, exceeding the revenue multiple Alphabet paid for Wiz.
- Upwind was founded in 2022 by Chief Executive Amiram Shachar and had previously raised more than $400 million, including a $250 million round in January 2026 at a $1.5 billion valuation.
- Major Upwind investors include Bessemer Venture Partners, Craft Ventures, Cyberstarts, Salesforce Ventures, and Greylock.

## Industry consolidation

- Major software and security firms including Google, Palo Alto Networks, and CrowdStrike have aggressively acquired cloud security startups.
- Google completed a $32 billion acquisition of cloud security provider Wiz earlier in 2026.
- The proliferation of AI agents that independently access corporate databases and cloud applications has driven rapid demand for specialized agent security and monitoring tools.
