# Notes: Palo Alto Networks CEO Advises Daughter on New AI-Focused Venture Capital Fund

The Information, Julia Hornstein and Valida Pau, 2026-10-19 (report).

## The new fund and executive advisory

- Ayesha Arora-Hughes, daughter of Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora, is raising a new venture capital fund called Permanent Partners Collective Fund I.
- The fund targets more than $100 million to invest in AI startups spanning applications and underlying computing infrastructure.
- Nikesh Arora serves as an adviser to the firm and brings deep investing background from his prior role as president and chief operating officer of SoftBank, where he oversaw investments in companies like Ola and Snapdeal.
- Nikesh Arora also serves as the first lead independent director on the board of venture firm General Catalyst, appointed in April 2026.
- Ayesha Arora-Hughes previously worked at venture capital firm S32 until July 2026, where she backed coding agent startup Factory, legal tech firm Legora, and custom chip designer Etched.
- The effort follows similar second-generation tech funds, such as Yosemite, founded in 2023 by Reed Jobs (son of Steve Jobs), which started with a $400 million target and reached $1.2 billion in assets under management.

## Cybersecurity consolidation around AI agents

- Nikesh Arora has led an aggressive acquisition strategy at Palo Alto Networks, seeking cybersecurity startups to prepare for security shifts caused by autonomous AI agents.
- The company believes the increasing sophistication of autonomous software agents will fundamentally alter enterprise IT defense.
