# Notes: At Apple, Pigs Are About to Fly

The Information, Nick Wingfield, 2026-10-19 (column).

## Touchscreen MacBooks and the Ternus product era

- Apple sent invitations for a product event next Wednesday, teasing the phrase "Can't wait to pro and tell next week" on X, widely seen as announcing a touchscreen MacBook Pro.
- This reverses a decade of corporate policy under former CEO Tim Cook, who in 2012 compared touchscreen laptops to combining a toaster and a refrigerator, saying Apple was not going to that party.
- Touchscreens are now standard in the market, present on roughly one in five laptops sold.
- The move highlights leadership changes under CEO John Ternus, a longtime hardware engineering executive who is more focused on products than Cook was.
- Ternus' early product slate includes the iPhone Duo foldable phone, the HomeView smart display, and now a touchscreen MacBook, showing Apple entering categories it avoided for years.

## Industry briefing notes

- Elon Musk pledged to give every American child one share of SpaceX stock next year, an endowment potentially worth nearly $11 billion.
