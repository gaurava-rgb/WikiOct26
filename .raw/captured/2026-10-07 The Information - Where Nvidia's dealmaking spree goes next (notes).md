# Notes: Where Nvidia's $100 Billion Dealmaking Juggernaut Will Go Next

The Information, Valida Pau and Phoebe Liu, 2026-10-07 (exclusive).

## The core story

- Nvidia has been on a record deal spree and is expected to keep going. Next targets: robotics, self-driving technology, and AI models that run locally on phones and home computers (per bankers, lawyers and investors who have worked with Nvidia).
- The reporters' framing of the motive: Jensen Huang wants a world with thousands of thriving AI models, not a few, so that OpenAI and a couple of other big buyers do not hold too much power over Nvidia's sales.
- Previously unreported: in July 2026 Nvidia was interested in buying OpenRouter (an AI model marketplace) but asked for more time; the founders would not wait, and Stripe won with an $8 billion bid (per a person with knowledge).

## Key facts and numbers

- Over roughly two months after July 2026, Nvidia signed more than $140 billion in deals, including a $105 billion credit guarantee (reporters' tally).
- As of late July 2026: nearly $100 billion in equity investments plus $25 billion in future commitments; $99 billion in cash and securities.
- Customer concentration: three customers made up 44% of sales in the six months to July 2026 (Nvidia disclosure).
- Hugging Face acquisition: $12.9 billion, over 80 times its $150 million annualized revenue (per people close to the deal).
- Figure (humanoid robots): Nvidia has discussed investing another $1 billion; Figure seeks a valuation around $38 billion before new money (per one person). Previously unreported.
- Poolside: $6 billion license-and-hire deal, partly to help Nvidia's own Nemotron open models.
- Thinking Machines Lab: talks to invest about $2.5 billion (earlier reporting by The Information).
- Perplexity: talks ranged from an outright purchase to a license deal of at least $20 billion; ended as a partnership (late August 2026) plus a planned roughly $3 billion investment at a $35 billion pre-money valuation (per a person with knowledge).
- SB Energy / OpenAI Ohio data center: Nvidia first discussed $250 billion in credit support for OpenAI, then guaranteed $105 billion for phase one only, plus a $3 billion investment in SB Energy. Huang wrote the campus could hold about $600 billion of Nvidia compute.
- Nvidia invested $30 billion in OpenAI's recent round; the final $10 billion closed October 1, 2026.
- Early August 2026: Huang gathered Wall Street firms (Blackstone, Apollo, Goldman Sachs) to finance $500 billion of Nvidia hardware; Nvidia may backstop up to 25% in some deals.

## Named people and roles

- Jensen Huang, Nvidia CEO: personally negotiates big deals, including prices.
- Vishal Bhagwati: leads Nvidia corporate development (ex-HPE, Oracle).
- Clem Delangue, Hugging Face co-founder: approached Huang after rival interest from OpenAI and Salesforce.
- Aravind Srinivas, Perplexity CEO: pitched Huang on buying Perplexity outright.

## Change versus earlier views

- New limit on spending: Nvidia's credit default swap spreads (the price of insurance against its debt) widened in August 2026, signaling investor worry about risk. Huang watched them closely, and the OpenAI guarantee shrank to less than half the size first discussed.
- Huang publicly downplays risk. He told a Goldman Sachs conference in September: "I'm not taking any risks."

## Why it matters for the wiki

- Who pays for compute: Nvidia is now a financier of AI demand (guarantees, equity, leases), not just a chip seller. That ties AI Compute Demand to Nvidia's own balance sheet.
- Aggregation risk in reverse: Nvidia funds many model makers to avoid a few dominant buyers gaining power over it.
- The local-AI push (DGX Spark, Perplexity's app, Mac mini demand for agents) is a new front where Apple and Nvidia meet.
