# Notes: How Thoma Bravo Found a Discount in a Hot Cybersecurity Market

The Information, Alex Eichenstein, 2026-10-21 (finance analysis).

## Proofpoint's $6.3 billion acquisition of Varonis

- Thoma Bravo-backed cybersecurity company Proofpoint agreed to acquire data security software provider Varonis Systems for $6.3 billion.
- The transaction values Varonis at approximately six times forward annual recurring revenue, representing a discount to peers like Qualys which trade around eight times forward ARR.
- Varonis shares had dropped nearly 50 percent from late 2025 to around $33 in June 2026 as it transitioned customers from software licenses to cloud subscriptions.
- Thoma Bravo acquired Proofpoint in 2021 for $12.3 billion; Proofpoint now generates more than $1 billion in EBITDA, and the Varonis acquisition aims to position it for an initial public offering.
- The deal expands Proofpoint from email and phishing defense to data security governance, controlling access permissions for human workers and autonomous AI agents.

## M&A surge around agentic cybersecurity

- Cybersecurity mergers and acquisitions reached a record 170 deals across North America and Europe in the third quarter of 2026, according to Arrowpoint Advisory.
- Massive corporate deals preceded the transaction, including Alphabet's $32 billion acquisition of Wiz and Palo Alto Networks' $25 billion purchase of CyberArk.
- Heightened interest follows model releases like Anthropic's Mythos, agentic breaches, and automated security discoveries such as AI finding a flaw in the PHP programming language.
- Thoma Bravo partner Andrew Almeida stated the firm requires target companies to possess verifiable revenue derived from AI agents, not just an agentic vision, and plans another large deal within twelve months.
- The broader technology IPO market remains cautious amid interest rate volatility, with market participants closely monitoring whether Anthropic's forthcoming public filing will reopen the window.
