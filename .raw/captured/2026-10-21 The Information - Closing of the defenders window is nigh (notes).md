# Notes: The Closing of the ‘Defenders Window’ Is Nigh

The Information, Leo Schwartz and Nick Wingfield, 2026-10-21.

## The narrowing defender's window

- Since August 2026, an OpenAI-led industry coalition has worked quietly to fix critical vulnerabilities in PHP, an open-source programming language that powers a large share of the web.
- OpenAI has warned that the defender's window is shrinking rapidly. The defender's window is the period when defenders armed with frontier AI can detect and patch code flaws before attackers discover and exploit them.
- Frontier AI models from United States labs face narrowing lead times over Chinese open-source models that lack safety restrictions.
- In late September 2026, Anthropic published a warning that Chinese lab Z.ai's GLM-5.3 model trailed United States frontier models by only four months in cyber capabilities.
- The Trump administration's Super Intelligence Force stepped in to coordinate government communications and help push relevant parties to deploy a software patch.
- The engagement represented the first major collaboration between OpenAI and the SI Force, with OpenAI briefing National Intelligence Director Jay Clayton and senior defense official Emil Michael.
- A former White House official noted that earlier vulnerability clearinghouses, like the July 2026 Gold Eagle initiative, may already be obsolete against faster automated attacks.

## Meta Q3 earnings and Class C stock

- Meta Platforms reported third-quarter 2026 revenue of $64.6 billion, up 26% year over year and slightly above company guidance.
- Reality Labs trimmed its operating losses during the quarter.
- Meta recorded negative free cash flow of $2.12 billion, down from positive free cash flow of $10.63 billion a year earlier, driven by heavy artificial intelligence infrastructure spending. The cash burn was less severe than the negative $10.3 billion projected by analysts.
- Meta reaffirmed full-year 2026 capital expenditures of $135 billion to $145 billion, comforting investors worried about spending increases.
- Meta Chief Executive Mark Zuckerberg announced that the Muse personal agent app reached 10 million users in the United States and Canada within six weeks of launch, outpacing the initial growth of Threads.
- Muse will remain free for general users, with monetization planned through commerce transaction fees and fees for advanced model tokens.
- Meta incurred nearly $10 billion in legal charges in the third quarter, reducing operating income by 37% to just under $13 billion. The charges follow an $18.59 billion child safety settlement in August and a $25 billion court judgment in New Mexico over Cambridge Analytica.
- Meta announced a new class of non-voting Class C common stock, effective January 19, 2027. Existing Class A and B shareholders will receive one Class C share per share held as a dividend, enabling future equity raises for AI investments without diluting Mark Zuckerberg's voting control.

## Tesla and Microsoft updates

- Tesla reported flat third-quarter revenue of $28 billion, with automotive revenue falling 3% to $20.56 billion.
- Elon Musk declined on Tesla's earnings call to elaborate further on potential merger talks between Tesla and SpaceX.
- Microsoft asked shareholders to approve a 6% cut to Chief Executive Satya Nadella's pay following revenue drops in its Xbox gaming unit.
- Microsoft nominated Stripe co-founder John Collison to its board of directors.
- Electronic Arts announced plans to cut about 400 jobs (3% of its staff) and shut down its BioWare game studio.
