# Notes: Private Equity Firms Weighed $100 Billion-Plus Buyout of Microsoft's Xbox

The Information, Valida Pau and Aaron Holmes, 2026-10-20 (exclusive).

## The buyout deliberations and Microsoft's stance

- Private equity firms recently explored a buyout of Microsoft's Xbox gaming unit that could have valued it between $100 billion and $150 billion.
- Discussions were exploratory and did not progress into direct talks with Microsoft, but indicate potential private equity appetite if Microsoft's gaming turnaround stalls.
- A Microsoft spokesperson stated that Xbox is not for sale.
- The high end of the valuation would align with the valuation multiple of Electronic Arts, which was taken private earlier in 2026 by Silver Lake, Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, and Affinity Partners in a $55 billion leveraged buyout (about seven times annual revenue of $7.5 billion).

## Xbox's financial headwinds and restructuring

- The Xbox division, which includes Activision Blizzard (purchased for $75 billion in 2023) and employs about 14,000 workers, has struggled with falling sales and thin profit margins.
- Revenue dropped 7 percent in the fiscal year ending June 2026 to $21.8 billion, while its adjusted accountability profit margin slumped to just 3 percent.
- Xbox CEO Asha Sharma, who took charge in February 2026, launched a plan in July to lay off 3,200 staff (roughly 20 percent of workforce) over twelve months and focus capital on core franchises like Halo and Minecraft.
- Xbox represents only about 7 percent of Microsoft's revenue, while Microsoft directs immense capital expenditure into AI data centers, compressing corporate free cash flow.
- The gaming hardware business faces severe cost inflation driven by the AI boom; Xbox internally estimated that its next-generation console planned for 2028 could retail between $1,200 and $1,800.
