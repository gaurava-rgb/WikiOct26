# Notes: SB Energy Considers Waiting Until December or Later for an IPO

The Information, Phoebe Liu and Valida Pau, 2026-10-22.

## Delayed IPO timeline

- SoftBank Group-controlled data center developer SB Energy is evaluating whether to delay its initial public offering to December 2026 or into 2027.
- The company had previously targeted going public as early as September 2026, filing its registration statement on September 2 and amending it on September 21.
- Investor appetite for data center and AI cloud listings has cooled following the abrupt cancellation of Firmus's initial public offering earlier in October.
- SB Energy executives are tracking Anthropic's expected public offering ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday in November 2026 to evaluate institutional investor sentiment.
- SB Energy had aimed to raise between $5 billion and $7 billion from the offering to fund the massive Ohio data center campus being built for OpenAI.
- The Ohio campus is designed to reach 8.5 gigawatts of power capacity, house approximately $600 billion in Nvidia chips, and is backed by a $105 billion credit guarantee from Nvidia.
- Building the full Ohio campus will require more than $100 billion in total capital, which going public would help facilitate through lower borrowing costs.

## Balance sheet and valuation hurdles

- SB Energy can maintain operations without an immediate public listing, supported by a $1.5 billion investment from Nvidia in mid-August 2026 alongside $1.2 billion in cash and cash equivalents reported as of June 30.
- SB Energy could also seek further private equity financing from existing shareholders SoftBank and Nvidia.
- Underwriting bankers proposed a valuation around $60 billion for SB Energy, but prospective investors have balked at the price.
- The company generated $139 million in revenue during the first half of 2026, primarily from electricity sales to utilities, meaning a $60 billion valuation represents an annualized revenue multiple exceeding 200.
- SB Energy holds $430 billion in contracted data center lease revenue, but that revenue is tied entirely to the OpenAI Ohio facility, will be recognized over 15 to 20 year terms, and depends on buildings opening in phases starting in 2028.

## Headwinds across data center developers

- GPU cloud builder Nscale is attempting to proceed with a fall 2026 public listing at a valuation around $30 billion despite investor pushback, aided by talks with Anthropic over a $45 billion cloud contract.
- Singapore-based data center operator DayOne is considering delaying its initial public offering or downsizing the capital raised.
- Blackstone-backed data center firm AirTrunk postponed a planned $1.5 billion real estate investment trust listing on the Singapore Exchange.
