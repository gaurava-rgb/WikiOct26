# Notes: CoreWeave Co-Founders’ Stock Sales Aren’t Helping Investor Confidence

The Information, Martin Peers, 2026-10-14.

## The core story

- CoreWeave shares plunged 15.5% on October 14, 2026, amid a broader market drop caused by inflation data and interest rate hike expectations.
- Publicly traded neoclouds, which operate specialized AI data centers financed largely through borrowing, were hit hard by investor worries over debt.
- Market chatter indicated CoreWeave bonds declined to distressed levels.
- CoreWeave CEO Michael Intrator sold company shares every single week from April through late September 2026, per securities filings, while other co-founders also sold stock regularly.
- Continued insider share sales have shaken investor confidence in the company during a period of market anxiety.

## Stock price drop and debt financing

- CoreWeave shares fell to $65 on October 14, down sharply from $86 in September and $128 in May 2026.
- CoreWeave stated its co-founders were making normal personal financial planning decisions to diversify concentrated equity holdings.
- The company has borrowed aggressively to fund rapid expansion, issuing $4.2 billion in convertible notes in September 2026.
- CoreWeave also announced plans to sell as many as 35 million additional shares over time.
- Heavy borrowing leaves the firm exposed to elevated interest rates and softening investor demand for AI debt.
- Co-founder Brannin McBee told the Financial Times that CoreWeave seeks to borrow from insurance companies with unlimited capital, arguing operators must achieve massive scale.

## Neocloud sector headwinds

- Less-established neocloud operators Nscale and SB Energy have tried to complete initial public offerings without much success.
- Singapore data center operator AirTrunk postponed a planned real estate investment trust public listing due to higher borrowing costs and market volatility.
- Morgan Stanley CEO Ted Pick warned on October 14 that the AI build-out will face bottlenecks and periodic overbuilding, though long-term investment will remain enormous.

## Berkshire Hathaway investment in Bending Spoons

- Shares of Italian tech company Bending Spoons jumped 41% to $53.46 on October 14.
- Berkshire Hathaway agreed to invest $330 million in Bending Spoons through a private placement at $33 per share.
- Berkshire previously bought shares during Bending Spoons' July IPO and now holds roughly 4% of the company.

## Industry personnel and tech moves

- Anthropic reached an agreement to license technology and hire engineers from Opaque Systems, a five-year-old startup building security software for confidential data in AI agents.
- Sachin Katti, vice president of compute strategy at OpenAI who joined from Intel, announced he will leave OpenAI on November 15, 2026.
- Defense tech firm Anduril Industries is in talks for an employee share tender valuing the company at $90 billion, capped at $1.3 billion at $94.80 per share.
- OpenAI accused a former employee of leaking unreleased hardware device plans to Apple executives, mirroring allegations Apple made against OpenAI in a July 2026 lawsuit.
