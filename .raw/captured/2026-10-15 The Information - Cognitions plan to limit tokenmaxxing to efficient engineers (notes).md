# Notes: Cognition’s Plan to Limit Tokenmaxxing to the Most Efficient Engineers

The Information, Rocket Drew, 2026-10-15.

## Dynamic token limits and efficiency grading

- Cognition, developer of AI software engineer Devin, introduced dynamic resource allocations that adjust employee token limits based on efficiency ratings.
- The company measures productivity by calculating the human-hour equivalent of work produced during a coding session and dividing that by the underlying compute cost.
- Engineers who achieve healthy or satisfactory efficiency grades automatically receive higher token limits when they hit initial caps.
- Calvin Giroud, Cognition vice president of customer engineering, compared the dynamic limit mechanism to credit cards that grant higher borrowing ceilings to reliable borrowers.
- While Cognition charges customers based on consumption, management argues curbing wasteful token consumption builds long-term customer trust and contract durability.
- Giroud noted that unrestrained token consumption is unsustainable for large corporate clients facing public shareholder pressure over AI software returns.

## Devin Coach for workflow optimization

- Cognition introduced Devin Coach, an automated supervisory agent that monitors user prompts and flags inefficiencies.
- The coaching tool identifies roughly 20 to 30 common user errors, including underspecified task prompts and selecting unnecessarily expensive AI models.
- The system prompts developers to split complex multi-part requests into concurrent parallel tasks to save time.
- Devin Coach also flags non-work usage such as personal vacation planning, addressing corporate concerns over compute waste.

## Executive moves and corporate funding

- Defense technology maker Anduril Industries opened tender discussions to allow employee share sales at a valuation of approximately $90 billion.
- The tender is capped at $1.3 billion at a price of $94.80 per share, scheduled to conclude in early November 2026.
- Sachin Katti, vice president of compute strategy at OpenAI and former Intel executive, announced plans to leave OpenAI on November 15, 2026 to launch a new venture.
- Serverless AI cloud provider Modal closed a $750 million Series D funding round led by Accel at a $15.75 billion post-money valuation.
- Berkshire Hathaway agreed to invest $330 million in Italian software firm Bending Spoons through a private placement of common stock.
- Nvidia announced a $250 million five-year compute commitment for agentic security research in the United Kingdom, alongside investments in five British AI and quantum startups.
- European payments firm Mollie committed 50 million euros, approximately $54 million, to build internal banking and payment infrastructure for autonomous AI agents.
