# Notes: SpaceX's Musk Lauds Its Soaring Cloud Business and Possible Tesla Merger

The Information, Nick Wingfield, 2026-10-20 (earnings report).

## Superintelligence segment and cloud growth

- SpaceX reported third-quarter results showcasing rapid expansion in its SI (superintelligence) division, which encompasses X, Grok Bot, and an external cloud infrastructure unit renting compute to customers including Anthropic.
- SI segment revenue nearly tripled in the third quarter to $7.56 billion, becoming SpaceX's single largest business line.
- The SI unit achieved an operating profit of $431 million, swinging from an operating loss of $1.26 billion in the second quarter.
- CEO Elon Musk said the company is overwhelmed by compute requests from hyperscalers and smaller firms, noting SpaceX could rent out its capacity several times over.
- CFO Bret Johnsen described cloud demand as extraordinary and stated current pricing yields a payback period of less than one year on new data center deployments.

## Tesla merger deliberations and other corporate news

- Musk addressed questions regarding combining SpaceX and Tesla, stating special board committees have formed to examine a potential merger, though he recused himself as CEO of both entities.
- Musk expressed enthusiasm for a merger, arguing there is substantial merit to combining the businesses, though noting shareholder approval would be necessary.
- In a draft IPO prospectus, Anthropic reported a net loss of more than $80 billion for the first half of 2026, largely stemming from accounting for financing deals, on revenue of $16.3 billion with roughly 20 percent from two customers.
- Salesforce agreed to acquire customer engagement platform Braze for approximately $5.66 billion including debt.
- Joel Kaplan, Meta's chief global affairs officer, plans to depart after more than 15 years.
