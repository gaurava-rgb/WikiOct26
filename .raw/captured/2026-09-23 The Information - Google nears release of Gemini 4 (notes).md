# Notes: Google Nears Release of Flagship Gemini 4 AI Model

The Information, Laura Bratton, 2026-09-23 (exclusive).

## The core story

- Google is close to releasing Gemini 4, its next flagship AI model, said Koray Kavukcuoglu, head of Google DeepMind, on Wednesday.
- He spoke at The Information's AI Agenda Live Summit with editor-in-chief Jessica Lessin. It was his first media appearance as DeepMind's new leader.
- The release is long-awaited because Google has fallen behind Anthropic and OpenAI in the model race.
- Gemini 4 is early in post-training (the stage where a raw, pretrained model is tuned to behave reliably and follow instructions).
- He hopes to ship it "much earlier" than the end of the year.
- Plan: release an early post-training version as soon as possible because the team is excited by the results, then keep iterating fast.
- Google is still building guardrails and doing safety testing. Engineers already use Gemini 4 internally to run Antigravity, Google's AI coding tool.

## How Google got here

- Gemini 4 would be Google's first new-generation flagship since the Gemini 3 line launched late last year.
- Since then: Gemini 3.1 (an update) in February, plus a series of smaller, cheaper Flash models that are weaker at complex reasoning.
- At Google I/O in May, CEO Sundar Pichai said a major update, Gemini 3.5 Pro, would come in June. It never shipped.
- Kavukcuoglu said Google stepped back after Gemini 3 and 3.1 and focused on Flash, to maximize "speed of learning." He did not say whether 3.5 Pro is officially scrapped, but the focus is now Gemini 4.

## The competition

- Anthropic launched its Mythos model family this spring and upgraded it this month.
- OpenAI started launching its GPT-6 models in early September.
- Both are more capable than Google's best current models.
- Asked whether he worries Google is behind, he said he is fully confident in his team and sees it as a certainty that Google will stay at the frontier.
- History: Google has come back before. Gemini 3 was such a jump that OpenAI declared a "code red" to catch up.

## Leadership change

- Kavukcuoglu took over in August, moving from chief technology officer to senior vice president of Google DeepMind.
- Demis Hassabis left the DeepMind CEO role to become chairman of the unit and chief scientist of parent company Alphabet.
- Kavukcuoglu is also Google's chief AI architect.

## On AGI

- Unlike many peers, he has no strong view on whether AGI (AI that matches or beats humans at all tasks) has arrived.
- He says the better question is whether we can build intelligent agents people can trust.

## Chips tie-in

- He says frontier model work also helps Google's fast-growing TPU business (tensor processing units, Google's own AI chips).
- This year Google began selling TPUs directly to customers, not only renting access through Google Cloud, as it competes harder with Nvidia.
- DeepMind's model work shows Google's hardware engineers how to design the next two or three generations of chips.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- Model labs vs clouds: Google, which owns both a cloud and a lab, admits it slipped behind the pure labs (Anthropic's Mythos, OpenAI's GPT-6) in 2026.
- Agents: DeepMind's leader reframes the goal from AGI to trustworthy agents, and Gemini 4 is first used internally for agentic coding (Antigravity).
- Compute scarcity: Google selling TPUs directly makes it a chip supplier competing with Nvidia, using its model work to guide chip design.
