# Notes: Salesforce Customers Brace For New Fees to Use Claude in Its Apps

The Information, Laura Bratton, 2026-10-22.

## Tollgates for third-party AI agents

- Salesforce plans to begin requiring enterprise customers to register external AI agents starting around November 2026, preparing to charge fees whenever outside agents analyze or update Salesforce records.
- The registration policy allows Salesforce to monetize customer adoption of external models like Anthropic's Claude or OpenAI models when customers bypass Salesforce's native AI offerings.
- Each action an autonomous AI agent executes within Salesforce applications will be categorized and billed as an interaction.
- In addition to per-interaction pricing, Salesforce account representatives have offered enterprises the option to purchase unlimited agent access via a headless license.
- Salesforce frames the registration rule as a cybersecurity protection, assigning distinct machine identities and restricted data permissions to AI agents rather than human user credentials.

## Pressure on per-seat enterprise software pricing

- Enterprise software vendors face structural disruption as autonomous AI agents operate apps faster and more effectively than human workers, undermining traditional seat-based software licensing models.
- Enterprise software peers Atlassian, Workday, ServiceNow, and Datadog are also establishing tollgates and outcome-based pricing models for external AI agents.
- Most corporate employees have not yet delegated core workflow tasks to autonomous agents, giving software vendors time to experiment with hybrid pricing.
- Software consultant Nick Hamm reported that most of his 150 enterprise clients currently deploy Claude and OpenAI models across Salesforce applications for free.
- Enterprise clients are reconsidering agent architectures and may transfer company data out of Salesforce into external repositories if tollgate fees are set too high.
- New enterprise customers will receive a three-month grace period to register AI agents after formal pricing is published, while existing customers will see fee changes at contract renewal.

## Enterprise deployment at Lumen

- Network infrastructure provider Lumen made significant investments over the past year deploying Salesforce native AI sales agents to automatically gather client requirements and generate pricing quotes for data center fiber connections.
- Lumen Chief Information Officer Jim Fowler stated that Salesforce has allowed existing enterprise subscription fees to cover usage of built-in AI agents.
- Lumen is waiting for final pricing terms before deciding whether to deploy external AI agent connectors in Salesforce apps.
