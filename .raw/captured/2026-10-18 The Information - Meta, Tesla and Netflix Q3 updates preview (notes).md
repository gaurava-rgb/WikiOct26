# Notes: Meta, Tesla and Netflix Q3 Updates Coming This Week

The Information, Martin Peers, 2026-10-18 (The Briefing Newsletter).

## Meta AI advertising boom and surging capital expenditure

- Meta Platforms is reporting third-quarter earnings ahead of its Big Tech peers (Google, Microsoft, Amazon), grabbing the earnings spotlight.
- In the first half of 2026, Meta digital advertising revenue grew 30%, double Google growth rate; second-quarter ad revenue hit $59 billion.
- Chief executive Mark Zuckerberg noted on the second-quarter call that 9 million small businesses use at least one Meta AI creative tool to build ads.
- Meta projected third-quarter revenue growth of up to 25%, compared to 28% in the second quarter.
- Analysts project Meta third-quarter capital expenditures of $40.5 billion, double the year-earlier figure, according to S&P Global Market Intelligence.
- That capex is projected to drive negative free cash flow of $10.3 billion for the quarter, compared to positive free cash flow of $10.6 billion a year earlier.
- Analysts anticipate this will begin several consecutive quarters of negative free cash flow as AI spending expands.
- Meta held $90 billion in cash and marketable securities as of June 30, 2026, but will likely require future debt or equity raises to fund continuing cash burn.

## Tesla sales plateau and robotics pivot

- Tesla market capitalization stands at $1.4 trillion, about $600 billion below SpaceX valuation of roughly $2 trillion.
- Third-quarter deliveries fell 2% year-over-year, leading analysts to project a meager 0.6% revenue increase for the quarter.
- Elon Musk continues pitching Tesla as an autonomous robotics and robotaxi company rather than a traditional carmaker.
- Tesla revealed its upcoming Roadster, but production is not scheduled to begin until 2028.
- Persistent market hints suggest a potential future merger between Tesla and SpaceX.

## Netflix Hollywood maturation and executive turnover

- Netflix projected third-quarter revenue growth of 11.7%, down 4.5 percentage points from 16.2% in the first quarter.
- Netflix shares have fallen 25% year-to-date in 2026, reflecting investor realization that streaming subscriber growth is maturing.
- Chief content officer Bela Bajaria suddenly departed and was replaced by former Disney and Fox executive Peter Rice, alongside corporate layoffs.
- Netflix shares trade at 5.6 times forward revenue, compared to 2.2 times for Walt Disney Co.

## Infrastructure and regulatory developments

- Microsoft withdrew from a planned United Kingdom data center that neocloud Nscale was constructing, following delays in securing grid power.
- China Ministry of Commerce issued a draft plan proposing export controls on 40 technologies across artificial intelligence, biomedicine, and photovoltaics.
- Anthropic created the Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, pledging at least 5% of company equity plus 2% for every subsequent 50% valuation gain to support causes addressing AI employment impacts.
