# Notes: Why Upstart Banks May Have to Keep Raising Money

The Information, Yueqi Yang, 2026-10-18 (The Information Finance Newsletter).

## The regulatory capital treadmill for de novo banks

- The Trump administration has encouraged entrepreneurs to start new venture-funded banks in the United States, but fast growth creates an ongoing equity-raising treadmill.
- Banking regulators require institutions to hold a capital cushion to absorb losses and protect depositors, measured by the tier 1 leverage ratio (capital divided by assets like cash, loans, and investments).
- Established banks fund capital through retained operating profits and maintain leverage ratios around 5% to 6%.
- In contrast, unprofitable new banks (de novo banks) must maintain 10% to 12% tier 1 leverage ratios for their first three years, forcing them to raise fresh equity as deposits and assets expand.
- Klaros Group partner Brian Graham noted that banks relying materially on their balance sheet for lending must continually tap investors for equity to support growth.

## Erebor Bank rapid expansion and unusual FDIC conditions

- Palmer Luckey defense- and crypto-focused Erebor Bank is raising $1.5 billion in fresh equity at an $8 billion pre-money valuation.
- Erebor launched in February 2026 with $625 million in committed capital; deposits surged to $7 billion at the end of September 2026, up from $4 billion the prior quarter.
- Regulators mandated a 12% tier 1 leverage ratio for Erebor.
- The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation imposed an unusual capital-call condition on Erebor, requiring backstop investors to contribute additional equity if capital drops below regulatory minimums.
- Erebor focuses on customers traditional banks avoid, including defense contractors, crypto firms, and businesses operating in Venezuela and Syria.

## Digital asset upstarts Openreserve and Augustus

- Openreserve, an a16z crypto-backed digital bank led by founder Dee Choubey, raised $25 million in September 2026 and expects another equity raise before launching in early 2027.
- Openreserve must maintain a 12% minimum tier 1 leverage ratio; its stated ambition is to become an on-chain globally systemically important bank by tokenizing trade receivables, issuing stablecoins, and offering tokenized deposits.
- Startup bank Augustus, led by chief executive Ferdinand Dabitz, raised $180 million in a round led by Tiger Global Management at a $1 billion valuation in July 2026.
- Augustus faces a 10% minimum leverage ratio and plans to open in late 2026, focusing on international dollar clearing for fintech and crypto firms like Kraken, competing with United Texas Bank.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- Financial infrastructure and fintech: shows how regulatory capital rules differ fundamentally from traditional venture capital software business models.
- Trump administration policy impact: deregulation opens bank charters to defense tech and crypto founders, but federal regulators enforce strict capital-call backstops.
- Crypto and digital assets: venture-backed banks seek to bridge traditional banking clearing with on-chain stablecoins and tokenized commercial receivables.
