# Notes: Utopai Becomes a Unicorn as Video AI Takes Off

The Information, Rocket Drew, 2026-10-22.

## Video AI renaissance and Utopai Studios

- Video artificial intelligence is undergoing rapid commercial acceleration as clip length and generation quality improve.
- Mountain View film startup Utopai Studios secured a $60 million funding round at a $1 billion valuation, attaining unicorn status, with term sheets received for a follow-on round valuing the business between $2 billion and $3 billion.
- Chief Executive Cecilia Shen, a former AI researcher on Google's X moonshot team, founded Utopai in 2022.
- Unlike software vendors, Utopai operates as an independent film studio, arguing that selling software alone offers negligible profit margins in entertainment and fails to guarantee creative quality.
- Utopai uses a mix of models and fine-tuned open-source model MiniMax H3 to build its own video engine, ranked second on Artificial Analysis text-to-video benchmarks.
- Deploying AI tools cuts scene planning and computer-generated imagery costs by 80% to 90%, though Utopai continues to pay human directors and actors.
- Utopai has more than 20 projects in development, including two television series and three feature films planned for release in approximately 3,000 North American movie theaters in 2027.

## Video AI peers

- AI video startup Higgsfield announced it crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue in September 2026, after quadrupling its valuation to $5.4 billion in August.
- Kling AI, the video generation division of Chinese social media firm Kuaishou Technology, raised nearly $3 billion at a $15 billion valuation in summer 2026.
- Media executive Jeffrey Katzenberg partnered with the former head of OpenAI's Sora app to establish an AI film production company in discussions to license and customize Sora.

## Infrastructure and frontier lab updates

- Chinese AI lab DeepSeek is completing roughly $15 billion (approximately 100 billion yuan) in fresh financing led by electric vehicle battery giant CATL and Tencent.
- GPU cloud provider Nebius closed a $3.5 billion senior secured credit facility arranged by Citi.
- Nvidia announced multi-year supply agreements with chip substrate manufacturers Ibiden, Samsung Electro-Mechanics, and Unimicron, including a multibillion-dollar reservation with Ibiden to expand substrate manufacturing capacity by 2.5 times over three years.
- OpenAI announced that its frontier models will be accessible via Google Cloud, reducing its historical exclusivity with Microsoft Azure.
- OpenAI expanded ChatGPT advertising across 26 additional countries to generate revenue to cover infrastructure expenses.
- Coding lab Recursive, led by You.com founder Richard Socher, launched Flint, a free open-weight code generation model small enough to run locally on personal laptops.
