# Notes: OpenAI Restricts Adobe and Other Competitors From Advertising in ChatGPT

The Information, Alix Coutures and Amir Efrati, 2026-09-09 (exclusive).

## The core story

- OpenAI told some business partners it will no longer take ChatGPT ads for products that generate images or audio. Those products compete with ChatGPT's own features.
- The change had not been reported before and is not in OpenAI's published ads policy.
- It caught existing advertisers off guard, including Adobe, which sells such tools.
- An OpenAI spokesperson declined to comment.

## How it came out

- Doug Wyatt, senior director for Americas Media at Adobe, said OpenAI notified ad partners they could no longer advertise image and audio generation.
- Adobe was part of ChatGPT's ads pilot early in 2026. It ran ads for Acrobat Studio and Adobe Firefly (its AI image generator), and kept running both after the pilot.
- Wyatt on LinkedIn: ChatGPT is narrowing "both the paid and organic pathways" for tools in categories where it now competes. He added that image and voice/audio are closed, while video is still open for the moment.
- Timing: OpenAI announced ChatGPT Images 2.5 (an image model update) on Tuesday. In July it unveiled ChatGPT Live, a set of voice models for hands-free use.

## Is this normal?

- There is precedent. Media companies often refuse ads from competitors. News outlets rarely run ads for direct rivals, and streaming services often turn away rival ads.
- Alan Chapell, president of consultancy Chapell & Associates (advises marketers on earning consumer trust): platforms usually do not cut off partners until they reach a certain size. As OpenAI's features overlapped with Adobe's, hosting Adobe ads got harder to justify.
- Chapell said he expected the clash to come only "a year or two down the road."

## OpenAI's ad business

- More than 1 billion people use ChatGPT at least once a month. Most do not pay, which pushes OpenAI to find other ways to earn from them.
- February 2026: ads started showing for some U.S. users, with big launch advertisers such as Ford.
- Early hiccups: some advertisers complained OpenAI lacked the tools to measure whether their ads worked.
- Ads have since spread to more than 40 countries, with more expansion planned, and OpenAI has streamlined parts of the process.
- Last month OpenAI said ad revenue hit an annualized pace of $1 billion, a fast rise over six months. "Annualized" means the current monthly rate multiplied by 12.
- But it had told investors it hoped for $2.4 billion in ad revenue for all of 2026, and it has given investors aggressive ad growth projections.
- In August OpenAI called ads one pillar of its business, next to consumer subscriptions, enterprise offerings and usage-based APIs. An API is the plug-in point developers use to build on OpenAI's models.

## The tension

- If the ban spreads to other competing products, it could shrink OpenAI's potential ad revenue, just as ads are meant to monetize free users.
- The more products OpenAI builds itself, the more categories of advertiser it may turn away.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- AI economics: OpenAI's ad business is at $1 billion annualized versus a $2.4 billion 2026 goal, and its own product expansion may cap it.
- Model labs vs the rest: a lab that also sells apps becomes a gatekeeper that can close distribution to rivals like Adobe.
- Retail and agents angle: ChatGPT as a shopping and discovery surface decides which brands get paid placement, much like a store choosing what goes on its shelves.
