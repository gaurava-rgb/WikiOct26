# Notes: Nvidia Has Put More Than $170 Billion on the Line for Neoclouds

The Information, Shane Burke and Phoebe Liu, 2026-10-20.

## Central bank for neoclouds

- Nvidia has acted as a financial backstop for companies building AI cloud infrastructure beyond the tech giants.
- As of its July 2026 fiscal quarter regulatory filings, Nvidia disclosed up to $164.5 billion in guarantees and other commitments to support new data centers.
- These commitments include agreements to pay landlords if cloud tenants default on leases, as well as promises to purchase compute capacity.
- On top of those lease guarantees, Nvidia has invested or agreed to invest more than $7 billion directly into AI cloud providers, putting over $170 billion on the line.
- Nvidia's total equity portfolio was valued at nearly $100 billion as of July 2026, spanning data center builders like SB Energy and AI research labs like OpenAI and Anthropic.
- Nvidia investments provide seed capital for project buildout costs and graphics processing unit purchases that lenders refuse to finance entirely.

## Market backlash and delayed public listings

- Public equity markets have soured on AI cloud providers and their capital-intensive expansion plans.
- Shares in publicly traded AI cloud firms CoreWeave, Nebius, and IREN dropped sharply in mid-October 2026.
- Nvidia-backed cloud startups Firmus and Lambda have pulled or postponed planned initial public offerings.
- Data center developer SB Energy and GPU cloud operator Nscale published IPO registration documents in September 2026 but have not proceeded with listings.
- Data center project financiers have become increasingly nervous about rising debt and refinancing risks.
- Investor demand has grown for financial protection against default on Nvidia's own corporate debt.
- Nvidia is in discussions to provide a fresh direct investment of hundreds of millions of dollars to Firmus after Firmus cancelled its IPO.

## Financial risks and revenue mix

- Non-hyperscaler customers, including neoclouds and large corporations, accounted for 45% of Nvidia data center revenue in the July 2026 quarter.
- That amounted to $40 billion in quarterly revenue from non-hyperscalers, more than double the level from one year earlier.
- Major hyperscalers Google, Amazon, and Microsoft are accelerating investments in their own in-house custom AI chips to replace Nvidia GPUs.
- Nvidia Chief Financial Officer Colette Kress stated on an August 2026 earnings call that the company's financial risk from backing AI labs is limited because it can redeploy graphics processing units to other buyers if defaults occur.
- Nvidia commitments would not all become due simultaneously if cloud customers fail.
