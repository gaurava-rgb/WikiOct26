# Notes: Networking Startup Eliyan Draws Takeover Interest, Held Early Talks With Arm

Dealmaker newsletter, Valida Pau, 2026-10-08. Cory Weinberg contributed.

## The core story

- Semiconductor M&A (mergers and acquisitions, meaning companies buying each other) has been busy. One of the next deals could be for Eliyan.
- Eliyan's technology lets separate chips inside an AI system talk to each other faster and more efficiently.
- The five-year-old startup was valued at $1 billion in July 2026.
- In the last few days before publication, Eliyan told some investors it had received a takeover bid, per people familiar with the matter.
- It has worked with an investment bank since summer 2026 and will likely aim for a valuation of $3 billion, per some of those people.
- Arm, which is owned by SoftBank and is already an Eliyan investor, held early talks about buying Eliyan. Arm did not bid and would be unlikely to at that price, per a person familiar with the matter.
- Eliyan and Arm declined to comment.

## Why founders expect rich exits

- The interest follows big exits for other chip firms. An exit is when early investors cash out through a sale or IPO.
- Examples given: Nvidia's $20 billion licensing deal for Groq, and Cerebras' $56 billion IPO (first sale of stock to the public).
- High-valuation funding rounds too, such as Etched's $21 billion fundraise.
- These have raised founders' hopes of a rich exit.

## The company

- Founded in 2021, Eliyan has raised more than $250 million.
- Backers include large chip and tech companies such as AMD, Cisco and Meta, plus venture firms Seligman Ventures and Tiger Global Management.
- Co-founder and CEO: Ramin Farjadrad.

## The problem it solves

- AI chips are often built from chiplets: smaller chip pieces packaged together.
- Chiplets send data to each other and to high-bandwidth memory (fast memory stacked next to the chip) over traditional copper links, which can be slow.
- Slow links can leave AI chips idle no matter how fast they are. That is a key bottleneck for large chipmakers.
- Eliyan says its product fixes some of copper's problems with a design that uses light and electronics together (an optical interconnect, meaning a link that carries data as light).
- Farjadrad, speaking to The Information's TITV in July 2026, said copper has limited distance, and Eliyan's optical solution matches copper on reliability, power and cost but with "much longer reach," to connect AI racks together.

## Other chip-component deals

- Big buyers have been snapping up chip component makers to make AI chips run more efficiently.
- In February 2026, Marvell Technologies bought Celestial AI, which develops optical interconnect technology, for up to $5.5 billion, even though Celestial had no revenue at the time.
- In July 2026, Analog Devices bought Empower Semiconductor, whose devices help AI chips manage electricity more efficiently, for $1.5 billion.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- AI shift 2024-2026 story: the bottleneck moves past the chip itself to the links between chips, and that plumbing now draws billion-dollar deals.
- Meta journey: Meta is an investor in Eliyan, part of its push into AI hardware.
- Nvidia journey: the $20 billion Groq licensing deal is now the benchmark for chip-startup exits.
- Arm and SoftBank appear as possible chip-startup buyers, though price-sensitive.
