# Notes: Did Anthropic Miss Its Peak IPO Moment?

The Information, Martin Peers, 2026-10-15.

## Slowing growth and IPO valuation ceiling

- Anthropic faces questions about whether it delayed its initial public offering too long after disclosing that third-quarter revenue growth decelerated from the second quarter.
- Columnist Martin Peers argues Anthropic may have experienced its peak valuation moment around June 2026, when SpaceX completed its own public listing.
- Manhattan Venture Partners managing partner Jared Carmel stated the IPO will need to price to sell, projecting a valuation range between $1.4 trillion and $1.5 trillion rather than the $2 trillion bankers discussed earlier.
- A valuation of $1.4 trillion to $1.5 trillion would position Anthropic as the ninth-largest tech company by market capitalization, behind Broadcom and ahead of Tesla.

## Frontier model moat and corporate spending pushback

- Decelerating growth highlights persistent doubts about Anthropic's competitive moat as enterprises increasingly rely on cheaper, good-enough models rather than costly frontier systems.
- Enterprise customers including Microsoft have cut back spending on Anthropic models in response to aggressive pricing.
- Model marketplace TrustedRouter now lists 665 AI models, primarily open source, giving enterprises abundant alternatives.
- In China, major consumer tech firms such as smartphone maker Xiaomi and delivery giant Meituan are developing in-house models rather than buying frontier access.
- Anthropic faces mounting financial risk from more than $500 billion in long-term compute commitments over the next decade.
- Strict safety standards and regulatory scrutiny have also constrained Anthropic's ability to release its top-tier models.

## Apple's capital-light AI strategy

- In contrast to frontier model builders pouring billions into data centers, Apple avoided the expensive race to build foundational models.
- Apple shares climbed 26% year to date through mid-October 2026, outperforming Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, and Microsoft, which rose 10% to 12%.
- Apple is the only tech titan whose valuation multiple expanded in 2026, per Koyfin data, despite reporting slower revenue growth than its hyperscaler peers.

## Industry developments

- Apple plans to launch Apple Intelligence on-device AI services in mainland China in December 2026 following regulatory clearances.
- Data center hardware startup Mueon raised $65 million to re-engineer AI server rack layouts, claiming up to 10 times more compute density per watt.
- Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. confirmed plans to construct an additional 3-nanometer fab in Tainan, scheduled for production in early 2028.
- CoreWeave and Broadcom formally objected to OKXICE plans to trade tokenized blockchain versions of their corporate shares.
- Anthropic began deploying embedded internal evaluators with direct system access to monitor safety protocols and publish independent assessments.
