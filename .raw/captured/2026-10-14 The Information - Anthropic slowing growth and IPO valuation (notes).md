# Notes: Anthropic’s Rapid But Slowing Growth Could Weigh on IPO Valuation

The Information, Cory Weinberg, 2026-10-14.

## The core story

- Anthropic reported rapid but decelerating revenue growth during a private meeting with prospective investors in San Francisco for its planned initial public offering.
- Revenue reached $16.8 billion in the third quarter of 2026, rising approximately 45% from the second quarter, per people briefed on the numbers.
- Third-quarter revenue was more than 3.6 times Anthropic's total revenue for the entire year of 2025.
- The 45% quarterly growth rate marks a slowdown from the second quarter, when demand for Claude Code propelled revenue up more than 140% compared to the first quarter.
- Slower growth and shifting market conditions have cooled investor enthusiasm, leading some attendees to expect bankers will trim Anthropic's IPO valuation target to less than $1.5 trillion, down from earlier private whispers of $2 trillion.

## Financial margins and distillation revenue

- Anthropic reported an adjusted operating margin of 11% for 2026 through the third quarter, per one person briefed on the figures.
- While the operating margin excludes significant expenses like stock compensation, it indicates Anthropic is making progress toward profitability.
- The company disclosed that 5% to 7% of its revenue comes from distillation, where other firms use Anthropic outputs to train their own AI models.
- Anthropic has accused Chinese AI laboratories of distilling its models and has taken technical countermeasures.
- Anthropic warned investors that distillation revenue will decline and continue to weigh on financial results in the current quarter.

## IPO timing and data center headwinds

- Anthropic plans to stage its initial public offering in November 2026 before the Thanksgiving holiday.
- The offering represents the biggest test yet of public investor appetite for debt-heavy artificial intelligence infrastructure.
- Rising borrowing costs and falling stock prices for publicly traded AI data center operators have soured broader market sentiment.
- AI data center operator Firmus Grid, backed by Nvidia, cancelled its planned Australian initial public offering due to market volatility.
- Anthropic's private market valuation rose to $965 billion in May 2026, compared with less than $20 billion two years earlier.

## Competitive pressures and revenue metrics

- Strong demand for Claude Code and Cowork enabled Anthropic to overtake OpenAI in revenue earlier in 2026.
- OpenAI has responded by strengthening its enterprise AI tools and discounting prices below Anthropic.
- Google and Meta Platforms have introduced frontier models that match Anthropic's performance across several benchmarks.
- Facing higher compute bills, corporate buyers are turning to lower-cost options, including open source models.
- Anthropic withheld an updated annualized revenue figure at the meeting. Annualized revenue reached $65 billion in July 2026 but faced heightened scrutiny following metric confusion at OpenAI.
