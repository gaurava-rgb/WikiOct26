# Notes: The Party Is Still Going Strong for Private Tech

The Information, Valida Pau, 2026-10-15.

## Public versus private market sentiment divergence

- A sharp sentiment disconnect opened between public and private technology markets at Morgan Stanley's annual private tech conference in Dana Point, California.
- Public market investors are increasingly alarmed by heavy debt loads among AI infrastructure operators, marked by surging yields on CoreWeave debt, a broad semiconductor selloff, and sharp share price drops for CoreWeave and Nebius.
- Australian data center developer Firmus pulled its planned initial public offering amid rising public market skepticism.
- Diana Doyle, head of technology equity capital markets at Morgan Stanley, noted that private markets still prioritize and reward growth above all else, whereas public markets now demand strict financial discipline.

## AI infrastructure debt and valuation pushback

- Decelerating third-quarter growth at Anthropic prompted conference attendees and investment bankers to lower the startup's expected IPO valuation below $1.5 trillion, compared to earlier $2 trillion discussions.
- Public investors expressed intense skepticism toward highly leveraged data center operators seeking public listings.
- Data center builder Nscale sought to raise up to $3 billion in an initial public offering at a valuation exceeding $35 billion, according to the Financial Times.
- Fund managers questioned whether debt-laden data center operators can successfully complete public offerings at requested price targets when revenue depends on unbuilt facilities.

## High demand for private inference and defense startups

- Private startup investors showed intense enthusiasm for specialized inference platforms including Fireworks AI, Baseten, and Fal.
- These inference providers are experiencing surging developer demand as enterprises adopt open-source models that undercut pricing from OpenAI and Anthropic.
- Defense technology startups including Anduril, Hadrian, and Saronic attracted strong investor interest after closing multi-billion dollar valuation rounds.
- Other heavily pursued private companies included Blue Origin, Neuralink, chip designer Etched, and enterprise software startup Legora.
- David Chen, global head of technology investment banking at Morgan Stanley, argued that public market access remains a decisive competitive weapon because public listings unlock flexible capital instruments like convertible bonds.
