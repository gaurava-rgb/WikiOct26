# Notes: OpenAI Accelerates Ad Sprint With Meta Hires, Slashed Prices

The Information, Alix Coutures, 2026-10-15.

## Rapid hiring and organizational churn

- OpenAI has accelerated its advertising push by hiring dozens of commercial staff from Meta Platforms and slashing ad rates.
- Nearly half of the roughly 140 OpenAI employees working on ads joined between July and September 2026, according to employee profile data from Crustdata.
- Ad chief Dave Dugan spent 12 years at Meta before joining OpenAI as vice president of global ad solutions.
- The ad division has reorganized seven times since its February 2026 debut, according to people familiar with the matter.
- Former Facebook executive Fidji Simo stepped back from her full-time role in early July 2026.
- Dugan restructured the sales staff into industry verticals such as retail, travel, and finance, plus a dedicated team for ad agencies, mirroring sales structures at Google and Meta.
- Senior recruits include applications CTO Vijaye Raji (10 years at Meta), Australia and New Zealand ad leads Mitch Pilar (12 years at Google) and Andy Sinn (10 years at Meta), and EMEA ad head Mark Bambury (Roblox and X).

## Financial performance and advertiser growth

- Annualized revenue from ChatGPT ads reached $1 billion in late August 2026, up from zero when the pilot launched in February.
- OpenAI neared a $50 billion annualized revenue run rate across its entire business at the end of September 2026, driven by subscriptions, Codex, and model sales.
- Despite rapid growth, OpenAI is likely to fall short of the $2.4 billion in total 2026 ad revenue it projected to investors earlier in the year.
- Active advertisers on ChatGPT jumped to roughly 60,000 in early October 2026, up threefold from 20,000 in late August, according to New Street Research estimates.
- U.S. ad spending on ChatGPT is projected to grow 40% in October from September, according to Sensor Tower, with advertising active across more than 60 countries.
- Most advertisers still spend experimental budgets of 1% to 5% of their total ad budgets on ChatGPT, though several marketing agencies report clients allocating larger shares.

## Pricing advantages, SMB shift, and customer acquisition

- OpenAI originally courted big brands including Expedia, Target, and Ford, but has shifted toward small and midsize businesses, using outsourcing vendors like TP, Concentrix, and Accenture for sales.
- Ad pricing has fallen significantly since launch. A click on a ChatGPT ad costs about $5, compared to $30 to $50 on Google for identical business software clients, per InterTeam Marketing.
- Marketing agency Nakora reported that $1,000 in ad spend yields about 10 customers on ChatGPT, compared to five on Meta and one on Google.
- Some marketing clients allocate between $1,000 and $30,000 daily to ChatGPT campaigns, with some allocating up to 40% of their total budgets.

## Product roadmap and targeting limitations

- About 20% of all queries on ChatGPT indicate an explicit intent to purchase a product or service, according to Dugan.
- The initial February 2026 ad format consisted of sponsored links displayed below answers to user questions.
- OpenAI is expanding beyond query-response ads into visual and video formats designed to reach consumers before they start searching.
- Advertisers complain that ChatGPT remains a black box because the platform does not reveal which specific user conversations trigger ad impressions.
- An analysis of roughly 187,000 ad placements by research firm Adthena found that one in nine ads was completely unrelated to the user query, including sensitive mismatches.
