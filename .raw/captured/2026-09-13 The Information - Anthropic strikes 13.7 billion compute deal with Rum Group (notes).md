# Notes: Anthropic Strikes $13.7 Billion Compute Deal With Trump-Linked Rum Group

The Information, Valida Pau and Phoebe Liu, 2026-09-13 (exclusive).

## The core story

- Anthropic signed a computing deal worth $13.7 billion over six years with Rum Group (per a person familiar with the matter).
- Rum Group has roots in social media and long-standing ties to the Trump administration.
- It is the newest in a run of cloud deals Anthropic made in the past year, with big clouds (Google), tech firms (SpaceX) and smaller "neoclouds" (GPU-rental startups) such as Nscale. Demand for Claude Code and Cowork drove them.
- Those earlier deals total at least 14.8 gigawatts and up to $517 billion over the next decade. The Rum deal adds to that.
- Rum had disclosed a $13.7 billion contract in an August securities filing but did not name the customer.

## Who Rum Group is

- Formerly Rumble. Runs the Rumble video platform, popular with conservatives, and hosts President Donald Trump's Truth Social. It also carries official White House livestreams.
- Founded in 2013 as a YouTube alternative for smaller creators; grew among conservatives after the 2020 election.
- May 2021: raised $25 million from investors including Peter Thiel and J.D. Vance, at a $500 million valuation.
- June 2026: bought German neocloud Northern Data in an all-stock deal (paid in Rum shares, not cash) valued at about $767 million, then renamed itself Rum Group.
- Northern Data brought 22,000 Nvidia Hopper-generation GPUs (mostly rented to customers in Europe and the U.S.) and a data center site under development in Georgia.

## The Georgia data center

- Anthropic would lease compute at a data center in Maysville, Georgia, still being built.
- Its key asset is scarce grid access: 120 megawatts, with potential to grow to 180 MW.
- Northern Data said last year it would be operational late this year; unclear if that still holds.
- It could cost more than $10 billion to build and fill with server chips, based on prior reporting that one gigawatt of AI data center capacity costs about $60 billion to build and power. Rum has not said what it will cost.

## Paying for it

- Rum's August filing said it did not yet have financing for the building, GPUs or other equipment, and expected to fund much of it with more debt or equity (selling new shares).
- Rum plans to borrow for the Georgia project (per the person).
- CEO Chris Pavlovski told The Information in June the company preferred debt for more chip purchases, possibly with outside companies providing credit support (guarantees that make the loans safer for lenders).
- As of June, Rum wanted to buy $270 million of Nvidia Blackwell 300 chips for a separate customer contract with Together AI, per earlier reporting.

## Anthropic's stock option

- Anthropic gets an option to buy up to 51 million Rum shares for one cent each (per the person). That means near-free shares if it keeps buying compute.
- Rum stock closed Friday at $7.17. With 400 million shares outstanding, its market cap (total value of all shares) is about $2.8 billion.
- Half the options are tied to the Maysville site. The other half unlock if Anthropic buys about 400 to 500 MW more at another site Rum is developing.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- Compute scarcity: Anthropic is now renting from a small, unfinished, unfunded site because 120 MW of grid power is that valuable.
- Model labs vs clouds: neoclouds tie themselves to labs, and labs take equity sweeteners (penny options) in return for big contracts.
- AI economics: a $13.7 billion contract against a $2.8 billion company that must borrow to build shows how much risk sits with small suppliers.
